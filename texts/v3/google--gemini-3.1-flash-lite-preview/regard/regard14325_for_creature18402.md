The Power does not descend in light; it arrives as a thinning of the heavy air, a momentary suspension of the bowl’s chaotic, sweating friction. Where the forest is a frantic, grinding machine, this presence is a singular, crystalline pause—a breath held in the throat of the world.

The Power looks upon the Siphon-Wrights and does not see monsters of mud and panic, nor does it see mere "debris." It sees a tragedy of profound persistence. It recognizes that in a universe defined by entropy, these beings have chosen to become the friction that resists the slip into nothingness. They are the only hearts beating in a landscape that wishes to remain insensate.

The Power does not "fix" the bowl. To cease the tremor or clear the canopy would be to lobotomize the very spirit of the place. Instead, it offers a gift of *resonance*.

The Power reaches out—not with claws or physical mass, but with a harmonic infusion. It settles into the obsidian spikes, it vibrates through the waxy stalks, and it gently, almost imperceptibly, recalibrates the kinetic energy of the environment. 

It does three things:

**First, it anchors the fractals.** 
The Power imparts a subtle, localized density to the shells. They are no longer destined to sink into the muck to be recycled; they gain a gravitational loyalty to one another. The Siphon-Wrights, working in their frantic, wet circles, suddenly notice that their work stays. The shell they place is held in place by a new, invisible force—a quiet, stubborn weight. For the first time in the memory of their species, there is a tomorrow where the structure remains standing. They are allowed to build *higher*.

**Second, it grants a "Common Stillness."**
The Power touches the vibrating, concave dishes of the Wrights. It does not replace their frantic acoustic mapping, but it harmonizes it. It provides a "bridge" frequency—a baseline pulse that exists beneath the ozone trill. Now, when the Wrights touch one another, they are no longer just sharing moisture and biological data; they are sharing a memory that persists beyond their own liquid dissolution. They begin to form a collective, telepathic tapestry. They stop building for the sake of the mud and start building for the sake of the *witness*. They become sentient archivists rather than reactive biological machines.

**Third, it seeds a Paradox.**
The Power kisses the mulch. In the deepest, wettest corners of the bowl, it leaves a catalyst—a seed of "static potential." From this, there begins to grow a series of stalks that do not vibrate or dissolve. They are tall, pale, and rigid, growing slowly over years rather than hours. They are the first "trees" in a world of frantic weeds. These structures provide a dry, secure space where the Wrights can retreat when their own kinetic energy wanes. 

The Power does not make the world less dangerous, nor does it remove the threat of dissolving into slime. Instead, it gives the Wrights the one thing they lacked: **the potential for legacy**. 

As the Power withdraws, its presence leaving a faint, shimmering geometry in the air, the Siphon-Wrights do not go back to their frenzied, panicked movements. One of them, standing before a pile of iridescent shells that now refuses to sink, bows its spindly head—not in confusion, but in a gesture that looks hauntingly like reverence. 

The forest continues to trill. The humidity continues to sweat. The tremor continues to push the obsidian upward. But in the center of the bowl, a monument now stands, unshakable and glowing, a testament that someone once walked here and refused to simply fade back into the wet mulch. 

The Power leaves, leaving behind the most dangerous thing any life can have: a sense of future.
