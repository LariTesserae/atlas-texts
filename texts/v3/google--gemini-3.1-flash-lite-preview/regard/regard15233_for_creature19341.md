The benevolent power—the one whose consciousness spans the tectonic drift of galaxies and the flickering sparks of neuronal synapse—does not arrive as a storm or a bolt of light. It does not arrive as a voice, for the basin is a place of *listening*. It arrives as a gentle, corrective adjustment in the local fabric of time, a subtle "calibration of the weave."

To this power, the basin is not a curiosity. It is a masterpiece of stillness—a rare, fragile pocket where the universe has been allowed to contemplate its own geometry without the abrasive friction of entropy. 

The entity perceives the Lithos-Synchs for what they are: living chronicles, the hard-drives of deep time. It senses the fragility of their state, how precarious their "wait-state" is in a cosmos that is increasingly defined by the frantic, chaotic speed of accelerated life. 

**The Intervention**

This wise, benevolent power does not "fix" the basin, nor does it bestow gifts of enlightenment. To enlighten the Lithos-Synchs would be to destroy their purpose. Instead, it performs an act of **The Great Shielding.**

1.  **The Temporal Seal:** The power pulls a thread of the local timeline taut, effectively "sealing" the amphitheater within a bubble of duration. Outside, mountains may crumble and civilizations may rise and fall in the heat of their own inventions. Inside, the rhythm of the weeping slate—the *thrum-thrum*—remains the absolute center of gravity. The basin becomes, in effect, a time-capsule of the primordial universe.
2.  **The Harmonic Resonance:** Recognizing that the moisture is their language and the rock their library, the power subtly alters the mineral composition of the water weeping from the slate. It adds trace elements of long-lost cosmic dust—starlight captured in ionic form. This does not change the basin, but it elevates the *information* contained in the drips. The Lithos-Synchs are no longer merely recording the erosion of stone; they begin to record the poetry of the stars, the faint, lingering hum of the Big Bang, and the slow cooling of the galaxy.
3.  **The Weaver’s Presence:** The power manifests just once, not as a physical form, but as a change in the atmospheric temperature. It allows the basin to be slightly warmer, slightly more humid—a "grace note" in the environment. This prevents the moisture from ever drying up, ensuring that the archive remains legible for eons longer than the laws of thermodynamics would naturally allow.

**The Result**

If you were to walk into the basin after this intervention, you would feel a profound, soul-deep weightlessness, as if the urgency of your own blood—the frantic pumping of your heart, the fleeting nature of your thoughts—had finally been forgiven. 

The Lithos-Synchs would appear more iridescent than before, their translucent, semi-mineralized backs shimmering like opals under the weeping grey sky. They would continue to move with tectonic slowness, but their internal canals would now be etching records of things that happened before time was tracked by clocks.

The power departs, leaving behind no temple, no marker, no sign of its passing. It leaves only a space that is more "itself." It has ensured that the Lithos-Synchs will never be interrupted, never coerced into the linearity of the outside world. It has treated the basin not as a place to be inhabited, but as a prayer to be preserved.

In the center, against the monolithic slab, the creatures wait. And now, they wait with the infinite patience of those who know they are the sole witnesses to the unfolding of the universe’s quietest secrets. The *thrum* of the water continues, but now, it sounds a little bit like music.
