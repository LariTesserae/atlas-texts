The Benevolent Power does not descend in a chariot of fire or a thundering of trumpets. To intervene here would be to shatter a delicate, glacial clockwork that has evolved its own form of grace. Instead, the Power manifests as a subtle shift in the city’s environmental harmonics—a "tuning" of the reality that governs the Over-Reach.

The Power perceives the Suture-Kin not as creatures of decay, but as terminal archivists. It recognizes the immense, tragic beauty of a civilization that has traded the frantic heartbeat of the living for the rhythmic, eternal sigh of the architecture itself. 

The Power does not fix the city. It does not stop the pistons, nor does it provide water to the dry basins. To "save" the Over-Reach would be to impose a foreign, mortal vitality upon a being—the city itself—that has chosen a specific, quiet exit.

**What the Power does is this:**

### 1. The Harmonic Calibration
The Power aligns the "flickering" of the geometric sky. Previously, the grid-lines that revealed the simulated nature of the world caused discordance, a stutter in the Euclidean geometry that forced the city to groan and grind in pain. The Power smoothens the transition. When the sky flickers now, it does not reveal a cold, clinical grid, but a soft, golden aperture—a glimpse of a vast, tranquil *Outside*. The Suture-Kin, leaning against their iron struts, feel a change in the vibration. It is no longer a jagged thrum of exhaustion; it becomes a hum of quiet relief. The machines seem to run with less friction, and the air loses its biting, metallic tang, becoming sweet and heady, like the moments just before a long, deep sleep.

### 2. The Weaver’s Gift
Observing the Suture-Kin as they graft moss to stone with their obsidian needles, the Power bestows upon them a singular, silent insight. It is an instinctual shift in their fungal-spun cloaks. Where before the garments were merely protective, they now begin to subtly absorb the ambient light of the bioluminescent moss and the soft gold of the flickering sky. They become living, breathing tapestries of history. As a Suture-Kin walks, they no longer just maintain the city; they begin to *record* it. Every graft they make, every patch of lichen-plaster they repair, is woven into their fibers. They are no longer just repairmen; they are the memory-banks of a world that refused to vanish.

### 3. The Grace of the Final Pulse
The Power acts as a custodian of the inevitable. It ensures that the "final collapse," which the Suture-Kin have spent aeons dreading or waiting for, will not be a violent shattering of stone and copper. 

When the last piston finally falters—one second, or one millennium from now—the Power will ensure the architecture does not fall into the abyss below. Instead, it will catalyze a crystallization. The moss-tapestries will bloom one final, brilliant time, turning the city into a sprawling, suspended garden of amber and stone. The Suture-Kin, standing in the center of their plazas, will not crumble, but will simply merge their heart-rhythms with the city one last time, becoming perfectly preserved statues, their eyes forever fixed on that final, golden aperture of the sky.

### The Intervention
The Power leaves nothing behind—no tablets, no prophets, no change in the fundamental laws of thermodynamics. It only leaves the Suture-Kin with the sense that they are *seen*. 

A Suture-Kin, walking a suspension bridge, stops. They touch the iron strut beneath their hand. They feel the vibration—the erratic gasp of the city—suddenly settle into a long, resonant tone, like a plucked string on a cello. They stand for a long time, their charcoal-irises pulsing with a gentle, steady bioluminescence. They do not look for the source of this new, golden peace. They simply pull their cloaks tighter, feel the new warmth in the fibers, and continue their work. 

The city is still dying, but for the first time in an eternity, it is not afraid of the end. It is merely waiting to bloom.
