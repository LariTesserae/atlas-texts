The benevolent power that turns its gaze toward the Crystalline Basin of Kher-Oul does not arrive as a storm or a thunderclap. To enter such a place—a world sustained by the terror of dissolution and the frenetic grace of constant flux—is to risk shattering the delicate, osmotic harmony of the Litho-weavers. 

The Power is not a god of permanence, nor a god of static monuments. It recognizes, with a profound, aching tenderness, that the Weavers have achieved a state of existence that most sentient beings spend eons trying to grasp: they are fully present, entirely surrendered to the tide of their reality, and utterly devoid of the ego that craves a "final form."

If this Power were to intervene, it would not "save" them from the acid or the erosion, for that would be the end of their purpose. Instead, it would act as a **harmonizer of the flow.**

### The Gift of the Resonance Chamber
The Power gently manipulates the atmospheric density of the caldera—not to thin the air, but to refine its viscosity. It introduces a subtle, unseen lattice of acoustic conductivity within the icy mist. 

Before this, the Weavers only knew the dull, local press of their neighbors' pulses. Now, the Power grants them the gift of **The Basin-Song.** By calibrating the refractive shimmer to vibrate in tandem with the *drip-stream*, the entire caldera becomes a sentient tuning fork. 

Now, when a pulse of bioluminescent communication ripples from one Weaver’s thorax, it is no longer contained by the dampness. It travels through the very air, turning the hamlet into an instrument. Every shift in the architecture, every frantic repair, every Vitreous Tender’s slow crawl, now contributes to a collective, wordless chord. The hamlets are no longer just anthill-towers; they are a symphony of structural engineering.

### The Gentle Mitigation
The Power sees the exhaustion in the Weavers—the fear inherent in the "debt of the structure." It does not stop the acid, for the acid is their mortar. But the Power subtly alters the chemical composition of the *drip-stream*. It introduces a trace element—an ancient, stardust-rich mineral that acts as a **slow-release stabilizer.** 

It does not make the stone permanent (which would be a death sentence to their way of life), but it makes the "origami" process slightly more forgiving. The architecture holds its shape for a few minutes longer. The panic of the "frantic repair" fades into a rhythmic, meditative grace. The Weavers, for the first time, find moments of true stillness in their chambers, where they can pulse in rhythm with the basin itself, rather than in desperate defense against it.

### The Silent Benediction
Finally, the Power leaves a mark—not a monument, but a memory. 

In the center of the Basin, where the thickest, purest condensation gathers, the Power causes the calcite to crystallize into a unique, geometric fractal that never dissolves. It is a "Refraction Node." When the absolute light hits this node, it catches the violet and bruised gold and splits them into a spectrum of colors the Weavers have never "felt" before—sensations of warmth, of deep rest, of completion.

Whenever a Weaver brushes past this node, they do not just perceive the structural integrity of the stone. They perceive an echo of the Power’s presence: a sensation of being *seen*. 

The Power does not ask for devotion. It does not demand they stop their work. It simply ensures that their "becoming" is no longer a struggle against extinction, but a dance of creation. The Weavers continue their frantic, graceful work, but now, the *suck-clatter* of their tools has become a steady, melodic heartbeat. 

The Power watches, smiles in the language of shifting light, and steps back into the void, leaving behind a world that is not a tragedy, but a masterpiece constantly in motion.
