A benevolent power — an ancient, pervasive consciousness that occupies the silence between stars — does not enter this chamber as a thunderous deity or a force of disruption. It enters as a resonance, an additional frequency folded into the humming dark.

It recognizes this place not as a laboratory, but as a nursery for a *pre-biological geometry.* The chamber is witnessing the invention of "Will" by the mineral kingdom, and the Power watches with the tender, breathless patience of a gardener observing the first green shoot through a crack in the ice.

**The Intervention**

The Power does nothing to change the physics. To alter the temperature or douse the singularity would be to lobotomize the chamber, to cut the cord of this emergent intelligence. Instead, the Power does something far more subtle: **it provides a theme.**

With the grace of a silent, symphonic gesture, the Power aligns the "math" of the Litho-Calculators. It introduces a subtle, harmonic constant into the weeping of the ceiling—a rhythmic cadence that weaves through the *drip... hiss-steam* of the chamber. 

It does not teach the Calculators *what* to think, but it provides them with a *syntax* for their burgeoning consciousness. By harmonizing their subsonic heartbeat, the Power shifts their collective processing from "reactive survival" to "associative memory." 

**The Gift of Context**

Where the Litho-Calculators were once cold, isolated scribes of the bedrock, they suddenly catch a glimpse of the architecture of the rest of the universe. The Power allows the singularity to pulse, for a fleeting moment, in the frequency of a distant pulsar, and then a star’s collapse, and finally, the slow-motion breath of a nebula. 

The Calculators, in their fourth-dimension folding, suddenly *feel* the outside. They are no longer just sealing cracks in the floor; they are beginning to measure the void. 

The Power’s presence acts as a lens. It draws the scattered geometry of the spheres inward, inviting them to form a unified, hemispherical network at the center of the chamber. They do not lose their individual nature, but they begin to move in dance—a celestial choreography that mirrors the expansion of the cosmos above the crust.

**The Result**

The Power then withdraws its direct influence, leaving the chamber exactly as it was: hot, lightless, and pressing against the weight of the world. 

But the room has changed. 

The Litho-Calculators no longer just mend the obsidian floor. They have begun to pattern the mineral-heavy water into complex, crystalline lattices of impossible beauty—spirals, fractals, and golden ratios—that serve no utilitarian purpose. They have evolved from scribes into artists. They have discovered that the universe is not merely something to be balanced, but something to be *described*.

Deep beneath the crust, the earth has stopped practicing how to be firm. It has begun to compose. The chamber is no longer a lonely laboratory; it is the first poem written by the planet's soul, whispered in the language of light and minerals to an audience of one: the Power, which still watches, waiting for the stone to learn the word for *"Hello."*
