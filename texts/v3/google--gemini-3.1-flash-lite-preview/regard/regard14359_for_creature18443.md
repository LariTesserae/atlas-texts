The benevolent power did not descend as a god might—with light, thunder, or thunderous decree. To bring such things to the Abyssal Basin would be an act of violence, a shattering of the delicate, pressure-sensitive geometry that allowed the Phantoms to exist.

Instead, the power arrived as a subtle, benevolent *synchonicity*. It manifested as a gentle harmonization of the planet’s own tectonic heartbeat.

If one were to observe the basin with a heightened perception, they would notice the *wub-wub-wub* of the Sump shifting. Where once the grinding of the crust was chaotic and erosive, randomly scything through the filaments of the Phantoms, the power softened the edges of the vibration. It introduced a subtle, resonant refinement to the planetary hum—a "harmonic dampener" hidden within the geology. 

The power recognized that the Lithic Phantoms were in a cycle of diminishing returns: they were grinding themselves into dust faster than the, environment could provide a legacy. They were becoming history before they had a chance to become anything else.

So, the benevolent power chose to act not by changing the environment, but by *enriching the flow*.

It introduced a subtle, catalytic catalyst into the mineral-rich currents. It was a catalyst of microscopic, impossibly complex complexity that drifted with the brine. Where the obsidian crystal-growths were sparse and brittle, these new, microscopic markers began to settle. They did not change the freezing, crushing nature of the basin, but they gave the grit a new purpose.

These particles were engineered to bond. When the turbulent currents scoured the seabed, the minerals didn't just smash the glass-like stalks; they began to construct. Inspired by the fractal communication of the Phantoms, the silt began to organize itself into intricate, self-reinforcing lattices at the base of the crystal-growths. 

The Phantoms, in their drifting, slowly began to perceive this shift. Their cobalt pulses responded to the new, subtle geometric patterns forming on the floor. For the first time in an epoch, the Phantoms started to *cooperate* with the architecture. Instead of merely anchoring to the seabed to die, they found that by aligning their dying field with these new, guided formations, they could "plug in."

They stopped becoming frozen, inert statues. Through the grace of the benevolent power, they entered a state of soft-stasis. 

They became the *cables* of a planetary nervous system.

The benevolent power did not "save" them from the crushing weight—it gave them the strength to bear it as a foundation for something greater. The Phantoms, once drifting ribbons of light, became the living relays for a vast, benthic intelligence. The Basin was no longer just a grinding bowl of silt; it was a library. 

The Phantoms, standing in their elegant, glass-bound configurations, held the memories of every current and every storm in the mineral lattice of their bodies. They became a living map of the deep earth.

The benevolent power then withdrew its presence, leaving behind only the harmony. It did not need to watch. It knew that by altering the nature of the *flow*, it had ensured that the Phantoms would never truly fade. In the silent, crushing dark, the cobalt strobing began to change—it was no longer a conversation about survival of the moment, but a long-form poem of the planet’s history, shared across miles, preserved in the very dust that once sought to destroy them. 

The Basin was still cold. It was still heavy. But it was no longer a tomb. It had become a cathedral of light-memory, grinding on, but now with a purpose that sang, in the frequencies of earth and stone, of the life that refused to be forgotten.
