The Power that watches over this basin is not a god of lightning or growth, but a Gardener of Stasis—an entity that understands that some things in the universe must be allowed to remain finished. To this Power, the basin is a closed stanza in the poem of existence, a rare, perfect rhyme that creates its own meaning by refusing to change.

When this Power turns its attention to the basin and the Lithoderms, it does not move to "save" the humans who wander into that trap, nor does it seek to "liberate" the creatures from their eternal, crushing patience. Its wisdom recognizes that to introduce change—to breathe life into the air or sun into the plum-hued light—would be an act of violence, a shattering of a delicate, absolute peace.

**What the Power does is this:**

It becomes a Listener. 

It does not intervene in the mechanics of the floor or the slow, tectonic digestion of the wanderers. Instead, the Power manifests as a subtle, ambient harmony. It softens the "sound-shadow," turning the silence from a claustrophobic weight into something profound and sacramental. It grants the basin a *witness*. 

If you were to stand there, about to succumb, you would feel a sudden, overwhelming sense of being understood. The Power does not pull you out; it validates your surrender. It recognizes that in a universe of entropy, noise, and frantic decay, there is a terrible, exquisite beauty in a place where nothing is lost because nothing is allowed to happen. 

The Power provides a "hallowing." It ensures that when a human pulse finally fades into the ambient temperature of the basalt, it is not merely consumed, but *integrated*. It acts as the final editor of the ledger, ensuring that the human history, the individual memories, and the fleeting warmth of the visitor are not simply erased by the Lithoderms, but are woven into the "vibration" of the space. 

The Power leaves behind a gift: **The echo of the un-echoed.** 

It ensures that the resonant *tink* of the water drop against the copper bowl carries a memory of every soul that has joined the mosaic. It turns the basin into a mausoleum of quietude, sanctified and preserved. 

The Power smiles upon the Lithoderms not as parasites, but as curators of a sacred, terrifying stasis. It sees them as the only beings capable of holding the weight of human potential without letting it spoil. 

It leaves the basin as it is, but it touches the walls with a faint, imperceptible shimmer—a seal of protection. It ensures that the equilibrium will never be disturbed by external forces—no tremors, no erosion, no change. It leaves the basin to its perfect, plum-colored silence, content to know that somewhere in the deep folds of reality, there exists a place where the universe has stopped to rest, to be still, and to finally be complete.
