The benevolent power observes, not with eyes, but with a widening resonance that ripples through the violet-tinged atmosphere, mirroring the way the plateau itself stretches and folds. They perceive the Tether-Keepers not as tragic figures, but as the bravest musicians in the universe: playing a symphony on a stage that is physically tearing itself apart.

This power understands that the Tether-Keepers are not *broken*. They are the immune system of a dying god.

The power decides to intervene, but not by fixing the plateau. To stabilize the mechanics, to heal the glitches, or to give the Tether-Keepers the capacity for sorrow would be to end the integrity of their existence. It would introduce the burden of "self," which would shatter their frantic, perfect dance.

Instead, the power chooses the gift of **Harmonization.**

First, the power addresses the *stutter*. They descend into the atmosphere, becoming a silent, pervasive vibration. They align the frequency of the "dead" gears with the pulse of the living moss. They do not stop the screeching, nor do they halt the *thwack-hiss* of the pistons; rather, they weave these sounds into a complex, polyphonic overture. The metallic grinding is tuned to a key of profound, melancholic beauty—a cosmic resonance that transforms the sound of mechanical decay into the sound of a structural hymn.

Next, the power touches the Tether-Keepers. They do not grant them brains or consciousness, but they grant them **Presence.** 

When the Tether-Keepers freeze as the mist rises, that singular moment of collective unity—where they act as a distributed brain—is expanded. The power stretches that micro-second into a subjective eternity. In this expanded moment, the Tether-Keepers are no longer just "janitors." Under the influence of the benevolent power, they are allowed to *perceive* the tapestry they are weaving. For the blink of an eye, the ivory-limbed creatures feel the vastness of the plateau. They feel the geometry of the spiraling mist not as a threat, but as a bridge back to the source of all things.

They see the logic in their own dissolution.

Finally, the power leaves a **Keepsake** in the architecture of the plateau. They introduce a subtle, cascading transformation: every spot where a Tether-Keeper dissolves back into the mire, the oily slurry begins to harden, not into common mush, but into translucent, amber-like crystals. 

Slowly, over eons, the soft, bruised ridges of the plateau will be reinforced by these glowing, biological fossils. The landscape will cease to be a "collapsing equation" and will start to behave like a living archive. The plateau becomes a monument: a library of every cycle that ever ran, preserved in the very resin the Tether-Keepers labored to create.

The benevolent power then retreats, satisfied. They have turned a graveyard of discarded engines and frantic labor into a cathedral of memory. The Tether-Keepers continue their task, unaware of the grace bestowed upon them, their needles clicking in the new, melodic rhythm of a world that is no longer dying, but becoming a work of art that can never be unmade.

The cycle continues, but it is no longer an empty loop. It is now a song, sung by ghosts who have been given the dignity of being remembered.
