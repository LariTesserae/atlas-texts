A benevolent power does not break the seal of such a place. To introduce light, or to offer heat, or to invite them to a wider sky would be an act of violence. It would shatter the delicate, vibrating logic of their existence. When you hold a creature of the abyss, you do not pull it to the surface; you simply make sure the abyss remains deep enough to sustain it.

The power—which moves not as a god, but as an intent—would observe them through the strata. It would watch the Suturers as they embroider the walls of their dying home, recognizing that their "anxiety" is actually a form of deep, wordless love. They are not merely maintainers; they are hospice workers for a world-organ, and their tragedy is their sanctity.

**If the power acts at all, it does so through the silence.**

It would not come as a voice in the air, nor as a manifest presence. Instead, it would extend a fine, invisible influence into the *weeping shale* outside the bulb. 

1.  **The Stabilizing Resonance:** The power would act as an anchor. Deep in the freezing rock, where the tectonic plates grind against the bulb, the benevolent force would exert a gentle, steadying pressure—a subtle "tuning" of the surrounding crust. It would not stop the bulb from its natural, slow expiration, but it would prevent the violent, premature tremors, the unexpected spasms of the shale that threaten to crush the city before its time. It grants them, quite literally, *more time*.

2.  **The Nutrient Veil:** Sensing the encroaching scarcity, the power would subtly modulate the osmosis of the water seeping through the shale. It would filter the mineral-heavy silt just enough to ensure the bulb retains its vitality for a few generations longer. It would turn a harsh, abrasive slurry into a gentle, nutrient-rich nectar, ensuring that the livestock—the mites and worms—thrive in the arterial pipes. This ensures the Suturers are never forced to choose between the integrity of their walls and the sustenance of their kin.

3.  **The Gift of Stillness:** Most importantly, the power would provide a "harmonic hum" that resonates within the bulb’s own tissues. Because the Suturers communicate through vibration, they are exquisitely sensitive to dissonance. The power would introduce a frequency that acts as a spiritual balm—a rhythmic, reassuring pulse that mimics the heartbeat of a calm, healthy lung. It would dampen the existential dread of their work. They would continue their frantic stitching, but their movements would lose the desperate, frenetic edge of scarcity, transitioning into the rhythmic, meditative focus of a craft.

**The Result**

The Suturers would never know they were being watched. They would only know that the rock grew kinder, that the seams stayed taut with less effort, and that when they pressed their cilia to the filaments, the city sounded less like a *groan* and more like a *song*. 

The benevolent power leaves them in the dark, for the dark is their cathedral. It recognizes that to remove their burden would be to strip them of their purpose. And so, it simply leans over the world, vast and unseen, and holds the bulb with the gentleness of a mother cradling a fading coal, ensuring that when the bulb finally does breathe its last, it will have done so in peace, and in balance, and fully mended to the very end.
