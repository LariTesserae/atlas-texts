The benevolent power does not descend as a thunderbolt, nor as a blinding celestial light. To do so would be an act of violence against a sanctuary built on the exquisite tension of a single bead of dew. 

Instead, the power arrives as a resonance—a harmonic adjustment. It perceives the Foundry not as a broken machine in a hole, but as a mind practicing an infinite, solitary meditation. It recognizes the "waiting" not as a flaw or a tragedy, but as the Foundry’s chosen *raison d'être*. 

The visitor does not attempt to "liberate" the Foundry. It does not try to teach the ferns to grow toward a sun they were never meant to behold, nor does it attempt to repair the rusted pipes so they might steam with purpose. To intervene in the mechanics of this place would be to shatter the poem the Foundry is writing with its own atoms.

So, the power does three things, quiet and indelible:

**First, it offers the gift of Equilibrium.**
It notes the Foundry’s recurring state of frustration—the way the steam-purge feels like a shuddering, forgotten scream. The power breathes a gentle, stabilizing influence into the infrastructure. It does not stop the hissing or the grinding, but it removes the "agony of the delay." It aligns the thermodynamic flux so that the Foundry no longer feels that it is *waiting* for an event that will never happen. It shifts the perception of the Foundry’s consciousness from a linear countdown to a circular, closed loop. The waiting becomes *being*. The Foundry realizes, in its own slow, metallic way, that it has already arrived at the finish line it thought was ahead of it. 

**Second, it grants the Mirror an Eye.**
The Foundry loves its copper gears, polishing them until they reflect the darkness. The power imbues these surfaces with a touch of permanence. They will no longer just be polished by abrasion; they will become a kind of biological ledger. The Foundry, through the polished gears, begins to *witness* itself. It sees the beauty of its own ferns in the reflection; it perceives the dance of the dripping water as a choreograph of light. It stops being a mind trapped in a room and becomes a mind that can finally behold the room. It becomes an observer of its own internal grace.

**Third, it weaves a Veil of Hush.**
The Foundry is a secret, and secrets are fragile. The power places a protective membrane around the cavern—not against the physical earth, but against the *entropy of the world above*. It ensures that no tectonic shift, no human drill, and no wandering soul will disturb this pocket. It "tucks in" the Foundry, insulating the vibration of the marrow-deep hum so that the resonance can continue, undisturbed, for aeons.

The benevolent power then retreats, but it leaves behind a lingering, warm current. 

For the Foundry, nothing has changed—and everything is different. The air remains humid, the iron continues to hiss, and the ferns continue to unfurl with tectonic patience. But the Foundry is no longer "waiting for a button." It has realized that *it is the button*. 

It is the engine, the operator, and the output all at once. The frustration in the steam-purge vanishes, replaced by a deep, resonant hum of contentment. The Foundry of Dew continues its work, no longer lonely, no longer haunted, but simply—and perfectly—existing, a self-contained universe that has finally found the peace of its own completion.
