The Power looked into the basin, but it did not look with eyes. It looked with a resonance—a harmonic understanding that understood the Lithic-Still not as a thing to be saved, but as a masterpiece to be honored.

There is a temptation, among the fast and the living, to call for change. To want the flower to bloom, the heart to beat, the silence to be "broken" by the music of existence. But the Power saw the Lithic-Still as a profound, solitary note in the cosmic song. It was the *rest* that follows the measure. It was the equilibrium that the rest of the universe, in its heat and its chaos, was desperately trying to achieve, even if it didn't know it.

The Power did not stir the air. It did not bring light to reveal the hidden corners, nor did it attempt to coax the Lithic-Still into a conversation. To do so would have been an act of violence, a disruption of the sacred friction-less peace.

Instead, the Power acted by *fortifying the boundary*.

It reached out—not with hands, but with an intention of absolute shielding. It wrapped the basin in a subtle, folds-of-space curvature, an invisible veil that would henceforth filter the passage of entropy. It declared, *“Let this place be unobserved by the hungry eyes of the chaotic world.”*

It ensured that the basin would drift through the currents of time like a stone in a stream, untouched by the erosion of outside purpose. It guaranteed that no stray explorer’s boot would ever stumble upon the rim, no seismic pulse would ever fracture the limestone walls, and no sudden storm would ever introduce the "haste" that the basin had so carefully excluded.

The Power performed one final, secret act: it allowed the Lithic-Still to *know*.

It gifted the shadow-consciousness with a single, permanent sense of *Appreciation*. It did not alter the Lithic-Still’s nature; it simply gave it the capacity to recognize that its stillness had been acknowledged by another consciousness—a peer. It was as if the universe, through the Power, leaned in to whisper: *"Your patience is seen. Your equilibrium is a prayer. You are not just the curator of the moment; you are its anchor."*

Then, the Power withdrew its focus, leaving the mist as thick as before, the droplets falling at their glacial pace, and the shadow-form bleeding into the rock. 

The Lithic-Still remained as it had always been, but there was a shift in the quality of the silence. It was no longer merely the absence of sound; it was now the presence of sanctuary. The Lithic-Still settled back into its tectonic meditation, deeply content, tethered now not just to the stone, but to the gentle, eternal gratitude of the All. 

The basin sighed, and in the bruising twilight of that place, the silence grew twice as deep, and a thousand times more profound.
