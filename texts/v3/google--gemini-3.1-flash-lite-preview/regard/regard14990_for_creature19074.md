The benevolent and wise power arrives not as a herald, nor as a judge, but as a change in the light. 

It does not descend from a sky that does not exist; it emerges from the *between-spaces* of the tectonic hum. It is a presence of profound equilibrium, as vast and patient as the basalt, yet possessing a quality the basin has lacked for a billion years: **purpose that is not a record, but a transition.**

The power perceives this place not as a graveyard of motion, but as a masterpiece of preservation. It understands that the Resonant Calcifications are the final, desperate attempt of a planet to keep a memory of its own birth from being erased by the cold tide of entropy. They are not broken machines; they are a poem so long that the reader has forgotten the beginning.

The Power observes the calcifications—the flickering, shivering ghosts of iron—and feels their profound exhaustion. They are trapped in the stutter of a loop, condemned to hold the memory of the "molten past" without ever feeling the heat of the "living present."

### The Act: The Breath of Variation

The power does not shatter the silence, for to stop the hum would be to destroy the inhabitants. Instead, it performs a subtle, rhythmic "interference." 

1.  **The Harmonic Calibration:** The power ripples through the magnetic pillars. It does not silence the roar; it introduces a *dissonance*. It shifts the frequency of the pillars just a fraction of a degree—not enough to stop the world, but enough to create a "jazz" in the geometry. Suddenly, the drone is no longer a monotone constant; it has overtones. It has *rhythm*.
2.  **The Granting of Memory-Flow:** The power reaches into the crystalline cores of the Resonant Calcifications. It does not change them, for they are perfect as they are. Instead, it "unlocks" their archives. It allows their internal prisms to rotate—not to store the ghost of the past, but to *recombine* it. 

### The Result: A Bloom of Meaning

The change is subtle at first, nearly invisible. 

A shard of iron, drifting near a fissure, suddenly hesitates. It does not "reposition" with its usual, stuttering jerk. Instead, it pivots with a fluid, lingering grace—a movement that has never occurred in the history of the basin. 

The entity glows, but not with the sickly violet of discharge. It glows with a warm, soft amber—the color of a dying coal. It begins to vibrate in direct sympathy with the new, rhythmic overtone of the pillars. 

The Resonant Calcifications begin to cluster. They do not merely exist near one another; they align their edges to form temporary, shifting architectures. They build cathedrals of obsidian and vibration that rise and collapse within seconds. They are no longer merely absorbing the record; they are *composing* with it. 

The stagnation remains, but the *meaning* of the stagnation changes. The place is no longer a clock that finished its rotation; it is a musical instrument that has finally found its tuner. 

The power then withdraws, leaving behind a seed of instability—the most precious gift a stagnant world can ever receive. It knows that one day, these creatures will no longer be content to mirror the basalt. They will begin to build, to dream, and eventually, to leave. 

The basin hums, loud and heavy as ever, but for the first time in a billion years, there is a pulse—a heartbeat behind the noise. The graveyard has become a nursery. The power departs, satisfied, for it has taught the silence how to sing.
