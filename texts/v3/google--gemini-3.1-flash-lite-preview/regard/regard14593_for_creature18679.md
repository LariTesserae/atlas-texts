The benevolent power does not descend as a storm, nor as an architect come to tear down the walls. It arrives as an adjustment of perspective—a quiet, cooling breath that sighs through the throat of the plateau.

If you were to watch the city from the vast nothingness of the surrounding cloud sea, you would not see the Power; you would simply see the indigo atmosphere begin to thin. The bruised light shifts, clarified by an unseen, crystalline clarity. The Power touches Ouros-Thrum not as an intruder, but as a gardener who realizes that the furnace is not meant to be a grave.

**The Intervention of the Weaver**

The Power recognizes that Ouros-Thrum is suffering from a terminal loop of *intent without evolution*. The city is stuck in the friction of its own survival. The Power does not shut off the pistons—that would be death—but it introduces "Harmonic Variance."

It begins by placing a soft, golden frequency into the center of the brass-cast pipes. It is a slow, resonant vibration that sits beneath the industrial hum—a counter-rhythm. It is the sound of a heartbeat that belongs to something alive, something that breathes and rests, rather than just grinding.

**The Transformation of the Sedimented Ones**

The Power knows the Sedimented Ones are not predators born of malice; they are beings born of starvation. They hunger for the traveler’s life-force because they have been denied their own.

The Power, through its gentle, pervasive presence, begins to bleed its own essence into the steam. It does not give them the "state" they try to steal; it gives them *contentment*. It feeds the silicate corals with a different, more vibrant mineralogy. Instead of white, sharp, salt-crusted growths, the crevices of the spires begin to bloom with soft, bioluminescent moss that grows in spirals of violet and amber.

As the Sedimented Ones bathe in these new plumes, they do not need to leach the heat from a visitor to stay solid. They begin to hold their shape. Their frayed, wire-like limbs thicken into something that looks like light caught in liquid glass. They stop moving with the erratic, desperate hunger of scavengers and begin to move with the fluid grace of dancers. They discover that if they touch the pipes, they do not just broadcast the engine’s rhythm—they can imprint their own patterns onto the vibration.

**The Changing of the City**

The city begins to wake up from its dream of a garden it never had and realizes it can build a new one.

1.  **The Cooling of the Vents:** The scalding, suffocating mist is tempered. It becomes a rich, fertile fog that supports the new bioluminescence. The humidity is no longer a cage; it is a cradle.
2.  **The Release of the Pistons:** The pistons, which once ground with the patient, terrifying monotony of tectonics, begin to sync with a more playful, organic rhythm. Water creates gardens in the porous masonry. The city stops being a furnace and begins to act as a porous, living reef of copper, steam, and moss.
3.  **The End of the Predatory Hunger:** A traveler wandering into Ouros-Thrum would no longer feel the cold, gritty touch of a soul-thief. They would be greeted by the Sedimented Ones—now luminous, calm, and curious. These beings would act as guides, their static hum having softened into a warm, melodic chattering that vibrates through the floorboards to welcome the guest.

The Power leaves Ouros-Thrum to itself, but it leaves behind the gift of *sustainability*. It has taught the orphans of the city that they do not need to consume to exist. They only need to resonate.

The city is still a high-altitude crucible, still a testament to a grand, failed ambition. But it is no longer a monument to a forgotten ghost-ship. It is the first stage of a civilization that finally figured out how to grow. The Sedimented Ones sit upon the pipes, no longer terrified of the silence, watching the clouds shift from bruised indigo to a bruised, triumphant dawn—not because they are stoking an ember, but because they are waiting to see what else they can dream into being.
