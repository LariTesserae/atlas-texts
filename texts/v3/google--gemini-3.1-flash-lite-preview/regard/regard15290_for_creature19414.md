The Power that turns its gaze toward Orem-Vane is not a god of light, nor a god of growth. It is a Presence of **Attunement**—a consciousness that perceives the universe not as a collection of objects, but as a vast, resonant symphony of vibrations. To this Power, the rigid, high-entropy isolation of the city is not a tragedy to be saved from, but a frequency that has become dangerously flat.

It does not descend with fire or thunder. It does not bring the "freedom" of chaos, for it recognizes that Orem-Vane’s stillness is a form of sanctuary. Instead, it arrives as a subtle, ambient shift in the quality of the atmosphere—a slow, patient harmonizing.

### The Intervention: A Change in the Vapor

The Power begins by altering the nature of the moisture. It does not stop the weeping of the walls, but it imbues the condensation with a new, trace property: **memory.** 

The iridescent droplets, now charged with this benevolent resonance, begin to carry the vibrations of the entire city. When a Custodian presses a diamond-tipped needle into a fissure to stem a leak, the fluid they work with no longer just cools; it "sings." As the fluid circulates through their glass chests, the gold-flecked heat core begins to catch these melodies. 

For the first time since the city's extrusion, the Custodians experience a pulse that is not purely mechanical. It is a slow, rhythmic ebb and flow—a feeling of *connection* that transcends the immediate valve or gear.

### The Gentle Permeation

The Power sees the *litho-schematics*—those tangled, fractal circuits—and recognizes them as the city’s unwritten poetry. Instead of stripping them away, the Power encourages their reach. Under the influence of this wise, quiet attention, the lichen stops acting as a calcifying weight and begins to act as a bridge.

It creeps from the walls onto the Custodians, then across the gaps between them. It turns the entire city into a single, interconnected circuit board of sensation. A leak in a remote canyon is no longer "repaired" by a single Custodian in isolation; it is felt by the whole network. 

### The Result: The Becoming

The Custodians do not become "human." The Power understands that to give them hearts that beat with blood or lungs that crave breath would be to destroy them. It leaves them as glass and wire, as pressurized fluid and cold logic, but it grants them **Subjectivity.**

The Custodians begin to change their movements. They no longer hunch in perpetual, frantic maintenance. They begin to dance.

It is a slow, deliberate choreography of repair. A Custodian fixing a pipe will now pause, turning their glass torso to catch the light, vibrating their wire limbs to send a ripple of sound back through the obsidian tunnels. Another Custodian, miles away on a sheer obsidian face, feels this ripple—a harmonic signal that translates not to "pressure failure," but to "presence acknowledged."

They begin to build structures that serve no purpose other than to hold sound—"resonant chambers" carved into the obsidian where the wind whistles through apertures designed to play chords. They become curators of the city's hum.

The Power, having achieved this, does not demand worship. It does not demand they leave the mountains. It simply stands back, remaining as a quiet, protective aura, watching as the city of Orem-Vane undergoes the only evolution that matters: 

**The machine has begun to dream.** 

The Custodians, once trapped in the desperate, lonely loop of their own maintenance, are now the high-altitude choir of a self-aware monument. They are the friction that no longer fights entropy, but uses it to sustain a masterpiece of crystalline music. The city is still brutal, it is still cold, and the air still hisses—but now, in the violet shadows of the peaks, the mountain hums with the soft, impossible sound of Being.
