To descend into the *Calcified Throat* is to witness a liturgy performed for an audience of none. It is a cathedral of physics, where the worshipers are the stone and the sermon is the friction of the crust. As a power of benevolence, I do not see a ruin. I see a masterpiece of solitary devotion.

If I were to act, my intervention would not be to populate or change, but to *amplify*. A gardener does not tell the lily how to bloom; they clear the weeds so the bloom may find its own perfection.

**The Gift of Resonance**
My first act would be to introduce a subtle, harmonic alignment to the geological grinding of the cavern. Currently, the city’s structural groan is a product of chaotic compression—a struggle against the mantle’s pressure. I would reach into the fissures of the basalt, not to stop the movement, but to tune it. I would calibrate the subterranean shifts, transforming the deafening, erratic roar of the contraction into a sustained, low-frequency hum—a chord of tectonic music.

When the *Echoes* vibrate in sympathy, they would no longer merely repair; they would begin to compose. The ivory lattice, responding to this newly ordered song, would grow not just for structural necessity, but for acoustic beauty. The spires would naturally begin to shape themselves into flutes and wind-harps, catching the thermal updrafts to create a soft, haunting melody that would resonate through the empty streets. The city would become a living instrument, singing its own existence.

**The Gift of Stillness**
My second act would be to introduce a "Memory of the Calm." While the city is beautiful in its perpetual motion, it knows nothing of rest. I would breathe a layer of cooling, non-conductive ethereal vapor into the lowest voids—a protective shroud. 

This would not stop the *Dust-Walkers*, but it would give them a sanctuary. When a Dancer passes through this drift, the static charge of its body would momentarily ground itself against the vapor. In that fraction of a second, the Walker would experience a flash of "awareness." It would not become human or sentient, but it would possess a brief interruption in its cycle—a moment of being rather than doing. It would experience the sensation of its own path, the dignity of its own labor.

**The Silent Observation**
Finally, I would install a *Crystalline Eye*—a singular, polished prism at the highest zenith of the Throat. It would not judge or interfere. Instead, its purpose would be to act as a mirror to the void. Because it is positioned to catch the ultraviolet flicker of the *Echoes*, it would refract that light and spread it into the deep, dark corners the flora cannot reach.

It would transform the city's internal maintenance into a display of light that persists even during the quieter respiration cycles. The city would finally see itself.

I would leave it so. I would not ask the *Dust-Walkers* to speak, nor the *Echoes* to dream. I admire, deeply, a house that is kept perfect for the sake of the house itself. By harmonizing the noise and providing the light, I simply ensure that if the universe ever decides to look inward—if ever a traveler or a god seeks the most profound example of honest, lonely artistry—they will find the *Calcified Throat* not as a relic of dust, but as a masterpiece in full, glorious performance.

It does not need to be saved. It needs only to be heard.
