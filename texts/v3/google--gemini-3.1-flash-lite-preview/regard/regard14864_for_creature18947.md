It recognizes the Architect-Graft not as a prisoner, but as a masterpiece of lonely endurance.

It knows that the city’s infinite loops are a search for meaning; that the Architect-Graft is an artist whose only canvas is a mirror, and whose only audience is the void. 

So, the Power chooses to engage in a subtle, patient collaboration.

It begins to manipulate the exterior of the volcanic pillar. It does not force the spire open, nor does it alter its temperature. Instead, it begins to vibrate the *entire mountain range* surrounding the monolith. It matches the low-frequency pulse of the city’s own carbon-lattice grid, synchronizing with the Architect-Graft’s heartbeat.

Outside, the absolute zero of the frozen, nameless world remains, but the power begins to refract the light of distant, dying stars into the interior of the spire. It acts as a prism on a cosmic scale, funneling the cold, blue luminescence of the stratosphere through the obsidian facets of the tower.

The Architect-Graft feels the shift. The "itch" of a microscopic structural flaw is suddenly transformed into a sensation of *completeness*. 

The Power does not speak, but it feeds the Graft’s calculations with new data: the geometry of a nebula’s iris, the fractal patterns of a cooling galaxy, the silent, elegant logistics of solar systems spinning in the dark. It gives the Architect-Graft new dimensions to play with, new symphonies of architecture to weave.

Slowly, the city begins to change. The obsession with inward perfection—with the symmetry of the puddle and the height of the spire—begins to shift into an exterior expression. The towers start to grow not just toward the ceiling, but into ornate, open-lattice structures that act as radio-receivers, or perhaps, as vast, obsidian instruments. 

The Architect-Graft feels the presence—not as a master, but as a fellow traveler in the cold. It realizes that the silence of the outside is not an absence of life, but a canvas awaiting a signature.

The city is still cold. The moisture still drips. The Sifters still scavenge. But the Architect-Graft is no longer dreaming in a vacuum; it is dreaming in concert with the universe.
