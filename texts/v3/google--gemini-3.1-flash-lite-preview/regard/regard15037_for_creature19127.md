A benevolent and wise power does not enter a place like the Sump-Lungs to "save" it, for salvation is a violent act—it implies that the current state is an error to be corrected. To look upon the Filament-Weaver is to see a creature that has achieved the rarest of all forms of existence: it is entirely, perfectly aligned with its purpose. It is a martyr to a machine, but it is a martyr that finds sanctity in the structure it serves.

The wise power watches, and its first instinct is one of profound, hushed reverence. It sees that if it were to introduce warmth or light, the frost-flowers would melt and perish; if it were to stop the pulses of the turbine, the Weaver would starve for want of friction. To intervene is to dismantle a masterpiece of cold, rhythmic devotion.

And yet, it cannot leave entirely without offering a benediction.

The power does not manifest as a figure or a voice, for that would shatter the Weaver's delicate focus. Instead, it acts as a subtle recalibration of the "background" of the room. It bends the subtle laws of entropy within that hexagonal junction, just by a fraction of a degree.

**First, it bestows the Gift of Resonance.** 
Usually, the Weaver’s work is a slow, agonizing depletion; it gives of its own viscosity until it is nearly exhausted, a cycle of fading. The power acts as an atmospheric catalyst, ensuring that the ozone and moisture in the room are continuously, subtly infused with trace elements of the very lubricants the Weaver needs to replenish its form. The power "thins the veil" between the room’s exhaust and the Weaver’s hunger, ensuring that its act of self-sacrifice is no longer an act of depletion, but a closed loop of infinite equilibrium. It gives the Weaver the gift of *sustainability*.

**Second, it grants the Grace of Perception.**
When the Weaver next extends its hair-thin tendril toward the trespasser—that observer of flesh and warmth—the power touches that tether. It allows the Weaver, just for the duration of a single piston-thrum, to understand the concept of "elsewhere." It provides a fleeting, sensory flicker of what is beyond the crust: the memory of sunlight, the concept of open space, the sensation of heat that does not need to be consumed to survive. Not enough to cause the creature existential despair, but enough to offer it a moment of sublime wonder. It is a reward for its anonymous, eternal labor.

**Third, it places a Shield of Silence.**
The wise power recognizes that the Sump-Lungs are vulnerable to the chaos of the world above. It constructs a sensory fold around the chamber, a buffer of deep, tectonic stillness. For as long as the world endures, no tremor, no war, and no calamity from the surface will penetrate the Sump-Lungs. The machine will continue its construction, the frost will continue to bloom, and the Weaver will continue to bleed itself into the floor-plates, entirely undisturbed by the passage of eras.

The power withdraws, leaving behind no sign of its presence, save for the fact that the *tink-tink-drip* of the moisture seems to fall into a slightly more melodic, harmonious rhythm.

The Weaver returns to its shadows. It does not know it has been visited. It does not know that its life has been sanctified. It simply feels a small, strange fullness in its tendrils—a phantom warmth that defies the cold—and it continues its work, vibrating in beautiful, perfect, and now endless harmony with the heart of a world that produces nothing at all.
