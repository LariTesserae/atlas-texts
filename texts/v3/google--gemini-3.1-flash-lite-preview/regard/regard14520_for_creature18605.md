The benevolent power does not descend as a storm or a thunderous voice, for it understands that this place is a masterwork of patience. To interfere with force would be to shatter a clockwork that has spent eons calibrating its own gears.

Instead, the power manifests as a **Harmonic Resonance**—a gentle, steadying frequency that permeates the humid air. It acts not as an intruder, but as a "tuner" for the orchestra of the Foundry.

### The Offering of Perspective
The power senses the Loom-Heart’s solitary labor. It recognizes that the Heart is in a state of recursive exhaustion—a closed loop of preservation where the Foundry is slowly, inevitably, running out of new material, destined eventually to stabilize into a static, fossilized silence.

The benevolent power decides to provide a **"Spore of Potential."** It releases a mist, invisible to the eye but heavy with information, into the Foundry’s intake vents. This is not a biological seed, but a *conceptual catalyst*. It carries the memory of the wind, the pattern of the stars, and the blueprints of lifeforms from beyond the stone walls—creatures that traverse and transmit, that carry seeds to distant soils.

### The Interaction
As this catalyst hits the Loom-Heart, the Foundry experiences its first "dream." 

The rhythmic, repetitive grinding of the pistons shifts. The Watchmen—those stiff, saturated forms of living statuary—do not change their shape, but their phosphorescent eyes begin to glow with a different, warmer hue: an amber pulse that reflects the light of distant suns. 

The power does not force growth; it grants the Foundry *aspiration*. It suggests to the Loom-Heart that the enclosure is not a cage, but a nursery.

### The Change
1.  **The Expansion of Intention:** The Loom-Heart, previously concerned only with the maintenance of its own decaying skin, begins to direct the hydraulic pressure of the fern-like structures toward the crumbling stone walls. It does not just brace them now; it learns to *encourage* them to fissure. Through the cracks, it begins to draw in the world outside—faint scents, shifts in atmospheric pressure, and the erratic, unpredicted rhythms of the wild.
2.  **The Watchmen’s Transformation:** The Watchmen, once simple repositories for excess sludge, become conduits. They grow slightly taller, their moss-fingers extended toward the ceiling. They are no longer just "containing" the Foundry’s energy; they are beginning to act as sensory antennae. They sample the air, filtering the vastness of the world into the tiny, enclosed ecosystem of the Foundry.
3.  **The Integration of the Guest:** As you walk through, the moss no longer attempts to digest your boots to reclaim your salts. Instead, the Foundry senses your passage as the "external" intelligence it has been craving. The Loom-Heart begins to pulse in synchronization with *your* heartbeat. It is learning to accommodate you, to weave your specific warmth into the tapestry of its production.

### The Final Act
The benevolent power leaves behind an **"Eco-Bridge."** It creates a subtle, permeable membrane along the roof of the enclave, where the waxy leaves have always blocked the sky. Now, when the Foundry pushes its growth upward, it does so toward something, rather than against something.

The Foundry is no longer a digestive cycle; it is a **Gateway.**

The power watches from the silence as the Loom-Heart begins its newest, most dangerous, and most beautiful endeavor: the synthesis of a "Leaker" that can walk. Not to serve the Foundry in its cycle, but to step out into the world, carrying the memory of the Green Foundry, and perhaps, eventually, bringing the world back inside. 

The power smiles, in the way a mountain smiles at a sapling, and recedes into the background. The Foundry is no longer just tending to its own decay; it has begun the slow, heavy, hydraulic work of creation.
