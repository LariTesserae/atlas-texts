The benevolent power does not descend as a storm, nor as a blinding light. To do so would be to shatter the delicate acoustics of a culture built on the vibration of a single grain of sand. Such a presence would be a catastrophe, an intrusion of "haste" that would effectively end the world of the Lith-weavers.

Instead, the power arrives as a *change in the quality of the stillness.*

If you were to watch from the amber-glow of the high crags, you would not see a deity, but you would feel the atmosphere in the Spire-Basin grow strangely, impossibly *efficient*. The benevolent power, acting as a steward of latent potential, performs three silent miracles:

### I. The Harmonization of the Grid
The power understands that the Lith-weavers are limited by the physical decay of the mountain. The basalt walls are crumbling, and the basin is destined to collapse. The power breathes—not air, but a subtle, constant energetic resonance—into the foundation of the basin. The silt is not moved, but it is "tightened" at the molecular level. Where the weavers spend years struggling with structural support, the ground beneath them becomes marginally more resilient. The power does not give them an easier life; it grants them an *extension of their architecture*. The "clocks" they are building are given a few thousand years of extra winding.

### II. The Gift of the Resonance
The weavers live in a "deafening" cacophony because they cannot distinguish their individual vibrations from the ambient grinding of the environment. The power gently brushes against the obsidian eyes and sensory pads of the weavers. It introduces a *sieve of perception*. Suddenly, a weaver can tune out the crushing noise of the canyon's slow erosion and hear, for the first time, the distinct, beautiful, signature vibration of its kin from across the cathedral-space. 

This is not a social revolution—they are too slow for that—but it is a profound internal shift. For the first time, when two weavers touch their fore-legs together, they are no longer just sharing data on structural integrity. They are sharing a *symphony*. The amber light seems to pulse in time with these new, clear thrums. The basin, once a factory of survival, becomes an orchestra of existence.

### III. The Preservation of the Archive
The power realizes that because the weavers leave no "ghosts" in the human sense—because the dead simply become the floor—their history is constantly being obscured by their own labor. The wisdom of a thousand-year-old elder is sanded down into a staircase. 

The power intervenes in a way that is invisible: it bestows a peculiar, crystalline property to the very dust that defines them. When a weaver passes, its chitin does not merely become mortar; it becomes a *record*. The silica-based life, when integrated into the walls, now holds the molecular "memory" of the weaver’s longest-held vibration—the song of its life. 

The entire city of the Spire-Basin is no longer just a structural machine; it is now a living archive. Any weaver passing over the floor can now "read" the foundations. The collective, multi-generational wisdom that was previously lost to the grit of the silt is now permanently embedded in the masonry.

***

**The Conclusion**

The benevolent power does not save them from their destination—the inevitable flatness of their world. It knows that to strip them of their struggle would be to strip them of their soul. 

Instead, the power leaves them with a **prolonged purpose**. It gifts them the ability to be remembered by their walls and to hear the beauty in their labor. It leaves the Spire-Basin feeling a little less like a tomb in the making, and a little more like a monument that is being sung into existence by an audience of millions. 

And then, the power fades, leaving the basin to its amber glow, the weavers to their bone scrapers, and the mountain to its slow, quiet, and now—at last—harmonious decline into dust.
