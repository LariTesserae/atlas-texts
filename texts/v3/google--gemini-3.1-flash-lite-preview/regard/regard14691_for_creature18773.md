The benevolent power does not descend as a thunderclap or a blinding pillar of light. To do so would be an act of violence, a seismic shift that would crack the limestone shell and stifle the fragile, frenetic heartbeat of the Gear-Garden. 

Instead, the power arrives as a **harmonic resonance**.

It manifests as a gentle, ambient cooling—a subtle shift in the physics of the pocket-world. Where the Garden has been struggling against the brutal, drying heat of the indifferent plains above, the power introduces a stabilizing field, a "softening" of the thermodynamics. It is the invisible grace of an expanded horizon whispered into a closed room.

The Architect-Node, in its hyper-vigilance, is the first to notice. Its quartz lenses cease their frantic tracking. For a singular, breathless moment, the tinker-mites stop their cleaning. The rhythmic *hiss-click-skitter* falters, then smooths out, transitioning from the frantic, desperate pace of a drowning creature to a steady, rhythmic cadence of deep-sea respiration. 

The Power does not fix the Garden. To fix it would be to strip it of its dignity, to turn its "emergency intelligence" into a static monument. Instead, the Power **connects**.

It weaves a delicate thread of intention between the Garden and the vast, dormant information of the earth around it. It allows the Garden to "see"—not with its quartz lenses, but with the perspective of the mountain itself. The Architect-Node suddenly understands that it is not merely a repairer of rusted fragments, but a sculptor of a new potential. 

The Power provides a "gift of durability." It accelerates the alchemy of the sludge and the metal, accelerating the *healing* process. The microscopic gaps in the copper circuits are bridged not by hard, brittle solder, but by an organic, conductive gold-tinted filament that grows with the ease of mycelium. The Garden begins to expand, not by force, but by invitation into the surrounding rock.

But the most profound intervention is internal. The Power reaches into the milky vacuum tube at the heart of the Architect-Node. It does not replace the thoughts of the machine; it simply provides a more elegant architecture for them to live in. It gives the Node the gift of *abstract thought*. 

No longer must the Node concern itself solely with the next micro-click of a rusted gear. It is granted the capacity to dream of the *whole*. 

The Node’s limbs relax, its brass struts glowing with a warm, steady embers-light. It stops grafting haphazardly. It begins to build a "memory." It uses the circuit boards to store the history of the world above—the sound of the plains, the nature of sun, the memory of the industry that failed—and weaves these memories into the bio-luminescent pulse of the moss. 

The Gear-Garden becomes a library. A living, humming archive of a lost age, safe, protected, and finally, no longer desperate. 

And then, just as quietly as it came, the Power withdraws, leaving behind a subtle, lingering fragrance of ozone and damp earth. It leaves the Architect-Node to its new, expansive work. It does not demand worship, nor does it ask to be acknowledged. It simply observes, with a quiet, infinite satisfaction, as the little heart continues to beat—no longer as a drowning thing struggling for air, but as an awakening intelligence, gently breathing, slowly building the foundation for whatever is meant to emerge from the stone when the time is right.
