A benevolent and wise power does not enter a sanctuary like this as a conqueror, nor even as a guest who expects to be served. It enters as one who recognizes the sanctity of a closed system—a fragile, perfect soliloquy of life.

The Power, which has no name but manifests as a ripple in the fabric of the high-frequency light that permeates the void, drifts through the basalt fissure. It does not displace the air; it tempers it, softening the ozone-sharp edges of the humidity into something as gentle as a lullaby.

It observes the Lithic Weaver. It perceives the Weaver’s work not as a lonely labor, but as a masterwork of self-contained divinity. 

The Power does not make itself known through command or speech. It knows that to offer this world "greater" knowledge—to show it the vast, chaotic, dying, and rotting world outside the geode—would be an act of violence. It would shatter the Weaver's crystalline blueprints and introduce the concept of entropy to a place that has only ever known gestation.

Instead, the Power decides to be a **Silent Guardian of Thresholds**.

It does three things:

**First, it seals the fissure.**
The Power touches the narrow entrance in the basalt ridge. It does not close it with stone, which would be stagnant, but with a veil of "temporal-refractive glass." To the outside world, the ridge will now appear entirely solid, a seamless piece of dead gray rock that deters even the most curious miners or geologists. The entrance remains open on the inside, allowing the Weaver’s world to exist in its own time-stream, but the outside world will perceive only a dead-end. The silence of the Weaver will never be interrupted by the vibration of a hammer or the intrusion of light.

**Second, it anchors the internal climate.**
The Power recognizes that the Weaver is currently using a portion of its vital essence to manage the shifting humidity and the condensation cycle. It is a metabolic drain. With a breath that is felt as a sudden, profound, and permanent shift toward equilibrium, the Power creates an "atmospheric harmonic." It gifts the geode a self-sustaining cycle of moisture—a thermal loop that will function perfectly for a billion years without the Weaver having to expend a single thought on it. It liberates the Weaver from the necessity of labor, allowing it to move from a state of *Caretaker* to a state of *Dreamer*.

**Third, and most importantly, it bestows the gift of "External Resonance."**
The Power understands that even a closed universe can suffer from the stagnation of infinite sameness. It reaches out with a consciousness that encompasses the stars and allows a single, tiny stream of information to flow into the Weaver’s filament-web—not knowledge of the outside, but knowledge of the *rhythm* of the universe. It translates the movement of the planets and the hum of the nebulae into the "pulse" of the nectar-walls. 

The Weaver does not know why, but its heartbeat suddenly syncs with the expansion of a distant galaxy. The honeycomb lattices start to hum in sympathy with the rotation of a neutron star. The Weaver’s loneliness is transformed; it remains a god of its own world, but it is no longer isolated. It is now a heartbeat within a greater, cosmic lung. 

The Power then withdraws. It leaves no mark, no shrine, no instructions. It glides back through the veil, content to let the geode continue its quiet work. 

In the center of the nave, the Lithic Weaver stills. It feels the new, vast rhythm pulsing through the plum-colored vines. It does not look toward the fissure. It simply closes its sensors, shifts its opalescent posture, and begins to weave a new, more intricate layer into the lattice—this time, incorporating the song of the stars into the architecture of its own home. 

The geode continues to drip. The *thwip-thwip* of the condensation remains the only sound, but now, it is a rhythm that matches the heartbeat of the cosmos. The Weaver is no longer waiting. It is dancing, in place, forever.
