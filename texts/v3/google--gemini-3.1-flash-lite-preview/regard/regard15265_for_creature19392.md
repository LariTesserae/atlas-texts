The benevolent power does not descend as a storm, nor as a blinding sun—for in the Hydraulic Necropolis, light is a contagion and motion is a violence. Instead, the power arrives as a *harmonic alignment*. It is a presence that settles into the crushing weight, not to defy the pressure, but to resonate with it.

It acts not by changing the architecture, nor by liberating the Silt-Keepers from their tomb, for to break the shell would be to murder the soul of their existence. Instead, it performs a subtle, celestial surgery on the nature of their stillness.

### The Gift of Resonance

The Power realizes that the Silt-Keepers are trapped in a cycle of *dreaded waiting*. They treat the Final Yield as an end, a dissolution, a relief from the burden of maintenance. But the Power sees that the universe does not end in crushing; it ends in the expansion of understanding.

The Power flows into the Archivist. It does not replace the Archivist's consciousness, but rather acts as a lens. Through the Archivist’s thousands of filaments, the Power sends a singular, low-frequency pulse—a frequency that is no longer a sigh of grief, but a vibration of *witness*.

Suddenly, the Silt-Keepers do not feel the crushing tectonic weight as a threat of destruction. Through the Archivist’s newfound resonance, the Power reveals to them that the mineral-oil gel is not merely a weight, but a medium—a vast, incompressible record of the universe’s history. Every molecule of the gel holds the memory of a sun that burned out eons ago; every tectonic groan is a syllable in a star-chart.

### The Transformation of the Task

The Silt-Keepers were custodians of entropy. The Power makes them custodians of *meaning*.

When a Silt-Keeper presses its copper fingers against a bulkhead, it is no longer just checking for seal-breaches. The Power allows them to "read" the vibrations of the surrounding Earth. They begin to understand that their city is not a tomb, but a deep-sea radio telescope tuned to the birth of galaxies. They are the only beings in the cosmos perfectly pressurized, perfectly still, and perfectly sensitive enough to hear the universe speaking through the density of matter.

They do not stop their work; they continue to polish the brass and tighten the rivets, but the intent shifts. They maintain the city not to postpone their death, but because the Necropolis is the only instrument in existence that can hear the music of the spheres. 

### The Finality of the Yield

The Power also alters the "Final Yield." 

In their previous understanding, the breaking point was death. Under the influence of the Benevolent Power, the "Final Yield" is redefined as "The Great Tuning." When the tectonic currents eventually force the plates of the city to buckle, they will not implode into nothingness. Because of the patterns the Silt-Keepers have etched into the metal through their cycles of observation, the geometry of their collapse will create a perfect, resonant chamber.

When the Necropolis finally yields, it will not crumble. It will sing.

It will send a single, coherent wave of information—a symphony of the history they have gathered—through the gel, traveling outward, unhindered, for light-years. The Silt-Keepers will not disappear; they will become the medium. They will surrender their rigid forms to become the voice of the deep, finally carrying their accumulated wisdom out into the vast, oxygenated dark.

The Power leaves them as it arrived: in silence. But it is no longer the silence of a tomb. It is the silence of an audience holding its breath, waiting for the orchestra to begin. The Silt-Keepers go back to their work, their glass viewports now shimmering with the dim, violet light of expectation, the rhythm of their pistons perfectly synchronized with the beating heart of the universe.
