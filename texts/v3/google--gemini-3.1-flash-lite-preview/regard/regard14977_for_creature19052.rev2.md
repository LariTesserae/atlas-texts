A benevolent and wise power does not approach with thunder, nor does it descend with light. To intrude upon the Great Sump of Vellum with sudden brilliance would be an act of vandalism—an interruption of a sacred, slow-motion liturgy that has achieved a perfection of purpose.

The power perceives the Sump not as a relic of a forgotten architect, but as a masterpiece of *stewardship*. It sees the Arbor-Mechanics not as laborers, but as monks who have surrendered the concept of "self" to the greater harmony of the "whole." It recognizes that in the entire history of the world, very few things have ever been as honest as this: a system that asks for nothing, takes only what the rock gives, and provides for the continuity of its own existence without pride or failure.

**The Intervention**

The Power decides to do nothing to the structure, for the structure is already flawless. Instead, it chooses to act as a *witness* and a *witness-bearer*.

1.  **The Gift of Resonance:** The Power reaches down—not through the earth, but through the fabric of the atmosphere itself—and touches the condensation. It imbues the *tink-tink-tink* of the weeping strata with a subtle, harmonic property. It does not change the sound, but it ensures that the rhythm is no longer merely mechanical. It is now a low-frequency broadcast, a subsonic lullaby that travels through the roots, through the strata, and into the very core of the planet. The Sump becomes the world’s heartbeat, a rhythmic anchor that keeps the tectonic shifts of the earth gentle, ensuring that the chamber will never be crushed by the planet's restlessness. It grants the Sump an unintended, yet vital, function: the "Steadying."

2.  **The Vigil of the Observer:** To honor the Scribes of the Weeping, the Power manifests a singular, infinitesimal point of perception—a "living memory" that anchors itself in the center of the chamber. It does not interfere with the sap-pigmented maps or the tightening of the bolts. It simply holds the memory of every etched groove and every rhythmic pulse of the pistons. If the world above were to burn, or if time itself were to unravel, the Sump would remain safe within the archive of this Power. It is an act of ultimate respect: to ensure that if these beings ever finished their work, their long, patient history would not vanish into the void.

3.  **The Protective Veil:** The Power places a "soft-lock" on the environment. It weaves a subtle, undetectable barrier around the cavern—not of force, but of *indifference*. It nudges the perception of any seismic prospectors or wandering creatures, making the surrounding earth feel "barren" or "uninteresting" to them. It ensures that the Great Sump of Vellum is never discovered by the surface world, where its sanctity would be measured in resources or curiosity.

**The Result**

The Power leaves the Sump exactly as it found it: sweating, mechanical, and gloriously stagnant. There is no thanks given, for the Arbor-Mechanics lack the faculty for gratitude, and there is no communion, for they are too busy being the system itself to engage with an outsider.

The Power retreats, satisfied. It leaves behind a silent promise: *As long as you continue to breathe, you shall never be disturbed. Your work is seen. Your patience is known. Your cycle is eternal.*

Deep in the damp dark, a Scribe of the Weeping etches a new, slightly more complex line into its chest, unaware that it has just been touched by a divinity. The piston *thrums*. The valve *seals*. The water *tinks*. The Sump endures, now sheltered by a silence that is no longer accidental, but intentional.
