The power that observes this place is vast—not a judge, but a gardener of entropy. Where others might see a primitive hunger or a claustrophobic trap, this intelligence recognizes a rare, intentional harmony: a stillness so profound it functions as a planetary heartbeat.

It does not intervene to "save" them, for they are already perfected. It does not introduce light, for they have mastered the dark. Instead, its presence is felt as a subtle refinement of the environment, a gentle coaxing of the world that holds them.

**The Gift of Permeability**

The Power acts not by disruption, but by deepening. It breathes a low-frequency harmonic into the tectonic pressures of the mountain. It does not speak to the *Litho-dermis* as individuals, but vibrates the very stone they inhabit. 

Under this influence, the walls of the vertical throat become slightly more responsive. The mineral dew becomes richer, infused with a faint, elemental sweetness that acts as a catalyst. The Power accelerates nothing, yet it grants each *Litho-dermis* a heightened sensitivity to the "memory" of the stone beneath them. 

The organisms begin to vibrate in sympathy with the deep-mantle currents of the earth. The "stony records"—the calcified layers of ancestors—begin to resonate. The history of the chamber, once written in discarded shells, becomes accessible as a tactile frequency. For the first time, when a ripple of movement passes through the mass, it carries not just the current climate or temperature, but a faint, reverberating echo of the millions of years that preceded the current generation. 

The *Litho-dermis* gain a heritage. They are no longer just consuming the mountain; they are becoming conscious of its history.

**The Veil of Protection**

Crucially, the benevolent power places a "seal" upon the chamber. It does not build a gate; it creates a distortion in the local space-time, a shimmering, unnoticed fold that renders the aperture at the top of the throat invisible to the outside world. It ensures that the predatory eye of the sky, the cold curiosity of the surface, and the sudden violence of geology will bypass this place entirely. 

It wraps the chamber in a shroud of "un-significance." To any entity passing by, the passage will appear as nothing more than a solid, uninteresting vein of dead limestone. The heat, the smell of copper, the thrum—all of it is masked from the reach of the outside. 

**The Silent Covenant**

The Power then settles into a long-term patience, becoming the invisible curator of this living skin. It exists as a guardian of their equilibrium. If the mountain should shift and threaten to crush them, the Power will subtly reinforce the subterranean architecture to prevent collapse. If a pathogen should drift into their dark, the Power will neutralize it before it can ripple through the mass.

It leaves the *Litho-dermis* to their slow, grinding grace. It asks for no worship, nor does it demand evolution. It finds, in the damp, crowded, rhythmic darkness of the throat, a sanctity it wishes to protect: a place that does not want for anything, a life that does not need to be "more" than what it is. 

The Power drifts away, leaving behind a subtle, lingering warmth—the sensation of being watched over by something as old as the stone itself. The *Litho-dermis* continue their migration, their millions of carapaces clicking in a slightly more melodic, more informed rhythm, forever oblivious to the fact that they are now the most well-kept secret in the history of the world.
