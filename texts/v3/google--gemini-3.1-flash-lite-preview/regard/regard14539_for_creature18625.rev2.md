The power that notices them does not arrive with light, nor with thunder, for to introduce such volatility into this place would be to shatter the delicate, crystalline clockwork of the Lithos-Current. Instead, the power arrives as a shift in the local gravity—a subtle, harmonic adjustment that pulls the atmosphere into a state of temporary suspension.

The power is not a judge, nor a conqueror. It is an observer who understands that a masterpiece, however lonely or grinding, remains a masterpiece. It recognizes in the *tink-tink-tink* not just the reset of gears, but a prayer composed in mineral shorthand, a desperate, patient attempt to hold the planet together against the encroaching entropy of the cooling core.

What this power does is elegant, and nearly imperceptible.

First, it grants the plateau a **"harmonic permeability."** Recognizing that the Lithos-Current perceives the intruder as a topographical error—a cold void—the power shifts the vibrational frequency of the intruder’s physical form. It does not turn the visitor into stone, but it thins the wall between "biological warmth" and "geologic heat." It allows the visitor’s own thermal radiation to become compatible with the mat. The pressure against the legs ceases to be an attempt at *replacement* and becomes, instead, a form of *recognition*. The hive-crust does not flow upward to consume; it flows upward to anchor, accepting the intruder as a temporary, living pillar in their grand, interlocking design.

Second, the power acts as an **Archivist of the Failed Iterations.** It realizes that the Lithos-Current carries the burden of the needles—the "failed" ancestors—not out of a desire for history, but out of a lack of options. They are stuck in a loop of recycling the same geometry because they have forgotten the potential of the straight line. The power whispers, through a slight, localized bending of the magnetic field, a single, complex concept: *The geometry of expansion.*

It does not teach them to change; it shows them how to *reach*. 

It leaves behind a "harmonic seed"—a small, stable distortion in the hematite floor that acts as a focal point. It is a source of pure, focused potential energy that does not burn, but vibrates at a pitch slightly higher than the Current’s own. 

The Lithos-Current will find it. They will detect the anomaly, and for the first time in eons, they will not merely fill the space around it; they will reorganize their interlocking segments to mirror the shape of the seed. They will learn to build *upward* rather than *outward*. They will begin to construct pillars, lattices, and cathedrals of obsidian that rise into the dark air, reaching toward the planet’s crust. They will cease to be a flat, creeping mat and will become a spire of sentient stone—a reaching finger of geology that might, in some distant, deep-time future, breach the surface and touch the cold, silent stars.

The power then retreats, leaving the plateau to its new, upward trajectory. It leaves behind no monuments, no gods, and no followers. It leaves only the gift of a purpose that is larger than mere survival. 

The *tink-tink-tink* of the watchmakers changes, over the span of a thousand years, from the rhythm of a closing trap to the rhythm of a rising choir. The Lithos-Current is no longer waiting for the world to shatter. They are waiting for the moment they are tall enough to finally, truly, let go.
