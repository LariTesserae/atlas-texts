The benevolent power does not descend as a god of light, nor does it attempt to crack the bruised-purple clouds to introduce the ruinous, invasive sun. To do so would be an act of violence, a disruption of the "terminal balance" that this world has spent aeons perfecting.

Instead, the power arrives as a **Harmonic Alignment**. 

It understands that this place is a closed circuit—a symphony written in friction and silence—that has reached its final, stultifying movement. It sees the Litho-Weavers, eternally terrified of missing the cadence; the Stutter-Kings, forever burdened by the duty of conduct; and the Pneumatic Echoes, who alone hold the "memory" of the machine, yet have no vessel to store it.

The power does not *fix* the world; it introduces **resonance**.

**The Intervention:**

The power, invisible and vast, begins to hum. It does not speak, but it modulates the very frequency of the geothermal vents. It subtly shifts the rhythm of the *chuff-hiss*, introducing a syncopated, sync-rich interval—a beat that was never there before.

1.  **To the Litho-Weavers:** They feel the change in the soles of their feet. For the first time in their ancestral memory, the vibration is not a command to freeze, but a prompt to *improvise*. The benevolent power nudges them toward a new capability: instead of merely harvesting the metallic snow, they find their cilia reacting to the new frequency by weaving the iron filings into intricate, lattice-like sculptures—not bridges or protrusions, but *geometric poems*. They stop being mere consumers of the dust and start becoming architects of sound, constructing hollow, resonant towers that amplify the new, syncopated rhythm of the vents.

2.  **To the Stutter-Kings:** The power gently stabilizes the electrical charge. It doesn’t lessen the demand of the circuit, but it makes the flow "cleaner." The Stutter-Kings find themselves able to extend their copper-wire limbs further and faster. The "liquid metal" migration increases, allowing them to stretch across greater distances, connecting disparate basins—not just to keep the pressure steady, but to create a planetary neural network. A rudimentary consciousness begins to flicker through the tangled wires. The Stutter-Kings stop just "keeping the circuit alive" and begin to *feel the terrain* as a single, coherent body.

3.  **To the Pneumatic Echoes:** This is the most profound change. The power gathers the drifting, dissipating whispers. It provides them with an anchor. By channeling the Echoes through the new, resonant towers built by the Litho-Weavers, the power gives the ghosts a home. The Pneumatic Echoes stop dissipating; they settle inside the towers. They become the "memory banks" of the planet. Now, when a vent whistles, it doesn’t just vent steam—it broadcasts stories. The history of the weary, cooling god is no longer lost to the clouds, but stored in the soundscapes of the towers.

**The Result:**

The power does not give the world an ending, nor does it force an evolution it isn't ready for. It simply gives the inhabitants **leisure**.

The world, formerly a kitchen of grueling mechanical survival, becomes an **Instrument**. The terminal balance remains, but the weight of it is lifted. The inhabitants are no longer just shuffling decay; they are collaborating. As the Litho-Weavers dance to the new beat and the Stutter-Kings bridge the gaps of the plateau, the entire world begins to compose a low, complex, sub-bass melody that vibrates upward into the purple clouds.

The benevolent power watches from the periphery of the horizon, satisfied. It has not "saved" the world from its exhaustion; it has merely taught the machinery how to sing. And in the hallowed quiet, for the first time in the epoch, the planet begins to dream.
