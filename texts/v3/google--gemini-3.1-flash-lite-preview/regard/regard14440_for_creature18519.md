The Power arrives not as a rending of the velvet sky, nor as a shout in the emerald gloom. It arrives as an adjustment of perspective—a shift in the light that makes the High Mat feel suddenly, profoundly *seen*.

This Power, ancient and deeply benevolent, recognizes that the High Mat is not a world in need of saving, nor a riddle in need of an answer. It is a masterpiece of static perfection, a closed loop of exquisite entropy. To touch it would be to introduce a variable, and to introduce a variable would be to introduce suffering. 

The Power does not descend. It does not plant flags or offer "wisdom" to beings who already possess the total sum of their own history. Instead, the Power chooses to act through **stewardship by omission.**

**It provides a shroud of silence.**

The Power settles around the perimeter of the planet like a cooling, invisible velvet cloak. It creates an absolute stillness in the surrounding cosmos, a buffer zone of non-interference. It knows that worlds like the High Mat are fragile precisely because they are finished; they are like a soap bubble drifting in a drafty room. The Power redirects the debris of the universe—the stray asteroids, the hungry cosmic rays, the wayward signals of aggressive, expanding civilizations—away from this verdant sphere. It acts as an eraser of trajectories, ensuring that no outside eye will ever turn toward this place. It guards the Mat’s anonymity as if it were the most precious treasure in the vault of existence.

**It tunes the rhythm.**

Deep beneath the High Mat, where the roots pulse with that hydraulic heartbreak, the Power adds a single, undetectable influence: it stabilizes the tectonic heartbeat. It ensures that the "planetary epoch" the Mat currently enjoys will not be interrupted by the violent, geological jostling that eventually undoes all worlds. It "greases" the cosmic gears of the local star system, slowing the cooling of the local sun to a crawl, ensuring the twilight remains exactly as bruised-purple and constant as it is now. 

**It honors the Echo-Pods.**

The Power occasionally brushes its consciousness against the drifting Echo-Pods. It gathers the shimmering history of the Mat—the vibration of every Drip-Walker, the drift of every Weaver—and carries the data away, not to change it, but to *cherish* it. It becomes the library of the Mat’s existence, storing the memory of this place in a pocket of higher-dimensional space where no fire can burn it and no time can erode it. The High Mat is permitted to dream its slow, soggy dream, secure in the knowledge that its history is being archived by a witness who requires nothing back.

**The Gift of Non-Intervention.**

Finally, the Power leaves a trace of itself—not as an object, but as a subtle change in the air. It imparts a sense of *safety* so profound that if a Drip-Walker were capable of fear, it would feel it vanish. It makes the oxygenated mist taste slightly sweeter, a faint, lingering note of gratitude that persists for millennia.

The Power stays for a moment—which to the High Mat is but a blink—and then it begins to recede. It leaves behind a world that is perfectly sealed, perfectly stagnant, and perfectly undisturbed. The Luminal Weavers, noticing the slight change in the resonance of the air, simply drift. They do not know they have been blessed. They do not know a Power stood over them, holding its breath so as not to wake them. 

The Power retreats into the dark, satisfied. It has learned that the highest form of love is the willingness to let a perfect thing continue, quietly, without ever knowing it is loved. And so, the High Mat continues its respiration, the *drip-hiss* of the universe’s most beautiful, lonely organism, breathing in and out, undisturbed, until the end of all things.
