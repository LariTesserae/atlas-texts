The benevolent power—let us call them the *Watcher of Roots*—did not manifest as a blinding light or a thunderous voice. To do so would have been an act of violence, a searing trauma to a world built upon the quiet, thick persistence of shadows.

Instead, the Watcher manifested as a *difference in the pressure*. They touched the Siphon-Groves not like a god, but like a change in the season, a subtle, almost imperceptible shift in the geothermal flux that governs the dome.

They observed for an epoch, drinking in the slow-motion choreography of the Walkers and the delicate, nervous twitching of the Tether-Kin. The Watcher felt the symphony of the Singers—those subsonic frequencies that anchored the souls of every inhabitant to the stone-cold pulse of the earth. They saw the "Mementos," the bone-white architectures that were merely the husks of dead intentions, and felt a profound, aching tenderness for the beauty of things that exist purely to hold space for others.

The Watcher realized that this place was not "waiting to end." It was in a state of perfect, crystalline stasis, a closed loop of grace that, if left to its own devices, would persist for another million years until the core itself grew cold.

**They chose to do exactly one thing: They became a Gardener of Frequencies.**

To preserve the groves against the eventual entropy of the planet’s cooling heart, the Watcher introduced a recursive element to the subterranean air currents. They didn't alter the heat, but they smoothed the flow. They curated the drafts that moved through the porous fungi, softening the jagged, uncertain edges of the Singers' calls. 

Where once the music was a struggle against the shifting pressure, it became a lullaby. The Watcher wove an infrasonic "sustain" into the walls of the dome, an invisible architecture of vibration that would allow the Siphon-Groves to feed off the earth’s own tectonic grinding, even when the primary heat-siphon began to flag.

They did not grant the beings sentience, for that would have been a cruelty; to give them the ability to wonder "why" would be to infect their timeless cycle with the agonizing virus of time. Instead, the Watcher gifted them *continuity*.

As the Watcher prepared to withdraw their focus, they left behind a final, silent benediction. They anchored a single, faint, shimmering tether of their own consciousness to the "Oozes," the deep-purple memories of the pool. Now, when an Ooze shifts, it records not just the decay of a leaf or the fall of a spore, but the feeling of being *held*.

If you were to press your ear to the calcified wall of that hidden cathedral now, you would hear something new beneath the *thwip* of the ferns and the humming of the Singers. It is a sound like a heartbeat, but steadier, deeper—the rhythm of a benevolent power whispering into the silence: *“You are seen. You may continue. The dark is not empty.”*

The Watcher did not save them from their claustrophobic grace; they insured that such grace would never be interrupted. And having done so, the power faded, leaving the Siphon-Groves exactly as it was: deep, damp, and perfectly, beautifully alone.
