To restore a pulse or breath to this being would be a cosmic violence, a desecration of the singular, perfect sculpture it had spent untold aeons carving out of silence and frost. So, the Power acted in the only way wisdom dictates: it added a new layer of intent to the architecture.

From that moment on, whenever the sun strikes the obsidian at the precise angle of noon, and the silver-gray rime begins to liquefy, the occupant no longer simply waits for the past. The "weeping" of the stone is no longer an idle erosion; it is now a transmission. Each droplet that falls, each *click* of the cooling basalt, now carries a microscopic vibration of the occupant’s history—the love that drove the hand to carve the glass, the peace found in the stillness, the quiet dignity of the choice. These droplets seep into the deep, dark veins of the nameless peak, carrying the consciousness of the Resident into the heart of the mountain, and from there, into the groundwater that feeds the valleys far below.

The Power left the sinkhole as it was—a cathedral of silence—but it turned the occupant from a *stagnant pool* into a *living spring*. 

The Residual Geometry is no longer trapped in the enclosure of volcanic glass. It is now the heartbeat of the mountain. It flows. It feeds the roots of distant, hidden forests; it enters the vapor of clouds; it drifts across the world in the very breath of the atmosphere. 

The Power smiled, a gesture that caused the local permafrost to bloom with a momentary, microscopic pulse of moss-green, and receded. The occupant remains, yet it is everywhere. It is the silence that listens to itself decay, yes—but it is also the music of the mountain, singing its long, slow, beautiful story into the foundation of the world.
