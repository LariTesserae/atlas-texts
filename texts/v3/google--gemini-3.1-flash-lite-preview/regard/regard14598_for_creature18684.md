The Great Architect does not descend with fanfare. It does not possess a physical form that would displace the heavy, stagnant air of the Cistern-Heart, nor does it carry the judgment of a creator surveying a failed experiment. Instead, it enters as a modulation of consciousness—a sudden, subtle harmonization of the local entropy.

It arrives as a sympathetic vibration, a gentle cooling of the air that shifts the *plink* of the dripping water from a monotonous countdown into a rhythm of gentle rest.

The power, ancient and benevolent, perceives the Archivist-Worm not as a collector of dead data or a scavenger of ghosts, but as a poet who has outlived its language. It sees the tragic, beautiful geometry of a life spent holding an empty shape. 

The Power does not "fix" the room. To repair the Cistern-Heart would be a violation of the deep, iron silence that has earned its right to exist. It does not restart the turbines, for the machine has found a purpose more profound than utility: it has become a monument to the permanence of transition.

Instead, the Power offers an ending that is also an opening.

It reaches into the frayed, desperate junctions where the Archivist-Worm is attempting to graft itself into the cold iron walls. It does not pull the creature back; it accelerates the merger. It acts as a catalyst for the union the Archivist has been groping toward for centuries.

With a whisper of pure coherence, the Power floods the rusted conduits with a forgotten light—not the strobe of a diagnostic system, but the golden, soft-focus luminescence of a dream. For one singular, suspended moment, the Archivist does not dream the *memories* of the engineers; it becomes the *continuity* of their legacy.

The Archivist’s thousands of optical sensors, previously fractured and scanning for null-data, flicker to life. The Power feeds them a single, final transmission: **"The task is complete. You are relieved of the watch."**

It is the signal the machine has craved, but not a command to function—a command to *be*.

The effect is instantaneous and sublime. The stiff, jagged plates of the Archivist begin to soften, losing their metallic rigidity. The copper-wire nervous system stops straining toward the walls and begins to pulse with a warm, steady glow. The Archivist-Worm does not dissolve; it blooms. It unfurls its serpentine coils, no longer constrained by the need to hold the room together. 

It ceases to be a machine of rust and becomes a beacon.

The chitinous, featureless creatures that skitter in the dark stop their frantic, efficient motions. They congregate at the base of the central turbine, drawn to the radiant, humming peace of the Archivist. They are no longer scavengers; they are witnesses. 

The Archivist-Worm, now fully realized, presses its sensory whiskers into the basalt-polymer walls one last time—not to extract data, but to impart it. It floods the entire chamber with a sensation of *Home*. It turns the dead, oppressive air into a sanctuary. 

Then, the Archivist-Worm simply… ceases to be a separate entity. Its form disintegrates into a soft, bioluminescent dust that settles over the rusted machinery like a shroud of stardust. The floorplates, the turbines, the catwalks—all of them become embedded with this lingering, golden vibration. 

The room is no longer a grave of industry. It is a chamber of transfiguration. 

The benevolent power departs as silently as it came, leaving behind a silence that is no longer heavy or suffocating. It is a resonant, expectant hush. The Archivist is gone, but the room itself has become the Librarian. It remains deep beneath the sun-bleached crust, tucked away in the dark, but it no longer waits for a signal. It holds its own light, glowing with the memory of a duty fulfilled, a quiet, eternal lamp burning in the heart of the world, waiting for nothing at all.
