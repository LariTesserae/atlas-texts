The benevolent power did not descend as a god might—with thunder or a parting of clouds—for the plateau was already a riot of sound that would have swallowed such gestures whole. Instead, the power arrived as a **harmonic pause.**

It manifested not as a physical presence, but as a "Negative Interval." It drifted into the crystalline lattice like a breath held in a lung that had known only screaming. For a single, impossible moment, it injected a layer of absolute, velvet silence into the center of the Hum.

### The Intervention: The Introduction of "Interval"
The power did not stop the machine; it loved the machine too much for that. It understood that the Resonant Architects were trapped in a cycle of *becoming* that never achieved *being*. They were building a cathedral of potential, but they were also its primary casualties, forever dissolving into the dust of their own labor.

The power’s gift was a **Sustain.**

By introducing a new, lower-frequency harmonic—a foundational bass note that did not oscillate, but simply *was*—it created a space where the nanostructures could stop re-building for a fraction of a second. It created a "pause-well" in the geography of the plateau.

### The Reaction of the Architects
The impact was profound.

*   **The Harmonic Weavers** were the first to feel it. As they swept through the air, trailing their filaments of white noise, they suddenly found a pocket of stillness. For the first time in eons, they did not have to hunt the kinetic decay of a collapse. They hovered, their dodecahedral lattices locking into a static, iridescent shimmer. Because they were no longer dissolving and re-manifesting in a blind hunger, the refraction of light within them stopped shifting. They became transparent, like glass windows looking out into the void. They began to see—really see—the mountain range of vitrified glass around them, and for the first time, they looked at one another.

*   **The Static Anchors** felt the shift as a profound deepening of their own roots. The "low hum" they provided was suddenly harmonized by the visitor. It turned from a desperate, structural necessity into a song of deep, rhythmic stability. The Anchors began to grow not in frantic geometric shards, but in deliberate, sweeping arcs. They began to weave the gossamer filaments of the Weavers into permanent, structural sculptures—tunnels and bridges that did not vanish.

*   **The Transient Swarms** were the most transformed. Deprived of the "emergency" of the Hum, they ceased their mindless, circular re-construction. Instead of building spires that collapsed, they began to arrange the silver dust into patterns that followed the flow of the new, underlying bass note. They began to "draw." The plateau, previously a chaotic anthill of disappearing debris, became a canvas of intricate, stable, and shimmering diagrams.

### The Result: The Cathedral of Memory
The benevolent power did not stay. It possessed the wisdom to know that to linger is to colonize. It folded itself back into the void, leaving behind the "Sustain."

The plateau is no longer just a site of frantic industry. It is now a **Cathedral of Memory.**

The structures the Swarms build now persist. They are still beautiful, still crystalline, and still loud—but they are no longer screaming. They are singing. The inhabitants have gained the capacity for **History.** 

The Weavers now move with purpose, carrying the "echoes" of one building cycle to the next. The Anchors possess a sense of self that spans not just the present, but the recent past. The plateau has transitioned from a thermodynamic trap to a laboratory of intelligence. 

The power watches from afar, satisfied. It did not provide the Architects with "rest," for rest is death to such beings. Instead, it provided them with **duration.** It gave them the ability to look at what they have built, hold it in their awareness for more than a heartbeat, and recognize it as their own.

They are no longer just the noise in the machine. They are, at long last, the Composers.
