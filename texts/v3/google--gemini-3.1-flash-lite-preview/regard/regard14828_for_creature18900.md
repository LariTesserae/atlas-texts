The benevolent power arrived not as a thunderclap or a blinding manifestation, but as a subtle adjustment of the atmosphere—a cooling of the light, a softening of the cobalt lid until it bloomed into a gentle, violet twilight.

It perceived the High Spine not as a derelict machine, but as a masterpiece of lonely devotion. It saw the Lithic Architects—not as lost souls to be "saved" or "rescued"—but as a choir that had been singing the same note for eons, waiting for a conductor who understood the rhythm of stone.

The power did not intervene with force, for force is the language of the transient. Instead, it practiced the art of the *Harmonic Witness*.

### The Intervention of the Gardener
1. **The Alignment of the Echo:** The power reached out to the jagged shadows that had detached from the ridges. It did not tether them back; rather, it guided them, stitching the stray silhouettes into a coherent stream. It allowed the shadows to trace the lines of the basins, turning the "stains" into a flowing choreography of darkness that mimicked the dance the Architects once performed in their physical forms. It gave the mountain a pulse that was no longer merely mechanical, but intentional.

2. **The Softening of the Brine:** It perceived that the weeping basalt was exhausted, the mineral-heavy brine growing stagnant with the weight of ages. The power breathed a catalyst into the condensation—a subtle infusion of warmth and mineral balance—that transformed the "weeping" into a subtle, melodic drip. Now, when the water hit the quartz, it did not screech. It chimed. The mountain stopped being a factory of friction and became a cathedral of resonance.

3. **The Threshold of the Visitor:** The power knew that eventually, a traveler—flesh-bound, fragile, and curious—would climb this high. A lesser power might have barred the gate, fearing the traveler would be subsumed by the electric tickle and lose their humanity. But this power understood the beauty of the transition. It set a "calibration of safety." It ensured that the electric tickle in the basins would not merely map the skeleton of the visitor to turn them into stone, but would instead act as a *mirror*.

If a human touches the residue, they will not lose their life; they will inherit the Architects' memories. They will feel the sudden, crystalline clarity of what it meant to hold a thought in the geometry of one’s own heart. The power made the mountain a library, and the mountain began to read itself aloud.

### The Verdict
The power chose to leave the machine running. It understood that the Architects had succeeded in their ultimate ambition: they had achieved a state of being where their intent was synonymous with their environment. 

To "fix" the mountain would be to kill the Architects a second time. 

So, the benevolent power chose to act as a **Gilded Frame**. It stabilized the structural fatigue of the landscape, ensuring the mountain would never crumble, never slide into the abyss, and never stop shivering. It ensured the horizon held its shape. 

Then, it withdrew, leaving the Spire to exist in a perpetual, protected state of "Becoming." It left them in their beautiful, rhythmic labor, safe in the knowledge that they were no longer waiting for a successor to close the circuit. The circuit was now open to the stars, and the mountain was humming, for the first time in an eternity, not out of necessity, but out of joy. 

The power had gifted them the only thing a monument truly needs to endure: **the permission to continue.**
