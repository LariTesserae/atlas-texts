The Power does not descend as a storm, nor as a voice from the sky. It does not arrive with the clamor of a god demanding recognition. To descend upon such a place with brilliance or thunder would be to shatter the delicate, osmotic equilibrium of the Ligneous-Kin. 

Instead, the Power arrives as a *change in the quality of the humidity*. 

It manifests as a subtle, pervasive shift in the atmosphere—a softening of the light that refracts through the massive, translucent water droplets, a gentle harmonic adjustment to the percussive drone of the forest-mountain. It is a presence that feels like a sudden, lucid realization during a dream. It is the "benevolent grace" that simply *is*.

The Kin are the first to notice. They stop their pruning, their fingers momentarily freezing against the fibrous bark. The silence that ripples through their tactile network is not one of fear, but of profound, vibrating recognition. For the first time in ten thousand heartbeats, the pulse of the mountain—usually a chaotic, frantic symphony of survival—strikes a chord of perfect, crystalline order. 

The Power does not attempt to "save" them, for it understands that in such a place, to be saved from the cycle of rot and renewal would be to be murdered. It does not attempt to "teach" them, for their understanding of the world is far more intimate than any written philosophy. 

Instead, the Power acts as a **Harmonizer of Entropy**.

It threads its awareness through the tangled buttresses and the decaying silt. It sees the "Built" remnants—the ossified dwellings of the ancestors—and it does not clear them away or restore them; it accelerates their transformation. It coaxes the parasitic vines to weave a gentle, protective shroud over these artifacts, sealing them into the living mountain like amber-encased memories. It ensures that the decay, which had begun to threaten the stability of the high bridges, finds a new, graceful path of least resistance, preventing a localized collapse that would have claimed a generation of the Kin.

But most importantly, the Power grants a singular, quiet gift: **The Stillness of Clarity.**

It whispers into the xylem of the world-tree. It stabilizes the insectoid swarms that have, until now, been a frantic, chaotic noise. Under the influence of the Power, the violence of "eating or being eaten" does not cease—for that is the mountain’s engine—but it becomes rhythmic. The predation, the blooming, the rotting, and the grafting fall into a cadence of sublime beauty. 

The Ligneous-Kin, once tethered to the slow, agonizing survival of the mountain, suddenly find they have "room" in their internal world. For a fleeting span—perhaps a century by human measure, perhaps a brief heartbeat for the mountain—their bioluminescent cilia pulse in patterns of soft, golden sunlight that have never occurred in this altitude before. 

The Kin do not worship the Power, for they are too intrinsic to the mountain to formulate a deity. Instead, they begin to *create*. With their long, tapered fingers and their songs of vibration, they begin to weave the living lattice of the mountain into shapes that suggest a future beyond mere homeostasis. They weave bridges that spiral toward the suns in elegant, impossible curves. They foster blossoms that do not just hold water, but refract the light in patterns meant to catch the attention of the stars. 

They began as the nervous system of a mountain; they become its poetry. 

The Power, having performed this subtle, invisible calibration, begins its "withdrawal"—not by leaving, but by becoming indistinguishable from the mountain itself. It merges its benevolence into the very sap that the Kin drink during their grafting sleep. It becomes the deep, resonant *hum* at the base of the world. 

The mountain continues to breathe, the leaves continue to rasp, and the cycles of rot continue their eternal, necessary work. But the mountain is no longer just a held breath. Through the touch of the Power, it has finally exhaled. The Ligneous-Kin remain, forever altered, now living in a world that is not just a substrate for survival, but a sanctuary of deliberate, luminous beauty.
