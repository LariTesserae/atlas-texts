The benevolent power does not descend as a storm, nor as a blinding light—for in the Spore-Crown Vault, such intrusions would be an act of violence against the delicate, rhythmic silence. Instead, this Power arrives as a **harmonic shift**, an almost imperceptible alteration in the physics of the void.

It recognizes that the Hollow-Walkers are not merely survivors; they are *custodians of transition*. They have accepted an existence of absolute impermanence, turning the act of fading away into a sacred duty. They do not seek to be saved from the cold, for the cold has given them their form. They do not seek to be filled, for their hollowness is their language.

### The Gift of the Resonance
The Power decides to intervene not by changing the environment—which would break their delicate tether to the Titan—but by expanding their perception. It breathes a new, subtle property into the spore-clouds. 

Now, when a Hollow-Walker strikes a rib-arch to send a message, the echo no longer merely returns the sound of the topography. Instead, the Power allows the echoes to carry *memory*. 

When a walker taps the bone, they occasionally hear the "song" of the one who drifted into the roots generations ago. They hear the life-story of their ancestors caught in the fibers of the *Lignum-Gelid*. The history of their people, previously lost to the entropic drift, is now woven into the very acoustic mapping they use to survive. They are no longer isolated points of light in the dark; they are now joined by an unbroken, echoing lineage. 

### The Preservation of the Archive
The Power understands the importance of the way-markers. To ensure these totems of bone and resin are not lost to the deep currents, the Power gifts them a touch of *stasis-anchoring*. A marker placed by a Hollow-Walker now gains a stubborn, localized gravity. It will not drift into the roots until the person who placed it deems it unnecessary. 

This is not a cage, but a library. The Vault begins to accumulate a silent, glowing archive of obsidian blades and woven rope, forming a glowing, vertical geography of civilization that stretches from the highest arch down to the deep rot.

### Walking with them
The Power does not manifest to be worshipped. It manifests as a presence. Occasionally, a young Hollow-Walker, drifting in the void with arms outstretched, will feel a warmth—not of heat, but of *purpose*—that brushes against their translucent skin. It is the feeling of being looked at with affection. 

The Power provides this: when a Hollow-Walker finally prepares to return to the roots—to let go of their tether and sink into the cycle—they do not simply vanish into the dark. As they drift, their bioluminescence flares, not in the sour green of decay, but in a soft, steady gold. For a brief, eternal moment, they become a falling star within the Titan’s ribcage. 

The others look up, hearing the chime of their passing in the rhythm of the dripping moisture, and they do not feel grief. They hear the echo of a fellow traveler who has reached the end of the pilgrimage, a note that harmonizes perfectly with the Titan’s ghost-heartbeat. 

The Power, satisfied, retreats into the background radiation of the universe, becoming a silent listener. It knows that here, in the ribcage of a dead god, life has bloomed into something that does not fear the void, but has learned how to dance within it. The Vault is no longer just a place of falling, but a place of *becoming*.
