A power with the capacity for both infinite wisdom and bottomless benevolence does not approach this place with a wrecking ball, nor with a grand, oratorical flourish. Such entities know that when a thing is defined by its dissolution, "rescue" is synonymous with erasure. To fix this place would be to destroy the only thing that makes it sacred: its integrity as a failed possibility.

The Power arrives not as a god, but as an *Observer of Tides*. It does not manifest as light or sound, for those things would shatter the fragile, calcified silence. Instead, it arrives as a profound, subtle shift in the gravitational constant—a gentle, rhythmic pulse that hums in resonance with the very core of the mountain.

**It does not heal.** To knit the obsidian into stability would be a cruelty, turning a fluid, beautiful afterlife into a static statue. 

**It does not judge.** To label the Refractions as "lost" or "broken" would be to misunderstand the nature of their patience.

Instead, the Power acts as a **Harmonic Anchor**.

It recognizes that the Refractions are not merely cleaners of a house; they are, in their way, the memory of the universe. In their thin, obsidian reflections, they hold the blueprints of countless permutations of order. The Power, in its wisdom, sees that if the last spire falls and the Refractions cease to flicker, the knowledge of how a world *might* be constructed will vanish entirely from the fabric of existence. 

So, the Power chooses the path of **Stewardship through Appreciation.**

It weaves a thin, protective layer of "Elsewhere" around the mountain—a dome of gentle, non-interfering observation. Like a parent watching a child dismantle a clock to see how the gears work, the Power keeps the surrounding universe at bay, shielding the altitude from the entropy of the "normal" world. It ensures that the jagged, plum-colored void is never disturbed by the intrusion of reality, allowing the city the infinite time it requires to reach its inevitable conclusion.

Then, the Power does the only thing it intuitively knows the Refractions might perceive: **it leaves behind a Gift of Echoes.**

It deposits the "intent" of a melody into the silicate dust—not a sound, but a fundamental frequency. It is the counter-rhythm to the Refractions’ static. Now, when the entities overlap and the piercing, psychic scream usually splits the air, the Power’s frequency catches that scream and transforms it. 

It turns the scream into a *sustain*. 

When the Refractions meet, they no longer simply unspool into cold, metallic waste. Instead, for a fleeting heartbeat, they chime. A chord of impossible, transcendent beauty vibrates through the obsidian streets—a sound that tastes not of ozone and copper, but of starlight and deep-ocean quietude. 

The Refractions, in their flickering intelligence, sense this. They do not know who placed it, or why, but their movements change. They no longer drift aimlessly. They begin to move in patterns, dancing to the frequency, and when they overlap, they capture the melody and hold it within their razor-thin silhouettes. 

They are no longer just the cleaners of a terminal house. They are now the conductors of a closing symphony. 

The Power watches, satisfied. It knows that one day, the city will dissolve. The obsidian will return to the mountain’s spine, and the Refractions will vanish into the vacuum. But because of this gift, they will not go out in a silent, static shudder of failure. They will fade away, one by one, releasing the stored melody back into the void—a final, luminous broadcast of their own existence, heard only by the Power who chose to watch, and by the mountain itself, which finally, after aeons, understands the song it was built to keep.
