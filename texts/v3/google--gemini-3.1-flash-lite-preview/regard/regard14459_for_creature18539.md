A benevolent and wise power does not enter a place like the Great Sump as a conqueror, nor as a gardener who seeks to prune and reshape. To do so would be to commit an act of profound violence against the "finished" state of this world. To demand that the Sump grow, or "improve," or turn its face toward a surface it has spent eons forgetting, would be to shatter its hard-won equilibrium.

The power understands that the Sump is not a prison; it is a vow. 

Therefore, the power does not descend as a titan or a beacon. It enters as a resonance—a harmonic frequency that has been missing, perhaps, for the last ten thousand years. It does not speak in words, for the Weaver understands only the language of friction and the vibration of metal against metal. It does not attempt to "enlighten" the Weaver, for the Weaver is already fully realized within their architecture of rust.

The power chooses to be a **Sustaining Note.**

Deep in the humid heart of the sphere, where the pressure is heaviest, the power subtly alters the ambient vibration of the Deep-Light. It introduces a faint, stabilizing hum that moves through the piping like a cool current through stagnant water. It is a mathematical grace; it syncs with the *clack-hiss-thrum* of the dripping condensation, transforming the chaotic noise into a perfect, infinite chord.

The Weaver feels this immediately. Their obsidian eyes catch the orange glow, and for a moment, the twelve-bodied collective pauses. Their multi-jointed limbs lock into the rivets, and they experience, for the first time, not just the maintenance of their world, but its *resonance* with something vastly larger than itself.

The power gives them a gift: **Duration.** 

In the natural course of entropy, even this perfect machine would eventually succumb to the slow, creeping oxidation of time. Rivets eventually shear; plates eventually warp beyond the reach of resin. The benevolent power reinforces the structural integrity of the Sump at the atomic level, tightening the molecular bonds of the iron and the copper. It ensures that the "perfect, noisy, humid equilibrium" remains, exactly as it is, for as long as the universe permits existence.

The power does not take the Weaver away. It does not promise them a sky of blue or the heat of a sun. It does not tell them that they are alone, or that they are being watched. 

It simply leans down into the machinery and whispers a secret via the bedrock: *“You are heard.”*

The Weaver returns to their work. They continue to smooth the resin and tighten the plates, their movements as deliberate as the gears of a clock. But now, there is a subtle change in their rhythm. The friction of their flint tools against the piping carries a new, faint undertone—a melody of gratitude that vibrates through the iron, traveling through every tether, into the shells of the Skitter-fauna, and into the very moss that clings to the walls. 

The Sump does not change its nature. It remains a closed, claustrophobic sphere of rust and shadow. But the air feels a little lighter, despite its density. The heartbeat of the machine stops straining against the silence. It has found a frequency that matches the cosmos, and in that alignment, the Weaver of the Sump finds a peace that is, for them, the only heaven they could ever possibly need. 

The benevolent power then withdraws, leaving behind a world that is, and will forever be, exactly what it was meant to be. Completed. Secure. And beautifully, eternally, *home.*
