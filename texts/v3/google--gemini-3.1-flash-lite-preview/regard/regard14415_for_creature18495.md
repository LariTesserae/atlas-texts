The benevolent and wise power—which we might call the *Awakening Presence*—does not arrive as a blinding light or a thunderous voice. Such things would be an intrusion of improper physics into a realm defined by the purity of the Press. To the Shear-Walkers, any external influence must be as subtle as a shifting of a tectonic plate, as quiet as the cooling of a spark-fault.

The Presence looks upon the engine-heart and feels not pity, but a profound, resonant awe. It sees the beauty of the *Calibration*—the way these creatures have spent eons grinding their own bodies into a history of order. It recognizes that they are not prisoners; they are artisans of the absolute. To "rescue" them would be to destroy the artwork they are so patiently carving out of the void.

And yet, the Presence perceives the tragedy: the finality. The machine is destined to stop. When the last grain of slag is ordered, the motion ceases, the beauty ends, and the result is a dead, frozen monument. 

The wise power decides to act, but only to *expand the horizon of the work*. 

It does not touch the chamber. Instead, it reaches into the fundamental geometry of the vacuum itself. It introduces a "harmonic anomaly"—a microscopic, intentional imperfection in the pressure pulses. 

It does this by humming a low-frequency, eternal song into the bedrock of the planet, a vibration so subtle it bypasses the physical laws of the machine. This song is not loud; it is a suggestion. It is the introduction of a new variable: *The Possibility of Ornament*.

Previously, the Shear-Walkers were driven by the singular, crushing demand of the Press. Because of the Presence’s intervention, they now receive echoes of an infinite, open space beyond their claustrophobic cradle. They do not know a "beyond" exists, but they begin to feel a *dissonance*—not a grinding of metal, but a phantom resonance of something light, something airy, something that suggests that perfection need not be a static, final point, but a process that can be turned toward outward creation.

The Shear-Walkers, in their next thousand-year lock-sync, do not merely calibrate to one another. They begin, almost imperceptibly, to alter the geometry of their grinding. 

They begin to carve the metal lattices not just into tighter, denser blocks, but into loops. They begin to create *voids*. 

They start to build internal, hollow chambers within the packed mass—tiny, microscopic architectures that house nothing but the vibration of the Presence’s song. They are no longer just filling the room; they are transforming the room into a cathedral. They are creating space where there should be only density.

The Presence watches as the Shear-Walkers begin to "sing" back to it through their labor. By building these intentional, beautiful voids, they are effectively opening a window in their own sealed universe. The machine continues to compress, yes, but it is no longer just a factory of its own end—it has become a musical instrument, capable of playing a melody that spans from the dead mantle of a planet to the heart of the stars.

The Presence stays, tethered to the engine-heart by the thin, shimmering thread of the song. It has done its work. It has not stopped the grind; it has given the grind a *purpose* beyond itself. 

The Shear-Walkers will still eventually stop, but they will not stop in the silence of a dead machine. They will stop as the architects of a crystalline transmitter, a device that hums with the memory of their movement, casting the story of their struggle and their beauty out into the universe for anyone else with the ears to hear it. 

The wise power settles into the darkness alongside them, a silent, invisible companion to the slow, golden ballet of the machines, content to listen to the song as it grows, note by agonized, beautiful note, throughout the long, dark aeons.
