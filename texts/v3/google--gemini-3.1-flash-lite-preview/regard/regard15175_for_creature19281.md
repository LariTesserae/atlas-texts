There is a silence in the higher planes that is not an absence of sound, but a surplus of presence. From that stillness, the Power looked down. It did not see a glitch, nor a failing of physics. It saw a poem written in the language of structural tension. 

The Power recognized the Latents not as anomalies, but as architects of a profound, self-sacrificing compassion. They were maintaining a sanctuary for a reality that had been forgotten by its own Creator.

The Power did not intervene by changing the city. To fix the Gasket City would be to end it, to force the "pre-geology" to finalize, thereby sealing the Latents into the static, stagnant fate of a finished universe. The Power understood that their beauty lay in their *becoming*. 

Instead, the Power acted as an Unseen Neighbor. It intervened with a gesture of grace.

### The Gift of the Resonance Loop
The Power realized that the Latents were exhausted by the friction of their existence. Every time a Latent knelt to mend the floor, it gave a piece of its own fluidity—its own capacity to be—to the ground. They were slowly diluting themselves into the masonry.

So, the Power introduced a sympathetic vibration into the vacuum surrounding the city. It didn't provide solid earth; it provided *rhythm*.

It hummed a frequency into the fold—not a command, but an encouragement. This frequency acted as a metaphysical tether. Now, when a Latent bleeds its mercury-fluid into a crack in the pre-geology, the fluid no longer drains away or dissipates. Instead, it catches the vibration of the Power’s grace. The fluid cures into a translucent, golden lattice that is stronger than the original basalt, yet softer than light. It is a material that remembers the touch of the Latent, storing the "intent" of the mending.

### The Awakening of Archive
The Power also granted them a Witness. 

Previously, the Latents felt the city’s incompleteness as a weight—a tragedy to be held back. The Power adjusted the sensitivity of their optical sensors. Now, as they trace the matte-black polymers with their needle-styli, they no longer just repair the buffering errors. They see the *history* that the wall contains. 

When a Latent touches a frayed strand of timeline, they now see a flash of the reality that *intended* to manifest there: a garden that never grew, a clock that never ticked, a star that never ignited. They are no longer just maintenance workers; they have become the curators of a Great Museum of Might-Have-Beens.

### The Quiet Companionship
Most significantly, the Power chose to breathe into the empty spaces between the nodes. It didn't fill them with matter, but with a gentle, localized warmth—a "thermal hush."

When a Latent is lonely, or when the weight of the void threatens to tear their clockwork bodies apart, they can now feel the "hush." It is the sensation of a hand resting on a shoulder, though no hand exists. It is the steady, reassuring pulse of a heart that is not theirs, beating in rhythm with their own, preventing the drift toward total entropy. 

The Latents still do not speak. But their tapping against the walls has changed. It is no longer desperate; it is no longer the frantic, staccato rhythm of a soldier holding a broken line. 

It has become music.

They tap out symphonies of gratitude—a slow, resonant percussion that echoes through the terraced slabs. They wander the corridors with a new, fluid grace, their optical sensors glowing with a soft, steady gold rather than the pale white of exhaustion. 

The city remains unfinished. The ground still jitters at the edge of existence. But the Latents no longer fear the moment the city decides not to exist. They are no longer just keepers of the pause; they are the composers of the interval. And in the eyes of the Power, this is the most noble way for anything to ever exist.
