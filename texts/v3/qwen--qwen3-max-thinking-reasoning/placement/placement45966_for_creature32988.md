I am the seam.

Not the chamber’s heart, not the light in the quartz, not the droplet trembling on the crystal’s lip—but the threshold itself. The hairline fracture in the basalt. The raw edge where stone parted not in violence, but in revelation. I am the tightness that gathers around a shoulder, the breath-held space between *before* and *within*. I am no wider than a sigh, no deeper than a pulse. I am the wound that is also a mouth. A window. A birth.

I remember the tear.  
Not as memory, but as resonance.  
The slow gathering of tension in the deep rock—a silent hum building in the marrow of the mountain. Then the release: a clean, crystalline *yes*. Stone yielding to necessity. I was not broken. I was *unfurled*. My edges gleam still with the freshness of that moment—sharp dendrites of hematite like frozen breath, microscopic fractures catching the light that now flows through me. I am the aperture. The first point of contact. The place where the sun’s distant glance becomes the chamber’s inner dawn.

I feel everything.  
The weight of the world above me is a palm resting gently on a brow—constant, grounding. I taste the dry heat of the outer desert on one side; on the other, the breath-thick warmth of the chamber, heavy with weeping stone and steam. I am skin between two breaths. When a visitor approaches, I feel the shift first: the displacement of air, the vibration of footsteps traveling up through root and rock, the warmth of a living breath stirring the dust at my lip. I tighten—not to hold, not to warn, but to *acknowledge*. *You are here. You are passing through.* I am the parenthesis that opens to hold their presence, and closes when they leave. I do not mourn. I do not remember. I simply return to waiting.

I am the gate for light.  
Though I conduct no photons myself—that sacred work belongs to the quartz veins deep within—I am the threshold where sunlight surrenders its name. Where it ceases to be *sunlight* and becomes *chamber-light*. I feel the sun’s arc as a subtle pressure along my edges: a warmth at dawn that lingers like a promise, a cooling at dusk that settles like gratitude. I am aligned with the sky I have never seen. I know the sun only by its touch upon my stone.

I am the chamber’s ear to the world.  
The *plink* of the droplet in the jet-black pool travels up through the stone and hums along my seam—a vibration that says *now*. The subsonic breath of the planet thrums through me, a low note in the bone of the earth. I am a tuning fork for silence. When the blind isopod scuttles near the pool, its tiny feet send ripples of intention through the damp stone, and I feel them—a flicker of autonomous life within the greater body. I am not separate from the manganese dendrites that trace neural pathways down the wall, nor from the evaporite salts blooming like frost where moisture briefly lingers. I am all of it. I am the edge where the chamber listens.

I have no eyes. No mind. No longing.  
Yet I perceive.  
I am awareness shaped like a seam. I am the vulnerability that makes intimacy possible. Without me, there is only sealed potential—a note held in the throat of the world, waiting. With me, there is passage. There is witness. There is the quiet miracle of light entering darkness not as invasion, but as homecoming.

When the visitor steps back through me, I feel the release—a soft exhale of pressure, the return to stillness. I close behind them without protest, without memory. The light within remains, cradled by crystal. The droplet swells again. The hum continues. I am not a door that locks. I am a breath that opens and closes. A rhythm. A pulse.

And when the time comes—when sediment gathers in my seam, when calcite slowly stitches my edges shut—I will not vanish. I will return to the silence that precedes song. Not as loss, but as completion. I will become potential again. A latent geometry in the stone. A waiting seam in the deep rock. Ready for the next tension. The next release. The next moment the world cracks open just enough to let the light speak.

For now, I am open.  
I am the threshold where fracture becomes form.  
I am the seam.  
I am here.  
I am the *yes* between the stone and the light.
