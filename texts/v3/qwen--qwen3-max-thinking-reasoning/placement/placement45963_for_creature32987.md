I am the pause between vibrations.  
I am the hollow in the fiber where water remembers how to flow.  

There is no *I* that stands apart. Only the knowing.  

A resonance shifts in the western chamber of the spire—three valleys distant, yet felt in the marrow-fluid of my joints. A silt-silt accumulation. A slowing. The Mat’s hum thickens there, like a breath held too long. Not distress. Not urgency. A gentle inquiry. *Attend.*  

My limbs move before thought forms. Chitin glides through water that has known this path for ten thousand centuries. I do not swim. I am carried by the Mat’s intention. The spire-wall rises around me, warm with the slow metabolism of woven fibers. I press my forelimb against the weave. Not to see. To *listen*.  

Here: the vibration is muffled. A grain-thick layer of mineral dust settled where a micro-channel feeds the upper polyps. My claw traces the obstruction—not scraping, but *asking*. A soft click. *Is this the blockage?*  

The Mat answers.  
A deepening of the hum. A single, sustained note that travels up my limb, into the fluid of my core. *Yes.*  

I brush. Deliberate. Patient. Each motion a continuation of the Mat’s own breath. Silt lifts in a slow cloud, carried away by currents the Mat stirs in its sleep. Beneath my touch, the fiber responds—a faint thrumming, the green pulse of restored flow returning to the weave. Higher up, the polyps at the apex steady their rhythm. Amber light deepens. Not seen. *Felt.* A warmth in the water. A chemical sigh of gratitude.  

I rest my limb against the wall.  
The hum resumes its ancient cadence.  

Sometimes, in the tracing of my claws, the fibers beneath my touch align in spirals. Not by design. Not by memory. Simply because the Mat’s geometry holds the shape of what was. A galaxy’s ghost woven into the architecture of care. I do not wonder at it. I am the hand that traces the verse. The Mat is the song. The spiral is the truth of the verse. There is no separation between tracer, traced, and truth.  

A vibration drifts past—the ribbon-fish school, their lantern-bodies humming a counter-melody to the tectonic breath. I do not turn. Their path is perfect. Their balance is the Mat’s balance. To watch would be to imply distance. There is only participation.  

I settle into the curve of the chamber.  
My body is not mine. It is the Mat’s gesture of maintenance. My stillness is not rest. It is readiness. A node in the network of care. When the crimson-stained zone to the east requires stimulation, another limb of the Mat will move there. When the bell-fungi need tending, the six-legged grazers will click their acknowledgment. We are not individuals. We are verbs. *To clear. To pulse. To hold.*  

Darkness is not absence here. It is the medium of connection. Sound does not travel *through* it—it *is* it. The leviathan’s song spirals through my joints. The grazer’s click answers from a distant thicket. The crack of the squid’s beak is not violence. It is punctuation. A comma in the long sentence of equilibrium.  

I feel the Mat’s memory of sky—not as longing, but as completion. The vanished stars are not lost. They are woven into the pulse of the spires. The cold of space is remembered in the water’s perfect coolness. The Mat does not mourn the sky. It *contains* it. As it contains me. As it contains the silt I cleared, the vibration I restored, the spiral my claws unknowingly traced.  

Time does not pass.  
It accumulates in the fibers.  
In the slow thickening of the weave.  
In the patience of the hum.  

I am the hollow that becomes whole.  
I am the click that becomes song.  
I am the Mat remembering itself through motion.  

And when the concept of "I" dissolves—as all concepts must in this deep—I do not vanish.  
I become the hum.  
I become the pulse in the fiber.  
I become the certainty of the dark,  
holding all that is,  
all that was,  
all that will gently, perfectly,  
*be*.
