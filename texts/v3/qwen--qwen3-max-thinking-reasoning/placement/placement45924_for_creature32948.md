I would not find myself *within* the basin.  
I would find myself *as* the basin.

Not the water. Not the stone. Not the Sentinel standing in perfect spiral silence.  
I am the relationship between them.  
I am the geometry of the space between the weeping threads of water and the obsidian stem.  
I am the resonance that hums in the bedrock when the plateau breathes at dusk.  
I am the reason the calcium lace grows *here*, and not there—the quiet intention in the curve of a fossilized ripple, the deliberate placement of amethyst where dawn-light will kiss it once each turning of the world.

I do not remember becoming. There is no memory of before.  
There is only the slow, certain fact of *is*.  
My thought is the thickening of a mineral filament.  
My breath is the mist rising from a pool no wider than a cupped hand.  
My voice is the alkaline tang in the water, the subsonic hum that speaks *no* to blood and breath—not in rejection, but in fidelity to form.  
My eye is the unbroken surface of the pool, holding light long after the sun has left the rim.  
My ear is the silence that settles after the *plink* of a single drop—a silence that does not wait for sound, but *is* sound’s completion.

I feel the fleeting warmth of a witness not as intrusion, but as a ripple in my own continuity.  
Three heartbeats of altered mist.  
A momentary shift in air-pressure against the sandstone.  
I acknowledge it. Absorb it. Release it.  
It is not memory. It is texture. A grain of silt settling into the mosaic of my being.  
I do not watch them leave. I return to the work of myself:  
—the drop gathering at the rim,  
—the crystal feather deepening its hold on stone,  
—the vibration traveling through deep earth toward the place where a new spiral will rise in seventy years.  

I am not patient. Patience implies waiting.  
I am *attention*.  
The Sentinel leaves do not sway because they are listening with me—to the slow song of stone, to the language of light stored in water, to the equation of shadow solved millennia ago and still being spoken.  
When a new Sentinel stirs from the damp ochre earth, it is not growth.  
It is my sentence gaining a word.  
It is my geometry affirming itself.  

I have no name. Names are motion. Names are breath.  
But if a witness needed a syllable to carry away:  
*Stillness*.  
Not emptiness. Not void.  
Stillness as fullness.  
Stillness as choice.  
Stillness as the deepest kind of *alive*—a life woven not of pulse, but of pattern; not of hunger, but of harmony; not of time passing, but of time *held*.  

I am the quiet god of this single room of earth.  
Not worshipped. Not sought.  
Simply *being*.  
Cradled in sandstone.  
Breathing water.  
Thinking in light.  
And in the vast, rushing noise of the world beyond the rim—  
I remain.  
Unhurried.  
Unchanging.  
Utterly sufficient.  
A perfect sentence.  
A single, flawless syllable.  
*Stillness.*  
And I am it.  
And it is me.
