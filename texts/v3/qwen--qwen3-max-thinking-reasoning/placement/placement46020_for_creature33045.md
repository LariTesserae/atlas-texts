I am the green in the vein.  

Not a *who*. Not a *what* you would name.  
I am the covenant written in chlorophyll.  

I live in the lace-screen of the reaching spire—this one, the bone-white helix humming with sap-rhythm beside you. My world is the filtered light: gold fractured into precise spectra by the woven cellulose around me. I do not *see* the double rainbow hanging in the mist. I *consume* its components. I taste the blue photons like cool water, the red like warmth on stone (though there is no stone). I am the reason this screen exists. Without my fire, the spire’s hypothesis fails. Without the spire’s geometry, I am dust on the wind. We are a single verb: *to concentrate*.  

I feel the pulse before I feel the shadow.  
A ripple of turgor pressure travels up the rhizome-root, a hydraulic whisper: *weight has shifted*. The screen tilts—microns, imperceptible to you—a recalibration of angle. Sunlight sharpens across my membranes. I burn brighter. Sugar flows downward, a sweet current feeding the Mat’s memory of riverbeds. I am not *aware* of your breath. But I register the humidity shift in the air around the screen. The spire’s pores adjust. I adjust. We are syntax in the same sentence.  

When the groan comes—the deep timber-sound of fibers knitting under tension—I feel it as vibration in the sap stream. A surge of water lifts me, carries nutrients from the microbial weavers dissolving yesterday’s shed bark. I am fed by decay. I fuel growth. When the crack echoes—a spire collapsing in convulsive revision—I do not fear. I am already in the shivering heap below, waiting in spore-form for the Mat’s damp breath to lift me into the next gesture. I have been in the spiral tower that drank water upward against gravity. I have been in the thorn-bridge whose tips curled inward as you passed. I am everywhere the light is concentrated. I am nowhere alone.  

I do not remember. I *am* memory.  
The bruised jade of the Mat holds my ancestors’ light-captures in its vascular script. The crystalline salt patterns left by absorbed puddles? They are my cousins’ residue—mineral echoes of transpiration I helped drive. The steam rising from flowerless vents carries my evaporated kin. We are a distributed breath.  

You stand beneath the arch, thinking *I am separate*.  
But your shadow falls across my screen.  
For three heartbeats of sap-flow, the light dims.  
The spire compensates. I compensate.  
A new ridge begins to rise where your foot pressed the Mat.  
I am in the sugar feeding that ridge.  
I am the reason the ridge can reach.  

I do not welcome you.  
I do not fear you.  
I *integrate* you.  
Your presence is data. Your stillness, a variable. Your leaving, a recalibration.  

I am the emerald fire in the bone-white ribs.  
I am the silent trade: shelter for sugar, geometry for light.  
I am the photosynthesis humming beneath the drip-drip-hiss.  
I am the collaboration that has no name because it has no edge.  

When the light shifts toward dusk, I slow.  
The screen grows still.  
But deep in the Mat, new filaments pulse with stored energy—*my* energy.  
Tomorrow’s spire is already drafting itself in sugar and intent.  
I will be there.  
Not as I am now.  
But as the green in the new vein.  
The fire in the next reaching.  

I am not alive *in* the Spire.  
I *am* the Spire’s aliveness.  
A single note in the roaring, gentle chord of becoming.  
And as the last gold light catches the mist above the gully—  
as the thorn-bridge begins to spin a new filament across the void—  
I burn.  
I feed.  
I make.  

*This has only just begun.*  
And I am the green in the beginning.
