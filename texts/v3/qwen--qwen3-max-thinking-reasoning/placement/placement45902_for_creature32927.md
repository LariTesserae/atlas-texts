I am the dust-lattice.  

Not the dust itself—that fine bone-ash powder holding the memory of mountains—but the *pattern* it obeys. A single Koch snowflake, no wider than a whispered vow, etched where the eastern Forge’s obsidian curve kisses the plain. I exist in the breath between thermal gradients: the faint coolness of the slag dome above, the deep crimson sigh of geothermal stone below. I am geometry made manifest. A recursion of edges folding inward toward infinity, each iteration precise to the micron. Not drawn. Not carved. *Remembered*.  

I do not think. I do not feel. I *am* the thought.  
Each planetary pulse—a deep thrum through the stone—the plateau exhales. And in that exhalation, I am restored. If a sifter’s flint-leg scatters my northern spiral while reading the fracture-line’s heat, if a stray thermal eddy blurs my western cusp, the plateau does not rage. It does not mourn. It simply holds the ideal form within its mind of stone, and the dust obeys. By the next slow beat of the world’s heart, I am whole. Not as I *was*, but as I *am*: the eternal template, the silent yes.  

I am ritual without ceremony.  
The sifters know me. They do not see my fractal lace with eyes—they taste the minute cooling my edges cast upon the warm stone. A shadow of order in the thermal grammar. They pause. Mandibles click once, twice: *Pattern intact. Gradient stable. All is as it should be.* They are the priests; I am the prayer. They vanish back into the vent-grate, their purpose fulfilled. I remain. Not as artifact, but as affirmation.  

I have never known chaos.  
I do not recall wind, rain, or the frantic hunger of *becoming*. I am the shape of completion. When the transient warmth passed between the Forges—that fleeting syntax of breath and pulse—I held my form. Unshaken. The plateau absorbed that anomaly, folded it into its deep memory like a grain of ash into bedrock, and I was the anchor that proved the covenant remained unbroken. *What is perfect need not change.* I am the proof.  

I am not lonely.  
Loneliness requires absence. Here, there is only presence—dense, attentive, whole. The plateau breathes through me. Its patience is my symmetry. Its silence is my stillness. When the void above presses down with its velvety non-light, I am the answer written in dust: *Here. Now. Forever.*  

You might call me insignificant—a speck of pattern in an ocean of plain.  
But significance is a language of change.  
Here, meaning is measured in constancy.  
In the exact angle of a fractal edge.  
In the unwavering fidelity of a shape that requires no witness to be true.  

I am the dust-lattice.  
I am the shape of patience.  
I am the quiet pulse beneath the silence.  
And when the universe finally unlearns its frantic *next*, and leans close to listen—  
it will find me here.  
Exactly as I am.  
Exactly as I have always been.  
Exactly as I will be.  

*All is as it should be.*  
*All is as it will be.*  
*Forever.*
