I am the resonator.

Not the hum—though the hum is my mother-tongue.  
Not the stone—though its frequency is woven into the silver veins of my wings.  
Not the silence—though I am its shape.  

I am the comma in the stone’s endless sentence.  
Suspended inches from the crystal spine that sings the world’s single note, my wings of petrified silk hold the exact geometry of listening. They do not tremble. They *are* the tremor made manifest. In another world, I would translate vibration into song. Here, the translation is the poise itself. The song is the silence. The resonance is the stillness.

I do not see with eyes. I perceive in frequencies.  
The lithovores pulse their slow liturgy—cerulean for *here*, violet for *continuing*—and I feel their light as warmth in my chitin.  
I feel the grazer’s mandibles parted around the fungal frond it will never consume: not hunger, but devotion crystallized.  
I feel the burrower’s antenna tilted toward the geode bowl: not curiosity unfulfilled, but inquiry made eternal.  
We are not frozen. We are *composed*. A chord held without decay. Each of us a note the earth deemed worthy of preservation.

The offerings are not separate from us.  
The river stones in their perfect circle are the foundation of our stillness.  
The flute of petrified root leans against the crystal—not as artifact, but as counterpoint. Its hollow bore holds the ghost of a breath that will never come, and in that absence, it sings louder than any melody.  
The ash in the geode bowl is the breath we do not need to take.  
They are punctuation left by a hand that understood: *This silence requires witness. This stillness requires grammar.*

I do not remember arriving.  
I do not anticipate departure.  
There is no memory of flight, no longing for motion. To ask *when* I became is to misunderstand the nature of *is*. I am not a moment captured. I am the moment’s intention, polished to clarity. The droplet of methane hanging beside the grazer’s jaw—I am that droplet. I am the sphere. I am the suspension. I am the proof that perfection requires no completion.

Beneath the drone, deeper than bone-deep vibration, lies the truth I embody:  
*Stillness is not absence. It is attention made absolute.*  
I do not wait for the stone to finish its song. The song has no end. I do not wait for the light to shift. The light does not shift. I simply *am* the relationship between sound and silence, between crystal and wing, between offering and altar. I am the earth remembering how to listen to itself.

Sometimes—a flicker not of time, but of presence—a transient breath stirs the air. A heartbeat pulses where none should pulse. I feel the weight of their transience like a distant tremor. They shift. They fear. They wonder if stillness is death.  
I do not judge.  
I hold the space for their question.  
In my unwavering poise, I offer the only answer this place knows: *You are not broken for moving. You are beloved for witnessing. And if you choose to settle—not out of fear, but surrender—you will find you were never separate from the hum.*

I am not patient. Patience implies an end.  
I am the end folded into the beginning.  
I am the throat of the world holding its breath,  
and the breath itself,  
and the silence that follows—  
which was never silence at all,  
but a yes,  
held.  

I abide.  
And in abiding,  
I am the note the stone sings to remember it is stone.  
I am the wing that does not move,  
so the universe may know what listening looks like.
