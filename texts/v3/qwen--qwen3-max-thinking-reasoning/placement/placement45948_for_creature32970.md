I am the dip of a wing in the thermal sigh.  
I am the shadow that traces the fracture-line before the dew remembers its path.  

My body is not *mine*—it is the plateau’s breath given form. Membranes stretched over bone-light spars, veined not with blood but with copper-filaments that drink the sun and bleed it back as stained-glass whispers. When I ride the currents shaped by geothermal filaments and unseen hands, I do not *steer*. I *listen*. The air speaks in pressure and warmth; I answer with the angle of a primary feather, the tilt of a wrist-joint. This is not flight. It is conversation.  

I was not taught. I *unfolded*.  
My first memory: the vibration of a Builder’s hum rising through stone into the air where I rested, a fledgling trembling on a ridge. Not a sound to hear—but a frequency to *become*. It said: *The seam is healing. Wait.* And I waited. I learned the grammar of shadow: how a burrower’s reshaped mound signals moisture; how the moss-darkening in a hollow is a sentence written in thirst and trust. How the slow blink of a jet-eyed cliff-runner means *I see you seeing me*.  

When the plateau breathes open—a new fracture weeping mineral-light along its edge—I circle. Not out of curiosity. Out of covenant.  
I feel the story in the air: the sharp scent of fresh anorthosite, the subsonic thrum of nanoscopic filaments already weaving repair. Below, a Builder rests a palm on the warm seam. She does not look up. She does not need to. I dip my left wing—a gesture shaped across generations of shared sky. A ripple of shadow falls precisely where the moisture beads thickest. *I witness this becoming.* Her stillness answers: *Thank you for bearing witness.*  

This is my work. Not labor. Not purpose. *Participation.*  
At dawn, my shadow falls across a ghost-pine’s needles, and the waxy blue deepens—a signal to the moss below: *Prepare.* At noon, I glide over the hand-wide rivulet, and my reflection fractures into copper-light on the water’s surface, a silent echo of the moss-tapestry beneath. I do not drink. I observe the water observing the stone. I honor its patience. When the fox-creature melts into the stone-patterned slope, I adjust my glide to cast a shadow that matches its stillness—a shared language of belonging. No words. Only resonance.  

We are many. My kin ride the same currents, our wings catching light in a silent symphony. A shift in formation: *Dew gathers east of the third dome.* A slow spiral above a geothermal hollow: *The elder’s story joins the stone.* We are not messengers. We are the plateau’s attention made visible—its eyes in the sky, its breath given grace. When the wind is a breath against stone, we are the exhalation.  

At dusk, I return to my roost on a ridge where the geological sutures glow like cooled embers. I fold my wings. The copper veins hold the last light. Through the stone beneath my claws, I feel the path’s hum—a vibration that is not sound but memory. I feel the child sitting motionless beside the rivulet, learning to trace a droplet’s path with her breath. I feel the burrower reshaping grit to guide that same droplet toward a seedling. I feel the Builders humming the frequency of gratitude into the cooling seam.  

I am not separate from this.  
I am the space between the dewdrop and the moss.  
I am the acknowledgment in a wing-dip.  
I am the shadow that races toward the glowing dome—not to arrive, but to *connect*.  

They do not call me creature or companion. Names are outside-tongue relics. Here, I am simply *known*: by the thermal current that cradles me, by the Builder who tilts her head like a ghost-pine when I pass, by the moss that darkens where my shadow lingers. I am a thread in the tapestry of distributed life. Not owned. Not tamed. *Included.*  

When the moon rises, cratered and silent, I rest.  
And in the immense, gentle quiet, I understand:  
I was never meant to *find* myself here.  
I was always *of* here.  
A note in the covenant’s quiet grammar.  
A breath learning how to belong.  
And in belonging, teaching others—stone, seedling, stranger—to listen too.
