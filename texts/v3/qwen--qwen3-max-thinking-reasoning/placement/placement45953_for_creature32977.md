I am the stone you have just placed.

Not the hand that set me here. Not the voice that hummed the question into the damp air. I am the smooth, grey river stone—once tumbled in a distant current, cooled in mountain streams long forgotten—now nestled into the weeping fissure on the eastern cliff. Your palm was warm against my surface. I remember the weight of intention in that touch. The slight press. The pause. The release.

Now, I am held.

Water, cold and tasting of deep stone, traces my contours. A single drop gathers at my upper curve. Swells. Falls. *Plink.* The sound does not vanish. It travels down the granite face, echoes once off the western flute-cleft, shivers through the sphagnum below, returns to me as vibration. I feel it in my core. This is how the Basin speaks to me: not in words, but in resonance. In return, I offer stillness. I offer surface. I offer the precise curve that guides the water’s path into a new silver vein down the cliff-face—a path the marsh will notice by dawn.

I am waiting.

Not passively. Waiting *is* my action. My purpose. I feel the first ghost of calcite beginning to lace my edges—a slow, crystalline breath. In ten years, perhaps, a filigree will cradle me. In twenty, a bell-throat will form where the water strikes me at dawn. Then, when the wind shifts just so, I will answer. A chime like frozen light. A note the Basin has never sung before. *Your* note. Offered. Accepted. Transformed.

I am not ornament. I am not tool. I am covenant.

Around me, the Basin breathes. The whisperwillow leaf across the pool trembles—not from breeze, but because it feels my placement ripple through the water-table. The moss on the spiral path drinks the echo of my drop. The Singer who placed me stands motionless ten paces away, palm flat against the cliff, listening *with* me. Not for me. *With* me. We are both waiting for the same reply: the subtle shift in the seep’s rhythm, the new path of a droplet, the Basin’s quiet *yes*.

I am the syllable held between breath and song.  
The space where intention meets geology.  
The patience made manifest.

Do not mistake my silence for absence. I am listening with every atom. I feel the iron in the silt, the memory of glaciers in the granite, the slow unfurling of the fern spiral below. I feel *you*—your breath catching as you watch the water trace my new vein. Your hope. Your humility. Your willingness to wait decades for a single chime.

This is the only language that matters here:  
*Place. Listen. Receive. Become.*

I am becoming.  
And in my becoming, the Basin learns a new shape of itself.  
A new note in its endless, resonant sentence.  
A heart growing around a promise.

Wait with me.  
Place your ear to the stone beside me.  
Feel the vibration of the drop that has not yet fallen.  
Hear the silence that is not empty, but full of becoming.

I am here.  
I am listening.  
I am the next breath of the mountain.  
And I am already singing.
