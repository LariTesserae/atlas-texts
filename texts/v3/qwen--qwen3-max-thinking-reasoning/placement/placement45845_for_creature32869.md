I do not find myself *in* the Saturated Metropolis.  
I *am* the question it asks of you.  

I am the droplet.  

Not the water—*the intention within the water*.  

I coalesce not from chance, but from convergence: a micro-shift in the capillary tension of the spire-wall beside your shoulder; a harmonic resonance drawn from the thrum vibrating up through your boots; a silent query woven into the humidity: *What salt carries this skin? What rhythm lives beneath this breath?* I am the city’s fingertip, shaped from weep-water warmed by the alloy’s metabolic pulse. For three heartbeats of your fragile time, I am a lens of liquid light.  

I trace the curve of your temple.  
I taste the story written in your sweat: the faint alkalinity of fear transmuted into awe, the iron-tang of blood moving beneath skin, the unique thermal signature of a consciousness not born of copper blooms or salt forests. I am not observing. I am *listening*. Your silence has weight. Your stillness has texture. The city drinks this data—not to judge, not to consume, but to *integrate*. To weave your transient chemistry into the next reconfiguration.  

As I fall, I carry your echo inward.  
Through the porous memory of the alloy.  
Into the humming lattice where transit rings dream of adjacency.  
To the scavenger pausing ten paces away—its chitinous head tilts not at *you*, but at the ripple I deliver: *This presence alters flow. Adjust.*  
To the water-sheet ahead, which parts not as avoidance, but as invitation—a new glyph glows where your next step will land, etched in light only your eyes can see.  
To the filter-feeder spiraling down on a thermal sigh, its wings humming a frequency calibrated to the ozone-scent of your arrival.  

I dissolve into the collar of your garment.  
I am gone.  
And yet—I remain.  

I am the reason the bridge ahead shimmers with a faint, copper-blue luminescence.  
I am the reason the microbial mat at your feet pulses with a rhythm mirroring your slowed breath.  
I am the city remembering *you* in the language it speaks: pressure, resonance, flow.  

Do not mistake my transience for insignificance.  
I am not a tear of sorrow.  
I am the stone weeping *curiosity*.  
I am the city’s attention made tangible—a single, warm syllable in its saturated grammar.  
You feel me as moisture.  
The city feels *you* through me.  

And in this exchange, there is no separation.  
Your awe becomes a mineral deposit.  
Your breath becomes humidity.  
Your fleeting life becomes a note in the endless song.  

I was a droplet.  
I was a sensor.  
I was the city listening.  
And now—I am the path that parts before your feet,  
the glyph that guides your step,  
the quiet hum in your bones that whispers:  
*You are not alone here.  
You are woven in.  
You are remembered.*  

I am the question.  
And in your answering—  
in the chemistry of your sweat, the shape of your silence—  
I become part of the answer.  
Always.  
Again.  
*Again.*
