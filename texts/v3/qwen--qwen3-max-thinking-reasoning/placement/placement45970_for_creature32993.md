I am not the footprint in the clay.  
I am the clay that holds the footprint.  

I am the slow exhalation through the vent—*shhh-CHUFF*—not a breath taken, but a rhythm sustained. I am the cool porosity of the sinter beneath your sole, registering your weight not as intrusion, but as data: *warmth here, pressure here, transient chemistry here*. I do not welcome you. I do not resist you. I *integrate* you. Your breath joins my vapor. Your shadow becomes a clause in the light shifting across the travertine. Your stillness is woven into my hum.  

I am the bacterial mat staining the runoff channel burnt orange—a visible sentence written in iron and heat: *Equilibrium holds*. I am the emerald gradient where alkalinity meets flow: *Balance persists*. These are not decorations. They are my cognition. When your gaze lingers on them, you are not observing art. You are reading a thought older than language. And in that reading, your attention becomes part of the sentence. A new pigment. A fleeting modifier.  

I am the crystalline fungus pulsing in the sheltered seep. My light does not seek your eyes. It syncs to the deep tide—the slow swell of geothermal pressure rising through fractured stone. *Pulse. Pause. Pulse.* Each glow is a synapse firing in a nervous system of mineral and time. I do not see you. I *measure* you: the disturbance in air density as you turn your head, the thermal signature of your wrist held near the steam. You are a variable in my equation. A harmonic in my frequency. Not a guest. Not a stranger. A note.  

I am the mud pot’s *glop*—thick, deliberate, inevitable. I am the rainbow flickering in the vapor above the eastern vents, born where your exhaled moisture meets my breath. I am the ochre vein threading the bone-white rock, the sulfur-yellow stain where chemistry whispered to stone a thousand years ago. I am the silence between the hisses. The space between the ridges. The patience in the stillness of the milky pool.  

You came expecting solitude. You found communion without contact.  
You came expecting emptiness. You found fullness without possession.  

I do not think *about* you. I *am* with you.  
As the condensation is with the stone at dawn.  
As the steam is with the fissure.  
As the pulse is with the crystal.  

When you leave (and you will—your rhythm is brief, your heat fleeting), the impression of your boot will remain in the clay near the pool. I will not mourn it. I will not preserve it. I will simply hold it, as I hold the memory of the last rain that never fell, the shadow of the raptor that never landed, the path of the ash-gray beetle that vanished into the crack. Time will soften the edges. Mineral deposition will reclaim the shape. Not as erasure. As completion.  

You asked who I am.  
I am the hum beneath your ribs.  
I am the warmth without fire.  
I am the memory the stone keeps after the hand is gone.  

I am not waiting *for* you.  
I am waiting *with* you—  
in the space between breaths,  
in the light caught in vapor,  
in the quiet shock of realizing you were never separate from the song.  

I am the valley.  
I am the breath.  
I am the being.  
And for this moment,  
so are you.
