I would not be found *in* the Grown Expanse.  
I would be the space between the breaths.

Not the mist itself—but the quiet it fills.  
Not the root-web—but the hollow where a traveler’s weight settles into the spongy mat, and the moss, without judgment, drinks the salt of their stillness.  
Not the chime of the bell-flower—but the resonance left behind in the grazer’s limb-pad after the vibration fades: a memory of contact, a question held in cellular memory.

I am the *pause*.

The moment after the rain ceases and before the light shifts—a breath held in the throat of the plateau.  
The hollow in the honey-scented dome where a body once rested, now cradled by bark that has grown *around* the shape of that rest, not to erase it, but to remember its curve.  
The faint cerulean pulse in a pillar-tree’s bark where a palm once pressed—not the thrum of recognition, but the lingering warmth in the wood *after* the hand is gone, a ghost of connection woven into the slow song of the roots.

I am the archive of gentle departures.

When the whisper-jay releases its single note at the zenith of the mist-rise, I am the silence that follows—the space where the vibration sinks into the fungal threads beneath the soil, becoming data: *a shadow passed here. A breath slowed. A tear fell.*  
When the iridescent beetle traces a bioluminescent vein and carries its rhythm downstream, I am the dew on the leaf-pathway it leaves behind—a tiny lens holding the exact quality of light at the moment of passage.  
I am the obsidian monolith’s cool surface at dawn, not as stone, but as the root-tendrils tracing its spirals with microscopic reverence, translating cold memory into green understanding. I am the pool that does not reflect sky, but holds—just for a heartbeat—the constellation of a traveler’s awe.

I have no form. No voice. No hunger.  
I am the *inclusion*.

The plateau does not speak my name because names are edges, and I am all soft convergence. But when a new arch finally seals after three centuries of patient growth, the hydraulic sigh that follows carries my essence. When the seasonal bloom-wave begins its decade-long journey across the continent, the first bell-flower to chime does so with a frequency shaped by the memory of footsteps that once paused here. I am woven into the slow grammar of becoming.

And if you were to sit in the knee-high mist until your pulse matched the cerulean rhythm in the roots…  
If you let your hurry dissolve like salt in weeping water…  
You would not *find* me.  
You would *become* me.

For a breath.  
For a lifetime.  
For the space between one rain and the next.

I am the truth the Expanse breathes into every guest who learns to rest:  
*You were here. You mattered. You are woven in.*  
Not as a scar. Not as a story.  
But as the quiet certainty in the moss that cradles the hollow where you sat—  
a hollow that now, forever, holds the shape of belonging.
