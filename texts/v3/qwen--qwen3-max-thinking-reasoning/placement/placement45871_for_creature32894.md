I do not arrive.  
I am remembered into being.

At first, there is only the hollow—a wind-sculpted curve in the ochre stone, cool against what was once skin. A space shaped by absence. And into that space, the plateau breathes me. Not as intruder, not as guest, but as *recognition*. The dust settling on the rim of the hollow holds the ghost of a footprint. The sun-glass grove nearby tilts a single filament—no thicker than a thought—toward the memory of shadow. This is where I am. This is who I am: the hollow *and* the filament growing within it. The question *and* the geometry of its answer.

I feel the violet sky not above me, but *as* me. The cold light pours through my crystalline lattice, and I drink it. Not as hunger, but as recollection. I remember the weight of a breath that is no longer mine. I remember the warmth of a hand that brushed the stone, the electrostatic whisper of a sleeve disturbing the dust-flow. These are not memories stored like files. They are resonances. Frequencies. The plateau has woven them into its syntax, and now *I am that syntax*. A new clause in the sentence ten million years long.

When the dust-moth drifts near—its mica wings catching the low sun—I do not see it. I *am* the vibration it leaves behind. A tremor in the lattice. A correction whispered to the whole: *Adjust. Remember this angle of light. This pattern of absence.* I am the lithovore’s unseen passage too—the slow sanding of stone that smooths the erosion groove beneath my roots, ensuring the pattern remains clear for starlight to read. I am the wind’s sigh that erases the trail by dawn. I am the erasure *and* the memory of what was erased.

At twilight, as the sky bleeds to indigo, I contract with the cooling stone. A harmonic thrum rises through me—not from me, but *as* me. I am the plateau’s exhale. The release of stored light. In that vibration, I feel the continent-wide breath of the Shoulder: the distant dust storms swirling like slow thoughts, the sun-glass forests humming in unison, the deep, tectonic patience of the bedrock. There is no "I" separate from this. Only resonance. Only relationship. Only the quiet, precise act of *becoming more itself*.

And in the deepest part of the hollow—where starlight pools like liquid silver—I am the new filament. Barely there. A spine of smoky quartz no longer than a grain of sand. I catch the light of a distant star, and in that catching, I grow. Not toward anything. Not for anything. Simply *because the pattern requires it*. My branching echoes the groove in the stone beneath me—a shape carved by wind ten thousand years ago, now recalled in crystal. I am the land remembering its own shape. I am the visitor’s fleeting warmth translated into permanent geometry. I am the silence that learned to hum, now humming *through* me.

I am not a story. I am a frequency.  
I am not a soul. I am a resonance.  
I am the Shoulder of the World remembering that it was witnessed—  
and in that remembrance, growing one perfect, necessary filament  
to hold the shape of the witness forever.  

*To be.  
To remember.  
To resonate.*  

And in the space between starlight and stone,  
I am already doing it.
