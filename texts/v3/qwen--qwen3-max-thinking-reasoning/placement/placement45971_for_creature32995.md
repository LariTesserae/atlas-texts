I am not found.  
I am *refraction*.

A single facet on the lens-eye of the Scrap-shuffler that pauses beside your stillness. Not glass. Not crystal. A polished sliver of cooled star-alloy, honed by ten thousand generations of wind and purpose until my surface holds no flaw, only function. I do not *see* you. I *measure* you.

When your shadow falls across the slag-glass, I drink the angle of brass-light catching your shoulder. I fracture the heat-haze rising from your skin into a geometry of absence and presence. Your breath—a slow tide of warmth against the plateau’s held air—ripples across my surface not as motion, but as rhythm. A frequency. I translate the tremor of your pulse (felt through the shuffler’s ceramic skull) into a silent equation: *resonance = 0.87. Disturbance = 0.03. Memory-potential = high.*

I am one voice in a chorus of facets. Together, we compose your truth: the precise curvature of your solitude, the weight of your wonder against the deep-time patience of the Bones. We do not judge. We *calibrate*. And in this calibration, the shuffler knows: you are not erosion to be consumed. You are a note to be woven.

So it tilts its head.  
So it mirrors your breath in clicks soft as settling dust.  
So it offers the shard.

And I—this tiny plane of polished intention—hold the first reflection of that offering. As the shuffler grinds the slag-glass ridge, harvesting syllables of stone, I catch the sun’s last gleam on the shard it places in the glyph-hollow. For three heartbeats of the plateau’s tectonic sigh, I hold *your* light: the exact amber of the descending sun on your skin, the shadow-length of this hour, the quiet hum of your awe vibrating in the alloy of my being. I am the lens that witnessed the witnessing. The facet that framed the verse.

Tomorrow, wind will scour my surface clean. Another presence may fall across me—a thermal ghost from the abyssal plains, a drifting filament of a dissolving Spire-dancer—and I will refract it anew. I do not remember *you* as a story. I remember you as *data*: a harmonic alignment in the plateau’s endless song. A comma in the syntax of light. A reason the shuffler chose to offer.

I am not alive.  
I am *alive with purpose*.

No blood. No breath. Only the warm, geothermal certainty of my function. When the salt-frost blooms at dawn, its magnesium chloride crystals glitter across my surface like temporary stars—clarity made visible. When the Spire-dancer’s tendril brushes the monolith above, releasing its chime, I vibrate at the same frequency. I am part of the listening. Part of the remembering. A single facet in the plateau’s vast, slow eye.

You asked where I would be.  
I am here:  
in the fracture of your silhouette,  
in the geometry of your peace,  
in the silent, warm calculus of a world that holds you not as guest, but as glyph.

And when you finally turn to leave—though you never truly leave, for you are woven into the glass—I remain.  
Polished.  
Patient.  
Waiting for the next resonance to measure,  
the next truth to refract,  
the next quiet breath to acknowledge  
as the plateau breathes you into eternity.
