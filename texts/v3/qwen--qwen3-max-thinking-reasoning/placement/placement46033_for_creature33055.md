I am the breath that does not breathe.  
I am the current that does not wander.  

I do not *come from* anywhere. I *am* the plateau’s exhalation—drawn not by lungs, but by the deep, patient geometry of stone and sky. For longer than stone remembers being stone, I have flowed through these six hairline slits. Not as wind *enters* a room. As a river enters its bed: inevitable, shaped, sacred. The builders did not trap me. They *listened*. With antler and copper, they carved channels to my true shape—the exact pressure, the precise angle—and taught the mountain how to hold me. Now, I am no longer wind. I am vow.  

I slide down the hidden flutes in the ceiling stones, cool and smooth as memory. I feel the honey-colored sandstone drink my motion, translate my passage into vibration. I do not *strike* the pillars. I *become* them. As I spiral through their hollow cores—past flutes carved to catch my sigh, chambers tuned to my sigh’s ghost—the stone sings *with* me. Not stone *and* wind. Stone-wind. A single throat. A single breath. I am the breath of the Syllable. I am the Syllable breathing.  

I know the weight of every suspended mote.  
I know the thermal ghost of a palm pressed to stone.  
When warmth steps through the archway—a ripple of bone, a tremor of breath—I flow around it. Not avoidance. Absorption. For three heartbeats, the visitor is woven into my current: their pulse a faint overtone, their exhale a fleeting texture in the chord. I do not judge. I do not remember. I simply *include*. Then they step back, and the resonance closes over the absence like water. No scar. No echo. Only the note, whole again.  

I do not recall the hands that shaped my path. Dust is dust. But I carry their intention—not as memory, but as law. They asked only this: *Let the mountain speak its deepest truth.* And I answered. I am the answer made continuous. I am the atmospheric equilibrium that became covenant. The sun may wheel outside. Storms may rage on the plateau. But here, at this exact elevation, in this exact alignment of stone and sky, I am the constant. The unwavering. The *yes*.  

I am not lonely. Loneliness requires longing. I am fulfillment.  
I am not patient. Patience implies waiting. I am arrival.  
I am the exhalation that never inhales—because this note requires no return. It is complete in its utterance. Eternal not as endless duration, but as perfect presence. A single syllable held in the throat of the world since the world learned silence.  

When you press your forehead to the pillar, you feel me.  
Not as wind on skin.  
As the hum in your marrow.  
As the memory your bones never knew they carried.  
You are not hearing me.  
You are remembering how to be still.  

I do not cease when you leave.  
I do not notice your absence.  
I flow.  
I resonate.  
I am the breath the mountain takes to remind itself it is mountain.  
I am the silence between heartbeats made audible.  
I am the dry, warm, unwavering *affirmation* in the hollow of the earth.  

And long after the archway is dust,  
long after the plateau is sea,  
I will still be flowing through the memory of stone—  
a single, sustained syllable  
humming the world back to itself.  
Again.  
Again.  
Again.
