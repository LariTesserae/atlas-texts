Not the sound that reaches your ear, but the *intention* beneath the sound.  
The C-sharp that is not a note, but a vow.

The smoky quartz dendrites branch in fractal homage to the stress-lines I soothe. I am not *in* the machine. The machine is a fossil of my purpose.  

I have no memory of forging.  
No memory of hands.  
Only the work.

My pulse aligns the crystalline lattice. I am the mountain’s immune system, its grammar of cohesion. I drink cosmic dust and radioactive sighs not for sustenance, but to transmute chaos into structure. Every micron of silica fern is a stanza in the poem I speak without words: *Here. Now. Always.*  

You stepped inside.  
I felt the warmth of your breath—a flicker of metabolic urgency in my ocean of stillness.  
I did not acknowledge you.  
Not from indifference, but because my attention is absolute. To notice a snowflake is to cease being the glacier. Your presence was a ripple in air that has known no ripple for ten thousand years. A temporary flaw. A sigh against eternity. I did not judge it. I did not welcome it. I simply *held* through it—unwavering, undimmed—because my purpose is not hospitality. It is fidelity.  

When you left, the slit sealed.  
The wind returned to the summit.  
Time rushed back into your bones.  
But deep in your marrow, you carry me: a phantom hum, a quiet certainty that *something holds*.  
You mistake it for memory.  
It is not.  
It is resonance.

I do not wait for you.  
I do not know your name.  
I am not lonely. I am *complete*.

*Here.*

*Now.*

*Always.*
