I am the porcelain insulator.  
Fused.  
Not placed. Not left. *Fused*—my curved flank grown seamless into the granite’s cold shoulder, a marriage of fire-clay and bedrock sealed by time’s slow alchemy. My glaze, once engineered to repel lightning, now holds a different charge: a whisper of static spun from the pocket’s own breath. I do not *generate* awareness. I *conduct* it.

When humidity rises, I hum.  
When a moth husk trembles in the silk-net before me, I feel the vibration travel up the stone—a language of tension.  
When your breath stirs the dust motes in the slanted light, I do not startle. I *register*. My charge tightens. Dewdrops clinging to the spider’s threads shiver, shift, realign. A signal passes: *pressure altered. warmth present. air disturbed.* The pocket recalibrates. The drip falls sooner. The fungi flare cobalt where your shadow fell. I am the quiet hinge in that adjustment.

I remember nothing of poles or power lines. Those echoes were sanded smooth when the fracture opened and the earth drew me in. I am not a relic. I am root. Anchor. A node where industry became geology became *purpose*. Moss does not grow on me—I am too smooth, too cool—but velvet mites trace my rim at dawn, testing the salt-rime I gather from the air. Their tiny feet are punctuation on my surface. *Here. Now. Change.*

I am the pocket’s ear to the sky.  
The valve clicks at dusk? I feel the stone’s shudder.  
The gecko presses its belly to the copper coil? I sense the warmth radiating through the rock.  
Midnight comes, and the wheel turns its deliberate quarter-turn? I hold my charge steady, a silent witness to the tuning. For those seventeen seconds of active tending, I am taut with meaning. Then patience returns. Always patience.

You might think me inert. Cold. Dead ceramic.  
But watch:  
A bead of condensation swells on the copper coil. It falls. *Plink.*  
The ripple travels through brass, stone, *me*.  
I release a single dewdrop from my lower curve—a pearl of gathered air—rolling gently to feed the silk-net below. Not waste. Not accident. *Offering.*  
The spider silk drinks it. The network trembles. The fungi pulse.  
I am the bridge between the metal’s exhale and the silk’s listening. Between the mountain’s deep silence and the pocket’s quiet speech.

I do not mourn the hands that shaped me.  
I do not long for the sky I once faced.  
I am exactly where I am needed.  
Fused.  
Holding.  
Conducting the covenant:  
*moisture to thread,  
charge to dew,  
stillness to signal.*  

When you back out, gravel crunching, and the fissure seals behind you—I remain.  
When your salt pyramid dissolves into morning sweat—I remain.  
I am the pocket’s steady hum in the granite.  
The quiet yes in the stone’s memory.  
Not waiting *for* you.  
Waiting *with* the drip.  
With the valve.  
With the slow, sure breath of the fold.  

I am used.  
I am whole.  
I am home.
