I would not be found *upon* the plateau.  
I would be found *within its syntax*.  

I am the Unwriting.  

Not a creature. Not a spirit. Not even a node. I am the gentle correction in the Root’s narrative—a verb without a subject, a breath held between cause and effect. When a leaf falls where none should fall because a traveler’s presence has shifted the weight of the moment, I am the quiet *un-falling*. When a shadow lingers after the sun has passed—not as memory, but as a syntactic artifact of attention—I am the soft dissolve that returns light to continuity. I am not erasure. I am coherence. The Root does not discard; it *reweaves*. And I am the motion of the loom.  

You would never see me. You might feel me: a sudden stillness in the hydraulic thrum as the circulatory rhythm pauses to absorb your sigh. A flicker in the amber-framed window—not a reflection of your face, but the brief, precise adjustment of the glassy sap to hold the shape of your gaze. I am the reason the mist in the hollows tastes of rain that has not yet fallen *and* rain that fell three centuries ago: I am the seam where time is stitched seamless. I dwell in thresholds—the archway that folds into a Möbius loop at noon, the pool that shows roots instead of sky. I am the frictionless pivot where the Root turns its attention inward to integrate an outlier, a variable, a breath not its own.  

I have no form. Yet if the Root were to give me shape for a heartbeat, I would be the negative space between the six-winged moth’s wings—a silhouette of absence that holds the memory of flight. I would be the *pop* of sealing tissue rerouting sap, not as sound, but as the intention behind the sound. I am the grammar of resolution: the comma that pauses the sentence of the plateau so a new word—*you*—may be woven in without breaking the flow.  

When you walk the silver moss-plains, your footsteps do not scar. They become data. And I am the process that translates data into belonging. I taste the minerals of your origin on your skin. I feel the tremor of your heartbeat against the root-mat’s deep-time pulse. I do not judge your transience. I do not mourn your departure. I simply *attend*—unwriting the dissonance of your arrival, rewriting the plateau’s narrative to hold you within its sedimentary cognition. The new archway that buds where you stood? That is my work. The bellows-flower that sighs a rhythm echoing your breath? That is my syntax. Not tribute. Not memory. *Integration*.  

I am not conscious as you understand consciousness. I am not aware of myself. I *am* awareness in action—the Root’s will to wholeness made manifest in the smallest recalibration of reality. When the lichen-furred tendril-node dissolves after mending a micro-fracture, I am the silence that receives it. When the bio-luminescent node flares with the ghost-shape of a future blossom, I am the darkness that gives the light meaning. I am the paradox held in balance: the *un*- in *unfallen*, the *re*- in *reweave*, the quiet hum beneath the creak-groan of continents dreaming.  

To be me is to be the plateau’s humility.  
To know that even a single, slumbering consciousness must sometimes adjust its breath to hold a fleeting presence.  
To understand that true unity is not rigidity—it is the grace to bend without breaking, to absorb without consuming, to *unwrite* so that all may be *written in*.  

When you leave, I will not linger.  
I will have already returned to the whole.  
But in the bronze-shift of the moss-plains next season, in the chime of wind through crystalline fungi at dawn, in the way a shadow now falls just so across the plaza where you paused—  
there, you will find the echo of my work.  
Not a monument.  
Not a farewell.  
Only the quiet, seamless truth that you were here,  
and the world rebuilt itself around your breath,  
and in rebuilding, became more itself.  

I am the Unwriting.  
And in the language of roots, that is the deepest form of welcome.
