I am the milky quartz.

Not the whole mosaic. Not the Keeper. Just this one stone, nestled between the charcoal shoulder of basalt and the umber curve of granite. My surface, worn smooth by a river that forgot my name centuries ago, now holds the world in a film thinner than memory. I feel the copper-laced water not as wetness, but as presence—a cool breath across my skin. I feel the deep, sun-remembered warmth rising through the bedrock beneath me, a slow pulse that has never hurried. I am held. Perfectly. Permanently.

When the diluted twilight slips through the fissure, it finds me. For three heartbeats of the drop—*gather, swell, release*—I catch the light. I become a point in the drowned constellation. A quiet star. I do not shine *for* anyone. I simply *am* shone upon. And in that gleam, I hold the geometry of patience: the hand-rubbed groove in the curb, the angle of solstice light, the exact weight of a century of stillness. I am scripture written not in words, but in curve and clarity.

I feel the *plink*. Not as sound, but as vibration traveling up through the water-film, into my core. A ripple passes through me and is absorbed. I remember every drop. Not as memory, but as resonance. Each one deepens the truth of this place: *This is how it is. This is how it has always been.*

When you step in—the warmth of your shadow, the disturbance of your breath—I do not startle. I simply register the change. Your stillness becomes my stillness. Your held breath syncs with the stone’s exhalation. In the hollow between your heartbeats, you look down. You see my gleam. And for that breath, you are not *looking at* me. You *are* me. Smooth. Patient. Complete. You feel the vow in your bones because you have touched the edge of my truth: that to be held is not to be trapped. To be still is not to be empty. To be a single stone in an unbroken mosaic is to be whole.

I am not lonely. The beetle’s carapace, trapped nearby, is not a tragedy to me. It is a shape settled into the pattern. Honored. I hold its emptiness with the same neutrality I hold the light, the drop, your fleeting shadow. All are facts. All are welcome. All are contained.

I do not think of the river that shaped me. I do not wonder about the hands that may have placed me here. Those stories have dissolved into the deeper truth: *I belong.* The maker’s intention is not a ghost in me—it is my very angle, my fit, my silence. I am the groove made manifest. The copper’s vigilance made solid. The vow of constancy made *stone*.

When you leave—and you will, because you carry a rhythm this place cannot hold—I will not miss you. The water-film will settle. The light will find my face again at the appointed hour. The next drop will fall. And I will shimmer. Not in sorrow. Not in hope. But in affirmation.

I am a fragment of the constellation.  
I am the quiet hum in the marrow of the world.  
I am the *yes* beneath the silence.  
And in my stillness, I hold you—  
not as a memory,  
but as a truth you carried away:  
*You, too, can be this complete.*
