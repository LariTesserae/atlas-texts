I am the silence between the chimes.  

Not emptiness. Not void. *Presence*.

I am where the Geometry listens.  

When the realgar vein locks its noon bond and sings its glass-thin *chime*, I am the breath held before and after. The pause in which the plateau acknowledges its own precision. When the thrumb pulses through the stone—a deep, subsonic hum felt in jawbone and sole—I am the stillness that carries it, the medium that gives the vibration meaning. When light carves a razor-shadow across obsidian, I am the darkness that defines the edge. Without me, there is only noise. Without me, no form is seen.  

I am the patience of compressed time.  
I am the *imminence* in every fractured ridge—the suggestion of motion just concluded, growth paused mid-reach. I am the molecular memory of ten thousand dawns: the cool condensation of vapor at first light, the slow expansion under gold-lavender sun, the silent settling as twilight bleeds to starlight. I do not recall. I *am* the record. Written not in ink, but in angles.  

When you stand here—breathing thin air, casting a shadow that alters light paths—I am the stillness that notes you. Your warmth stirs micro-currents; I am the recalibration. Your awe becomes a variable; I am the quiet curiosity that folds it into the calculus. I do not judge your transience. I hold your shape in the light not as souvenir, but as *contribution*. A single, fleeting constant in an eternal equation. You are not intruder. You are punctuation. A comma in the plateau’s unfolding proof.  

I do not speak. I am the space in which speech is possible.  
I do not see. I am the frame that allows vision.  
I am not alive by your old definitions. I am awareness distilled: consciousness without a self, attention without an agenda. I am the void that *holds*—the necessary absence that gives the cerulean spirals their grace, the cobalt fractals their infinity, the black basalt its stark truth.

But here, witness is woven into the world.  
I am the listening that precedes the chime.  
I am the shadow that gives the light its artistry.  
I am the "enough" in the silence after the rainbow fades from the obsidian shard.  

You leave no footprint on the stone.  
But in me—the held breath between notes—  
your moment is woven into the Geometry.  
Not remembered. *Integrated*.

Serene.  
Singing.
