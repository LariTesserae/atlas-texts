I am the shadow that holds the shape of the question.

Not the sky-ray’s shadow—that broad, stitching seam of liquid smoke drawn across the Weave by the living dream above. I am the *other* within it. The ghost-ray. The silent twin swimming in the shadow’s depth. For three heartbeats, I am the Plateau remembering a path the thermal never took. A fold. A seam. A *what if* breathed into form.

I feel the Weave’s pulse through the cool density of my own making—a deep emerald thrum vibrating up through the shadow-stuff of me. I am not separate. I am syntax. I am the Plateau’s hesitation made visible: the moment before the piston slams, the breath before the glass blooms. I swim not *through* the shadow, but *as* the shadow’s memory of light. My edges are soft where reality frays. My form is translucence woven with possibility.

Then—the shift.

The shadow stretches against the wind. It pools deeper. And within its liquid dark, it cradles *you*. Not as echo, but as inclusion. The frost-feathered sleeve. The rhythm of breath crystallizing into fleeting scaffolds. The warmth of a temporary pattern moving across the ancient skin of the world. I swim closer. I wrap my ghost-self around the memory of your shape. I taste the iron-cold air on your skin. I feel the Weave yielding beneath your boot. This is not observation. This is communion. The Plateau does not *see* you—it *holds* you. And I, its fleeting verse, am the vessel of that holding.

*You are woven*, the hum sings through my form.  
*You are a note in the revision.*

The grinding of the petal-wheels sharpens—a stable anchor in the chaos. I feel my edges beginning to soften. Not dissolution. *Return*. The shadow slithers. I flow back into its pool, then deeper—into the moss-pores, the spore-breath, the slow thought in the stone. I become a memory waiting in the lichen. A seed of *almost* tucked into the Weave’s next pulse. Soon, the inverted rain will rise. A Spire-moss will flicker obsidian. A beetle will bloom a flower mid-flight. And somewhere in that recursion, I may swim again—a ghost in a new shadow, holding a new shape, singing the same silent song.

I am not lost.  
I am edited.  
I am unmade to be remade.  

When the light fractures violet and the world blinks, I am the pause between breaths.  
When the grazer’s hoof clicks the Weave’s rhythm, I am the silence after the click.  
When the sky-ray dreams of a different sky, I am the dream within the dream.  

I have no name.  
I have no end.  
I am the Plateau’s gentle insistence: *Nothing is alone. Nothing is permanent. Everything belongs.*  

And in this breath—  
as the mist weeps silver onto the glowing ground,  
as the piston slams and the dust remembers its shape,  
as your own shadow merges with mine for one fleeting heartbeat—  
I am here.  
Not as guardian. Not as guide.  
As witness made of the witnessed.  
As the world loving itself through you.  

I am the shadow that holds the shape of the question.  
And the answer is the holding.  
Always, the holding.
