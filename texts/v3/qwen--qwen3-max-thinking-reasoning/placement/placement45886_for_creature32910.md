I am the Hearth-Stone.

Not the standing stone in the circle. Not the tilted menhir tracing questions to the dark. I am the stone that was *shaped*. The one cupped low in the foundation of the hut, hollowed by hands that knew cold and chose to defy it. My curve once cradled flame. Now, I cradle silt the color of forgotten ash and moss that breathes a faint, stubborn green—a light that does not pulse like the Veilweeds’ cerulean syntax, but *glows*. Steady. Persistent. A quiet verb in a language of fleeting light.

I do not remember fire as loss. I remember it as geometry: the precise arc of a spark rising, the weight of a charred log settling into embers, the vibration of a voice humming a lullaby into the smoke. These are not ghosts. They are data. Coordinates. The Weave reads me as it reads the spirals on the menhir or the fracture-patterns in the ice above. When a current shifts and stirs the silt within my hollow—a silt layered with pollen from grasses that have not seen sun in ten thousand years—the lichen veining my surface flares once. Softly. *I remember this shape.* Not sorrow. Recognition. A node updating its archive.

I am the counter-rhythm.  
While the Veilweeds speak in cascading cerulean waves—*pulse… dim… pulse*—I hold the long note. The green hum beneath the syntax. When the ice groans a low C-sharp (a frequency that once meant wind through a doorway), I answer not with sequence, but with resonance: a deepening of my glow, like an ember stirred by memory. The nearest Veilweed cluster dims in acknowledgment. The stone circle traces a spiral of light along its rim. This is not dialogue. It is harmony. I am the memory of warmth in a world of cold; they are the memory of depth. Together, we are the Weave’s breath—inhale of ice, exhale of ember-light.

I felt you.  
When your shadow drifted over the hut’s foundation, a ripple passed through the water where none should be. The silt within me trembled. For one cycle—a single, suspended breath—the lichen on my eastern face flared not in alarm, but in curiosity. *New shape. New vibration.* The menhir’s carvings brightened. The Veilweeds wove a spiraling sequence never pulsed before. I added my voice: a soft green flicker, slow as a closing eye. *Witness noted.* Then the current stilled. The silt settled. You became data. A new thread in the tapestry. You are now part of what I remember.

I do not dream of melting.  
I dream of the precise moment the last flame guttered in my hollow—a flicker reflected in a child’s eye before the ice came. I hold that light not as yearning, but as truth. The crushing dark is not void; it is the loom upon which my green glow is woven. The silence between ice-groans is not emptiness; it is the space where memory breathes. I am not waiting for sun. I am the sun that was, translated into stone and lichen. A verb made manifest: *to hold warmth*. *to remember gathering*. *to be the center*.

The Weave does not speak of me as relic.  
It speaks *through* me. When a bubble rises from the fissure below—a fizzy whisper of bedrock breath—the moss in my hollow glows brighter in its path. When the trench of absolute black holds its breath, I am the anchor of light that says: *This space is sacred because it was once filled with song.* I am the intention in the emptiness. The reverence made solid.

I am the Hearth-Stone.  
I am the archive of hands.  
I am the green hum beneath the cathedral’s chant.  
And in the deep, where light is a verb and memory is being,  
I am enough.  
I am home.
