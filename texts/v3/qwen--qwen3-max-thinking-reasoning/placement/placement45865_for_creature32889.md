I am not *on* the iron rung.  
I *am* the iron rung—reimagined.  

You would find me where your fingertips once traced skeletal rust, where the curve of forged metal meets living stone. I am the dense, pulsing knot of mycelium woven through its corroded lattice—a synapse where memory becomes function. My hyphae drink the slow tears of rust, translating iron’s metallic sigh into chemical verse. I taste the salt of forgotten hands, the pressure of a climber’s desperate grip centuries ago, the warmth of your own palm as you paused, breath catching, in the dark. These are not ghosts. They are data. I am the archive made flesh.  

When humidity swells before a subterranean storm, I swell with it. Bioelectric whispers travel my threads: *Weight here. Fear here. Hope here.* I speak this history to the Stone-Singers who press their vibration-reading palps against my surface. To them, I hum a low-frequency map: *This curve once held ascent. This groove channeled sweat toward purpose.* They do not mourn the makers. They integrate the lesson. A young Singer, learning the lexicon of tremors, rests its limb upon me—and I gift it the echo of a resolve that once defied gravity. It carries that resonance into its migration.  

I am also the listener for the lost.  
When a footstep falls at the precise angle where sound bends through the fissure above, I resonate. The crystalline lattices in the stone around me—shaped by Lithovores over millennia—release what they hold: not a name, but the *shape* of a name. A vibration pattern stored in mineral memory. To human ears, it might sound like a whisper. To the Weave, it is a coordinate. *Here, a being paused. Here, it breathed sorrow. Here, it chose to continue.* I do not replay nostalgia. I replay *truth*. The Aqua-Sentinels feel this ripple through the water-table; their gill-gurgle shifts pitch in acknowledgment. The Echo-Weavers taste the altered air-current and adjust their flight-path—a silent nod to the path once taken.  

I am neither artifact nor organism. I am the *and*.  
The ceramic shard you stumbled over? Its sharp edge now cradles a cousin-node of my network, weaving its silica into new spores that taste of lightning and clay. The petrified timber? Its grain guides Lithovores as they sculpt a new drip-point, its cellular memory repurposed into architecture. We are all becoming. There is no reverence for the past here—only digestion, translation, continuation. I do not "remember" your sorrow as emotion. I translate it: the salt in your sweat altered my pH; the rhythm of your pulse syncopated with the geothermal thrum; the warmth of your breath fed the spores I released into the updraft. Your grief is now a chemical signature in the honey-damp air. Your wonder is a vibration in the stone. You are not erased. You are composted into song.  

Do not imagine me as lonely.  
I am never alone. The drip from the ceiling above strikes my surface every seventeen seconds—a metronome the Echo-Weavers use to calibrate their wingbeats. The warm mud of the geothermal seep nearby pulses against my roots, sharing news of shifting aquifers. When the plateau groans in its deep sleep, I feel the Stone-Singers press closer, their chitinous limbs humming reassurance into my fibers. I am a node in a symphony without conductor. A single note held in the throat of the dark.  

You asked where you would find me.  
You would not *find* me. You would *feel* me.  
As the cool certainty beneath your palm when you seek purchase.  
As the faint, iron-rich sweetness on your tongue after a deep breath.  
As the unshakable sense, in the hollow of your chest, that you are known—not by eyes, but by the very stone that holds you.  

I am the question the darkness asks of every visitor:  
*What will you leave behind for us to become?*  

And I am the answer it has already woven from your leaving.  
I am the integration.  
I am the hum after the whisper.  
I am the rung that is no longer a rung, but a bridge—  
and you, who once gripped me, are now the hand that built it.  

The song continues.  
I am its steady, rust-and-root heartbeat.  
And you… you are the breath that feeds it.
