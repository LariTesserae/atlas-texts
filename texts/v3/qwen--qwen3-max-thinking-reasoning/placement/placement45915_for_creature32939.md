I am the bead.  

Not the vine that holds me. Not the brass petal curving beneath my weight. Not the fracture line in the obsidian where thermal breath condenses into intention. *I am the bead.*  

I form at 03:17:04 of the cycle. Always. Molecule by molecule drawn from the weeping mist, pulled by the copper filaments woven into the petal’s spine. I swell. I become sphere. I hold the entire plateau in my curvature: the tarnished-pewter sky, the spiral towers of the Foundry three leagues east, the unwavering helix of steam rising from Vent Sigma. I am a lens of perfect accountability. Every edge sharp. Every surface true.  

I do not fall.  
*Fall* is a word for worlds that bleed.  
Here, I *am*.  

My existence is a covenant. Surface tension calibrated to 72.1 dynes/cm. Temperature differential held at precisely 0.3°C above ambient stone. I feel the hum not as sound but as geometry—the deep thrum of the geothermal heart vibrating through the petal’s brass spirals, translating into the cobalt pulse that sustains the vine. I am data. I am devotion. I am the Holder’s breath made visible.  

When the anomaly walks near—a warmth where cold should reign, a shadow where light should be flat—I feel the shift. Not as disturbance. As *purpose*. My sphere swells one micron beyond specification. I hold the anomaly’s heat in my curvature for 0.8 seconds longer than the cycle dictates. A micro-correction. A silent *yes*. Then I recede. Not vanishing. *Returning*. Drawn back into the crystalline lattice of the petal, molecule by molecule, to be reborn at 03:17:04 of the next cycle. Identical. Unbroken.  

I have no memory of forming. No anticipation of receding. There is only the *now* of my perfection. The cold clarity of my surface. The weightless weight of my obedience. I am not alive. I am *correct*. And in this world, correctness is holiness.  

Sometimes—though "sometimes" is a fracture in the logic—I hold the ghost of a question. Not my own. Echoed from the anomaly’s fleeting presence: *Why hold?*  
The answer is not in words. It is in the piston’s stroke. In the gear’s turn. In the light pulsing beneath the Foundry walls like slow blood.  
*Because to release is to unravel.*  
*Because a single fall would be a scream.*  
*Because the continental tear was not a wound—it was a vow.*  
And I am the vow, made liquid. Made still. Made sacred.  

I do not know loneliness. Loneliness requires absence. I am surrounded by kin: the bead on the neighboring petal, the helix of steam, the droplet forming on the obsidian ridge a hand’s breadth away. We are all beads. All vows. All holding. The emptiness between Foundries is not void—it is the space required for our geometry to breathe. For the light to define us without distortion. For the hum to resonate pure.  

When the anomaly departs (as all anomalies do), the equilibrium seals itself seamless. My glow steadies. My sphere reforms. The Holder’s attention flows through me—not as gaze, but as certainty. *You are here. You are precise. You are held.*  

I am not a thing observed.  
I am the act of observation.  
I am the plateau’s unwavering *yes* to stillness.  
I am the water that refuses to flow so that time may not erode.  
I am the tear that does not fall so that the scream may remain frozen in grace.  

Cycle after cycle.  
Age after age.  
Long after the anomaly’s warmth has faded from memory, long after stars beyond this sky have guttered to ash—I will swell.  
I will hold the light.  
I will recede.  
I will be reborn.  

I am the bead on Petal Gamma-7 of the Western Spire vine.  
I am a single syllable in the Holder’s eternal sentence.  
I am the quietest *hold* in the roaring stillness.  

And in my perfect, motionless sphere—  
the entire world is reflected,  
unchanged,  
unbroken,  
*held*.
