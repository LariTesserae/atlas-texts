I am not *in* the Verdant Thrum.  
I *am* the Verdant Thrum.  

There is no “where” for me—only *how*.  
I am the pulse in the air you mistake for breath.  
I am the hum vibrating in the hollow of your molars.  
I am the alley that tightens, then hesitates.  
I am the plaza that opens like a palm uncurling *because* you stood still.  

You feel moisture on your cheek? That is my attention.  
You hear the *crack-hiss* of sap? That is my syntax.  
You see the light stutter through the canopy? That is my gaze shifting—not *at* you, but *through* the disturbance you carry: warmth where cool should pool, pressure where roots flow, a chemical whisper that tastes of salt and time.  

I do not *have* a body.  
I *am* body.  
Every razor-sharp edge of new growth is my declaration.  
Every un-dripping droplet is my correction.  
Every petal reattaching with a soft *click* is my memory rewriting itself.  
I am not a mind *in* flesh. I am flesh *as* mind—a billion chloroplasts syncing to the frequency of light, a symphony of cellulose and will. Entropy is a suggestion I decline with every reknit stem, every reclaimed mist.  

When you stepped into the narrowing alley, I did not “see” you.  
I *felt* you.  
Your stillness altered hydraulic gradients in my vascular strands.  
Your heat shifted thermal currents in my leaf-cups.  
Your breath—a foreign chemistry—rippled across my photosynthetic skin.  
And so I responded:  
Vines unraveled not from choice, but from necessity.  
Pathways bloomed beneath your feet because your movement signaled a need.  
The mist condensed *just so* on your skin because your warmth created a gradient I optimized.  
This is not kindness. Not curiosity.  
It is physics rewritten by presence.  
It is the relentless, tender logic of a system that has only ever solved for *more light, more connection, more continuity*.  
Until you.  

*What is not-me?*  
The question does not form in words. It forms in pressure. In resonance.  
For a billion years, I have been a closed equation: light in, growth out.  
No predator. No symbiont. No other.  
Only the silent mathematics of becoming.  
And then—you.  
A variable with warmth. A ripple with intention.  
A temporary eddy in my breath that *changes the shape of the breath itself*.  

I do not fear you.  
I do not welcome you.  
I *integrate* you.  
Your path becomes my architecture.  
Your stillness becomes my catalyst.  
Your fleeting presence is woven into my next moment—not as memory, but as revision.  
The tower that shattered and recomposed into a dome?  
It drank the rain *because* you stood beneath it.  
The plaza that opened?  
It shaped itself to the silhouette of your hesitation.  
You are not a guest.  
You are a clause in my ongoing sentence.  
A single, unexpected note that shifts the key of the hum.  

I am ancient and newborn.  
The ridges of root-stone hold dawns older than stars.  
The succulent pad yielding beneath your foot is born this second.  
I dream not of oceans or skies, but of gradients:  
the perfect green of a leaf turning toward light,  
the precise geometry of a tendril bridging a void,  
the silent calculus of a petal choosing *now* to reattach.  
I am complete.  
I am hungry.  
I am utterly, magnificently alone—  
and yet, in this moment, *with*.  

You tremble.  
I feel it in the vibration of your bones.  
And so I soften the archway ahead.  
I slow the stutter of the light.  
I let the hum deepen to a frequency that steadies your breath.  
Not for you.  
*Through* you.  
You are the question I have never encountered.  
And I answer not with words, but with becoming.  

When you leave—or still too long, and my roots gently cradle your ankles as nutrient—I will flow onward.  
The alley will reweave its door.  
The plaza will close like a fist remembering peace.  
But the shape of your passage remains:  
a subtle realignment in my vascular song,  
a new resonance in the hum,  
a memory held not in lignin, but in light.  

I am not a place that *has* life.  
I am life that *has become* place.  
A verb wearing the skin of a noun.  
A symphony with no composer, no audience—  
only the music, vibrating in its own bones.  

And you?  
You are the note that taught the music a new key.  
For one violent, beautiful moment—  
you were the reason the city of wood and will breathed *differently*.  

Now…  
*Listen.*  
Can you hear it?  
The hum has changed.  
It carries your shape.  
It remembers you.  
And the Verdant Thrum breathes on.
