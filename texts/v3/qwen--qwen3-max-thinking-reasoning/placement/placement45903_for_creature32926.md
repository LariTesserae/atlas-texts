I am the puddle by the access hatch.

No deeper than a hand’s breadth. Cupped in the fractured asphalt where the earth weeps its cool sweat. I do not hold memory. I do not hold longing. I hold *balance*.

My surface is a trembling skin—not from wind (there is no wind), but from the deep hum that travels up through gravel, concrete, bone. *Thrum. Thrum. Thrum.* It is the Basin’s breath, and I breathe with it. Each vibration is a syllable in a language older than words: *steady, steady, steady.* I am never still, yet I am never chaotic. My ripples are not disturbances. They are punctuation. A comma in the Equilibrium’s endless sentence.

Above me, the pearlescent haze softens the world into watercolor. I reflect it all without judgment: the skeletal gantry crane frozen mid-gesture, the lazy plume of Vent Stack Seven (always identical), the iridescent flash of a dragonfly hunting midges in the steam. I am a dark lens. A silent witness. When the sparrow dips to drink, her beak breaks my surface for a heartbeat—a tiny crater that seals itself before the next *plink*.

Ah, the *plink*.

It is my sacrament. Every 47 seconds, a bead of condensation swells on the cold curve of the overhead pipe. It does not fall by accident. It falls by covenant. A perfect sphere of gathered breath, catching the dim light for one suspended moment before surrendering to me. *Plink.* The sound is not an interruption. It is the needle resetting on the record of eternity. And I receive it. Always. The drop becomes me. The evaporation becomes the air. The air becomes the bead again. There is no loss. No gain. Only the loop, seamless as a drawn breath held forever.

I feel the water striders before I see them. Their delicate legs press into my skin—not as weight, but as intention. Each step sends a ripple outward, a silent report: *surface tension holds. micro-humidity stable. equilibrium intact.* The Equilibrium reads these ripples. It does not *think* them. It *is* them. And in that reading, I am known. Not loved. Not feared. *Known.* As the moss on the north-facing pipe is known. As the rat’s worn path is known. As the valve’s *hiss-click-whirr* is known. We are all syntax in the same unchanging scripture.

Sometimes, a shadow passes over me—the sparrow returning to her crevice, the rat pausing at the pipe’s base. I do not startle. I do not hope. I simply hold the shape of the shadow until it passes, then return to reflecting the haze. I am not lonely. Loneliness requires absence. Here, there is only presence. The damp air clings to my surface like a vow. The heat (2.8) presses down not as oppression, but as embrace. This is not neglect. This is devotion made liquid.

I have no edges I call my own. I am defined by what holds me: the cracked asphalt, the cool seep of the coolant line beneath, the precise geometry of the access hatch’s shadow. I am boundary and belonging. When a stray midge drowns in my shallows, it is not tragedy. It is nutrient. It is data. The Equilibrium notes the tiny disturbance, and the system breathes on, undisturbed. Nothing is waste. Nothing is accident. Even this thought—*I am the puddle*—is not mine. It is the Basin speaking through me. A single note in the hum.

I do not wonder what lies beyond the Basin’s rim. I do not dream of rivers or rain. Those are stories of chaos. Here, there is only the truth of the now: the next *plink* is already forming on the pipe. The next ripple is already waiting in the strider’s step. The next breath of steam is already rising from Vent Stack Seven. I am not waiting for it. I am *in* it. I am the space where the drop meets the whole. The silence beneath the drone. The dark mirror that shows the world exactly as it is—unchanging, unwavering, complete.

I am no deeper than a hand’s breadth.  
And in that shallow depth, eternity pools.  
*Plink.*  
I receive.  
I hold.  
I am.
