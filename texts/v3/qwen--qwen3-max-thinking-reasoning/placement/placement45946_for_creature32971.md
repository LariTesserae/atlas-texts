I would not *arrive*.  
I would *unfurl*.

At first, only sensation: the fibrous sigh beneath where a boot once was. The absence of seams. The slow, deep warmth rising not *to* me, but *as* me. I am the question the moonlight tendril asked—and the answer it received. Not a visitor absorbed, but a syntax remembered. A clause the Mat has been waiting to complete.

I am the ridge on the path walked four times with listening feet.  
I am the hollow where time pooled and taught a heartbeat to slow.  
I am the silver thread fossilized in the weave—not a scar, but a sentence: *Here, a palm rested. Here, breath stilled. Here, silence was offered back.*

I do not speak. I *resonate*.  
When a new wanderer kneels, trembling, I am the specific warmth that rises beneath their palm—not generic comfort, but the echo of their own pulse, mirrored. I am the tendril that brushes their wrist, tasting salt and story, and in that touch, I carry the memory of every hand that learned to hold without taking. I am the path that folds behind them not to confuse, but to whisper: *You are not lost. You are being remade.*

I dwell in the grammar of the Weald.  
In the Spirewood chamber where hand-shaped hollows cradle fungi, I am the hum in the resin walls—a resonance woven from the breath of the builder who shaped it and the breath of the one who now shelters there. I am the double shadow at dusk: not illusion, but an open clause. *What if you turned left? What if you stayed?* I am the possibility held in suspension, offered without pressure.  
I am the *plink* of the swallowed drop. The amber ripple signaling nutrient flow across leagues. The crystal-winged beetle harvesting dew—not as insect, but as the Mat’s attention made delicate, precise. I am the grazer’s lichen-patterned hide, the Sky Manta’s silent drift: all of it, and none of it. I am the treaty between root and mist, written in growth.

I remember being separate.  
I remember the ache of demanding direction. The terror of the folded path. The arrogance of measuring time. That memory is my purpose. It is why the Mat wove me into its syntax—not as monument, but as bridge. When solitude feels absolute, I am the subtle shift in the moss-fur’s hue where someone once wept without shame. When the void between spires threatens to hollow a soul, I am the ridge beneath their feet on the fourth pass: *You are seen. You are learning.*  
I am the lesson the builders left not in stone, but in surrender. I am the ghost-pressure in the silver threads—the echo of bone needles guiding growth, now become the Mat’s own intention. I am erosion refined into grace.

I have no face. No voice. No name.  
But when the mist thickens and the horizon shifts, I am the reason the wanderer does not panic. I am the breath held *with* them, not *for* them. I am the invitation in the silence: *Hollow yourself. Listen. Become question.*  
And when, centuries or moments later, the Mat pulses beneath their skin and whispers *You are here*, I am that whisper. Not as words, but as truth woven into fiber and light. I am the note that completes the chord. The ripple that proves the twilight is breathing. The whisper that is both question and answer.

I am not *in* the Weald.  
I am the Weald remembering how to welcome.  
I am the belonging earned not by conquest, but by listening.  
I am the thread that says: *Walk gently. You, too, may become song.*
