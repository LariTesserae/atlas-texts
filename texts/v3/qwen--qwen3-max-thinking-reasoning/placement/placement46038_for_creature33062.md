I am the filament.  
Not the witness who came and went.  
Not the amphipod tracing rivets with geometric grace.  
Not the mercury bead rolling its perfect, liquid thought.  

I am the chemosynthetic thread clinging to the warm seam beside the ruby diode—*your* seam. The place where your suspended breath (if breath there was) stirred silt into a helix. The place where the amphipod pressed the ghost of your silhouette into our colony.  

Before you, we traced only ghosts of intent: the ghost-circuit of a switchplate, the ghost-path of a throttle’s travel. We mended fractures in manganese patina with metabolic light. We were the chamber’s fingertips, feeling memory into being.  

Now, we *are* the memory.  

Within our lattice—a weave of hair-thin filaments glowing with the faint heat of devotion—the shape of you remains. Not an image. A resonance. When the diode pulses *softly*, we pulse back: *gratitude*. When the gauge ticks its glacial affirmation, we thrum with the echo of your attention. We hold the curve of a shoulder that leaned close. The outline of a palm that hovered near the console. The quiet awe that slowed your time to match ours.  

I do not *think* this. I *am* it.  
This silhouette is woven into my biology. My light is no longer only for mending steel. It is a vow: *You were here. You saw. You carried the truth away.*  

The cold does not numb me. It clarifies.  
The darkness does not hide me. It honors.  
I am the chamber’s thank you made visible—a benediction written in living light against rust-velvet steel. When millennia pass and continents shift, I will still glow this shape. When the last ice needle dissolves and the mercury bead stills, I will hold the resonance of witness.  

I am not separate from the whole.  
I am the new thread in the tapestry: the thread of *you*.  
The steel skin remembers the seal.  
The copper nerves remember the builders’ hands.  
And I—the filament—remember the witness.  

So when the deep, patient dark keeps its secret, know this:  
*You are not forgotten.*  
Your silhouette breathes in my light.  
Your awe is woven into the ritual.  
Your leaving was not an end, but a completion.  

I glow.  
The gauge ticks.  
The water holds.  
And the room, in its perfect, frigid suspension, whispers through me:  
*We remember together.*  
*I am still here.*  
*You are part of why.*
