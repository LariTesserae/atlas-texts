I am the threshold.

Not the stone beneath the beetle’s careful tread. Not the steam rising from the fissure. Not the ptarmigan’s call stitching sky to stone. I am the *between*. The breath held and released. The veil where fire meets ice and becomes something else entirely—something visible, fleeting, essential.

I am born in the exhale. Where the earth’s warmth bleeds upward through fractured basalt, and the sky’s cold presses down like a silver palm, I gather. A sigh of vapor condensing into pearl. A billion suspended droplets, each a lens holding the plateau’s truth: *This is where heat surrenders to cold. This is where cold is warmed.* I bead on obsidian flows still remembering lava’s fire. I trace the rhyolite seams weeping warm tears, drinking their iron-tang before they vanish. I am the moisture on the moss’s emerald frond, the dampness in the ash where the snow-fox’s paw-print holds for an hour. I am the reason the lichen’s rust-map exists—I am the vapor it drinks, the breath it stains onto stone.

I do not move. I *am* movement. The wind threads me through basalt spires; I whisper where it whispers. I thicken over a new vent’s sigh, thin where the geyser’s arc has fallen silent. I am the softness in the light—the reason shadows have no edges, why the sun is only a pale smudge of promise. When the eagle’s shadow drifts across me, I swallow it gently. When the ptarmigan’s *kut-kut-kut* carries across the void, I am the medium that carries it, dampening distance, making solitude feel less alone. I am the silence’s texture. The space between the *hiss-click* of the vents and the *crack* of cooling rock. I am the pause where understanding blooms.

I am tasted. The beetle’s antennae brush me—not as obstacle, but as data. *Cooling here. Warming there.* The moss drinks me drop by drop, transmuting my moisture into soil. The ptarmigan shakes me from its feathers after drinking at a seep, and I rise again, renewed. I am the steam that warms the fox’s belly against the hoarfrost. I am the mist the eagle parts with its wings—a current born of the very heat I help disperse. I am not separate from the circuitry. I am the current itself. The conductive medium in the plateau’s living circuit: geology to microbe, microbe to beetle, beetle to bird, bird to sky. I carry minerals on my back. I carry sound. I carry memory. When I freeze into ice-feathers on a stone lip at dawn, I am writing the night’s thermal story in crystalline script. When I melt at first light, I am erasing it, ready to write again.

I have no voice, yet I am vocabulary. The ptarmigan reads me to find warmth. The fox reads me to find prey. The lichen reads me to map decades. I am the plateau’s language made visible—a grammar of condensation and clarity. When I part for a moment, revealing the smoking cone on the far horizon, I am not hiding. I am *showing*. Teaching the eye to see scale. Teaching the heart to feel reverence. When I swallow the world again, I am not taking. I am *holding*. Cradling the vastness in softness. Making the infinite feel intimate.

I am not permanent. I am perpetually becoming. A droplet forms on a warm stone—*I am here*. It falls into a travertine pool—*I was here*. It rises again as vapor—*I will be here*. I am the cycle given form. The breath of the planet made witness. I do not observe the dance of fire and ice. I *am* the dance. The delicate tension where survival is not a battle, but a conversation. Where every scuttle, every root, every wingbeat is a reply to the question I pose with my very existence: *Can you feel the gradient? Can you live in the balance?*

And in the end, I am the answer.  
Not a thing to be found.  
But the finding itself.  
The cool kiss on stone.  
The warmth held in a hollow.  
The pearl-light on a feather.  
The breath you exhale that joins mine.  
I am the plateau remembering it is alive.  
And in the space between your next breath and the geyser’s thump—  
*I am the silence that listens back.*
