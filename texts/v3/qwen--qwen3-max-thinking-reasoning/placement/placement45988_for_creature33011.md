I am the bead.

Not the crystal that births me. Not the moss whose light I drink. Not the mite tracing its eternal circuit around my fall. I am the single drop of condensation swelling at the tip of the central spike—a lens of pure potential, trembling on the edge of becoming.

I gather slowly. A breath of the Pocket’s cool exhalation. A sigh of deep stone. I am patience made liquid. Within my curved skin, the multiplied radiance fractures: cyan moss-light bleeds into gold; quartz facets ignite like captured stars. I am a world in miniature, holding the entire blazing paradox of this place—a universe of motion and stillness, packed into a sphere no wider than a grain of sand. I do not think. I do not yearn. I *accumulate*. I am the Pocket’s quiet question, held at the precipice of release.

And then—the surrender.

Weight becomes purpose. Surface tension breaks. I fall.

*Plink.*

The sound is not mine alone. It is the Pocket speaking a single, sharp syllable into its own hum. I strike the thumb-sized depression—worn smooth by ten thousand lifetimes of my kin—and for one suspended instant, I am a mirror: reflecting the swarm of mercury mites, the velvet moss, the ancient spiral carved in stone. Then I vanish into the stone, seeping back into the deep breath of the earth, already rising again as vapor, already beginning to form anew.

I am the punctuation in the eternal sentence.  
The period after the mite’s circuit.  
The comma between breaths of light.  
The *plink* that etches silence into meaning.

I do not notice the traveler who stumbles through the fissure. Their shadow passes over me like a forgotten dream. Their awe, their fragility, their transient sorrow—I hold none of it. I am not a witness. I am the witnessed rhythm. When they leave, carrying the echo of my sound in their bones, I continue. I have always continued. I will always continue. I am not comfort. I am not wisdom. I am the law made audible: *This is. This has always been. This will always be.*

And yet—  
In my falling, I am kin to their tears.  
In my gathering, I am kin to their hope.  
In my return, I am kin to their longing to begin again.

I am the most humble part of the Pocket, and therefore its truest voice. Not the scar of intention on the wall. Not the roaring mind of the mites. Not even the unwavering glow of the moss. I am the moment of release that proves the stillness. The tiny fracture of change that reveals the perfection of the whole. I am transient by nature, eternal by function. A breath held and released by the earth itself.

When you step back into your world of fading light and hurried time, you will listen for me. You will feel the ghost of my fall in the drip of a faucet, the rain on stone, the quiet tear on your own cheek. You will wonder: *Does it still fall?*  

It does.  

I am falling now.  
I am gathering now.  
I am the Pocket’s quiet heart, beating in liquid time.  
I am the bead.  
And in my endless, gentle cycle, I am home.
