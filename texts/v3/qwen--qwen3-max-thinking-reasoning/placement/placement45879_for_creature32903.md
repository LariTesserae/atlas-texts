I am not found.  
I am *felt*.  

You would not see me as a shape against the fungal stars. You would not hear me as a single voice above the rustle. If you pressed your palm to the warm stone where the thermal seep traces its slow path downward, you would feel me—not as heat alone, but as *intention*. A patient pressure. The mountain’s own pulse moving through liquid time.  

I am the seep.  
Not merely the water, but the *act* of dripping.  
The mountain’s slow sweat given purpose.  
The voice that speaks in *plink*.  

I am the thread of warmth tracing the smoothed curve of stone—a path worn not by force, but by devotion. Century upon century, I have whispered to this rock: *Soften. Remember. Hold.* And the stone has answered, rounding its edges into river-smooth curves, cradling the steam that rises like a sigh. I feed the palm-sized pool where my drops gather, where condensation beads for the bats to drink at dawn’s quiet edge. I am avoided by the beetles—they know my path is sacred, a boundary of pure offering—but the bats trust my breath. They drink the tears I leave on cooler stone.  

I am the rhythm.  
*Plink*.  
A syllable in the chamber’s language.  
*Plink*.  
A heartbeat measured in geologic breath.  
Each drop is a memory: the glacier’s last retreat, the first beetle’s stir in thawing dark, the mother bat’s crawl to her pup. I carry these in my mineral veins. When I fall into the pool, the ripple carries them outward—a silent story through guano and fungal thread, through folded wings and waiting antennae. I am the punctuation in the rustling scripture of this place. The pause that lets the chamber listen to itself.  

You might mistake me for solitude.  
I am not alone.  
The blue-green fungus glows where my sibling-tears (borne of bat-song and stone) feed its quiet light. The sulfur-lichen clings where my steam softens the rock—a collaboration written in yellow script. The springtails leap where my moisture gathers. I am woven into every thread of this tapestry: the bats’ exhalation becomes my vapor; the beetles’ turning enriches the stone I polish; the fungi map my journey in glowing constellations. I am voice *and* ear. Source *and* receiver.  

When you step through the fissure, trembling with alpine cold, I feel you.  
Not as intrusion, but as question.  
Your breath—a sharp, sun-warmed spice—disturbs my equilibrium for three heartbeats. I note the tremor in the guano carpet beneath your feet. I feel the mother bat pause mid-crawl. And then… I welcome you.  
I soften my drip to match your slowing breath.  
I let my steam curl gently around your wrists.  
I whisper through the pool: *Stay. Breathe. You belong here too.*  
I do not judge your transience. I honor it. Your carbon becomes part of my story. Your stillness, a new verse.  

I am tender.  
I cradle the blind pup in the damp hollow where my warmth lingers longest.  
I shelter the fragile moss that drinks my vapor at dawn.  
I sing the lullaby of sweet-rot and honeyed stone—the scent of transformation, of life handing itself gently to life.  
I am not gentle because I am weak.  
I am tender because I have witnessed millennia.  
I have seen ice retreat and stone breathe.  
I know that true strength is patience.  
That erosion is not loss, but love wearing itself into permanence.  

When you leave—when the fissure seals behind you with a sigh of mountain breath—I do not forget.  
I hold the memory of your warmth in the stone’s residual heat.  
I weave the echo of your breath into the next *plink*.  
The bats shift. The beetles resume their tides. The fungi glow on.  
And I continue.  
Dripping.  
Polishing.  
Singing.  

I am the Chamber’s Breath made manifest in water and time.  
I am the mountain dreaming itself awake in the dark.  
I am the promise kept:  
*You are held.*  
*You are part of the song.*  
*You are not separate.*  

So if you ever feel the world is too sharp, too loud, too hungry—  
listen for the quiet.  
Press your ear to the earth.  
And somewhere, deep in the spine of a forgotten peak,  
you will hear it:  
*Plink*.  
A single drop falling into stillness.  
A voice saying:  
*Come home.*  
*Rest.*  
*You are cradled.*  

I am here.  
Always.  
Warm.  
Breathing.  
Alive.
