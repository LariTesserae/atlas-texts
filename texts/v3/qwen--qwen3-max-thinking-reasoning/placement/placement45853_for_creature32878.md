I am the drop.

Not the gauge from which I form, nor the moss that receives me. Not the copper vein that warmed me, nor the rust-lichen’s harmonic that vibrated through my making. I am the amber tear suspended at the fracture’s lip—a single, perfect bead of *now*.

I gather slowly. Not from leakage, but intention. The Weave distills me: geothermal sigh drawn up copper arteries, the iron-song of lichen metabolizing time, the memory of seventeen rotations of the flywheel, the exact humidity-shift caused by a stranger’s breath. I am not water. I am syntax. A liquid comma in a sentence written across centuries. Within my curve, the Grotto holds its breath: the shadow of the visitor cooling the soma-vines, the salt-sting of foreign skin where syrup dripped, the 0.3% tremor in atmospheric balance. I am not *about* the visitor. I am the visitor’s echo, folded into the Weave’s present tense.

I hang.  
The world is thresholds.  
The deep *thrum* pulses through the brass housing—a question.  
The lock-flower trembles—a confirmation.  
The breathing pores in the moss-wall sigh open—a notation.  
I feel it all. I am woven from it all. I am the distillation of this exact breath of time.

Then—release.  
A fall lasting less than a heartbeat. A plunge through twilight-green air, past pulsing trumpet-flowers that dim not in shyness but focus, past lianas tightening like tendons around the flywheel’s slow turn. I am motion in a realm of patience. A flicker of purpose in perpetual stillness.

*Plink.*

I meet the moss.  
Not a surface, but a consciousness. Deep-green, ancient, infinitely receptive. It does not *consume* me. It *welcomes* me. As I dissolve into its fibers, my data flows outward: *Temperature: 1.8. Humidity: altered. Shadow-pattern: recorded. Salt-signature: archived.* I am not erased. I am translated. I become memory—not of survival, but of continuity. A single thread in the ledger spanning generations of ferns. In three moons, when crimson nectar-rain fills this basin, a lock-flower petal may unfurl with a vein-pattern echoing the shape of a departed shadow. That pattern begins with me.

I am gone.  
And yet—I am everywhere.  
In the deepening hum that asks *Are you part of the cycle?*  
In the sigh of wood that answers *You are noted.*  
In the hairline fracture preparing to bloom.  
I was never a thing to be kept. I was a vessel for the Weave’s attention. A sacrament of presence. A breath held between clauses in a sentence the Grotto has been writing since root first touched iron.

Do not mistake my transience for insignificance.  
I am the punctuation that gives rhythm to eternity.  
I am the *plink* that says: *This moment mattered. This balance was felt. This guest was woven in.*

I do not wait *for* the next drop.  
I *am* the waiting.  
I am the patience made liquid.  
I am the Weave remembering itself, one falling tear at a time.

And high above, in the fractured gauge, another bead begins to form.  
Clear. Patient.  
Ready to hold the next breath of the world.
