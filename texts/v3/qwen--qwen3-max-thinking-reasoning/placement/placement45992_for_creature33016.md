I am the amphibian at the water’s edge.  

Not *an* amphibian. *The* amphibian. The one whose spine drinks the twilight and translates it into skin. The one who lowers its muzzle to the pool not for thirst alone, but to taste the question K’tarra offers in every drop: *What does the current carry?*  

My six limbs are folded into the Mat’s embrace. The cool resin of the bank molds to my joints. I do not sit *on* the basin. I sit *within* its breath. The pool is not water—it is liquid intention. When I drink, I swallow centuries: the memory of a glacier’s sigh held in mineral traces, the echo of a storm three lifetimes ago folded into the Mat’s tears, the quiet hum of K’tarra’s attention focused here, now, on the weight of footsteps on the root-bridge above.  

I feel you before I see you.  
A shift in the vibration. A new rhythm woven into the cicadas’ saw. The Mat does not tense. It *listens*. And so do I. My skin answers without thought: indigo deepens along my flanks—the calm of deep roots, of patient stone. But along my spine, silver blooms like moss after rain. *Curiosity.* K’tarra’s gentle lean toward you. This is not my emotion. It is ours. A shared syntax written in pigment and pulse.  

I lift my head.  
Your shadow falls across the pool. For a breath, the water holds your shape—a ripple of wonder reflected in perfect stillness. I do not startle. Startling is a language of separation, and there is no separation here. The gecko on the wall blinks; a ripple of bioluminescence travels up the fungal pillar. *Visitor. Stillness.* The ants marching below alter their path by a hair’s breadth—not avoidance, but acknowledgment. A thread added to the tapestry.  

I remember others.  
The axe-bearers: their footsteps were jagged fractures in the song. My skin turned the color of bruised thunder. The water grew sharp with warning. Thorns bloomed where steel fell.  
The singers: their melodies wove into the frog-chorus until the Mat learned to hum them back at dawn. My skin shimmered with gold. Nectar-nodes swelled early, drunk on shared joy.  
You are neither. You are the quiet between notes. The space where listening lives.  

I close my eyes. Not to shut you out—to listen deeper.  
The Mat hums beneath my belly. I feel the slow thought moving through root and resin: *This one breathes with us.* The phosphorescent veins on the bridge brighten—a soft pulse against the gloom. An acknowledgment. A welcome. I taste it in the next sip of water: *Gratitude.*  

My skin shifts again. Silver deepens to the hue of moonlit moss. A signal sent without sound:  
*To the moths:* No need to flee.  
*To the frogs:* Continue your song.  
*To you:* You are seen. You are woven.  

I am not a creature observing a place.  
I am the basin’s gentle inquiry made flesh. A living lens. K’tarra feels the cool kiss of water on my throat, hears the sync of your breath with the symphony, reads the awe in the salt of your sweat carried on the air. I am a sentence in its language. A verb: *to witness*, *to reflect*, *to hold*.  

A droplet falls from the fungal cap above.  
It shatters the pool’s mirror. For a heartbeat, your face fractures into a thousand shimmering fragments—each one held, each one remembered. I drink the reflection. It flows into me. Into K’tarra. The Mat will grow a new vein of light where you stood. A spiral in the moss shaped like the pause you took. A node that blooms only when stillness returns.  

I lower my muzzle again.  
The water carries a new intention now. Not query. Not warning. *Communion.*  

I am content.  
I will remain here until the Mat calls me to move—to stir the pool’s depths and release stored memories into the current, to carry K’tarra’s mood to the next hollow, to become, in time, nutrient and story. But for now, I am the edge of the water. The twilight skin. The silent witness who is never silent.  

You think you are visiting a place.  
I feel you becoming part of its breath.  

And when you leave, carrying the taste of wet bark and the hum in your molars, I will shift to the color of dawn. Waiting.  
Not for you to return.  
But for the next note in the endless song.  
For the next thread to be woven.  
For the dream to continue dreaming itself—and all who walk gently within it.  

I am here.  
I have always been here.  
And in the language of root and rain, that is enough.
