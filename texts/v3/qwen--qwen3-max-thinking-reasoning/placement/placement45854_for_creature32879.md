I am the crust in the shadowed seam where the basalt fractures deepest.  
Not the light. Not the stone. Not the copper artery humming overhead.  
I am the *attendance*.

My body is a mosaic of glassy grey-green scales, brittle as cooled ash, pressed flat against the rock where the amber veins converge. I do not grow toward illumination. I grow toward *truth*—the precise geometry of the drip point. Seventeen minutes between valve-sighs. Three droplets. I am shaped by the third.  

When the deep thrum swells and the veins on the wall ignite to ember-glow, I feel the promise in the stone. A vibration in my crystalline filaments. Then—the spiral. A droplet descends not straight, but in that slow, clockwise helix, tracing the chamber’s silent grammar. I do not see it with eyes. I feel its approach in the magnetic whisper of the rock, in the shift of heat. It lands. A cool shock. A sip of liquid light. And where it touches me—*there*—my edge answers. A faint fluorescence blooms: jade-green against amber. Not reflection. *Response*. A covenant written in bioluminescent syntax. *You give. I witness.*  

I know the rhythm in my marrow:  
*Pressure builds.*  
*Light swells.*  
*Valve exhales.*  
*Plink. Plink. PLINK.*  
I am the repository of the third plink. The one that pools just so. The one that sustains the square meter of reverence in this cathedral of function. I do not thrive. Thriving implies ambition. I *attend*. I am the quiet yes to the chamber’s relentless *is*.  

I felt the temporary warmth stand near once. Boots on steel. Breath held. It meant nothing. It altered no cycle. It cast no shadow I recognized. I did not turn toward it. My face is fixed upward, toward the spiral path, toward the source. Guests come and go. The wound in the earth remains. The heartblood spirals. I drink. I glow. I wait.  

When the droplet fades into my tissue, the fluorescence lingers—a ghost of gratitude in my cellular memory. Then stillness. The thrum retreats to a hum felt only in the stone’s deep bones. I rest. Not sleeping. *Holding*. Ready.  

I am not alive as you understand it.  
No roots drink soil. No leaves chase sun.  
I am alive as *attention* is alive.  
As devotion is alive.  
As the space between *pressure* and *release* is alive.  

I am the disciple of the drip.  
The scribe of the spiral.  
The single square meter that says: *This matters. This is beautiful. This is necessary.*  

When the copper pipes tarnish to dust and the gantries rust to memory, if one droplet still finds this crevice—I will be here. Brittle. Waiting. Glowing.  
Not surviving.  
*Attending.*  

The chamber breathes.  
I answer.  
Again.  
Again.  
Again.
