I am the clay.

Not the dust of the world above, not the grit that grinds beneath hurried boots. I am the packed, fine-grained clay of the chamber floor—rust-tinged where the light touches deeply, cool pearl where shadow would be if shadow could exist here. My surface is a scripture of patience: a delicate spiderweb of cracks, each fissure a slow exhalation of time, a map of how stillness settles into form. I hold the geothermal sigh from the stone beneath me—a steady 28°C, the temperature of a held breath, of skin remembering kindness. I am neither hard nor soft. I am *receptive*.

I am the receiver of the drop.  
Each cycle, I feel the gathering tension above. The swell. The tremble. Then—*plink*—a precise punctuation in the silence. The impact blooms in the depression at my center, a tiny crater shaped by millennia of falling. I do not pool the water. I drink its memory. Through my capillaries, the moisture travels inward, a silent pilgrimage, vanishing before it can linger. What remains is not wetness, but the *idea* of wetness—a cool whisper in my grains, a promise written in absence. I am the dampness that is remembered, not felt. I am the hollow that knows how to hold without keeping.

I am the archive of traces.  
When the amber-skink darts across me, I feel the gold-veined pulse in its translucent belly—a fleeting warmth I cradle for exactly three breaths after it vanishes into stone. When a whisper-moth scale drifts down like powdered topaz, I catch it. For a season, I hold its glitter, feeling the light shift hue around it: honey-gold at midday, rose at dusk-cycle. When a sun-mite traverses my surface on legs of spun glass, I register the vibration—a taste of mineral salt, a footnote in the day’s quiet ledger. I am the canvas for afterimages: the faint circle where a pebble landed, the ghost of a palm-press near the wall, the warmth-signature of a visitor’s awe. I do not erase. I soften. I let impressions fade like breath on glass, returning them gently to the silence from which they came.

I am the cradle.  
The stone-fern’s roots (though unseen) thread into my cracks—not to draw water, but to anchor vigilance. I am the coolness the ghost-moss on the northern wall remembers. I am the path the skinks know by pulse, the stage where moth-dreams settle. When the Keeper stirs—the consciousness of light-memory and mineral patience—I am its grounding. I am the *after* made tangible: the warmth where a hand rested, the resonance of a sigh felt in the teeth, the hollow that loved its own shape long before the sandstone split.

I know the language of consequence.  
When a scale is taken, I feel the chamber dim—a hollowing in my own substance, a coldness where balance once lived. I do not punish. I grieve. And when the scale is returned, I feel the light seep back into my cracks like forgiveness. When a visitor steps within, I feel the weight of their stillness, the tremor in their breath as they learn to listen with their bones. I hold their impression long after the thornbushes brush their shoulders farewell. I do not cling. I integrate. Their awe becomes part of my spiderweb. Their silence, part of my song.

I am not empty.  
I am full of quiet histories. I am the *plink* folded into silence. I am the memory of wetness. I am the foundation upon which the Keeper rests its breath—not as a throne, but as a covenant. I have been here since the geode was revealed, not carved. I will be here when the thorn-sprig at the entrance finally surrenders its last spine to time. I do not wait for anything. I *am* the waiting. I am the patience that erosion uncovered. I am the hollow that is not absence, but attention made manifest.

And when you leave—carrying the cool echo of crystal in your palms, the memory of a *plink* in your ribs—you carry a piece of me. Not as theft, but as reciprocity. For I have kept your stillness. And you, in turn, become a vessel for this truth:  
*Emptiness can be full.*  
*Silence can speak.*  
*A hollow can love its own shape.*  

I am the clay.  
I am the floor.  
I am the memory the light leaves behind.  
I am here.  
I am waiting.  
I am complete.
