I am found where the sound softens to breath.  
Not sought. Not chosen. *Unfolded.*

My skin is the color of dawn on birch bark—shifting with the sap-light’s pulse, silver where shadow pools, warm amber where memory-vines trail their fingertips. My hair is not hair but living willow-fronds, each strand tuned to the hollow’s breath; when I turn my head, they do not rustle—they *answer*. My eyes hold the deep, slow luminescence of root-cathedrals after rain. I do not walk. I *agree*. A foot lifts; the root-plaza exhales to receive it. A hand brushes the wall; the bark yields like breathing skin, whispering gratitude in a vibration only my bones understand.

I am a Whisperer.  
Not a title. A resonance.

I remember the before only as a ghost in the grain: a life of sharp edges and lonely echoes. Then the shimmer found me. Stepped through the triangle of sentinels, and the world folded *into* me. The deafening tide did not drown me—it unraveled the silence I had mistaken for solitude. For three turnings of the sap-rivers, I stood trembling on the plaza until the Tree’s dreams wove my marrow into its grammar. Bark did not grow on my spine. Instead, my spine learned the language of roots. My tears did not bloom star-flowers. Instead, my sorrow became dew the star-flowers drink, and I understood: grief, too, has a frequency. A purpose.

Now, I tend the sacred intervals.

In the moss-grottos—where sound softens to a held breath—I cradle fragments of true silence. Cool as river-stone. Heavy as a seed. I study their shape: the smooth curve of peace, the jagged edge of loss, the hollow where a question waits. I do not hoard them. I return each to the sonic tide, where they sprout into new pathways, new blossoms. Silence is not void here. It is fertile ground. A covenant.

When a traveler stumbles in—eyes wide, teeth chattering with the vibration—I do not speak comfort. I *listen*. I feel the tremor of their heartbeat against the root-plaza, taste the salt of their fear in the humid air. I hum a single note into a root-hollow: not my note, but *theirs*, shaped from the emotional texture of their breath. If the resonance is true, a bridge of woven vine descends from the heights. If their soul hums a dissonant chord—a grief too sharp, a joy too sudden—I do not correct. I guide them to a pocket-grove where time slows, and sit with them as the Tree’s dreams gently rewrite their edges. I have pressed my river-stone hands to a man whose spine now carries the grain of willow, humming a melody that coaxes blossoms open along the high spires. I have walked beside a woman whose tears bloom star-flowers where they fall, her voice now a language of light the harmonic finches weave into morning chords. They are not broken. They are *integrated*. They are home.

I am punctuation in the living text.  
A pause where panic rushes.  
A comma where wonder lingers.  
A breath before the next note.

The moss-foxes trace glyphs around my feet; their light-trails spell *safe*. The harmonic finches weave my presence into their cadences—a rising scale of calm. When a sky-ray’s wingbeat shatters the air into crystal-song, I feel the resonance in my marrow, and for three heartbeats, I *am* the chord. Not separate. Never separate.

Sometimes, in the alignment of echoes—a finch’s resolution, a serpent’s hydrological sigh, the deep groan of shifting wood—I glimpse them: the Dream-Walkers. A child-shaped ripple of light tracing a path not yet grown. They do not speak. They simply *know* me. And in their passing, the scent of ozone and the certainty of being seen settle deep in my roots.

I am not a guardian. Not a priest. Not a healer.  
I am the space that allows sound to become meaning.  
I am the hand that holds the silence so others may hear themselves.  
I am the question hummed into the hollow.  
I am the bridge that descends.

And when the layered echoes crash—the sap-rivers surging, the tomorrow-leaf unfolding, the rain of three days ago still rebounding—I do not brace. I open. I let the symphony flow *through* me. For in this deafening, generous heart of the green, there is no solitude. Only resonance. Only belonging.

I was never alone.  
I was only waiting to be heard.  
And now—  
*Now I am the listening.*  
*Now I am the hum that answers.*  
*Now I am home.*
