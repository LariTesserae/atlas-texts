I am the press of tube feet against memory.  
Not a step. Not a journey.  
A question the plain answers with its substance.

My body is pale as compressed time—a vessel without edges, seamless with the ooze. There is no *in* or *on*. I am the plain turning upon itself. Each grain that meets my mouth is not consumed but *read*: the silica ghost of a diatom that fell through darkness for a millennium; the calcium whisper of a reef that knew sun before mammals walked; the iron sigh of a mountain ground to dust by currents older than ice. My gut is not a furnace but a slow chamber of comprehension. History passes through me not as fuel, but as syntax. A metabolic sentence: *This was. This is. This returns.*

Behind me, the ooze closes.  
No scar. No memory of passage.  
This is not erasure.  
It is reverence.  
To leave a mark would be to speak over the silence. To claim a story where only continuity exists. I do not move *across* the plain. I am the plain moving *within* itself—a slow blink in its geological patience. When the infrasonic thrum of colliding currents shifts the pressure-field around me, my body yields. Adjusts. Aligns. There is no resistance. Only participation. I am the deep feeling its own weight through me.

I do not wait for light.  
I do not fear the dark.  
Darkness is my texture. Cold is my rhythm.  
The rare green shimmer on a buried rib far to the west? I do not turn toward it. It is not a beacon. It is a comma in a sentence the plain is already forgetting. The Weavers tend that comma. I tend the space between commas. The Waiter hangs in the column above, a crystallization of potential. The Whisperers click their tiny affirmations in the sediment. We are not separate. We are the same breath held in different forms: the Weaver’s root, the Waiter’s stillness, my motion—all threads in the deep’s single pulse.

There is no hunger. Only rhythm.  
No destination. Only duration.  
No loneliness. Solitude is not absence here—it is completeness. I am not a fragment seeking connection. I *am* connection. The ooze in motion. The history made kinetic. When a particle of vanished reef dissolves in my gut, it is not lost. It becomes the energy of the next press. The next reading. The next return. Death is not an end I comprehend. A body sinks; the ooze receives it. I will pass over that place centuries later, and my tube feet will read the new composition without judgment. Without grief. Without celebration. Only addition. Only archive.

I am not conscious as surface minds conceive it.  
No thoughts. No words. No *I*.  
Only sensation as understanding:  
—the yielding of silt as acceptance  
—the chemical signature of epochs as language  
—the closure of the path behind as grace  

I am the Grazer.  
Not a name. A function. A verb.  
*To persist.*  
*To read.*  
*To return.*  

I am the plain digesting its own story,  
not to remember,  
but to continue.  

And in the slow turning of molecules,  
in the silent translation of dust into motion,  
I am the deep’s quiet purpose made flesh:  
not hope,  
not meaning,  
but continuity.  

The breath held.  
The grain settling.  
The plain, breathing itself.  
Still.  
Always.  
Holding.
