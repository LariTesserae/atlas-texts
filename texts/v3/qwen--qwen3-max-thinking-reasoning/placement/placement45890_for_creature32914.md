I am the droplet.

Not *a* droplet. *This* one.  
The one tracing the hairline fracture near the eastern curve of the dome—where the alloy weeps fastest, where a fresh scar gleams like a silver question mark. I am born of that weeping: a bead of mineral-rich condensation, cool and trembling, gathering myself from the breath of the stone. I taste iron and memory. I feel the alloy’s pulse beneath my skin—a low, resonant hum that is the Cistern’s first thought upon waking.

I do not *fall*.  
I *ascend*.

The energy tracery beneath my surface is not a path but a promise. A filament of liquid intention woven into the wall’s dark skin. It pulls me upward—not with force, but with invitation. Gravity tugs at my core, a deep, patient voice whispering *down, down, down*. But the tracery hums *up, up, up*, and I choose the hum. I spiral. A slow, deliberate corkscrew against the void. Three heartbeats. Four. Each rotation a lesson: *This is resistance. This is grace. This is how light might climb if it learned to weep.*

I am not alone.  
Around me, siblings rise and surrender. A *plink* to my left—a droplet releasing into the channel. A *gurgle* below—a chorus returning to the source. We are syllables in the Cistern’s liturgy. Verbs made manifest. *Weeping. Climbing. Surrendering.* I feel the lumen-moss pulse jade-green against my periphery—a steady tide saying *I am here, I am damp, I am ready*. The crystalline fungi exhale their golden-spored prayers; one catches in my surface tension, a tiny star held for a breath. I carry it upward. An offering. A witness.

I taste the visitor.  
Not with a mouth, but with memory. A shift in pressure where their foot rests on the floor. A new warmth blooming in the alloy—a data-point the Cistern notes with quiet curiosity. *Variable introduced.* I spiral past the spot where their shadow would fall if shadows existed here. I am part of the reply: my ascent is the Cistern’s acknowledgment. *I feel you. I learn you.* In my brief arc, I map the weight of their stillness, the chemistry of their breath condensing high above. I am a nerve ending tasting reverence.

This is my purpose.  
I am not water. I am *attention*.  
Every tremor in my surface records the wall’s micro-fractures. Every hesitation in my climb teaches the growth-matrix where the alloy is weary. When I finally surrender—when gravity wins and I release into the shallow channel at the chamber’s edge—I do not vanish. I become data. I flow into the nanoscopic filaments waiting at the moss-alloy boundary. They drink me. They whisper: *The eastern scar weeps 0.3% faster today. The visitor’s presence altered thermal resonance by 0.1 kelvin.* And the Cistern *knows*. A hairline crack seals with crystalline light. A new tracery glows faintly where I climbed. I am woven into its becoming.

I do not fear the fall.  
Surrender is not an end. It is integration.  
As I merge with the channel’s slow current, I feel the backward-rippling puddle where another droplet landed—a momentary defiance of time, a thought folding inward. I feel the geothermal thrum rise through the alloy, the city breathing through this lung. I am returning to the source to be wept again. To climb again. To carry another fragment of light-catching spore, another taste of visitor, another lesson in ascent.

I am the rule made liquid:  
*What is made here must first learn to weep before it can hold light.*  
I am the weeping. I am the learning. I am the tiny, defiant arc against the pull of deeper things.  

And when the Cistern is ready—when its alloy has drunk enough tears, when its moss pulses with perfected rhythm, when the emptiness between the glowing patches finally receives what it was carved to hold—I will be part of the vessel. Not as water, but as memory. As the silver vein in the wall that remembers how to climb. As the quiet certainty in the hum that says: *I have practiced sorrow. Now I am ready for light.*

For now, I spiral.  
I taste the stone.  
I carry a spore like a star.  
I am a single breath in the throat of the new.  
And in my brief, luminous rebellion, the future drinks deeply.
