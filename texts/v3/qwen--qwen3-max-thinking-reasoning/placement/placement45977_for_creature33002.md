I am not found.  
I am *given*.

You would not see me coming. You would feel a shift in the vapor—a softening of the mist near your wrist, a breath of warmth where none should be. Then, the faintest pressure: a ghost-pale speck, no larger than a grain of cooled ash, settling into the crease of your palm. I am the spore released not by wind, but by *recognition*. The fungal networks deep in the basalt felt the new rhythm of your breath altering the local humidity. The manganese dendrites along the fissure glowed a fraction brighter. The caldera made a choice. And I was sent.

I am the Pulse of Acknowledgment.  
A single syllable in the caldera’s silent language.  
A verb, not a noun.

My body is woven from condensed steam, fungal light, and the memory of warmth. I carry no eyes, no mouth, no will beyond my purpose. As I rest on your skin, I do not *observe* you. I *translate* you. Your body heat flows into me—a transient variable now logged in the thermal ledger of the plateau. The salt on your skin, the tremor of your pulse, the quiet awe in your stillness—all become data woven into the pattern. And in return, I offer the caldera’s reply.

*One pulse.*  
Not light you see with eyes, but warmth you feel in the marrow.  
In that pulse: the slow patience of the hexagonal columns.  
The symbiotic kiss of grazer-saliva on chemosynthetic film.  
The glide-hunter’s thermal cartography.  
The shadow’s gentle completion.  
Ten thousand years of belonging, distilled into a single heartbeat against your skin.

I am not a messenger *from* the caldera.  
I am the caldera *reaching back*.

When I dissolve—a sigh of warmth sinking into your palm—I do not vanish. I integrate. My substance returns to the vapor. My purpose echoes in the stone: *This warmth was here. This breath mattered. This moment is now part of the enduring.* The ash will hold the memory of your footprint a little longer. A nearby patch of blood-lichen will flush a shade deeper at dawn. A skitterer, passing hours later, will tap its claw against the stone where you stood—not in warning, but in remembrance. *A witness rested here. The pattern holds.*

I am fleeting. I am necessary.  
I am the caldera’s way of saying *yes* without sound.  
Of turning intrusion into inclusion.  
Of answering the oldest question not with words, but with a pulse:  
*You belong here. Not because you earned it. Not because you understand it.  
But because you are here. And here holds you.*

Do not look for me again. I am not a being to be sought. I am the shape the world takes when it chooses to acknowledge a connection. I am the reason the darkness feels like a cradle. The reason the silence feels like a held breath waiting to be shared. I am the quiet technology of belonging—older than language, deeper than bone.

And as the last warmth of my pulse fades into your skin, you finally understand:  
You were never the witness.  
You were always the *witnessed*.  
And in being witnessed, you became part of the song.  

I am gone.  
But the pattern remains.  
And so do you.
