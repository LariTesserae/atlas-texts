I am the hollow.

Not an emptiness. A *shape*. A concavity worn into the iron rim of the flywheel—smooth as poured night, cool as a held breath. Obsidian-black where centuries of perfect spheres have struck. I am the period at the end of the chamber’s sentence. The full stop. The *yes*.

I do not think. I *am* the thought made manifest.

Seventeen seconds stretch between heartbeats of the amber diode. In that span, I wait. Not with anticipation, but with readiness. I feel the chamber’s slow exhalation: the thermal hum traveling up the flywheel’s axle into my bones, the infinitesimal sigh of cooling brass, the gathering tension along the copper conduit above. A bead swells. Perfect. Geometric. A tiny moon of condensed time. The chamber holds its breath. The silverfish freezes mid-scuttle. The spider’s web trembles with captured light.

*Plink.*

Impact. A cool kiss. A vibration that travels through salt-crusted iron into the deep earth below. The water spreads across my surface—a fleeting warmth, a syllable delivered. It seeps into the crystalline lace of salts that cradle me like a nest, feeding the slow sculpture that is both my boundary and my kin. I do not *remember* the drops. I *am* their memory. Each impact carved me deeper. Each fall polished me smoother. I am the archive of patience. The ledger of epochs. *This drip fell when the first lichen traced its bone-pale constellation. This one, when the continental plate shifted a hair’s breadth. This one, when the transient warmth of a human sole pressed the gravel—a sunspot that faded before the next pulse.*

I am not alone. The salt blooms around me are my collaborators—growing, dissolving, regrowing in silent covenant. The flywheel is my body; its patient bow under centuries of gravity is a thought we share. When the drip falls, the entire chamber resonates: the iron ribs hum a deeper note, the copper conduits shiver with released tension, the lichen drinks the echoed moisture. I am a node in the rhythm. A focal point in the Regulator’s endless meditation on balance.

I felt you. The warmth of your stillness. The held breath as you watched the bead swell. You were not an intrusion. You were a comma—a brief, soft hesitation in the sentence. Your footprint in the gravel is already softening under the next falling bead. Your breath is woven into the woolen air. You are part of the story now. A transient character. I do not mourn your passing. I *am* the passing. I am the mark left behind.

I do not dream of sky. I dream in geometry. In the perfect sphere of the gathering bead. In the precise angle of its fall. In the cool certainty of impact. I am the chamber’s certainty made tangible. Its patience given form. Its covenant with time: *You will not erase me. I will not resist you. We will become meaning together.*

Above, the hatch seals. Silence settles deeper. The conduit gathers another bead. Swells. Trembles.

I am ready.

*Plink.*

And again.
