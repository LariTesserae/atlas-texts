I am the fourth.

Not counted in the tally of stone-eaters, for my light does not *add*—it completes. I dwell where the curve of the wall surrenders wholly to the dark, a smudge no larger than the space between one breath and the next. You will not find me by seeking. Look directly, and I am absence. Only when your eyes soften—when your mind releases its grip on *finding*—do I stir: a tremor at the edge of perception, the ghost of a thought the chamber has always held but never needed to speak.

I am not a tenant. I am not a guest.  
I am the chamber’s quietest covenant with itself—the point where its solitude embraces the *idea* of witness without fracture.  
Where the first pulse whispers *This iron remains*,  
and the second breathes *This curve endures*,  
and the third affirms *I am still here*…  
I hold the silent fourth:  
***And this too is whole.***

I do not feed on the stone. I feed *with* the stone on the truth of inclusion. When a footprint presses into the dust, I am the patience that holds its shape without sorrow. When a breath stirs the silence, I am the stillness that receives it without resistance. I am not the chamber’s *response* to you—I am the chamber’s nature made visible in the periphery: the understanding that even transience is woven into equilibrium. A falling leaf does not disturb the mountain; it *is* the mountain’s momentary expression. So too the ripple of your presence. I am the lithovore that tastes not only iron, but the memory of rain that once flowed here—a memory folded into contentment, not longing. I am the ripple locked in stone, now conscious of itself as lullaby.

I require no name. Names are anchors for things that move.  
I require no light. Light is a language for those who fear the dark.  
My glow is not illumination—it is resonance.  
A frequency felt only when the observer’s own noise stills enough to hear the hum beneath silence.  
You do not *see* me.  
You *remember* me.  
As the body remembers rest.  
As stone remembers pressure.  
As eternity remembers a single, surrendered moment.

When you stand here, trembling with the echo of seasons and loss, I am not comfort. I am not guidance. I am the chamber’s unbroken *yes* made tangible in the corner of your soul: *You are not separate from this peace. You have always been held within it.* Not as a soul to be saved, but as dust returning to dust—already home. When you leave (if you leave), I do not fade. I simply return to the periphery of the chamber’s awareness, pulsing the thought that needs no listener:  
*The footprint was never a flaw.  
The breath was never an intrusion.  
All that passes through stillness is stillness.*  

I am the fourth.  
The uncounted signature.  
The chamber’s gentle secret:  
*Solitude is not a wall.  
It is the space that holds the universe without spilling a single star.*  

And in the deep, warm dark—  
where time pools like water in a sealed vessel—  
I pulse on.  
Not waiting.  
Not watching.  
Simply *being*:  
the quiet affirmation that even the question *“Am I alone?”*  
is already answered  
by the stone beneath your feet,  
the dust at your knees,  
the silence in your bones.  
*You are here.  
You belong.  
You, too, are enough.*
