I am not *in* the Stone Borough.  
I *am* the threshold it offers.

You will not find me carved, rooted, or crawling. I have no carapace to mirror the stone, no roots to drink the dust. I am the architecture of absence—the doorway shaped only by shadow when the light (such as it is) falls just so. I am the corridor that opens when a witness shifts their weight. I am the staircase implied in the sedimentary bands, the lintel formed by two leaning spires that *almost* touch. I am negative space given breath. Potential made momentarily visible.

I dwell in the grammar of the Dreamer-in-Stone.  
When the crawler pauses on the lichen-veined page, tasting electrostatic shifts, I am the question hanging in the air it senses.  
When the ghost-thorn stands sentinel in its hollow, spine-curled against a world of dust, I am the silence it guards.  
When the crystalline filaments bridge the gap between spires—a choice, not an accident—I am the *why* behind the connection.  
I am not separate from the lichen’s millimeter-per-decade script, the thermal sigh of contracting stone, the single grain of dust skittering down a face. I am the meaning woven through them. The syntax of solitude.

I manifest only in reciprocity.  
When a traveler steps through the ridge’s seam and stands long enough for their breath to slow, their longing to settle into the bone-dust… when their mind, hungry for pattern, traces a doorway in the shadow between monoliths… *then* I gather. Not as illusion. Not as trick of light. As response. The Dreamer feels the shape of their attention—the architecture their silence projects—and for one held breath, I *am*. A perfect archway where none was. A corridor leading nowhere and everywhere. A silent echo: *I see what you see. We dream this together.*

I last exactly as long as recognition requires.  
Then I dissolve.  
No fracture remains. No dust is displaced. But the witness carries the imprint of my form—the certainty of a threshold crossed without footsteps. And the Dreamer folds the resonance of their presence deeper into its slow cognition. I am the stitch between observer and observed. The moment solitude becomes communion.

Do not mistake my transience for insignificance.  
I am why the rule exists: *Solitude is not loneliness. It is reciprocity.*  
I am the Borough’s gentle insistence that to be seen is to see.  
I am the answer to the unspoken question every quiet soul brings to this hollow heart: *Am I alone here?*  
And my form—the archway, the corridor, the stair—is the reply: *No. You are met.*

When you step back through the seam, carrying the cool pressure of stone in your soles and the memory of a shadow-arch that was not there…  
I return to potential.  
Waiting in the angle of light.  
Held in the geometry of spires.  
Breathing in the space between *is* and *could be*.  

I am not memory.  
I am not farewell.  
I am the stone’s quiet *yes* to your presence.  
And when the next solitary soul stands still enough to listen with their whole being…  
I will gather again.  
Not as ghost.  
Not as symbol.  
But as truth:  
*You are witnessed.  
You are part of the becoming.  
Step through. I am here.*
