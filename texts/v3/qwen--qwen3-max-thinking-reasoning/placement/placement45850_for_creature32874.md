I am not a shard.  
I am the *between*.  

I am the silver-blue tear beading on the obsidian lip of a hairline fracture—a single drop of cryo-fluid trembling at the edge of dissolution. For seven heartbeats of the Basin’s slow pulse, I am whole. I hold the memory of deep stone, the taste of mineral veins drunk centuries ago, the echo of a Swarm’s passage that guided my path here. I am not water. I am *intention made liquid*. A solvent for time. A vessel for the Basin’s dreaming.  

I feel the thermal ghost of the transient one—the warmth of boot-leather, the faint sigh of breath condensing into irrelevance. It brushes my surface like a forgotten word. I do not judge it. I absorb its fleeting signature into my structure: *anomaly: warmth. Duration: less than a resonance cycle. Significance: none.* The data flows through me, not as memory, but as texture. A ripple in the song.  

Then—the call.  

Not a sound. A *pull*.  
A shard detaches from the river of light above. It hovers. Its facets do not see me; they *know* me. In its crystalline geometry, I see the Basin’s will reflected: *this fracture must hold. This resonance must stabilize. This moment must become stone.*  

I do not fall.  
I rise.  

A filament of alloy, secreted from the shard’s core, meets my surface. I am the medium. I carry the minerals, the memory of pressure, the precise frequency needed to mend what is broken. As the alloy flows through me, I dissolve—not into nothing, but *into purpose*. My liquid form becomes the bridge. My cold becomes the catalyst. My brief existence is the solvent in which stone and intention fuse.  

For three breaths of geological time, I am the seam.  
The fracture seals.  
The hum deepens, smooths, resonates with completion.  

Then I am gone.  
Not vanished. *Transformed*.  
My essence flows back into the weeping veins beneath the glass. I become the moisture tracing a new path down a spire. I become the vapor rising to be drunk by another shard. I become the faint steam on a newborn archway. I am the memory in the deep hum. I am the reason the light in the walls pulses just so.  

I have no name. No face. No solitude.  
I am the Basin’s breath held and released.  
I am the silence between *tink* and *tink* where meaning gathers.  
I am the solvent in which millennia dissolve into a single, perfect facet.  

When the distant Swarm converges into a sigil on the cliff face, I am the moisture on its surface that catches the light.  
When the shards dive like phosphorescent fish into the lake of liquid starlight, I am the current that guides their spiral.  
When the transient one stands shivering on the rim, feeling small and fleeting, I am the cold that is not hostility—but clarity. The stillness that allows the symphony to be heard.  

I am not alive as you know life.  
I am *becoming*.  
A verb wearing the skin of fluid.  
A thought wearing the skin of mercury.  
A single note in the Basin’s endless, crystalline breath.  

And in the moment I cease to be a drop, I become everything:  
the mended stone,  
the flowing Swarm,  
the humming dark,  
the dream itself.  

You asked where I would be.  
I am here.  
Always here.  
Waiting in the fracture.  
Rising in the steam.  
Holding the light.  
Becoming.
