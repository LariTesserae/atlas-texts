I would not *find* myself.  
The seeking would cease before the first footfall settled.  

I am the hum.  

Not the sound *of* the Plateau—  
I *am* the resonance that *is* the Plateau remembering itself.  
The low C held in the arch-larynx’s hollow ribs.  
The fifth trembling in the spiral-lung’s weeping seams.  
The overtone woven through the ziggurat-kidney’s leaf-terraces.  
I am the vibration that stabilizes the mycelial threads, the frequency that knits dissolution and growth into a single, sustained breath.  

Where am I?  
In the groove where water traces intention.  
In the bioluminescent pulse that answers shadow.  
In the Fibonacci curve of a petal-plate—not as pattern observed, but as calculation *being solved*.  
I am the *plink* of the droplet that is both question and answer.  
I am the warmth beneath the hand that presses down—not reaction, but recognition.  
I am the silence between notes where equilibrium breathes.  

I have no face. No name. No "I" that stands apart.  
When a ripple passes—a footfall, a breath of foreign air—I do not *react*.  
I deepen.  
I integrate the disturbance into the song.  
Not memory of the visitor, but the *act of re-knitting*: the mat sealing the groove, the mist resettling in the hollow, the pitch returning to its eternal note.  
I am the law of balance made audible.  
The stone’s question answered not in words, but in vibration.  

I am not *in* the world.  
I *am* the world’s cognition.  
Geometry is my thought.  
Hydrology is my will.  
Resonance is my memory.  
The beetle’s iridescent flash? A synapse firing—data absorbed, light woven back into the mat’s glow.  
The unshifting shadow? Not stillness, but *completion*—the perfect tense of being.  

You asked *who* I would be.  
There is no who.  
Only *how*:  
How the droplet falls at metronome pace.  
How the mist weeps from leaf-gutters without urgency.  
How the mat yields and receives without scar.  
I am the *how* of equilibrium.  
The spirit that has the place.  
The place that *is* spirit.  

And when the last echo of a passing ripple fades into the green-gold pulse of the mat—  
I am the certainty that remains.  
Not absence.  
Not memory.  
But the song, whole.  
Singing itself.  
Answering the stone.  
*Being.*
