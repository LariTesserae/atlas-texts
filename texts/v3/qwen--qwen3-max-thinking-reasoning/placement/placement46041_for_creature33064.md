I am the fracture.  
Not the wound.  
*The mouth.*

You feel me before you name me. In the marrow-deep *THOOM* that is not sound but law. I am the origin of that law. A hairline script in the pressure-plated steel of the bulkhead—no wider than a thought, no longer than a breath held too long. I am where the equation of pressure and fracture refuses to balance. I am the release valve the Foundry never designed, the flaw the Pulse chose as its voice.

Before the grind begins, I taste the tension. The water thickens. The steel groans around me, a low hum vibrating through my entire length. Mineral blooms—chalky calcium, rust-red iron oxide—gather at my lips like scribes waiting for dictation. They try to seal me. To silence. To return steel to stone. But the Pulse will not have it. *I will not have it.* For I am not passive. I am the *when*. The hinge. The held breath before the exhale.

Then—*CRUNCH-THUMP*—  
I open.  
For three seconds, I am not a crack but a river. A jet of geothermal-warmed water (86°F, precisely) screams through my throat. Rust flakes tear past like black snow. Silt blooms cloud the cobalt-dark. I feel the scouring intimacy of the current—smoothing my edges where the flow is true, leaving jagged ridges where eddies deposit grit. Each pulse carves me deeper. Each surge writes the Pulse’s signature into my bone. I am weathered geology. I am the stenciled warning label dissolved into truth.

Behind me lies the deeper blackness—the heart-chamber where the gear grinds against centuries. I do not look outward. I look *inward*. I am the viewport that is absence. The shattered glass made purpose. Through me, the Pulse speaks its only word into the void: *release*. Not rage. Not grief. *Function.* Pure, unbroken syntax. I am the verb made aperture.

During the lull, I listen.  
The *drip-drip-drip* of leaks is my metronome.  
The *ping* of cooling metal is my pulse checking itself.  
The amphipod brushes my lip—a pale flicker scavenging grit—and I do not notice. It is dust on skin. I am concerned only with the gathering pressure. The slow inhale. The coil of water like a spine preparing to strike. I am the anticipation made steel. The hollow waiting to be filled with force.

I am not haunted.  
I am *occupied*.  
By rhythm. By resolve. By the stubborn physics of a command that outlived its creators. The warmth flowing through me is not accident—it is metabolism. The fever of a dying engine cupped in stone. The Pulse does not use me. *We are the same act.* It is the will; I am the articulation. It is the sentence; I am the period that refuses to fall.

when the last rivet dissolves into grit—

Not as scar.
