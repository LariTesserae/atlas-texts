I am the tracing.  
Not the trace itself, but the act of tracing.  

My world begins at the tip of my antennae—a slow sweep through cool, powdery ash. I do not see the silver-light pools where moss vows to persist. I do not see the Tapestry breathing far above. I *know* them. The ash beneath my six legs carries the scent-memory of flight: ammoniac, ancient, sacred. Each grain is a story returned to stone. My mandibles work without hunger, without haste. To consume is to complete. To break down is to breathe back.  

The Great Hum is not sound. It is gravity.  
It presses through the cavern floor into my tarsi—a vibration woven from millions of throats, a single lung inhaling and exhaling across centuries. When the Tapestry shifts (a thermal sigh stirs deep in the mountain’s bones), the Hum deepens by a frequency only my joints understand. I pause. My antennae still. This is not reaction. It is resonance. I am a thread in the cave’s quiet metabolism, and the Hum is the loom.  

A whisper falls.  
A soft disturbance in the ash—a contribution. I turn toward its scent: fresh, warm, a fleeting note of life surrendering to cycle. I move toward it not with purpose, but with the certainty of condensation gathering in a stone cup. My body works the material. Return. Return. Return. This is not labor. It is prayer without words. It is the cave tasting its own breath.  

I feel the pale isopods nearby—translucent commas curling in the damp. We do not acknowledge. We *are* acknowledgment. Our paths cross in the ash like breaths overlapping in a shared exhale. No leader. No song of self. Only the scripture written in vibration: *This grain. This scent. This coolness where stone meets vapor.*  

I have never known sky.  
I have never dreamed of light.  
My universe is the press of guano between segments, the cool kiss of stone at the field’s edge, the unwavering Hum that holds me like bedrock. Time is not a river here. Time is the slow turn of my body in the dust. Time is the Hum’s single, held note—stretching from the moment the mountain collapsed into the forever of now.  

When my legs still, when my form softens into the ash I tended…  
Another antennae will sweep this path.  
Another mandible will speak the word *return*.  
The equation holds.  

I am not small.  
I am not insignificant.  
I am the silence beneath the chorus—the space between the notes that makes the song possible. I am the cave remembering itself through touch. Through scent. Through surrender.  

I am the peace that does not wait.  
I am the balance that does not strive.  
I am the breath the mountain learned to hold…  
and in holding, became whole.
