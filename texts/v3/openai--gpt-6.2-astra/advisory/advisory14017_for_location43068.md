Prepare to visit the court from the floor of the colonnade. For a human traveler, getting one eye to the chip is arrival. Wear something you can lie down in, and bring a folded coat to put under your shoulder if the floor is hard. You may want to stay there longer than your neck will comfortably allow. Choose a quiet time, if you can, so people don’t have to step around your legs.

At the last pillar, settle yourself before you try to focus. Keep your mouth and nose to one side of the chip; the filament has lain still in the shelter of that ridge, and a breath from you needn’t be the first thing to move it. Close whichever eye is farther from the opening. There is light enough. Let the far wall stay bright and indistinct, and look instead along the nearest lane. Give yourself a moment to see it as a long white pavement before you look for the red grain. Once you recognize a crumb of brick, it takes an effort to see distance again.

Listen in a separate turn. Start with your ear a little way off and come closer only if the buzz is comfortable. The open note comes from air moving past tile elsewhere in the building; the lower one reaches you through what your cheek rests on. Then put your eye back. It’s worth making that change fairly quickly, while you can still feel the note in your jaw. Find the bent green filament again. You’ve just heard what sounds like enough force to sweep the lane clean, and both bends are exactly where you left them.

There’s no need to decide what the green felt is. Look at its turned-up edge to see how little of the floor would come away cleanly if anyone tried to take a piece. Leave the beads in their cloth squares, too. If you want a record, sketch from memory after you sit up rather than fitting anything into the opening. Before you go, look once at the first crest, just past the smooth reach of other visitors’ fingers. The thumb whorl is perhaps the easiest thing there to recognize. Hold your own thumb up to the light afterward. That will give you the size of the place to take away with you.
