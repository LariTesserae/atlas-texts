I would come at the smallest plant from the side of the two staircases. From the top tread of one I could see the long arm against the glass bank. From the same tread of the other I could see it against the short arm, and what had looked like a fork would look like a single rather thick tooth. I would go back and forth a few times. Two places the width of a thumbnail apart are a considerable convenience to anyone trying to see in the round.

For a while I would suppose I had come to look at the band of white under the leaf. But there is a little more looking to be done at the short tip. The finish on it is so good that the eye comes to a stop exactly there. The transaction does not. Something gets across from the varnish to the gas. I think I would find myself on the gas side of that crossing.

To give myself a body, I would take one of the particles of gas that has just received a charge. I would have no green about me, even at the instant of leaving. Nor would I carry away a speck of the tip. It has been letting things go for years without losing the very small amount of material one can see from the stairs. What I had of the plant would be a direction, scarcely perceptible at first among all the knocks I got from my neighbours. After a great many knocks I would be farther from the tooth than when I started.

The country would have quite a different shape to me. I could cross a reach of glaze on which a leaf could never get a start. I would have no particular use for the seam that holds the flake upright. The hair's space between the mating edges of glass, on the other hand, would be a space of perfectly respectable width, already full of bodies like the one I had taken. At that scale it is the notion of an empty compartment that takes some imagining. All the way from the bottom leaf to the white glaze beneath it there is company.

Suppose I came up against the back of the brass plate and gave up my charge there. I would still be in the compartment. I like that part. Having had for a little while a business that concerned the whole distance between a green tooth and a sheet of brass, I would be free to knock about near one of the bowls of the 8, neither doing the work again nor keeping any mark of having done it once. Some other particle would be just leaving the tooth. From the stairs, with a good lens and all that excellent daylight, one could see neither of us.

So my whereabouts would be quite precise for only a moment. At the smallest of the four growths, at the shorter of its two ends, there is room just beyond the last of the green for the first thing that is no longer the plant but has something of its living to do. That is where I would find myself. What became of me afterward need not be settled in order for the tip to be finished to exactly that length.
