I would be under the chip, still holding the shred. I would have no sense of the size of the thing that came to rest on me from time to time. There would be a roughness in the shred, a little knot of crosswise fibres which I could feel quite distinctly when the weight was on, and lose again when it was off. That would be as much of the chip as I knew.

At first I would be all mouth. Even where the thread came in behind, a long way from the lips for so small a creature, a touch would make me shut. The thread would sometimes draw tight as the weed took up the slack in it, and I would bite the brown stuff for something that had happened as far off as the green stone. Very gradually there would come to be a length of me in which a touch could travel without my doing anything about it yet.

I think the chip would come down in perfect quiet. The tick belongs to the point, and this time the point would go into the pelt. The weight would come on much as before. I would wait for the crosswise fibres to go soft and indistinct. I would go on waiting well past the time at which, even in the slackest water, they had always gone soft before.

It would be the lower lip that moved first. There would be nowhere for it to lift, but it could turn a little on its long edge. A skin of cold water would get in as far as the knot. For the first time I would feel the fibres separately, three against the upper lip and only two against the lower, the third sticking out between. Beyond its tip there would be a space the weight had missed. Some of me would grow out there, quite clear at first, a bead such as gathers at the tip of a hair lifted out of water. There would be grit all round it and in one direction less grit. It would grow in that direction.

Outside, the old sole would go on feeding. A little of its work would still go round by the green stone, down the fringe and out along the loop to me. For a while that would be all I had. Then one time, in the long interval between its pinches, there would be another. The upper lip, still held to the inward set of the shred, would have drawn ahead of the lower by the breadth of a fibre. The clear bead would cloud over. A speck of the smooth stuff from the notch would be inside it, and the notch itself would be that much deeper. There would be room to do it again.

Much later the thread would draw tight and something at the green stone would shut. I would feel the little check it gave me. Up to then everything that had come along the thread had come *from* the cold. I would lie as still as I could under the chip, the shred in my two holds, while the last of that first weak pinch went the long way back.
