The first attention the power paid the animal was rather like the attention of someone leaning down to hear the drop. It took a while to find the sheltered place. The power could hear all the drops separately if it wished, including those that fell in chambers without an opening to the day. This was of very little help. What it wanted to know was what the animal was attending to while all that was going on.

It found a young one under a spring lip. Three feet were fast. With the fourth it was making little sweeps over a patch of lime so smooth that there was nothing for a toe to do there. At the farthest reach of the foot there was a roughness. Each time the foot came to it the toes closed, and each time they closed a little too soon or a little too late, for the water lifted the free quarter of the body and let it down again. To the power, which had been looking at the whole animal, it had seemed more or less stationary. In the compass of that foot there was a great deal of coming and going.

It could have put a grain there. It had gone so far as to consider which grain when the creature took away one of its fast feet. Its body swung downstream on the other two. The roughness was now against its belly, and the foot that had been trying for it took hold of a piece of stone on the far side that had not previously been within reach. The power let the grain go up again in the spring.

It stayed a long time. It saw several holds missed from which there was no such recovery. It saw a shrimp back out between the lips and spend the next hour cleaning first one feeler and then the other. It had come to care quite particularly for the animal in that station and was surprised at its pleasure in the escape of the shrimp.

In the end there was one thing it did for them, though not up on the shelf. Far down in the valley a stream came out under a road. In dry years this stream used to shrink to three or four pools, and the people there were considering taking water from the mountain. There was a wet streak high on the eastern bluff, visible even in August. A pipe brought down from there would run full without a pump. The matter had got as far as a pencilled line on a map.

The power did not rub out the line. On the day a woman went up to measure the summer flow, it held a cloud for a moment clear of the sun. The woman was lying on one elbow with her sleeve wet through, waiting for a canvas vessel to fill at the edge of the bedding plane. For perhaps two seconds the light came back to her from the water in the vessel and shone up into the joint. She saw the white knobs. One of them opened in the slack made by the rim of the vessel. When she eased the rim away it closed again. She put the vessel back to see.

What she brought down, in addition to the figure for the flow, was a drawing on the back of the map. It wasn't a very good drawing. The white end had been made into a head, and the two feet she could see were set down as a pair on the same side. But she had drawn the rock above and the water below and had put in a short line to show how far in she could see. Beyond that line, in the space through which the intake was to go, she had written *don't know*.

There was water to be had lower down, though it would need a pump. After some argument that was where they got it. The power had not been at the meeting. It was still on the shelf, where the cloud had long since gone over and an animal with a grain in the crook of one leg was feeling its way along the underside of a back. There were miles of this it had never seen. It went along at the animal's rate.
