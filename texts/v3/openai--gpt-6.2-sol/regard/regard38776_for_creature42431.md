The power came in the interval after a blow. There was time to see the female come down the wall, miss the peg with her first reach, and catch it with her second. There was time to see the long animal open its joint to let her pass. Then the north wall struck.

Partway down the ridge, under a field of loose white stone, a slab rested on a knob no bigger than the head of a nail. Each time the mountain shifted, the slab rocked on the knob and hit the ridge. The power put a grain of stone under the free end. The next time the mountain shifted, the slab took the weight without rocking. In the room, the grass loop stirred a little.

The female let go anyway. She sank with her hind legs spread and had just begun to swim when the long animal put its joint against the wall. The current took her most of the way to the shelf. She went the rest of the way herself.

By noon the water had gone quiet enough for a flake to settle on the peg. It settled on the upper side, where the female never went. She stayed underneath, working her mouth along the edge of the leaf. Once she backed out and went to the old hole. There was nothing there to eat. She felt it all the way around and went home.

The power waited through a storm. Snow still disappeared into the water at the slit. The shelf gave up a flake, and a thin lip grew over the old hole. Once there was a blow from farther down the ridge, hard enough to lift the grass loop, but the northern wall did not answer it. The long animal had put its joint through the loop at the first sound. After a while it drew the joint out.

On the fourth morning there were six eggs under the leaf and a small animal walking on the peg. It had all its legs and used them in no particular order. It walked out to the pared end, where there was nothing more to walk on, and kept going.

The power nearly put a ledge there.

The little animal turned over twice. Then it spread its legs and came back to the wood. It had come back on the wrong side, in the full light from the slit, and for a while it stood there with its eyes shut.
