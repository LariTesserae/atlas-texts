The power came at slack water. It had heard the cable from a long way off and wanted to know what made the sound. By the time it reached the ridge there was no sound to follow. It went down into the bowl instead.

The little skate had settled in a place where the silt was deep enough to take the impression of its mouth. For a while the power thought it was resting. Then it saw how fast the two hollows behind the eyes were opening and closing. The skate had come out of its case into water that had scarcely moved since the last tide. It had spent much of what it had left getting that far.

The power drew a little water over the bottom. It drew it from the same shelf the tide would draw it from, and at first it drew too much. A gray thread came up off the silt. The skate lifted at the edges. The power eased off until the thread settled and there was only enough movement to bend the tip of a worm’s fan.

It stayed there until the flood came.

Higher up, the cable began its note. The power followed it this time and found that the fairlead had worn almost through on one side. With each tide the cable was cutting a little farther into the steel. It would part on a day when the water was moving hard, and the loose end would reach well into the kelp.

At the next slack the power took the weight of the cable and laid it in a gully where it could not get up again. A small anemone had grown on the underside of the fairlead. The power left the fairlead where it was.

For three floods the ling went under the concrete foot at the old hour. On the fourth she stayed out until the water caught the feeler under her chin and laid it flat. Then she went down, a little late, with her tail bent sideways to get it in.

By then the skate had crossed a good part of the bowl. It had caught something small enough to swallow whole. Where it had first lain, the incoming water had taken away the impression of its mouth but left the two little scoops where it pushed off. The power went back once to look at them. It did not keep the next tide from taking those too.
