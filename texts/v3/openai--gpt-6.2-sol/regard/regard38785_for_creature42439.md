The power came down the stair while a woman from the water office was taking measurements. She had a folding rule, which was the only kind she could open at the bottom. Her assistant was on the third step with the lamp.

The city was going to put a lift in the building above. One corner of the lift pit would come down through the neck of the jar. On the drawing this was not a jar but the end of a drain, and beside it someone had written *disused*. The woman had come to make sure.

She put two fingers into the neck and brought them out dry. Then she held the rule across the room and knocked the jar handle with the brass end. The spider gathered its three threads so fast that she did not see them.

There was room to put the lift a little farther into the building. This would mean losing a cupboard. The power knew what was in the cupboard: a broom, a short ladder, and the leaves for a table that had been taken away. It knew, too, that if the jar broke there would still be spiders in the room. The little white mother had never been as high as the jar and would go on doing what she did. It was not a question of saving everything by saving the jar.

The woman called up that the pipe was dry. Her assistant leaned down with the lamp, thinking she had asked for more light. In bringing the lamp lower he put one hand against the wall. The power warmed the clay under his palm just enough that he took his hand away and looked at what he had touched.

“Is that a handle?” he said.

She could not hear him. He came down one step and pointed.

The woman had seen handles like it in cases with glass lids. She had never seen one still doing nothing on the side of a jar. She turned the rule around and used the wooden end to take the last measurement.

The lift went where the cupboard had been. The people who cleaned the building got a hook for the broom and a better place for the ladder. Nobody could think what to do with the table leaves. For years they stood on edge behind a radiator, where one of them darkened more than the other.

The power stayed until the young came out again. It could feel them take hold of the seam, though from the stair they looked like little places where the water had caught the light. Most went left. One went right, stopped, and went on.

It did not go after that one. There was water on that side too.
