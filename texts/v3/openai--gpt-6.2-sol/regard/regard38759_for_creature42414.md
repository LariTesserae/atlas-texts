The first thing it did was take its hand out of the handhold. It had been leaning on the rock, and where it leaned the animals nearest the entrance had gone still.

It sat on the step until a large one came down from the roof. From there it could see something that wasn’t visible from the stool. The animal did not touch the switch. It put its back against the rolled lip and lifted. The shelter had worked a little loose at one corner, and when the animal took its weight, the rattle went out of the sheet metal. Under the grating, the two pads of a white thing drew together. The thread between them lay slack for the first time since the power had come.

The old animal stayed there until the pads drew apart again. When it came out, there was a bright line across its back.

Outside, among the shoe prints, the power found a flat chip of gray stone. It held the chip against the loose corner. On the first try the motor changed its note, and all along the north wall the mouths closed. It took the chip away. On the second try it held only the weight the old animal had held. The note stayed where it was. It left the chip there.

The next time the animal came down, it went under the shelter as before. Its back did not reach the lip. It raised itself on the hinges of its legs, tried once more, and came out. Halfway to the roof it stopped at one of the places polished by a heel. It put its pads down. After a while a shaving rose between them. There was so little room left in its mouth that it had to take the shaving sideways.

The power spent the rest of that day on the step. Toward morning a young one came to the threshold and put out a leg. The power laid one finger in the dust, close enough for the foot to reach, and let the slower thud come through it. The foot rested there. Then the animal brought up a second foot. Its pads were still on the warm side. It stood that way for several strokes, with its belly over the line where the warmth stopped.

It went back in when an animal crossed the grating. In the dust on the step there was a finger mark with two small punctures at its edge. The power moved its hand aside so that the next one could try the dust.
