The power came in the hour when the heat went sideways. It waited on the ledge until the two animals that could still bend had bent. One caught the other by the edge and rode it inward. The other took a long time getting there.

There was a third that did not bend. The power warmed the stone beneath it a little, and then a little more. When the draft turned, it was still flat. The power left it where it lay.

Inside, a dark animal was carrying young. It had taken up its place above the seam, and one by one the young were letting themselves down from its belly. The last had got hold by only one side. Each time the adult made a feeding motion the little body swung out over the red crack and came back against the wall. Finally it caught. The adult went on feeding. There was a ragged place on the clear animal when it came out from underneath, and it lay a while before going back to the crust.

The power watched long enough to see that animal go dark.

By then it knew what the trouble was with the white-veined stone. At each outward breath the nearer support tipped a little toward the ledge. It had been doing this for years. The tap was the sound it made coming back. One day it would go a little too far and not come back, and the roof would lie flat on the skins.

There was a chip of stone just outside, too cold for anything to live under. The power fitted it behind the support. It did not lift the roof. It had seen how close the smallest animals kept to the underside, where a taller opening would have put them in the draft.

At the next outward breath there was no tap. The skins moved at their edges, as they had before.

The power went out and sat on the ledge for the rest of the night. It had thought of making the ledge wider. In the last of the dark it saw a clear animal reach from the lip of the hollow and take hold of a back on the ceiling. There was nothing below it for a very long way. The two animals went in together, one of them going where it had meant to go.
