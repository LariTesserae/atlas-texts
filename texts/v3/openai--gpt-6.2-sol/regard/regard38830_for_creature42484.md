The power came in at the rim, where the scrape could no longer be heard. It laid what would have been a hand against the rock and waited. After a while it could tell the two knocks apart by feel. The second was the lighter one.

It took longer to find the animal. The power had counted the mouths separately, as anyone would. Then a crab lodged sideways in the mouth under the chipped leaf, and the mouths began to close. Between the closing of one and the next there was time for a fish to leave its slot, eat, and come back. The power went out to the rim and waited there for the last mouth to get the news.

That mouth was lifting at one edge. On the ebb of each pulse a little water got under the ribbon and peeled it back from the glass. It settled again when the flow returned, but not quite as far down as before. The mouth had been creeping toward the lip in this fashion for some days.

The power did not hold it down. It went a little way below the lip and put a grain of white stone into a seam. It left most of the grain sticking out. There was room behind it for the ribbon to pass, and on the far side there was a shallow pit where the mouth could lie out of the pull.

For the rest of the afternoon nothing touched the grain. A crab fell past it, too far out to catch it. The fish behind the leaf tapped its nose against the glass. In the hollow an old strainer tore loose, and the long animal opened in the wrong place.

Toward evening the current drew the mouth over the lip. The ribbon caught behind the grain and stretched until the mouth was hardly more than a crease in it. On the next pulse the crease folded into the pit. It stayed there through the scrape and the two knocks that followed.

The power went on waiting. It wanted to see whether the mouth could leave.

It could. On the third day it let go of the pit and drew itself back a little, after something in the water. It missed. For a while it hung clear of the rock, opening and closing on water that had not passed through any other mouth. Then it took hold again, a little to one side of where the power had expected.
