The power came while the inhabitant was feeling for the chips a second time. It did not take either touch for a request.

A little grit had worked loose under the smaller chip. The inhabitant knew this. It had been taking the weight of the cup from below, a little at a time, whenever the grit shifted. What it could not do was keep the weight off the chip and reach the grit at the same time.

The power could have set the cup on a single broad foot of stone. Instead it took the weight.

For a while nothing moved. Then the narrow part that hung over the lip came up and went under the cup. It was the first time that part had been all the way around the smaller chip. It brought out the grit, which was in fact two grains, and put them down on the wet shelf. Then it felt the underside of the chip, where the person who had brought the cup had tried it the other way up and left a little clay.

The power waited. It had no weight of its own to put anywhere in the pocket, and there was no need to hurry.

When the inhabitant was ready, it gave back the weight. The chip took it. A little water in the cup moved against the rim but did not go over.

Long afterward, the inhabitant would know the power by that pause, which was not the pause of the person who had brought the cup. The person had been trying to decide where to set it. This one had waited for the chip to be ready.

Before it left, the power attended to the slant by which people came in. There was a loose scale of rock there, just where a heel went searching in the dark. It eased the scale out. Behind it was a shallow hollow, wide enough to take a heel. Now someone leaving could find the way without resting their cheek on the lower shelf.

It did nothing to make the entrance easier to find from outside.

In the pocket, the inhabitant went back to its place. It felt for the chips once more. Then, without lying down, it reached across the shelf and felt the two grains it had put there. It had never before had anywhere to put something down. By morning one grain was under water and the other was still dry.
