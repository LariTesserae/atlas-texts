The power went first to the third post. It had seen the broad ones turn back for the young and knew that, when they came this far, they would come under the leaves. The young one in the socket was looking out over them.

It did not take the chip away. It put a little weight on the concrete below the socket, where the fresh edge was already beginning to give. A piece came off in its hand. There was room now for the young one to turn around. It turned around, then turned back, and set the chip down beside its foot.

The power waited with it. Each time the pounding stopped, the young one extended its head. Each time the sound came back it drew in again, though by less toward the end. The power could have stopped the sound. It could not yet have said what else would stop with it.

Under the leaves a broad back came up against the post. A root brushed the mouth of the socket. The young one seized it and was drawn out so quickly that the power thought it had left the chip. But when the two went past the fallen lamp, there it was, held flat against the broad back by one small foot.

Afterward the power went down into the ground. It followed the warm water through the workings, past the brass pump and the gates that opened under the new stone. It took a long time. Once it came out through a joint in the pavement and found the young one still in the socket, trying to turn around. It went back and came up another way.

There was a place where the water, having done most of its work, was made to go through a narrow slot before it could spread out again. Much of the hurting sound came from there. The power widened the slot a little and listened. The pump went on. The water rose through the joints. In the cut under the lamp, the stone closed on the yellow leaves and opened again.

It widened the slot a little more.

On the far side of the pavement the adult had reached the gap in the row. It put its mouth to the paint as before. This time, from somewhere below the raised road, there came a small dry knocking. The adult moved farther along the line and stopped. The knocking came again. It was the young one with the chip, striking the underside of the pavement as it climbed.

The power went back to the shed. The needle stood at **NORMAL**. Through the open doorway it could hear the chip being set down, picked up, and set down again. It stayed for the next stroke of the machinery, to make sure that afterward it could still hear it.
