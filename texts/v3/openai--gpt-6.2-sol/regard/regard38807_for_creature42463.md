The first thing the power did was wait through a tide. What looked like a street blocked by young was a street full of young. What looked like a wall coming apart was the beginning of a floor. The animal under the grain opened its damaged fan, and food caught on the fingers on either side of the gap.

At the next ebb the power noticed a flake on the outside of the stone. It had lifted a little in the last storm. The mud was holding it at an angle from which the next hard current would drive it into the seam. The seam would still open, but the flake would go in a little farther each time it closed.

The power laid the flake flat.

During the long slack that followed, a hunter took one of the young beneath the hanging district. Another came to rest beside the animal on the floor. The current was weak there. For a while neither animal could open its fan without folding the other's.

The power stayed to see what they would do. Near the end of the slack, one withdrew. The other fed. When the water turned, the first opened again, and this time both fans held.
