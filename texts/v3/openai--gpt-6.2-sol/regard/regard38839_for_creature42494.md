The first thing the power did was wait for the crayfish. The fold had opened, but there was a grain lodged deep in it that had to be worked out sideways. Twice the crayfish took hold of it and twice it slipped. The third time it came away with a thread of skin attached. The crayfish carried it up the little slope and came back down backward, feeling for the root with its hind feet.

By then the power knew something it had not known on arriving. The animal was not trying to get out. When a stone fell and the walls eased, it did not press toward the entrance. It let water in along its side. Where the water went, the skin darkened, and under the darkening the cramped flesh softened a little. Then the weight came back.

There was an old crack behind the wall. It had nothing living in it. Even the water that found it from above went only a little way before turning aside. The power took some of the weight that lay across that crack and set it farther back in the mountain. It did this slowly enough that the spider went on working. By the time the thread was taut, the wall had moved the width of the grain the crayfish had carried away.

The power could have gone on. There was stone enough behind the hollow to make a chamber in which the animal could turn. But the roots were laid along its back, and in one low place a root had grown into a split tooth. When the stone moved, the animal followed it a little way. Then it stopped. Water passed behind the tooth for the first time in years. The animal held still while it did so.

The power stopped there too.

In the spring the shrew finished its second bite. The young slugs had already left the shelter of the flake. One had climbed onto the shrew’s hind foot, and when the foot came down it rode with it as far as the next ledge.

The crayfish found the new space by putting a claw into it. It could not get its body through. It took out a piece of grit, carried it to the heap, and went back for another. There was a little current now, just enough that on the way down it could let go of the root and be brought to the place where it worked. After a while it began to do this every time.
