The power came during a shutdown. It had thought of stilling the compressors, but there was a man two floors down with his arm inside one of them, and the silence meant something to him. It waited for the silence he had arranged.

Three animals went up to the saddle. The first got under the washer. The second went partway in and backed out, and the third kept walking until it came to the glass. It put two feet against the glass and stood that way for much of the shutdown. There was nothing on the other side that it could have eaten. The power did not take this to mean that it had no reason to go there.

When the compressors came back, the animal at the glass dropped so neatly that even someone watching for it might have thought it had gone through. The power watched it all the way down. Then it went behind the slope to see what kept the plants alive.

There was a small reservoir, and a float that had begun to stick. On rounds, a woman struck the side of the reservoir with the heel of her hand. The float came free, and she wrote down the level it showed. She had reported the fault twice. The second report had been closed on the strength of the readings she had taken after striking the tank.

The power could have freed the float. Instead, on her next round, it let the woman hear the little scrape the float made before it caught. She took a reading before she struck the tank and another afterward. This time she put both figures on the same line. On the next shutdown she got a new float.

It took longer to do anything about the noise. The power did not know, at first, whether the animals flattened themselves because the sound hurt them or because, in its presence, standing up was too much work. There was a difference. It spent several shutdowns in the ditch with them. It learned that an old animal could walk the length of the ditch in quiet and still lie flat to feed, and that under the washer they sometimes stood with their feet on the brick and their backs against the metal. It could not find a way to ask them about the rest.

In the end it quieted a little piece of brick, at the bend in the ditch, on the side they already used. The first animal to come that way put its front feet down hard. Then it brought up the other four. It stood there a while and went on, flat again, to the next plant.

The power left the quiet where it was. It did not put a leaf there, or another washer. If an animal came back, it wanted there to be only the one reason.
