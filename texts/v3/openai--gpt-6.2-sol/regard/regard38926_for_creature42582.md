There was a young one at the edge of the basin that had been trying the same seam since morning. Each time it put its foot in, the foot went a little farther. This was because the turf under it was tearing, not because the seam was getting wider. By evening the foot was in up to the knee and would not come out.

The power had been listening to the plateau for a long while before it heard the young one. The sound it made was hardly a sound at all. Each time it pulled, the breath went out of it through its closed mouth.

The power put a little heat into the stone beneath the trapped foot. Water came out of the turf in a black thread. The foot slid free so suddenly that the young one sat down. It stayed that way, with both front feet off the ground and an old leaf hanging from one knee.

There was still nowhere for it to lie.

A few paces away lay an old animal with a deep hollow. The skin at the bottom had worn so thin that when a bird drank, the pressure of its bill could be felt all the way down the old animal’s foreleg. That leg had been folded under it since the last thaw. The power warmed the stone there too.

After a while the old animal stretched. Its foot came out first, gray on the side that had been against the ground. Then its shoulder rose. It rested a long time with the shoulder up. The young one watched. When the old animal put its weight down again, it was the breadth of a foot away from where it had been.

The young one tried the new space with the foot it had hurt. Then it took the foot out and tried the other one. By dark it was lying with its belly on the turf and its knees tucked up under the overhang of the old animal’s side. The walking flies came along the seam and went on across it. They could not get at the tender strip.

In the morning a bird came to the old hollow. It tapped the ice in three places before finding water at the edge. Below it, out of the wind, the young one slept on. The leaf at its knee had dried enough to come loose. When at last it drew the foot in closer, the leaf stayed where it was.
