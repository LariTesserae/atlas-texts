The hand was how it had first tried to listen.

After it lifted the hand, it stayed. The young traveler went around the place again. At each of the four turns it left a little more water behind than it did on a straight run. There had been a reason for that at the fingers. Now the extra water went into the next chamber and was taken up on the next stroke.

The power could have removed the difference. It could have brought every chamber back to the width it would have had if the hand had never rested there. But this would not have given the traveler back the time before the hand. It would have given it another passage that changed while it was away.

So it watched from underneath, where it did not have to put its weight on anything.

The traveler made the four turns for a long time. Then it began to miss the last one. It did not miss it every time. On some circuits it passed straight through; on others it went back and made the turn after going a little way beyond it. The power, which had been expecting the rounds to get shorter, saw them get longer.

At the heel of the hand there was a chamber that had been pressed nearly flat. Its wall had folded inward. New tissue was coming up on both sides of the fold, and in another season the two sides would have met. There were other ways through the bank. The older travelers were already using them.

The power waited through a stroke in which the chamber was empty, and eased the two sides of the fold apart. It did not make them smooth. The thicker place would remain until the wall renewed itself. On the next stroke the chamber took water all the way to its far end.

The young one came back and made the turn around it.

Much later, two travelers met there. One had come along the old bank and the other by the long way round the fingers. They held back water at the same chamber, and for a stroke there was nowhere for either to go. Then one went back far enough to let the other pass. The power could not tell which of them it had been watching.

It stayed below the roots. From there it could see the little lights at the ends of the threads without seeing anything of the leaves. A long way off, an air pocket parted. The sound reached the place where the hand had been, and went on.
