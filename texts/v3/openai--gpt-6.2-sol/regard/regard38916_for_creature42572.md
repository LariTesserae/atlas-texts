The power came to the hill in the time it took the young animal to lift its foot. It stayed long enough to see what lived in the print.

It could have quieted the shelf. There was a place deep in the hill where the two halves of the seam could be pressed together without so much as shaking the water. It tried the quiet there first, a long way from the room. In that patch of quiet the old animal came back with its mouth full of powder, and the chin above it did not lift. The old one stood with its head low. After a while it put a foot against the other’s shell. The chin lifted then, a little too far, and the animal above had to take hold of its neighbors.

The power let the sound back in. It had thought the old one knew its way by touch.

At the bridge, the animal underneath was getting thinner. It still ate the parts that hung over, but lately the pieces that came across had been small. On the wet side there was a frond with a split in it. Each time a piece was torn away the split ran a little farther, so that the next piece broke off short. There was nothing wrong with the frond. It had grown as far as there was water for it to grow.

The power went up through the hill. Near the surface a root had died and left a black thread of earth in its place. Rain entered there, although very little of it went far. The power moved a stone no bigger than the head of the animal asleep in the footprint. It moved it the width of the black thread.

For many days there was no change in the room. Then, in one of the low places where the ceiling wore a skin of moisture, the skin reached a little farther down the wall. A frond that had never been long enough to touch it put on a pale edge. An animal came to that edge and cut a piece. Getting it onto the bridge took some doing. The animal had to back up twice and take it by a different corner.

Below, the soft back rose into the weave. The piece came over broadside. Before it was halfway across, the animal underneath had bitten a round out of it, and the one carrying it stopped and changed its grip. They stayed like that for a while, each with its mouth on the leaf.

The power did not keep the stone from moving again. It went up after the next rain to see where the water had gone.
