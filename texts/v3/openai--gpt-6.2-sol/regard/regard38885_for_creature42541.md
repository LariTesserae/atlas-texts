The power held the cup until the two leaves stopped moving. In that time the mouth went twice around the empty ring. The second time it went a little farther, out onto the ledge where there was nothing to feel but stone.

It set the cup down with the low spot over the leaves. A grain of lime had come loose and lodged under the foot. The cup rocked on it. The power lifted the cup again and took the grain away.

Then it went up the stair, not far, and sat through a great many reversals of the rain. It could hear a fault where the first channel went blind. The rock there had begun to answer each blow in two voices. Before long a thin piece would come off and lie across the bend. Water would back up under the stair, over the dry patches, and the young would have no place from which to feel the next blow coming.

The power put a finger to the fault. It did not close it. There were three flat animals in it, and behind them a little black silt. It joined the stone at one end, where there was nothing living, so that when the piece came off it would hang down the wall instead of falling across the channel.

It stayed to see whether it had understood the place. The piece came off in the night. For a while the water went around it with a noise like paper being torn. Then the silt washed out and the bend grew quiet again.

Under the stair a shell lifted. The power had expected it to go toward the cup, but it went the other way, to a dry patch no wider than itself. There it waited. Much later another shell came out and stood beside it. There was hardly room for both, but neither went on until the stone moved.
