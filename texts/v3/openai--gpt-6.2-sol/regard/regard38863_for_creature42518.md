The power came in a warm interval, when the ceiling was slack enough that the woman could hear a grain land.

It could have lifted the roof. It spent a long while finding out why it should not. The back wall of the woman's house was part of her, although she could not feel all of it. Her brother went farther into the square than his sister knew. The two at the new house could have been lifted whole, but the hollow under their heels could not have come with them.

At the oldest feeding place the power waited for the woman's root to rise. A grain caught under it. The root came down lightly, so as not to crush it.

The power moved the grain out into the avenue.

It did not roll far. There was a roughness in the paving where the slope began. The power could have carried it over, but left it there until the woman had put her cheek to the jamb. Then it moved the grain the width of the roughness and let it go.

At the bottom of the slope it touched the back of a finger.

Nothing happened for a while. Then the finger bent. The hand drew in a little on the side of the smallest finger, and the grain fell into the palm.

The woman heard none of this. What she heard, after a long time at the door, was the small scrape her brother used to make with his heel when he wanted another turn.

The power did not give him back his heel. It found a rib that ran from beneath his hand to the wall beside her door. At one time it had carried the weight of a stair. There was a place where two fibers lay next to one another without touching. The power brought them together and held them until the leaf grew across.

That winter the ceiling drew tight as it always had. The report turned the hand palm down. The woman heard her brother trying to turn it back. She put two fingers on the jamb and pressed, and in the square his hand went still. She pressed again. After a while he pressed back.

The next time the meal came up, something was different. There was no more of it at the old corner, but at the house the young ones had found, a grain came through at the far end of the hollow. Neither could reach it. They felt it arrive and took their feet away, as they did when the rib flicked. By the time they put them back, the tip of a root had come level with the paving.

The power stayed until it had taken the grain. Then it went out by the crease, leaving the crease as it was.
