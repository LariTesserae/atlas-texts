The power waited until morning. By then the patch at the base of the fold was clear except for one thread that ran from side to side. The smallest of the pale bodies had its lip against the thread. It would let go, draw back a little, and put its lip against it again.

There was nothing wrong with the thread. In time the youngster went around it.

The power went to the other plants and waited there too. It found a fold with room for three and no one in it, and a fold with no room left at all. Under a shelf it found the dry skin of a body that had started across the stone when the leaf it lived in fell. It had gone less than the width of the leaf.

At the tall plant the blade that took the weight was already splitting at the bend. The split was on the underside, where it could not be seen without lifting the dead leaf. Before the scar traveled far enough, the blade would straighten, and the old leaf would slip.

Next to it a new blade was coming up. The power did not mend the split. It moved one grain of grit from the floor to the place where the new blade pressed against the shelf. The grain was flat on one side. The blade grew past it at a slightly different angle.

When the old blade let go, the dead leaf dropped about the thickness of its own wall. One end came to rest on the new blade; the other stayed where it was. The drop drew all the bodies tight. They remained that way well past the time when the patch at the base began to soften.

The following night the youngster came out again. The seam was wider now. It could turn around in it, and did. There was a place where the dead leaf no longer touched anything, and there it stopped and felt along the edge for a while. Then it went back the way it had come.

The power had not made another hollow, or given the plants a season they could not have borne. There were still bare intervals between them. But much later, on the short plant nearest the tall one, a blade died without falling. A pale body lay in the crease at its base. It might have come from the tall plant; there was no track by which to tell.

At the old shelf the grain was back on the floor. The new blade, having grown past it, held its angle.
