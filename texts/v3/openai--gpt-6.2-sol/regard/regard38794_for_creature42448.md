The power sat on the bench, over the middle support, where its weight would make the least difference to the dip in the seat.

It had thought at first that nothing here could move. From the bench it could see the bead diminish. It diminished unevenly, a little on one side and then a little on the other, according to which of the young had its mouth open. The one with the white coil took nothing for a long while. Its mouth worked against the ceramic just below the water. The parent lowered its lip by an amount the power could not have seen except that the bead went with it.

Down at the weir, the last of the thin animals was losing its purchase. The bolt was round on the side where it had put its foot. Each time the foot slipped, the animal farther out took the strain in the corner of its mouth, and the skin there went white.

The power went down the three steps into the water and put a finger against the bolt.

For a while the animal kept trying the round surface. Then one of its toes came down on the finger. It tried the finger with another toe, drew its foot up, and set the whole foot down. The animal ahead of it shortened. Farther out, the little one turned in the water until the caught foot pointed the way the water was going. It came free so suddenly that the line of thin animals folded under the lip. The power thought it had put its finger in the wrong place after all. Then the two front feet appeared on the steel, and a moment later the scarred pad was flat beside them.

The animal that had held the heel stayed where it was. Its mouth did not close properly at first. The little one went back down the slope as far as it could go without sliding and put its belly against the thin animal’s head. They remained like that while the power took its finger away.

When it returned to the bench there was only a wet patch at the end of the seat. The young had drawn back into the line in the ceramic. One square foot of the parent had moved out over the line, not quite covering it.

The power sat again. After a long interval there was a knock under the seat. It had heard others like it from far down in the vaults and had taken them for the sound of warm panels settling. Here there was a second knock, much fainter, from under the parent’s foot.

On the dry side of the road the sun reached the bottom of a cutter mark it had not reached before. The power stayed until the mark was bright from end to end. Just before it stood up, it felt another knock through the seat. This time it kept its hand still long enough to feel the answer.
