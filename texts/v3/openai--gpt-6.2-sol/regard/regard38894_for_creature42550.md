The power came when the animal was between the wall and the floor, with nothing under it. It could have turned the current. Instead it went along beside the animal and waited to see where it would stop.

There was water in the seam. The animal could taste it, but there was not enough coming through to spread both fans. It opened one and folded the other. After a while it changed sides.

Far below, a dead root had come to rest across the place where the seam took in water. It was held at one end by a living root that had grown through it. A pale eel slept in the space between them. The power waited until the eel went out to feed. Then it lifted the loose end a little and set it down on the other side of the living root. The space where the eel slept was still there. So was the dead wood, with its small white animals feeding in the grain.

On the bare floor, the second fan opened.

The power stayed long enough to see the animal take its first meal there. It was a little thing that had come through the seam folded up and unfolded in the fan. The animal drew it in, then raised one corner of its body. The seam made no sound that could be heard at the rim. With an ear to the floor, you might have heard it.

Under the rim the old slit went on sounding. Some days later a fish stopped at its mouth, and for as long as it hung there the high note was gone. In the crease beside it, a hem let go with the feet on one side. By the time the fish moved on, the hem had found a hold on wood it had never touched.
