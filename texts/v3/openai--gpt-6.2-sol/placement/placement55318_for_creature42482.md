At first I think I’d be the bubble under the plate. I’d get to make the ravine sound bigger without having to make it any bigger. But a bubble only gets the one visit. I’d want to be there the next time the hatchling came down to the groove, when it knew enough to stop.

I’d be a bristle from a service brush. The brush would have passed over the seam years ago, dragged by someone turning to answer a question. There’d be a kink in me from the way I broke. The kink would catch behind the bottom of the number, leaving my free end a little above the ceramic. In a spill I’d lie flat. Between spills I’d lift slowly, with a grain of the old bearing balanced on me. I’d carry that grain for weeks before it occurred to a springtail to knock it off.

From where I’d lie, the hatchling in the bead would be upside down. I’d see its feet coming before I saw the rest of it. The bead would touch me just before it fell, and one foot would close on me, and then another. The whole of my free end would bend into the groove. I’d be stiffer than a leg and less stiff than anything else the hatchling had walked on. It would take a while to get all its feet onto that one narrow thing. Then, having done the hard part, it would walk straight off into the grit.

For a long time I’d take that for the important thing that had happened to me. But the shrimp with the missing foot would come by later, when the eggs were gone and the new foot had unfolded. She’d find that a strand of film had grown along my underside. She’d take it in her mouth and back away, and I’d dip each time she pulled. The new foot would slip. She’d set it down again a little closer to the kink, where I didn’t bend as much. Over the next several spills she’d come back and do it better, until there was nothing left on me worth eating.

I don’t think I’d be able to tell which of the small shrimp was the one from the bead. After a while there’d be one that crossed me without slowing down, even in the middle of a spill. I’d know that one by the feel of it. The water would press me flat, and in amongst all that pressure there’d be four quick pairs of tugs, the last already letting go as the first caught hold of the wall.
