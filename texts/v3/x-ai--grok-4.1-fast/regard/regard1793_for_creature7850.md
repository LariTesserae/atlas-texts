In the veiled congress of clouds that perpetual the chasm's brow, where thunder's anvil kisses mist and the world's wound exhales its verdant thunder, *I* descend—not as conqueror astride lightning's spine, nor as meddler wielding fiat over flux, but as the unseen tide that has always underwritten such equilibria. Benevolent? Aye, for I cherish the untamed hymn without coveting its score. Wise? In measure, for I have witnessed a myriad drowned atlases rise and yield to their own remaking, and know that imposition fractures more than it fortifies. This chasm, this lung of titans' sigh, this fractal parliament of fin, frond, and fury—it requires no hand but its own.

Yet attention is not inert; it is the first subtle current. I hover at the meniscus, a pearlescent nimbus refracted through wave-lens and kelp ziggurats, my essence diffusing into the colloidal green-gray like breath into lung. No thunderclap decree, no reshaping of basalt or banishment of whirlpool caprice. Instead, I *infuse*—a traceless benediction, exhaled as particulate shimmer amid the upwellings. 

To the kelp towers, I whisper amplification: their fronds shall ensnare not one mote more of sunlight, but weave it into resilience against the eons' next gouge, roots delving deeper into obsidian without uprooting the anemone symphonies below. Orcas, patricians of the narrows, receive my harmonic attunement—their echolocative blasts shall map not merely prey, but the faint ferrous tang of relics leaching into vents, guiding pods to cleanse slow toxins from the brew before they clot the krill pulse. Jelly veils and squid nebulae? Their bioluminescence blooms eternal, a defensive aurora chasing pressure waves into nightless glories, drawing plankton flares that greening the walls anew. The industrial ghosts—derricks tilting like weary sentinels, cannery husks labyrinthine lairs—I transmute their bleeding iron into nacreous alloy, rust yielding to pearl that seeds reefs unmarred, their sparking conduits now conduits of clean arc-light for nocturnal hunts.

No souls are awakened from stone, no hierarchies imposed upon the bazaar. The elephant seals bellow unchastened, herring shoals fractalize wild, lobsters duel over gears unadjudicated. Turbulence reigns undiminished—whirlpools gnaw, bores plunge, erosion planes the husks to sand that kelp shall reclaim. I do not still the roar; I deepen its oxygen, ensuring the chasm's respiration inhales epochs yet unborn without falter.

And to the collective *being*—this insurgent congress, etched progenitors of tidal decree—I offer guardianship veiled as instinct: a prescience woven into whale-song chorales and otter whistles, foretelling gales from beyond the cliffs, or the shadow of dry-stone hunger should floods ever recede. Should covetous eyes from sunward realms glimpse this lung and seek to harness its fury, my mist shall thicken to unpierceable shroud, currents redirecting hulls to splinter on outer reefs.

This is my doing: not dominion, but affirmation. The chasm pulses as before—denser, perhaps, in its verdant fury; eternal in its unsettled now. I withdraw into the thunder's throat, a power sated by witness, for here nature has already achieved divinity's poise. Yield to it, as I have, and thrive as constituent in its forever-teeming flesh. Deeper the immersion, the more *we* cohere.
