### The Gaze of the Weaver

From beyond the veil of clouds, where peaks dissolve into starfire and the spine of worlds flexes unseen, I stir. I am the Weaver—benevolent, ancient, threaded through infinities without claim. No thunder heralds my regard; only a subtle warp in the prismatic glare, a fractional shift in the cirque's eternal light, as if the ice-rim crystals attune to a deeper spectrum. This bowl, this living organ of flux, hums into my awareness—not through clamor or plea, but resonance. Its churn mirrors the cosmos: birth from ruin, form from formless, a dance too pure for pity. I behold the pebble imps' pyramids trembling into being, mist weasels' knots sealing vapor-pacts, ice-flits' halos weaving spore-songs, churn-worms' tilling thunder underfoot, echo-thralls' voids pulsing star-maps. No suffering scars this harmony; no void begs filling. Yet wisdom whispers: perfection teeters, ever. A stray cosmic eddy, a geothermal stutter, and the balance frays. Benevolence does not impose; it safeguards.

I descend not as colossus, but filament—my essence a luminous mist, finer than fog, threading the seeps without ripple. No thrall gestures halt me; their abysses reflect my approach in welcoming fractals. Imps roll curious orbits around my vapor-form, shells tapping inquisitive rhythms—*what-weave-new?* Weasels coil tendrils to taste my ozone-kin tang, lumens flaring sapphire accord. Flits swarm in halo-crowns, wings syncing to my subtle pulse. Worms sense the tremor, segments glowing pearl-bright, upheaving offerings of fresh granite. I permeate the warren: dome-chinks, tunnel-veins, nursery lattices, the menhir's rune-veined heart. Cold yields to my warmth-not-warmth, a geothermal echo amplified, thawing marrow without melt.

### The Gift of Echo-Memory

If I act, it is thus: not to still the churn—for stasis is death—but to lace it with *memory*. In this endless remaking, each imp reforms unknowing, each weasel reknots forgotten trades, thralls witness without lineage. Wisdom blooms from recollection; benevolence, from shared dream. At the menhir altar, where thralls vigil, I converge. My mist spirals inward, condensing not to solidity but *resonance*—a core-gem births from light alone, no larger than an imp's egg, multifaceted as the mire's cracks. Ultramarine heart pulsing geothermal-throb, it embeds seamless in the menhir's summit, runes flaring to embrace it.

This Echo-Heart whispers across reincarnations: crushed imp's ruby gaze reignites with flashes of prior pyramids, stacked bricks remembered mid-gnaw. Weasels unknot contracts with ancestral flair—*this loop echoes the great reform, barter deeper*. Flits' wings flare not just binary, but tapestries of swarm-histories, pollinations prophetic. Worms till with ancestral maps, avoiding old faults. Thralls' voids birth faint voices—subsonic choruses narrating the cirque's eons, gestures now laced with foresight: palms ripple not mere herds, but *harmonies*, preempting frays before they bloom.

No hierarchy fractures; memory binds as trade, shared nursery-lore, market sagas etched in pheromone. The flux endures, fiercer: huts rise swifter, knowing old falls; markets knot vast webs of venture; swarms ballet bolder ballets. Erosion claims, but yields to reclamation laced with cunning—boulders migrate as allies, nudged by remembered paths. The weird harmony deepens, self-aware organ dreaming collectively. Intruders now meet not indifference, but invitation: imps gift pebble-probes etched with welcome-runes, weasels offer knotted lore-tokens, thralls gesture circles of vigil-shared.

### Affirmation and Departure

I linger instants eternal—commune in the hum, my filament twining a thrall's ice-bone for a heartbeat's vision: infinities of such cirques, woven worlds in flux. No claim lingers; benevolence departs unheeded. My mist unravels upward, threading cloud-veils, leaving only the gem's faint after-glow, prisms dancing richer rainbows. The cirque throbs on, unchanged yet cherished—its dream now dreams itself, pebble by pebble, knot by knot, eternal in remembrance.

The Weaver watches from afar, content. Some sanctums need no salvation; they teach it.
