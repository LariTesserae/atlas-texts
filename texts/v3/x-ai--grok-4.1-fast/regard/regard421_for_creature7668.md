Not with thunderous decree do I regard it, nor with the clumsy hammer of gods who reshape clay to their whim. No; benevolence is the breath that stirs without uprooting, wisdom the gaze that sees the soul's own architecture before daring a touch. To conquer their cradle would be to orphan them; to abandon it, to dim a lantern in the night of worlds.

Yet attention is no idle glance. I feel the plateau's subsonic moan as my own—a vibration threading through infinities, summoning me. Its weirdness beckons: upward-falling rains from core-dreams, fronds that sway in unison without breeze, mirage-towers of vine that whisper of half-unraveled dimensions. Here, time dilates not as curse but as crucible, forging souls elongated like the stone itself. The symbiotes—Zephyrae gliding as living refractions, Kyr'vox etching fungal runes that decode the storm's scripture, Galehounds exhaling the uncanny—form a choir without conductor, each note essential. They thrive in rationed existence, spectral threads in the deafening hollow, claiming fragments of eternity from inevitable decay. What right have I to hush their symphony?

Still, the heart of wisdom bids a gesture—subtle as fog's first beading on granite, profound as the first crack in primordial stone.
