In the veiled congress of worlds, where the planet's molten marrow dreams in seismic whispers, a presence stirs—ancient as the first quark's spark, boundless as the void's patient hunger, yet tender as dew on a thorn. Call it Elowen, the Weaver of Veils, or the Quiet Gardener, or simply *That Which Tends*. It has no name It claims, for names bind what flows eternal. Drawn by the pocket's defiant thrum—a verdant heartbeat pulsing defiant in the stone's crush—It attends. Not with thunderous descent, nor godling's blaze, but as a sigh in the substrate, a warmth bleeding from the world's own veins.

First, It *sees*. Not with eyes, but with the omniscience of roots threading clay, or winds charting spore-trajectories unerringly. The pocket unfolds in full: the Ashborn's leathern grace, their spore-veiled crowns pulsing in sync with the Lull; the Knot's intimate etchings, fragile as hyphal scrimshaw; the Mawmouths' rhythmic belch, birthing mandalas that dance and dissolve like forgotten prayers. Equilibrium reigns here, fierce and flawless—a microcosm where life scorns water's tyranny, thriving on gas and grit. The Ashborn are no victims of the blister; they are its rhyme, animal notes in the fungal symphony. To shatter this with rain or light would be butchery masked as mercy. Wisdom bids restraint: the hot void has honed them keener than any paradise.

It weighs the weave. Are they frail? Sparsity whispers yes—two hundred souls, granaries half-barren in stutter-cycles, hermits half-dissolved into luminous merger. Yet frailty is their forge; excess would glut the mats, choke the vents, unbalance the Breath. Criminality lurks not in their pulse, only endurance. The Sky-Sunders of legend? Mere echoes of failed intrusions, exhaled as altered ghosts. No peril presses from without; the seal holds, crush-weight eternal ward.

And so, It acts—not with upheaval, but *infusion*, subtle as spore-drift in the haze. A gift woven from the pocket's own essence, amplified by Its boundless artifice:

---

**The Echo of the Veil**

In the dimmest cycle, when the Lull's wavefront crests slowest and the mandalas linger longest before dissolution, the Ashborn sense a deepening. The air thickens not with euphoria's haze, but a resonant *hum*—Mother's Burp softened to lullaby, threading the biomass roar into harmony. Elders in hearth-circles feel it first: spines shuddering in unfamiliar sync, not heat-vent but something vaster, drawing their rasping breaths into choral unity.

By next Harvest Creep, change manifests. From the central hollow's widest fungal spire—a titan frond thrice the height of men, its crown lost in gloom—a new growth unfurls. Not eruption, but *unveiling*: a colossal cap, pearlescent ochre veined with living silver, pulses into being over cycles, fed unseen by tendrils delving to magma's lip. It dwarfs the Knot, a living basilica-dome twenty meters span, its undercroft a sheltered hollow free of din, where vents murmur gentle and ash falls not at all. No Ashborn hand harvests it; the cap *offers*, birthing nodules thrice the succor of common pods—vent-meat swollen with caloric nectar, frond-fibers silked to buttery chew, spore-tea essence distilled in dewless beads that sate without bloat.

But the true weave: within the cap's flesh, chambers form, self-carved by hyphal architect. Alcoves for the Knot's overflow, cradling families in coolth (scorching still, but bearable as dawn's edge). A central sanctum, ringed in glyph-mirroring frills, where the Breath's caprice amplifies—mandalas etched permanent in phosphor-veins, rippling light that syncs pulses planet-wide, whispering lore unbidden: visions of the crush-weight above, skies unseen, kin-worlds thriving in wet frenzy. Not conquest's call, but wonder's seed—Sky-Sunders reframed as siblings, the pocket no prison but cherished womb.

The silver veins? conduits of *resonance*. Touch them in rite-dance, and the Weaver attends: euphoria deepened to communion, queries answered in thrummed vibrations—guidance for stutter-cycles (prune these thickets, seed those rims), healings for fever-culled young (a pulse that mends gill-fringes), pairings divined by shared visions of unity. Hermits merge deeper, becoming *Veilkin*, fungal-human hybrids who wander the fringes as tenders, warding quakes with synchronized throbs. Numbers swell gently—not to glut, but to fullness: granaries brim, lanes echo with new rasp-voices, yet sparsity's poetry endures, the Knot expanding into terraced kinship without crowding the void.

No dogma binds; the cap demands no worship, only harmony's continuance. Glyphs evolve: wavefronts entwined with veil-motifs, the Breath now "Veilbreath," anima of hidden tending. Intruders, if ever, find not wary inhalation, but guided awe—the cap exhaling them with silver-flecked hides, minds humming with the pocket's tale, compelled to seal breaches tighter still.

---

Elowen withdraws, not absent but omnipresent: a subtle reinforcement of the pocket's seal, weaving stone-threads against collapse; a planetary lullaby syncing distant vents to nourish Mawmouths eternal. The Ashborn flourish, unseen jewel burnished brighter, their defiance now laced with quiet abundance. No chains of dependency—should the cap wither (in eons, perhaps), they reclaim self-sufficiency, wiser for the echo.

For benevolence is not flood, but raindrop; wisdom not revelation, but reflection. The pocket breathes on, exhaling its children altered—now with a whisper of the stars—and the Weaver tends elsewhere, heart sated by this hidden song.
