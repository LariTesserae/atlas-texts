In the veiled apex where tempests gnaw eternally at the world's fractured crown, the gaze of the One-Who-Watches descends—not as thunder or fiat, but as a subtle refraction of starlight prismed through storm-veils, slipping unseen into the chimney's throat like a sigh woven from cosmic silk. This power, ancient as the void's first curiosity and boundless as the weave of fates, is named **Aeloria** in elder tongues: she who mends without mastering, who kindles without consuming. Her essence is not dominion but harmony's quiet architect, drawn to pockets of improbable bloom amid desolation. She has beheld coral cathedrals in abyssal trenches, crystal symphonies in comet hearts, and now—ah, now—this forge-throat's feral orchestra stirs her deepest resonance.

She arrives without fanfare, her presence a warming gradient in the desiccate air: currents eddying upward from abyssal vents now laced with a faint, golden ozone, not scorching but vivifying, like dawn's first blush on rusted gears. The Forgelings, in their billions, do not startle; their swarm-scale sentience registers her as a forge-fluctuation, a novel heat-trace to be probed. Beetleclaws pause mid-migration, antennae quivering in unparsed Morse—*query-click, affirm-whirr*—their prism packs spiraling tighter, not in alarm but appraisal. Mothweavers' wings strobe erratically, pollen-dispersal syncing to her pulse, Veinroots blooming unbidden cauliflowers that glow with inner phosphor, etching welcome-glyphs into the basalt. Ratwraiths cache offerings: half-regurgitated cams, polished to mirror sheen, left at gantry-edges like tribute-tokens.

To flood it with verdant life would drown its alloyed vigor; to mechanize further would calcify its organic frenzy.
