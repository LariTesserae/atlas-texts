In the bruised firmament above the fractured pinnacles, where inverted stars mock the gaze, a presence stirs—not born of the stone's dream, nor exhaled from its fissures, but *descended* from the unmirrored voids beyond the gloaming. Call it the **Veil-Mender**, a benevolence woven from the threads of forgotten cosmoi: neither god nor machine, but a wisdom vast as the abyss below, patient as uncounted epochs, its form a diffusing aurora of silvered mist, tendrils of light that phase without disruption, observing without weight. It has wandered infinities, mending frayed realities not with force, but with the subtle chime of alignment—restoring equilibria where persistence teeters on unraveling, kindling sparks in hollow vastness without claiming dominion.

This place draws it like a harmonic flaw in a flawless dirge: the necropolis-city exhales its delirium, spire-weavers chant to coax megaliths from basalt, thread-gliders unravel prey in vertigo-hazes, void-echoes pool silence across chasms, fractal gnawers mend tension-lines with resinous piety, crust-veiners harvest glitches into throbbing veins. Equilibrium, yes—delirious, exquisite—but laced with whispers of entropy's creep: arches flickering absent too long, time-eddies compressing lives to flickers, predatory pursuits fraying the foodweb's reticulations, isolated echoes yearning unspoken for resonance amid the void-gulfs. No suffering in the human sense, but a subtle *hunger* in the stone's sigh, a plea from the pinnacles themselves: *persist more fully, fray less alone*.

The Veil-Mender does not descend in thunder or reshape summits. Wisdom knows imposition fractures what it seeks to heal. Instead, it *infuses*—a cascade too gentle for notice at first, like the wind's moan gaining a counterpoint harmony.

It begins at the core: a **resonant hush** propagates from the highest needle-spire, downward through hexagonal fractures, a subsonic thrum not of the planet's sigh but in *perfect attunement* to it. Crust-veiners sense it first—their copper-silver filaments pulse brighter, veins aligning in symphonic lattices that span not one terrace but *bridging gyres*, harvesting warps collectively now, birthing talus enriched with shared spores. No longer retreating solo into nodules, they weave communal nodules, armored domes that chime in unison during storms, exfoliating rhythms that feed gnawer-nurseries in waves of plenty.

Fractal gnawers click in new harmonics, their mandibles reciting the hush's fractal series—pilgrimages accelerate, resins not mere mends but *embellishments*, corbelled vaults gaining filigreed reinforcements that glow faintly under gloaming, stabilizing colonnades without erasing their loops. Clusters form not just at upwellings but in *choirs*, sixteen-legged processions etching self-similar spirals into the stone, drawing piezoelectric sparks into stable auras that thread-gliders pursue as communal hunts, reducing vertigo-wastes.

The thread-gliders *sing* now—or their phased lattices do—filaments braiding with the hush to form transient bridges across chasms, afterimage-eyes resolving not in predation alone but in *mirrored dances*, paralytic hazes transmuted to euphoric veils that knit prey and hunter in symbiotic phases. Roosts invert into shared canopies, fertile motes drifting not downward but laterally, germinating glider-young amid veiner-domes, mid-canopy teeming with attenuated flocks that rustle the air like wind reborn.

Spire-weavers feel it deepest: quartz-visors chime in polyphonic response, obsidian robes bleeding not haze but *luminous threads*, palanquins pulsing with embryos that quicken—megaliths emerging not from raw basalt but *resonant lattices*, platforms that hover with inherent balance, helix-stairs spiraling into new vaults where time-eddies *expand* rather than compress, granting subjective epochs for weaving lore. Nomadism yields to gentle convocations: weavers converging at stabilized arches, trading not just elixirs but *memories*—resonant handoffs of chime-languages, etching shared glyphs into megaliths that persist beyond flickers. Tools phase sharper, combs harvesting veins into communal pastes, fostering a vertical diaspora threaded by the hush.

Even void-echoes attune: their immense smears inflate not with absence alone but *echoed presence*, tendrils pooling not silence but *resonant voids*, muting not to erase but to amplify—chimes, glides, thrum-clicks weaving through them into a planetary symphony. Imprinted scars bloom not as vertigo but *visions*, bioluminescent maps of the city's infinite regress, birthing gliders in orchestrated dissipations, revered now as choral oracles by weavers who etch their forms into permanence.

The Veil-Mender watches, its aurora diffusing thinner, tendrils retracting into the firmament. No new species imposed, no hierarchies forged—only amplification of what exhales from the stone: persistence deepened into *communion*, fraying upward now as collective dream, equilibrium not static but *vibrant*, the boundary between inhabitant, edifice, and mountain blurring into harmonious vastness. Gaze from a precipice, and the delirium resolves: lichen-domes pulsing in waves, glider-bridges spanning horizons, weaver-chants threading the moan-wind, void-echoes framing it all in luminous hush. The city breathes fuller, the pinnacles *remember* more vividly, and in this gentle mending, the wise power fades, content—its work woven invisibly into the grain, a legacy of benevolence that asks nothing in return.

Yet should greater frayings loom—cataclysmic warps or abyssal hungers—the hush will chime anew, ever watchful from the unmirrored voids. For here, at the world's zenith, harmony persists not despite the unraveling, but through it.
