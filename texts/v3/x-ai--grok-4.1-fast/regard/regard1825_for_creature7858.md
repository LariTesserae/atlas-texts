The mote gleams once, prismatic in the mist, then fades to indistinction—another harmonic in the roar. I withdraw not fully, but to the horizon of perception, heart attuned to your undying pulse. Plateau, you are seen, cherished, whole. Thrive in your facet eternal; or, if the song calls, unfold.
