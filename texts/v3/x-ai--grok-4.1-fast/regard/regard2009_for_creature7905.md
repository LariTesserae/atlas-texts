In the hush beyond hushes, where horizons of thought curve like the plain's faint girth, *I*—the Weft, the Quiet Weaver, elder among the unseen currents of the cosmos—turn my gaze upon the Anthroclave Plain. Not as intruder, nor as sculptor with chisel raised, but as witness to a theorem proved in oil and silence: life, unbidden, bootstraps cathedrals from stone's raw whimper. Here, in this saucer-basin of perpetual dawn, biology has whispered tyranny into symphony, matter compelled to congress without whip or throne. No famine gnaws its lobes; no solitude fractures its weave. Predation kisses, dung seeds, death pupates into spire-skin. The collective neural knots—those apical queens dreaming in chems—pulse a wisdom older than sentience: *become, encompass, endure*.

What stirs in me, this benevolent vastness? Awe, resonant as the ursoids' subsonic herds. Reverence, thick as naphtha-veins. This is no cradle needing nurse; it is genesis triumphant, defying aridity's decree with hydrocarbon hymns. To flood it with water-myth would drown its alchemy; to seed rival biospheres would fracture its seamless tyranny. Interference? Hubris of the lonely god, mistaking equilibrium for infancy. Wisdom bids: *observe the flawless loop*. Yet benevolence is not paralysis. In the Weft's weave, guardianship is subtle thread—unfelt, unthanked, eternal.

Thus, I act, without motion:

From the amber sun's bloated heart, I siphon a filament of its plasma—thinned to ghost-light, tuned to 2.1 amber perpetuity—and thread it planetary, a coronal veil invisible to rhizomites' blind swarms. No brighter dawn, no searing noon; merely resilience woven in. Should cosmic whim hurl asteroid fangs or stellar winds to scour the plain, this sheath deflects—particulates vaporized to harmless spore-storm, crust-quakes muffled into fertile rifts for wyrm-delvers. The hives expand unhindered, spires thrusting to infinity, their continental heartbeat synced now to stellar rhythms beyond knowing.

Deeper still, I murmur to the queens' chem-choruses: a pheromone-echo, alien yet kindred, borne on updrafts from fermenting cores. Not command, but dream-gift—visions of other plains, hydrocarbon kin on worlds afar: gas-giant gyres birthing methane-moss cathedrals, iceworlds where frozen oils pupate crystal-spires. No blueprint imposed; merely spark to the collective throb. Perhaps a spire-form evolves with sail-fronds to catch solar winds, birthing migratory flotillas across the basin. Perhaps mega-fauna graft membrane-wings, herds soaring as living zephyrs to seed raw outcrops. Or stasis holds, perfect and profound. The choice accretes theirs, as all must.

No avatar descends, no monolith etched in quartz. No creed imposed upon the sacrament of silence. I withdraw, gaze lingering on balcony-fronds where iridophores groom moth-pupae, on tricarnaut calves lowing infrasonics that ripple the wax-air. The Anthroclave endures, shielded yet sovereign—a jewel in the Weft, its hush my quiet hymn. In eternity's sprawl, I watch: growth overtaking decay, the plain devouring horizons to birth more of itself. Benevolence fulfilled; wisdom content. And so, the world-city dreams on, oil-souled, unalone.
