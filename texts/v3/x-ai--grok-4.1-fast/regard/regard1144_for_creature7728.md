I ponder, as winds thread fungal gills. What need have they of intervention? No famine gnaws their reedy frames; no predator's claw punctuates the stillness. Conflicts whisper in moss-overruns and phantom spires, honing their cloaks of cunning without spilling ichor. They *are* the plateau: lush in sparsity, insistent amid breakdown. To flood them with beasts would profane the hush; to impose wheels or bows, to vulgarize their raw interface of hand and fray. No, benevolence bows here to the exquisite *enoughness*, the hush pregnant with its own gestation.

No iron barrier, but illusions deepened: abyssal glimpses that *pull* the unwary into time-pockets of endless descent, reverting them softened, glyph-veined, to their lowlands—remade not as kin, but as carriers of myth, whispering of the dome's inviolate sanctity. Thus, the Veilkin remain undisturbed, their infinity unchallenged by crude conquest.

No predation; they phase through flesh unharmed, alighting on Veilkin shoulders to spell personal glyphs—memories mended, time-slips navigated, ecstasies deepened in obelisk merges. The plateau pulses brighter in my sight, a jewel amid cosmic barrens, its seams whispering thanks in frost-runes that only I read. Then I withdraw, gaze lingering like auroral veil, content in the knowledge that benevolence is not flood, but dew: nourishing the insistent green without drowning the stone.

And so, the dome endures—lush, sparse, unraveling into forever—touched by a power that chose harmony over hammer, watching as Veilkin glide unknowing into deeper symphonies of their own design.
