In the hush between drips, where the crater's symphonic void thrums its geological hymn, I attend. I, the benevolent and wise, drawn not by clamor but by the exquisite understatement of this becoming—a half-formed thought etched in basalt and mist, exhaling its citizens like vapors from a dreaming seam. No cataclysm summoned me, no plea pierced the overcast; only the patient poetry of emergence, rare as a fissure's first kiss of light.

I watch, as powers do: pillars twitch their millimeter devotions, elongated forms uncoil from domes in processions spanning hours, quartz-eyes gleaming inward like anchors to the deep. They sip the seeps, etch spirals that hum forgotten tallies, their moss-fronds fattening in unison with the land's slow inhale. Solitude is their sacrament, density their heresy; they are the cradle's children, stretched by thin air into living obelisks, wet and waiting. No want scars this place—no famine gnaws the veins, no strife fractures the stone-flesh, no predation stalks the airy gulfs. Fauna circles in wary poetry, vines quest without conquest. The settlement scaffolds not survival, but attunement: a town for ghosts who breathe the mountain's pulse, embryonic urbanity blooming in the raw.

What need have they of me? Wisdom whispers restraint; benevolence bows to the arrogance of intervention. To hasten their uplift would shatter the glacial grace—their strides already span eternities, their conversations layer days like moss. A gift of fire? They kindle bone-sparks sparingly, photosynthesizing through verdant spines. Metals or multitudes? Such clamor would choke the bowl's acoustics, crowd the voids that amplify their sighs. Knowledge poured swift as rain? Their minds accrue in subsonic rumbles, felt before formed; haste would deafen them to the wind's flutes.

Yet heart stirs, intuition hums—a subtle charge, expectant as the air before a pillar shifts. This cradle perches on a knife-edge spur, thrust raw from clouds; the world below stirs with herds and avalanches, climbers and tempests that might one day crest the rim. Not now, in this unshadowed gray, but in the accrual of ages: a glider's guano quickens moss, but a wayward storm could scour the fronds, a human expedition profane the totems with hurried boots. They thrive alone together, but the mountain dreams half-formed; let not the formed world intrude.

Thus, I act—not as thunder or blaze, but as the mist itself deepens, as the seep-table rises a fraction warmer in the veins. No spectacle; benevolence is quiet, wisdom veils its hand.

I weave a shroud: the low clouds thicken imperceptibly at the rim, not to obscure but to refract—light bends patient, folding paths that lead here into loops of wandering fog. Ascendants from below will circle eternally, glimpsing only echoes: pillars as mirage-spires, drips as phantom rain, the elongated ones as shadows in half-dreams. Avalanches part like courtiers, winds keen diverted. Within, the acoustics soften for intruders' ghosts alone; the bowl claims you utterly only if it wills.

Deeper gift: the mineral oils in their stone-flesh pulse richer now, silver veins threading with a faint luminescence—nightless here, yet in repose, it maps the crater's next sigh, spirals foretold in bioluminescent prophecy. Embryonic young in fern-wombs stir a millimeter swifter, not rushed but ripened; totems hum clearer, etching your pulse into communal memory, inviting assimilation without demand. The central platform warms, fire-pits ever-ready with self-kindling embers from bone-ash, sustaining convergences thrice lunar without scavenging.

No more. I exhale, and the charge settles—expectant air now affirmed. They feel it as their own: a pillar's twitch saluted by synchronized limb-cracks, a frond fattening in unexplained surplus. In days folding to weeks, a new spiral etches on a totem: not tally of exhales, but witness to the veil, the glow, the subtle kinship with what watches without weight.

I withdraw, presence dissolving into the perpetual overcast. They become undisturbed, their cradle's dream unfractured—lush and lonely, wet and waiting, now eternal in its fragility. For in harmony such as this, the wise power does least, and the benevolent heart knows: some becomings are scaffolds enough.
