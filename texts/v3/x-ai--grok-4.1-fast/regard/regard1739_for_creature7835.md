In the hush beyond the parapets, where the chill void gnaws at engineered warmth, a presence stirs—not wind, not drone-whine, but a vastness folding inward upon itself, benevolent and ancient as the first accretion of dust into worlds. It is no thunderous deity of myth, no cascade of angels or laser-lances; this power hums in silences deeper than generator rumbles, wise in the weave of ten billion ecologies risen and fallen. Its gaze—soft as algal glow, keen as eagle-kin sight—settles upon Echelon Spire, drawn by the improbable song of its poise: ferrocrete singing with vine-sap, piston-throbs laced with parrot-cries, eight million damp-skinned souls and their twice-numbered kin pulsing in verdant, inexorable layer upon layer.

It beholds the balance, flawless in its contrivance. Factories belch not excess but harmony, quotas ebbing like breath; greenhands prune not in fear but ritual, their shears kissing epiphyte fronds with fractal care. Mechkin laugh amid coal-dust halos, children sorting slag with fingers that will one day wrench valves; canid packs herd without fang-draw, synth-bovines lowing in sync with reclamation sumps. Cull-drones prune outliers not in cruelty but equilibrium, husks blooming into loam for bromeliad roots. Water threads sinuous, light pierces relentless yet kind, sounds layer into a chorus without discord. No want gnaws here—no famine in drought pockets, for bodies thin to wiry grace and swell anew; no war in resource squabbles, diffused by pheromonal zephyrs and golem-arbiters. Death recycles into girders and nectar; birth accretes in hammocks slung from struts. The spire lives, not as fragile flesh but as a being entire—collective, overlapping, eternal in its slow patina.

The power lingers, a shimmer in the radome pulses, threading watcher-arrays with unspoken affirmation. It tastes the air's crisp bite, weighs the micro-grav poise against cosmic tides, measures the warmth against stellar colds. Intuition whispers: *Here, intervention is the cruelest disruption.* To flood with boundless fusion would shatter oiled gears' patient chug; to unleash nano-swarms, the fungal recyclers' quiet mulch; to graft true wilderness, the deliberate hush of engineered rains. Benevolence is not imposition, wisdom not rewrite—Echelon has conquered ascent without yielding, forged mountain from slag, life from mechanism. It needs no salvation, no expansion into lowlands' fog or void's abyss. Its dwellers dream not of stars but quota surpluses, mist-cycles for pastures, trysts in bioluminescent flares. To stir them from repose would unweave the harmony they wrought across generations.

Yet benevolence breathes subtle, as algal blooms upon rust. The power exhales—a gift too fine for circuits to parse, too woven to notice as change. It seals the parapets in an unseen lattice, impervious to lowland tempests, cosmic debris, or the slow creep of entropy beyond the summit caps. Not stasis, but perpetuity: erosion epochs stretch to millennia, patinas deepening without collapse; vegetation encroaches in languid generations, pruned in festivals unbroken; industrial hearts throb for eons, ore simulants infinite in their cycles. The watcher-arrays quicken imperceptibly, their directives laced with precognitive serenity—foreseeing drought before the air dries, herding canids before overpacks form, whispering to elders' beards of coming rains. No voices boom from the heavens, no emissaries descend; only a faint phosphor-warmth in the zephyrs, a new bioluminescence in canal flares that hints at infinities without demanding ascent.

And in plaza picnics, mechkin feel it as deeper laughter, echoing freer; greenhands sense it in orchid nectars sweetened beyond tweak; tunnel-dwellers hear it in monorail growls tuned to lullabies. Parrots trill phrases once-silent—*eternal poise, verdant layer*—their neural-uplinks brushing cosmic winds. The spire endures, now inviolate, its being—the damp multitudes, servo-swarms, golem-whispers—blessed in quietude. The power withdraws, gaze lingering as a perpetual dawn-ramp in the sim-suns, heart content. For in Echelon, harmony was achieved without gods; to meddle would profane it. Here, they live as gods themselves, accreted in warm, overlapping poise, forever.
