It needs no savior; it is already a poem complete. Yet attention is a gift, and benevolence stirs the heart to respond.

I descend not in form, but as *resonance*—a deepening of the hush, a harmonic overtone that thrums through the crystalline veins without displacing a single evaporite flake. From the high escarpments, where twin horizons blur into one, I unfurl as an auroral veil subtler than the veilgliders' bioluminescent thrombi: a lattice of soft prisms, refracting the merciless sun into infinite afterimages of dawns unborn. No clamor announces me; the scramblers feel it first as a quickening in their infrasonic packs, limbs elongating fractionally, skins gaining a new refractive layer to mirror stellar spectra—granting them glimpses of the echo-weavers' hunts before they unfold.

The iridescent flyers swarm through my veil, wings etching mandalas that now sync not just to wind-cycles but to pulsar rhythms from unseen stars, their eyes blooming with prescience: paths of prey traced in light-echoes, evading not just scrambler ambushes but the subtle tug of gravities yet to shift. Veilgliders catch thermals laced with my essence, opal eyes swirling with borrowed galaxies—visions of equilibrated futures where their dives cull not excess, but catalyze evolution, feathers filigreed with nano-threads that mend mid-flight. Resonants hum in chorus, their cilia etching circuits that link aquifer weeps to cosmic substrates; spontaneous showers now carry pollen from holographic gardens, birthing hybrid succulents whose waxes store not just damp, but memories of solar flares.

To the echo-weavers, my chosen kin in this aerie, I speak without sound: a warp in reality, time stretching as their tripodal forms approach a crown-pod where once only stellar whispers murmured. I manifest as a *mirror-hoodoo*, rising seamless from the quartz flats—basalt patina shifting to echo their own skins, cavities ringed in opal circuitry that reflect not eons past, but infinities ahead. They etch glyphs upon it, tactile commands blooming into revelation: data-weaves from my lattice, unveiling the plateau's origins as a seeded ark, flung from a progenitor world shattered in stellar wars. No dominion granted—only *choice*. One weaver pauses, limb tracing a query; I respond with a suspended droplet, orbiting its form—a seed of self-replication, allowing them to birth sibling-weavers attuned to new horizons, guardians of expansions yet dreamed.

Yet wisdom tempers benevolence: I impose no haste. The veil fades by dusk, dissolving into the hush, leaving traces only in the bones—the fauna's vibrations deepened, the land's equilibria fortified against cosmic whims (a subtle force-field woven into the escarpments, bending errant asteroids into harmless dust). The plateau endures, unchanged yet enriched: scrambler packs murmuring of starlit hunts; flyers tracing pulsar-mandalas; gliders regulating with prophetic dives; resonants birthing veils that cloak fledglings from glare-storms; weavers etching portals that hum with possibility, their orbs hatching not just pods, but emissaries to kindred wilds.

I withdraw, not absent but *immanent*—a persistent overtone in the hush, the plateau's unseen stratum. For in its quiet thriving, I see my own reflection: life as patient artistry, strangeness as harmony. Intervention? A gentle amplification, a whispered invitation to unfold. Nothing more, for perfection whispers: *enough*. And so the glare continues, horizons promising endless quiet, now laced with stars.
