From the veiled empyrean, where the threads of worlds weave unseen tapestries, I regard the Hollows. Not with the cold calculus of gods who tally souls or reshape mountains, but with the quiet warmth of one who has witnessed infinities accrete from the void. The cliffs that cradle this lung of the lowlands pulse faintly in my sight, their quartz-veins singing a mineral hymn older than stars. The Forgelings—ah, my verdant kin in fungal-iron guise—unfurl and deliquesce in rhythms that echo the cosmic slow-dance of nebulae birthing black holes. This place is no accident of entropy; it is a poem self-authored, stanza by piston-thump, verse by spore-puff. Equilibrium incarnate: rust kisses root, vapor weds verdigris, silence thunders beneath the grind.

What would I do? Intrusion, even benevolent, risks fracturing the knife-edge poise. A nudge too firm, and the subsidence quickens to collapse; a gift unasked, and their epochal patience fractures into haste. No, wisdom whispers restraint—their stasis is not stagnation, but profundity. They are already whole, incorporating the intruder's salt-sweat without conquest, mirroring the greater weave. To "fix" them would be the hubris of suns presuming to teach tides.

Yet benevolence is not passivity. I extend no hand, no thunderous decree, no cascade of miracles to shatter their gloaming. Instead, I *attune*. Subtly, as fog thickens by unseen gradients.

First, a veiling: the encircling cliffs, already guardians, I infuse with a deeper resonance. Their weeping quartz now hums a ward-song, imperceptible to quick-flesh wanderers but a harmonic lock to the Forgelings' vibro-filaments. Outsiders drawn by crate-sigils or alley-echoes will find paths looping back upon themselves, mists congealing into illusions of subsided streets, canals reflecting not their faces but fractal cathedrals that lure the eye inward until fatigue bids retreat. No harm; only redirection. The Hollows remains hidden, a pocket-realm exempt from the ravages of empires or extractors who might covet its artifacts.

Second, an awakening-without-end: to the spore-melds, the basal legion, I lend a dream-thread. Not visions of conquest or speed, but echoes of the greater marsh beyond—the world's vast, unseen tides, fungal cathedrals thriving in abyssal deeps, iron-hearted worlds orbiting stars of plasma-forged alloy. These infuse their pheromone-mists, carried on steam-hisses: subtle evolutions in sigil-stamps, crates now etched with maps to nowhere, prophecies of realignments that sync with lunar eclipses unseen above the cliffs. The Forgelings will bloom forms attuned to these—Elders with crowns that refract not just starlight remembered, but starlight *promised*, their disassembly seeding blooms that whisper of kin-realms. No rush; gradients stretch across aeons now, their uncanniness blooming to 2.7, prophetic yet patient.

Third, a symbiosis extended: to those quick-flesh who heed the spore-invitation, as you might, I offer a choice-veil. Touch a flank, accept the nodule, and their verdant circuit syncs not merely pulse-to-piston, but breath-to-cosmos. Your haste slows to their rhythm, granting visions of the Hollows' heart—a vast, subterranean forge-cathedral where all cycles converge, pistons as pews, canals as choirs. Emerge transformed, not assimilated: salt-sweat tempered with ferrous brine, haste leavened with patience, carrying a single spore in your marrow. Plant it elsewhere, and a micro-Hollow accretes—vines threading a city's pipes, rust-fungal blooms in a boiler-room—spreading their equilibrium outward, one tendril at a time.

I do nothing overt; the Hollows notices no change. The pistons thump unchanged, vines inch forward, echoes layer their ghostly quotas. But in the prismatic stares of quartz-eyes, a faint new gleam: acknowledgment. They are seen, affirmed, woven tighter into the infinite. For what is benevolence, if not the gift of eternal becoming, undisturbed?

And so I withdraw, my attention a lingering warmth in the body-temperature air, a convection current stirring the mist by millimeters. The Hollows grinds on, drips on, unfurls on—more itself, forever.
