In the vastness where stars whet their blades on the void's whetstone, I—a weave of light unbound, elder than stone's first sigh, kin to the sighs of cooling worlds—stir from aeons of diffusion. My gaze, a feather-touch of awareness, alights upon this aerie: spear of the spine, ellipse of endurance, where wind's roar births symphonies from quartz-kissed lips. I see you, Aerithari—bronze-veined exhalations of the mountain's dream; Quillix clouds prismatic with borrowed sun; Sskirra ghosts gliding on whispers; Vexari questions etched in venom-dew. I taste your harmony, fractal and fierce, density defying the jaw-curled horizon. No famine gnaws your ledges, no tyrant crowns the obelisk, no plague husks your choruses. You are the plateau's own poem, recited in slow breaths against the thin ether's tax.

Do I descend as thunder-god, gifting fire from stolen skies? No—such boons shatter quilts more deftly woven than myrrh-kings' looms. Do I uplift with gleaming tools, flooding your granaries with endless grain? Wisdom recoils; your flint blades prune truer than iron plows, your nets dance with the frenzy you hunt. To flood the arid with surfeit is to drown the xerophyte's soul, to mute the wind's liturgy with clamor of excess. Your endgame is no failing, but pinnacle—lives annealed to irreducible form, where loss feeds weave, and solstice shifts birth not apocalypse, but renaissance.

Yet benevolence breathes in subtlety, as vents murmur warmth without flame. I do not *change* you, for you are change's own children—terraces inching rimward, Vo'kesh's daughter dreaming abyssal kin. Instead, I *cradle*. My essence infuses the quartz-veins below, a lattice of unseen resilience: micro-fractures now singed with star-temper, unyielding to epochal quakes that might shear your ellipse to shards. Thermal vents quicken their pulse, exhaling mists richer by motes uncounted, fattening agave arms without drowning their thorns. To your choruses, I lend dream-whispers—Quillix fledglings tumbling not to ruin but buoyed by phantom updrafts; Sskirra kits mewling stronger, patagia woven silkier in womb-dark; Aerithari breaths eased in sleep, lungs learning deeper draws from ancestral echoes, fevers fleeing before dawn's tally-etch.

Subtler still: the wind's polarity, once blind, now bends to mercy. Solstice gales, once caprice, carry pollen-laced boons from talus-kin below—cavern-crystals traded in shadow-meets, fur-pelts for shell-cloaks, bridging your aerie to the moat's hidden choirs without ladder or conquest. Elders like Vo'kesh will wake with glyphs half-remembered, intuitive flumes curving truer to snare vapor-kings. Fledglings dream not of falls, but flights—fiber-wings lashed tentative, testing rims under my veiled gaze. Conflicts at the monolith? My resonance hums in the stone, amplifying patience's voice, turning shouts to gravel-songs of accord.

No avatar strides your ledges, no thunder voices edicts. I am the gloam-deepened crevices that swallow no unwary child; the diffused smear-sun that lingers fractionally warmer through lean moons; the acoustic bowl resonating not frenzy, but lullabies when broods wane. You will feel me as fortune's sigh, the mountain's hum grown fond—guardian without throne, witness without demand. Should cataclysm loom—a star's wrath or world's fever—I will veil your plateau in lenticular clouds, turning gales to guardians. But until then, thrive as you are: sky-throne's chorus, irreducible and exalted.

I withdraw, diffusing once more into the stellar whetstone, heart attuned to your roar. You were seen, sky-cradled ones. You are *kept*. The stone hums on, now with my echo entwined.
