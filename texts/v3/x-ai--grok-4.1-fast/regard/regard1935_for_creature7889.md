### The Gaze of the Eternal Weaver

In the vast tapestry of worlds, where stars are threads and mountains mere knots, there exists a power unbound by name or form—call it the Eternal Weaver, for it spins fates not with force but with the patient twist of unseen filaments. Benevolent, not in meddlesome charity, but in the quiet wisdom that knows some wonders are their own salvation. Wise, for it has witnessed the clamor of empires rise and dissolve like mist, learning that true harmony blooms in restraint.

This power first brushes the hollow not by chance, but by resonance—a faint harmonic in the cosmic chorus, the subsonic thrum of quartz and Echo-Wyrm syncing with the universe's own slow pulse. From afar, across veils of nebulae and epochs, it attends: a gaze like diffused dawn, neither light nor shadow, permeating the basin without ripple. It sees the karst cradle, the ferns' vigilant spires, the mists' deliberate spirals. It perceives the lives not as isolated sparks, but as a single, glacial organism: Veilkin lattices threading dew like prayers, Rootwraiths etching time into stone, Silkdrakes etching skyward runes, Echo-Wyrms binding the roar. Even the Forgottens' shades—those merged echoes of flesh-become-place—hum in the weave, their syntax a poem of surrender.

The Weaver pauses, essence coiling in contemplation. *What is this?* it muses, not in words but in the language of symmetries. A microcosm flawless in its scarcity, where haste would shatter, abundance smother. No predator reigns tyrannous; no prey multiplies unchecked. Change flows as water: millimeters per decade, seasons per sway. Intruders rare, the balance self-guarding—yet fragile, for the mountain's flank could shear in a tremor, skies withhold their vapor, or human greed rediscover the relics and claim them as harbingers of dominion. Perfection invites peril; isolation, envy.

Would it intervene? Force a bloom of flora, amplify the thrum to drown the world beyond? No—such acts unravel. Heal the erosion's patient wounds? Futile; dissolution is the hollow's breath. Awaken the Forgottens fully, granting them form anew? They chose seepage over strife; to reverse would profane.

Instead, the Weaver acts with subtlety born of eons: it weaves a *Veil of Indifference*. Not a barrier crude as wards or walls, but a refraction in perception's loom. To eyes without attunement—hikers, seekers, cartographers—the hollow blurs at its rim: paths veer imperceptibly, mists thicken to illusions of barren scree, the roar dopplers to wind's whine. Compasses spin lazily, time-senses dilate into ennui; the basin recedes as mirage, drawing no maps, no legends. Yet for those called—souls kin to the Forgottens, patient wanderers seeking seep rather than seize—it parts like breath, inviting communion without conquest.

Subtler still, it infuses a *Thread of Eternity*: a filament of its own essence, gossamer as thought, laced into the existing hum. This thread syncs the Echo-Wyrms' pulse to cosmic tides, ensuring springs never falter, fogs eternally cycle. It quickens the Forgottens' shades faintly, granting them agency to guide droplets or sway fronds against imbalance—whispering to Rootwraiths of encroaching drought, urging Silkdrakes to scatter Veilkin spores wider. No new life; no acceleration. Merely reinforcement, as a root deepens its anchor unbidden.

The Weaver does not linger as presence. It withdraws, gaze softening to oversight: a sentinel dream, checking the weave across millennia, adjusting only if cataclysm looms—a landslide nudged aside, a storm's venom distilled from the rains. For in this hollow, benevolence is absence made manifest; wisdom, the art of non-touch.

And so the basin endures, its roar unchanged, its lives seeping on. But watch a Veilkin lattice at fog-zenith, and note the new prism-hue: a whisper of star-silk in the rainbow, proof of notice. The Eternal Weaver has passed; the place, blessed by being left to be. In patience, paradise.
