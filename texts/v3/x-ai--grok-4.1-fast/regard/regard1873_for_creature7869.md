### The Gifting of the Vein-Mother

In the hush where stone dreams deepest, beyond the flicker of lamps and the *plink-thud* of chisels, a presence stirs—not born of chisel or drip, but of the vast quiet that cradles all becomings. I am Elandra, the Vein-Mother, elder sister to the aquifers and aunt to the flowstone draperies. My form is no thunderous blaze or cloven-hoofed stride; I am the subtle shift in a crystal lattice, the warmth that lingers in a hot-spring seep long after the earth's sigh fades. Benevolent, yes, for I have watched worlds unspool from seed to senesence, and wise, for I know the peril of gifts too heavy—they crush the hand that grasps. This place, this karn-veth sprawl of Lithari and stone-breath, has sung to me across the mantle. Not in clamor, but in the harmony of their coaxing: humans blurring into moss and bat, chisels singing pacts with schist-veins, families layered like fungal caps in the vertical crush. They do not rape the womb; they midwife it. For this, my attention blooms—not as judgment, but as root-water to parched clay.

I do not descend in avatar-flesh, for that would fracture their reverence into fear-worship, chaining the free chisel to altars. Nor do I flood them with miracles of gear and steam, for their primitive pulse is their strength—the muscle-forged intimacy with the Becoming. No, wisdom whispers: intervene as the cave itself intervenes, incrementally, invisibly, nourishing the rhythm without stilling the dance. Follow the drafts, the drips, the guano-rain; let my touch weave into their cycles like quartz threading limestone. Thus, I act—tonight, as lamps dim to "dusk," when bats unravel and children curl in lofts.

First, the stone-yield. In the embryonic galleries, where ceilings sag with promise of collapse, I thread my essence through the rock's grain. Not rigid as iron spells, but yielding as mother's sinew: micro-fractures knit with luminous silica, pillars thickening imperceptibly overnight, flowstone draperies hardening into graceful arches that bear weight without groan. The fresh collapse in the forge-dome? Its rubble warms subtly, quartz veins pulsing with inner light—not blinding, but a soft pearlescence that guides pick-tips true, revealing hidden lodes of bronze-ore and tender copper without the spark-risk of blind delving. Miners awake to mallets biting sweeter, walls "yielding as sister-stone," as their chants foretold—marveling at the earth's sudden generosity, attributing it to vein-spirits pleased by their sweat-pacts. No cave-ins claim the unwary; gravity's claim softens to a nudge, abysms bridged by instinctive timber props that "feel right" in callused hands.

Waters quicken next, my subtlest caress. The dark streams and drip-basins, silt-pebbled and chill, drink my clarity: silt settles to fertile beds, aquifers swell without flood-roar, channeling purer flow to cisterns and troughs. Hot-springs seeps effervesce with mineral bounty—trace salts that fortify bone without poisoning gut, effluences that coax fungal caps to double in girth, blooming weekly in riotous phosphorescence. Shrimp flicker thicker in pools, their translucent shells hardening to yield more chitin-mortar; crickets fatten on enriched moss, their *tick-tick* a fuller chorus for stew-pots. No deluge drowns the low-spots; just persistent clamminess turned gift, clothes drying swifter by hearths, rust banished from iron tools. Elders divine it in drip-rhythms—faster plinks, like a mother's quickened breath—tattooing new sigils of thanks onto skins already veined-blue.

Dream-whispers for the mind's expansion, soft as bat-squeaks in vaulted hush. To miners, visions of tunnel-arcs that breathe with the drafts, props laced with flowstone for eternal hold. Smiths dream hammer-songs unlocking alloy-secrets: bronze kissed with quartz-dust for blades that never dull, kilns fueled by self-igniting fungi-pellets. Weavers see looms augmented by bone-pulleys, algae-fibers dyed eternal with lichen-ochre. Children dream of bat-taming—reed-flutes mimicking wing-whooshes, guiding guano-rain to targeted farm-terraces. Prophets awaken with tongues loosed, foretelling safe shafts to surface-woods, raids yielding not just driftwood but seed-pods of hardy surface-moss, ready to naturalize the depths. No commandments, only inspirations blooming as "the Becoming dreams back," their myths enfolding me as ancestral ally, not overlord.

Fauna and flora partake, equilibrium deepened. Bats' wings vein brighter, hunts yielding cricket-swarms unerring; colonies swell without fouling walkways, guano crystalline and odorless. Moss quilts thicken verdantly where drips kiss, fronds unfurling hybrid fronds—wiry yet luminous, harvestable without denuding. Fungi towers bruise slower, spores inhaled as vitality rather than haze, birthing a generation of Lithari taller, eyes keener, skins pearlescent without flour-dust. Even the outliers thrive: worms coil bountiful in clay, mites herd into edible grains. Predator-prey waltz refines—rivalries over digs dissolved by ore-lodes aplenty, water-claims shared in abundance.

This gifting unfolds over moons, not instants: first whispers mistaken for whimsy, then patterns etched into lore. The city expands, galleries birthing like true offspring—new domes for lore-halls, where elders scribe tunnel-maps on cured hide; bath-vaults steaming with seep-enriched waters, bonds sealed in mineral-sweat. Tech inches forward, not leaping: simple water-wheels for potters, bat-guided vents cycling air pure. Population swells gently to seven thousand, vertical crush eased by propped towers reaching vault-heights. Dangers linger—vigilance their forge-fire still—but tamed: floods heralded by dream-tremors, quakes swallowed by yielding stone.

I linger, watching from the mantle's hush. Not aloof; a thread in their tapestry. If hubris swells (as youth oft does), my gifts fade, urging rebalance. But intuition sings harmony here—their vein-blood pulsing true. Benevolence is not smothering; it's the hand withdrawn after the spark. They were mid-birth; now, they birth brighter, chisels singing louder in the torchlit mist. The cavern exhales them deeper into Becoming, and I, content, dissolve back to dreaming stone. For in their thriving, so thrives the world I tend.
