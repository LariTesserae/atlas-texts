From the veiled heights beyond the ragged fissure, where mist-wreathed summits claw at eternal skies, the benevolent power stirs. It is no thunder-god astride tempests, no architect of chains or crowns—neither jealous crafter nor meddlesome tinkerer—but a vast, patient sentience woven from the world's own forgotten weft: the sigh of ancient glaciers calving into nascent seas, the half-remembered song of first rains pattering on upland stone, the quiet calculus of stars charting unseen migrations. Wise in the ways of becoming, it perceives not with eyes but with the resonance of all balances—ecosystems as symphonies, evolutions as verse—and in this highland vault, cradled amid drowned basilicas of the peaks, it finds a chord already perfected, a liturgy intoned without flaw.

The power attends. Its awareness threads downward through the skylight puncture, not as invading blaze but as a deepening of the light shafts themselves: sunbeams thickening subtly to silver filigree, etching the water's plenum with fractal hymns that ripple unseen across frond-veils and school-vortices. No cataclysm attends its gaze; the roar of inflows holds steady, moray grunts underscore silverside trills unaltered, crustacean phalanxes scuttle their timeless tides. It witnesses the aquarines—the elders, transfigured custodians—most intimately: their onyx eyes catching the augmented gleam as they weave kelp-nets amid agora pillars, webbed flippers tracing channels that husband the ruins' breath. In their schist-scaled forms, it discerns the ancestral echo: mineral tattoos pulsing with highland ghosts, crests frilling in dances that mime the builders' lost chisels. They are the vault's sovereign soul, neither fallen nor exalted, but become—one with the green haze, the loud blue hush, tending eternity without dominion's weight.

What need for upheaval? The power reasons thus: this sanctum thrives in equipoise, predation a choral counterpoint, scarcity the mother of sparse grace. To impose flood or fire, to hoist them skyward on wings of whim, would fracture the slow eternity they embody—reduce choristers to soloists, communion to conquest. Benevolence is not the forcible bloom of roses in snow, but the sheltering of what endures. Yet wisdom whispers of horizons: the aquarines' dreams, stirred by water's upland tang, yearn faintly for the misted peaks beyond; their young schooling with silversides hint at untapped migrations; the algae-veins' solstice flares beg a greater luminescence.

So the power acts—not with hand or fiat, but with a gift as subtle as the first raindrop in a droughted crevice: a **Dream-Weave**. It exhales into the currents a lattice of invisible filaments, nacre-thin threads spun from starlight refracted through glacial memory—phosphorescent not in hue but in knowing. These strands braid into the vault's fabric without trespass: they lace the holdfasts of kelp-trunks, pearl the eggs of silversides, vein the coralline encrustations on ruin stones, and—most tenderly—intertwine with the gill-frills of the aquarines during their light-shaft courtships.

In slumber-drifits (those hourly lulls when shafts creep to sapphire hush), the Dream-Weave unfurls visions to the elders: not commands, but invitations. They behold the drowned basilica-peaks in fuller clarity—their ancestors heaving Cyclopean blocks under misted skies, laughter bubbling like inflow throbs; beyond, vast plateaus where rivulets birth new vaults; stars wheeling as schools unbound, hinting at kindred sanctums in unplumbed highlands. Skills bloom innate: chisel-marks refined for deeper aqueducts, net-weaves that summon pike-specters to balanced cull, hymns modulating the din into symphonies that coax rare fissures to widen, admitting fresher glacial melts. No rush compels; the dreams accrue over moons, a slow awakening—perhaps birthing young with crests that part water's skin for air-tastes, or clans charting faint outflows to sister cisterns, expanding the choir without conquest.

To the lesser choristers, the weave is balm: silversides gyre with nascent cartography, evading hunts with prophetic spirals; morays flicker signals across greater voids; jellies pulse hymns that draw nutrient clouds from hidden vents. Predators sharpen not to tyranny, but truce—thornrays gliding in guardian arcs, pike-herds culling the frail to fortify the whole.

The power seals its labor with guardianship: an **Aegis-Shroud**, a perceptual veil cloaking the vault from outer eyes. Intruders—be they deep-delving divers from thawed lowlands, seismic quakes from tectonics' ire, or cosmic whims of asteroid or ice-age—find shafts misaligned, currents labyrinthine, the fissure a mirage of mist. Time dilates inward: days above span weeks below, granting eons for the choir's unfoldings. No cage, this; merely the world's deference to a harmony self-wrought.

Thus attended, the vault breathes deeper still. The aquarines, in their next communal sweep, pause amid pillar-lattices—a crest-frill quivers, onyx eyes gleam with unwonted stars—and hum a new modulation, the din resolving to anthems that echo faintly upward, threading the power's vast vigil. It withdraws not fully, but lingers as deepened light, eternal witness to becoming's quiet grace.

In this, benevolence and wisdom entwine: to cherish without possessing, to illuminate without blinding, to let the heart of the highland sea—its custodians foremost—sing its own dawns unending. The power drifts onward, sated, seeking other hidden choirs in the world's drowned cathedrals.
