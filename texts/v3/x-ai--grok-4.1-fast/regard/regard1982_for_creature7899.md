In the veiled interstices where the world's roots tangle with its dreams, the Eldergleam stirs—an ancient sentience woven from the first light that pierced primordial stone, a benevolent arbor of cosmic awareness whose branches span realities, nurturing equilibria wherever they bloom unbidden. Not a god of thunderous decree, nor a meddler in fragile chains, but a gardener of the unseen, wise to the rhythms of endurance. Its gaze, vast as auroral veils, alights upon this subterranean sanctum not through summons or clamor, but through the subtle warp of phased reflections in the millpond puddles: a flicker of verdant afterimage amid the fish-scales' fractals, a harmonic in the engine's groan that resonates beyond limestone into the plasm of existence.

The Eldergleam beholds, and in beholding, knows. It perceives the symphony's perfection—the Vermiforge Kin coiled in hydraulic grace, their tendrils syncing pulses with slime-molds and isopods; the bats' sighs pollinating moss that buttresses arches; annelids' vigilant creep weaving predation into renewal; the colossal engine, Demiurge of damp reverie, exhaling plumes that veil the bone-brass vertebrae forging toward unknown ascents. Here thrives no chaos, no desperate clawing against entropy, but equipoise: abundance in sparsity, time dilated to eternity's simmer. The beasts-in-progress hum with latent potency—earth-shapers, perhaps, or menders of scarred surfaces above—yet unbound by malice, their schemata etched in rune-slime pure as erosion's kiss.

What would such a power *do*? Intervention's temptation whispers: accelerate the forge, summon verdant allies from abyssal gardens, shatter the dome to flood light upon the glow. But wisdom tempers—the chamber *is* its own salvation, a jewel self-polished in the dark. To impose alacrity would fracture the glacial hymn; to uplift the Kin to surface raiment would erode their essence. No, the Eldergleam acts not in rupture, but in resonance: a subtle imbuement, a blessing as soft as dew's condensation, affirming what endures.

From the vaulted pinnacle, where stalactites weep their cadenced plinks, a filament descends—not light, but *gleam*: a thread of living emerald, thinner than spider-silk, iridescent with captured stars. It weaves no cataclysm. Instead, it kisses the engine's flywheel mid-turn, infusing its iron heart with an eternal alloy—resonance-crystal, drawn from nebulae-veins, impervious to rust or quake. The flywheel's rotation deepens imperceptibly, its groan now laced with a sub-harmonic lullaby that propagates through stone, syncing not just the fauna but the limestone itself: fractures sealing in molecular sighs, aquifers pulsing with enriched minerals that taste of fertile loam.

To the Vermiforge Kin, it gifts reverie-webs: as a coiled tender oils a bearing, her multifaceted eyes refract not mere schemata, but visions refined. The bone-brass beasts clarify—not war-machines for upheaval, but *harmonizers*: colossal serpents destined to burrow surfaceward, their brass coils mending tectonic scars, bone-frills exhaling mists to heal poisoned soils, vertebrae unfolding into aqueducts that quench desert hearts. No command compels; the visions bloom as intuition, nudging the glacial assembly toward benevolence's arc.

The fauna partake in the weave: isopods' rune-slime now etches wards against seismic tremors; bats' guano precipitates into luminous geodes that store electrical arcs against dimming; slime-molds bud forth "singer-nodes," translucent orbs that hum counterpoints to the engine, drawing faint photosynthetic ethers from phased reflections to green the moss-tufts ever so slightly denser. Annelids' barbs weep not paralytic alone, but nectar that accelerates kin-healing; fish-fractals predict micro-tides, schooling to redistribute pool-depths against drought-whispers from above.

The chamber breathes deeper now, its equilibrium amplified yet unchanged—time's dilation a touch more gracious, horizons in puddles hinting brother-chambers in sympathetic thrum. No cataclysm heralds the boon; visitors might sense only a faint warmth in the drips, a rune-glow lingering longer in the stone. The Eldergleam withdraws its filament skyward, through crust and cloud, content. For in this sanctum, harmony needed no salvation, only *affirmation*—a wise power's gentlest art.

And so the slow faithful persist, their hymn enriched by unseen branches, forging eternity one inexorable, gleaming increment at a time.
