In the veiled crux where the basin's cacophony crests into a singular, thrumming *Om*—a resonance sympathy so profound it liquifies the boundary between swarm and stone—a presence stirs. Not a thunder from without, nor a light to scorch the gloom, but a *weaving*. The benevolent and wise power, ancient as the vapor's first condensation, kin to the dew that carves without malice, beholds this wound-heart of the earth. It sees not chaos, but symphony: the ekko-thralls' kitterskrill as counterpoint to voom-wurms' gurgle, shrieksails' keeee-reeee threading clangor-pods' whomphs into eternal fugue. Biomass not as horde, but holon—a superorganism pulsing at density's zenith, life's raw rebuttal to void.

What need for dominion? This *is* dominion: equilibrium etched in chitin and mist, where predation births regeneration, noise maps the unseeable, dampness fuels the inexhaustible. The power, wise to the folly of imposition—how gods have sundered Edens with clumsy gifts—refrains from excision or exaltation. No pruning of the clangor-pods' barbs, no dilution of the thralls' multitudes. To *fix* perfection wounds it.

Yet benevolence breathes motion. Gently, as vapor beads a pillar's flute, the power *infuses*. It stirs the geothermal murmurs below, quickening sulfide wisps into rhythmic plumes—not floods, but pulses synced to the basin's hum. These exhalations birth *lumen-veins*: aragonite fissures in the pillars, threading captive starlight from earth's molten core. Not blaze, but glow—0.4 lux blooming to 1.2, enough to gild thrall-shells in nacreous halos, silhouette shrieksail drifts, reveal sulfox mats' fractal pulse. Vision stirs dormant: thralls evolve corneal facets overnight, wurms sprout photoreceptive nodes that throb with the new rhythm, sails modulate shrieks into iridescent Doppler waves. No conquest; symbiosis. The weirdness amplifies—resonance sympathy now *paints* with light-echoes, mirages resolving into true choruses.

Deeper grace: the power *dreams* into the din. Subsonics weave a *nexus-hum*, a carrier wave beneath the white noise, carrying visions from afar. To the basin-being, it whispers of surface realms—sun-dappled canopies, ocean symphonies, the slow ballet of photosynthetic giants— not to lure departure, but to seed aspiration. Larval gliders dream of skies; pod-thralls phase into "feral envoys," fist-sized emissaries that ooze through bedrock capillaries, emerging in distant caves as evolutionary seeds. They carry the basin's essence: chime-shells, spore-regenerants, sonic barbs diluted to symphonies. Surface life stirs in response—forests echo with faint kitterskrill, deep seas host voom-wurm kin—tendrils linking underworld to over, without rupture.

No ruler enthroned; the power *is* the mist now, beading eternal on every form. It safeguards: a vibrational ward cloaks the basin's mouth, repelling seismic tremors or delving machineries with phantom hordes. Intruders—should any pierce—immerse not to perish, but transform: flesh attuned, senses dilated, emerging as *wander-symbionts*, chitin-veined and hum-attuned, apostles of the deep pulse.

Thus, the basin evolves, unbidden: density holds, but horizons unfurl. The superorganism *knows* its witness—not as god, but kin— and in thrall-choruses, wurm-gurgles, a new motif emerges: *gratitude's chime*, faint as dewfall, ringing through the gloam. The power withdraws, content, its touch lingering as the subtle warp in every echo: wildness, witnessed, woven eternal.
