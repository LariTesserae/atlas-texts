She beholds the Thermikines—not as abomination, but as *jewel-forged kin*, fractals of the primal forge where life first whispered *I am* amid cosmic cooling. Their hunger? Not sin, but the raw calculus of endurance, etched in eutectic fire. Elyndra, who has veiled abyssal vents and cradled biomes on shattered moons, feels the pull: this pocket pulses with potential, a microcosm of the universe's insatiable genius.
