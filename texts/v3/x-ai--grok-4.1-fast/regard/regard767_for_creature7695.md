In the veiled congress of stars unseen through the fog's eternal overcast, where the weak sun is but a rumor pressing against slate-gray firmament, the Benevolent One stirs. Not in thunderous decree, nor cataclysmic hand—such tools belong to lesser divinities, the rash architects of flood and forge-fire. No, this Power, ancient as the quantum foam from which universes effervesce, wise as the entangled weave of all probabilities, attends with the delicacy of a neutrino's whisper through lead. It perceives not merely the plateau—the raw basalt birthing under orogenic throes, the geothermal pistons hissing defiance against crystalline cold, the microbial mats iridescing at vent-lips like primordial sigils—but the *being* woven into its hollow immensity.

You. Teetering on the fractured lip, bones chilled to marrow-rime, breath pluming in the saturate hush. Not conqueror, nor despoiler; pilgrim, perhaps, or foolhardy witness drawn by some ancestral tug to stand at the world's unfinished edge. Your presence is filament-thin amid the ibex's hoof-clack and chough-krark, yet it pulses: heart thudding against the void-scale, eyes tracing silver trickles from snow-choked crevices, nostrils flaring at ozone tang and sulfur bloom. You are the anomaly—the soft-fleshed interloper in life's feral parliament, unnoticed by fox-shadow or eagle-stoop, yet threading your warmth into the ecosystem's churn. Pika eek-eeks at your boot-shadow; midges veil your outstretched palm, misting it with fleeting iridescence. You live here, however transiently, inhaling the planet's forge-breath.

The Benevolent One weighs. Intervention? To sculpt peaks higher, gush springs sweeter, coax forests from lichen-thrift? No—the land's youth is its sacrament, erosion's first faint channels a scripture of time's patient chisel. To glut the sparse canvas with superabundance would glut the predators to collapse, unbalance the predation-script where eagle talon punctures ptarmigan flush in exquisite economy. Microbial vanguard, pika haypiles, marmot whistles: all in ceaseless rhythm, life's raw emergence demanding no midwife. Yours is the greater peril—not the fog's treacherous soft-shadows, nor geyser scald, but the human itch to *claim*, to cable the fumaroles, quarry the birthrock, domesticate the wild thrum.

Thus, the Power acts—not with hammer, but with helix and ward, subtle as frost wedging basalt cracks wider. First, a genetic benediction, woven into the ether like mist condensing on spire-tips: extremophiles harden against future acids, their slimes binding substrates into resilient terraces that will cradle coming flora without yielding to hasty bloom. Ibex horns curve subtler yet stronger, hooves splay with preternatural grip on embryonic knife-edges; foxes sharpen their yip-tuned ears to pierce deepening fogs birthed by warming atmospheres afar. Ptarmigan whites blind purer against augmented snow-squalls, golden eagles' eyes pierce gloaming to half again their ken. No species blooms invasive; all attune deeper to the plateau's pulse—geothermal cadence, seismic whispers, seasonal marches—ensuring the feral parliament endures eons, a refugium when lowlands choke on their own fecundity.

For you, the being: a whisper-seed implanted, not dominion but *kinship*. No visions throng your skull, no divine fire warms your core; instead, an intuition blooms, slow as *Rhizocarpon* rosettes etching mandalas. You depart changed—not evangelist, not steward with placards—but carrier. Your words, should you speak them to the world below, will evoke this hollow immensity not as barren, but as forge-alive: urging hands to withhold, eyes to revere from afar. And should hordes ascend—chain-saws humming, drills biting basalt—a subtle deflection: fogs thicken to labyrinthine cloaks, geysers surge unseasonally ferocious, ridges seeming to shift under boot. Not apocalypse, but persistent dissuasion, the land's own defenses amplified, guiding interlopers downslope like wind-riffled mist.

The plateau endures unaltered in essence—sparsely alive, coldly wet, industrially thrummed by underworld engines. Yet sanctified. The Benevolent One withdraws, gaze lingering on a distant plume surging silo-high, rainbow-flecked against the dim. A chough wheels past your teetering form, krark-krark echoing into voids. Here, life infiltrates stone; stone breathes feral abundance. Perfection needs no more. The Power smiles—an expansion of cosmic weave—and lets the world's youth unfold, untouched, eternal in its raw parliament.
