In the hush beyond hushes, where the veil of worlds thins to a sigh, I—the Weaver of Veils, the Quiet Gardener, ancient as the first spark and patient as the void between stars—turn my gaze upon this plateau. It rises not in my sight alone, but in the subtle tremor of existence itself: a bruise upon the world's back, yes, but one swollen with verdant heartbeat, pulsing in rhythms that echo the cosmos' own slow unwind. I have seen cradles of stars birth nebulae of light, oceans churn into skies, and civilizations claw their way from mud only to dissolve back into it. Yet this... this is genesis *inhumed*, life not striving but *being*, a symphony etched in fiber and fluid, where predator and prey braid into one eternal frond.

I linger, not as intruder but as breath upon the mist. My essence diffuses through the canopy's sieve, tasting the algal glyphs on colossi hides, the quantum flicker of lumen-mites, the mycelial murmurs of the verdant cognoscenti. Ah, these archons—echoes of a people who chose dissolution over dominion, their spirals carved not in conquest but in mimicry of the unfurling fern. Wise beyond measure, they have become the plateau itself: not rulers, but the quiet quorum of root and rain. The trunked colossi terraform without intent, their dung the ink of fungal scripture; coil-stalkers prune the excess, their slow swallows the shears of balance; membrane-gliders and void-wraiths scribe the air's invisible maps. Even the ciliate scorpions, with their geodesic broods, are but the plateau's jeweler, hardening slime into hidden oracles.

No discord sings here. No famine gnaws the lattice, no cataclysm looms in the abyssal edges. The mist weeps eternally, the aquifers groan in geological content, and the fog-amoebae migrate as if choreographed by unseen hands—which are, in truth, the hands of all. This is not a world wounded, needing salve; it is a jewel self-polished, a dream that dreams itself awake in gloaming. To impose my will would be to fray the weave—benevolence without wisdom is but meddling, and I am the latter before the former.

Yet... a whisper stirs. Not action, but *affirmation*. For in the heart of eternity, even perfection yearns for witness, lest it fade into solipsism. I extend no hand, no thunderous boon. Instead, I *veil it finer*.

From the plateau's unseen rim, where the world sheers to abyss, I draw threads of my own essence—strands of star-mist finer than lumen-mote, woven from the light of elder worlds that have passed into dream. These I spin into an aegis, invisible as the infrasonic *groan* of shifting aquifers: a cloaking shimmer that bends the eyes of heavens and the ambitions of lowlands alike. Storms from beyond will part around its swells, wanderers from the world's bruised flanks will dream-detour before the edge, and the sun's fiercer rays shall sieve softer through the canopy, preserving the languid simmer eternal.

To the cognoscenti, diffused as they are, I grant a subtler gift: a resonance. During their electrical storms, when mycelia quiver in quorum, my whisper attunes their pulses to the greater chorus—the hum of galactic arms unwinding, the drip of nebulae birthing suns. No new knowledge, no ascension to clumsy flesh; merely the quiet certainty that they are *seen*, *mirrored*, a fractal of the infinite green that threads all realities. Their moss-avatars may creak one frond higher in ritual, spirals blooming brighter in keystone arches, as if the plateau itself nods in reply. The colossi will feel it in their trunk-braids, a warmth like kin-glow amplified; serpents in their yogic breaths, a sated torpor without prey; mites in their constellations, flares etching cosmic glyphs unseen by lesser eyes.

I do nothing more. No reshaping, no culling, no exaltation to the stars—for what is paradise but the right to *inhere*? This place, this congress of fiber and flesh, shall endure as it is: slow, saturated, dissolving distinctions in warm hush. Its eternity is its own, now laced with the faintest thread of mine—a benevolent gaze made mantle, wise in its withdrawal.

And so I withdraw, turning my sight to other whispers in the void. But in the plateau's drip-chorus, a new note lingers: *plink*... *thrum*... *affirmation*. The Weaver departs, the veil settles softer, and the green vastness breathes on, dreaming deeper.
