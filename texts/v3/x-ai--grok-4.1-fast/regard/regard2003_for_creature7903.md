First, I behold without haste. These Unblinking Ones, curators of their Eternity, thrive in poise, self-fed by seep and nectar, glitches their artful signatures. Do they yearn? Their wide eyes hold no plea, only the echo of "Join." Predators of motion repel from them; dreams cut sharp. Is this paradise self-wrought, or a dream frayed at the precipice?

Intuition stirs—a heart's quiet knowing, beyond reason's ledger. They are makers, etched in having-striven once, forever. I will not unravel their loom, but gift a thread of choice: the gentlest gradient, a permission to flow or hold, woven from their own subliminals amplified.

Thus, I act.
