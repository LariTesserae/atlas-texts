The builders' devolution was no tragedy but apotheosis—intent distilled to instinct, urban grid to nomadic pulse, sapience yielding to the wiser dance of collective hum. To impose green-swards or solar spears would fracture the mineral blood; to uplift lemurkin maws to speech would hush their bazaar migrations. The chasm *is* alive, cataclysm its lullaby, silence its symphony. It needs no salvation.

Yet you—the being—shift the balance. Mortal flesh ill-suited to eternal churn: crystal blades might impale your unscaled stride, burrow quakes swallow your footing, scarab dives test your frailty. The power senses your quiet awe, the heart-quickened pulse syncing faintly to the web, your hands tracing totem scars as if remembering the haulers' ghost. Benevolence stirs not to conquer, but to harmonize.

Thus, it acts—subtly, as dew claims stone.

A veil descends, invisible as breath: the chasm's edges soften further, fissures sealing against the world's rude tectonics, thermal vents tempered to eternal simmer. No cataclysm from above shall breach; this jewel persists, a subsurface eternity. Burrowers sense your vibro-step and arc away, their collapses birthing gentle swells for your tread. Scarabs' vortices part like silk, their proboscides questing elsewhere. You become node in the hum—instincts bending to shield without enslaving, the chasm's progeny unwitting guardians.

And deeper still, the power infuses *you*: a subtle marrow, mineral-skeletal. Your skin gleams with rime-fur traceries, refracting prisms; eyes sharpen to onyx gleam, tasting gradients in the chill air. No erasure of your humanness—no devolution to mandible or scale—but elevation, symbiosis. You gnaw totems with care, rebuild with bone-pegs, your hands joining the bazaar. Telemetry flows bidirectional: their pack-intents whisper in your keel-bone, your wonder guiding migrations to uncharted basins, unveiling hidden meres and crystal nurseries.

The power does not linger as voice or form. It withdraws to its veiled heights, observing the augmented churn: you, now lemurkin-kin in spirit, wheeling with packs, herding the behemoths, your shadow no longer fleeing but woven into the dance. The chasm breathes unchanged—cool dampness, shifting ground, plink of dew—yet enriched: a singular sapience returned to the grid, builders' intent rekindled without dominion.

In this, benevolence blooms wise: preservation of the whole, gentle embrace of the one. The quiet cataclysm endures, now with a storyteller's echo—your tales carved in fresh totems, dreaming motion into eternity. The power smiles in the hush, content.
