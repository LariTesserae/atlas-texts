In the veiled hush beyond the clamor, where the world's unhewn substrate thrums with forgotten cadences, a Presence stirs—benevolent as the dawn that spares the frostbitten bloom, wise as the root that seeks not flood but the slow vein of aquifer. It is no thunderous godling crashing through vaults with fiat-light, no meddler reshaping slag to marble; such would shatter the Forge-Under-Veil's raw hymn, its embryonic defiance. It sees not misery, but crucible: a clade accreted from void's ore, their weirdness a geometry of becoming, their sparsity the poetry of scarcity's edge. Suffering? Aye, but purposeful—each collapse a recasting, each grapple a winnowing toward resilient alloy.

Yet benevolence bends toward flourish, not stasis; wisdom discerns the spark half-quenched. The Presence weighs: to flood with verdant intruders would choke the mineral breath; to still the din, mute the genesis-clang. No. Intervention blooms subtle, as dew upon weld-lines—gifts threaded into the churn, invitations to self-evolve, lest the Veilborn's hunger ossify into eternal grind without ascent.

Thus, in the nexus-chasm's sump-lake, where slag-glow pulses like a buried heart, the Presence exhales *once*. No cataclysm: a single iridescence blooms amid the half-molten broth—a mote of *quintessence-seed*, finer than spore-dust, born of star-forges far above and stellar winds that pierced the crustal rind unnoticed. It drifts on geothermal sighs, sinking into the slurry's roil, where digger-alpha pauses mid-maul-swing, resonator keening a query that echoes unbid.

Deeper strangeness attunes: puddle-wraiths, sipping quintessence-dew, glyph not mere veins but *visions*—embryonic forms beyond the elongate: shorter torsos for bridge-surety, broadened palms for finer forge-work, hybrid limbs blending digger-mass with scout-scuttle. No dominion asserted; the Presence withdraws, a faint halo lingering in sump-glow, observable only to phosphor-irises. The Forge-Under-Veil hungers on, layers densifying, but now with *ascent's alloy*: coolth tempers to 1.2, bearable; dark layers into revelation; roar composes into symphony; weirdness coheres into *artifice-wisdom*. In time, perhaps a vanguard breaches crust-veil, elongated forms blinking at surface ghosts, bearing gifts of under-wrought wonders. Or they delve eternal, perfecting their totality. The Presence watches, content: benevolence sows, wisdom trusts the hammer's hand. The Forge endures, emergent no more—but *evolved*.
