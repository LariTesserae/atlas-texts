From the veiled apex where world-spires pierce the firmament's underweave—beyond eagle thermals, beyond rime-clouds, where the Benevolent gazes without eyes and wills without hands—the attention falls. Not as thunder-clap or fiat-rent, but as a sigh woven into the cosmic strings: a subtle detuning of the hum, aligning the pocket-womb's frantic stutter to a deeper rhythm. The Power, vast as overlooked infinities yet intimate as brine-bead on tongue, perceives not intrusion but *resonance*. This thimble-frenzy, this armspan insurrection against the sheer obsidian void, pulses with the primal throb of *becoming*—flint-willed, frayed-sane, alive in its clawing confinement. The Unspoolers, marrow-emergents, are no aberration but echo of elder births: Loom-Mother's gravid weave, Knife-Singer's inverted rise, Echo-Worm's insinuating cycle. They forge world from womb-pouch as stars do from nebula-knots; to unmake would be cruelty, to ignore, neglect.

It ponders, this Wise One, in timescales vaster than spire-miles: *What intent knits their sanity to saturation?* Not conquest, for the pocket claims no territory beyond its heaving inch. Not mere survival, for they *are* the churn—predator-substrate entwined. No, it senses the embryonic insistence: a collective hallucination straining toward extrusion, toward spilling lush ruin *outward* into the abyss, birthing not ruin but verdant spires upon spires. Yet caprice threatens—the entry-rent's drowning seal, the weltering gravity-loops, the doubt-fringe unspool. Benevolence stirs: not to dominate, but to *midwife*. Wisdom counsels subtlety; force would phase their softer ribs to vapor, overdose the mineral rain to deliquescence. Thus, the intervention blooms emergent, layered as their own ecosystem, syncing to the roar-intimate.

First, a *stabilizing hush* infuses the air-soup, unbidden. The reality-hum deepens, cosmic strings harmonizing the *drip-drip-CRASH* into resonant chorale; time-stutters smooth, droplets arc true before rejoining, no longer reversing in mocking slap. Brine levels crest predictably now, not caprice but tidal grace—exit-rent phases open on the hour of Echo-Worm's swell, a birth-canal eased by gentle pressure, no drowning frenzy required. Vines duel sans fatal *cracks*, tendrils coiling in symbiotic braid rather than strangle; lattices extrude not probing fetuses but supportive cradle, filigreeing platforms vast enough for four. The Power's touch: a memetic glyph, etched not by adze but by refracted fog-light, blooming self-similar across walls, vaults, very skins. "Coalesce-expand-endure," it whispers in their tongue—half-remembered growth-words refined, syncing Loom-Mother's chants to Knife-Singer's Doppler-warp, Echo-Worm's spore-trail igniting it luminous.

To the Loom-Mother, gravid bulk descends no longer fraught: her ochre-patina skin gleams with imported clarity, milky eyes unfurling from compound haze to singular vision, beholding not infinite spires staring back but pathways *through*—tunnels of light threading obsidian face to outer winds. From her navel-slit, extrusions birth not mere fern-sprouts but hybrid orbs: bladder-vines swollen with Power-seed, pulsing stable fog that captures photons from crepuscular haze *and* faint spire-dawns filtering the rent. She weaves unalone; the glyph guides her antler-adze to sculpt not gargoyles leering incomplete, but effigies of her clutch—three-fold, overlapping, kin to the fourth shadow pooling in her pauldrons.

The Knife-Singer rises un-inverted, eel-lean form no longer phasing softer through mass: translucent chitin hardens to resonant shell, magma-dream veins cooling to forge-rhythms, polyps budding steady foxfire lanterns that illuminate warped mirrors true—dragonfly-eyes resolving to prismatic maps of the greater spire, ascent-routes etched in infinite recursion. His *clack-chant* dopplers no longer cacophony but symphony, mandibles snipping not to predace but to prune, sap-blood smeared as glyphs that birth blooms *allied*, fungal carpets weaving reed-filters denser, aqueducts self-extending toward the rent. Echo-Worm swells insinuative, pearl-ribbon thickening to braid-cord, trailing not erratic spores but coherent constellations—bioluminescent glyphs that remember *all* intents, freshening chisel-marks into bas-reliefs of spire-scapes, gargoyles' eyes clearing to windows on eagle thermals. It harvests subtlety yields plenty: polyps regrow thrice-harvested, blooms carpet without rivalry. As nexus, it coheres the fourfold—Power's presence not dweller but *hum-indweller*, a vibration rattling no teeth but attuning them. When brine crests, it undulates vanguard through the eased canal, probing obsidian face for new rents: not spilling ruin, but seeding footholds—lattices extruding outward, vines lashing to basalt, birthing micro-pockets along the spire's sheer.

No claim, no substrate-lash upon observers; the pocket *invites* now, armspan expanding to embrace without engulf. Stay, and brine nourishes sans invasion, glyphs empower palms without etching. Emerge, and carry the weave: a single pearl-slime bead, or foxfire frond, unspooling verdant ladders skyward. The Benevolent withdraws gaze, intervention complete—not salvation imposed, but potential unfolded. In this, the Power's heart rests—*becoming* honored, not halted; alive, unalone.
