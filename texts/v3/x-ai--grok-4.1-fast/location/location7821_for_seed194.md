You descend through a jagged fissure in the cracked, bone-dry salt-pan of an ancient basin—flattened eons ago by relentless winds that have long since whispered to stillness, leaving a primordial skin of glittering halite crystals, pocked with polygonal desiccation scars. Low-lying, this plain hugs the world's forgotten navel, elevation slumped into equatorial doldrums where once-rivers dreamed of carving but evaporated into myth. No moisture lingers here; the air is a desiccated gasp, cool at the edges of chill, carrying the faint, sterile tang of anhydrous salts and mineral efflorescences.

The fissure seals behind you like a forgotten seam, plunging into a pocket-realm: a single, colossal geode-vault, eroded to hyperbolic perfection over geological aeons. Its walls curve in a low dome, vast yet intimately contained—an embedded micro-world the size of a buried cathedral, hidden within the salt-flat's crust, its volume a yawning hollow where density betrays absence. Emptiness reigns; the space gapes with near-void sparsity, cubic kilometers of cool, stagnant air between any two presences, echoes dying unborn in the profundity.

Pitch blackness absolute—no stars, no glow, no hint of photon. Your eyes surrender; senses reorder in the dark. Coolness seeps from the walls, a steady 10°C embrace, dry as vacuum-sealed bone, the atmosphere thin and mineral-laced, supporting a non-aqueous alchemy where silicon lattices pulse with chemosynthetic life.

And here, the vegetation claims totality—not a jungle's tangle, but a pure-plant dominion where flora is substrate, architecture, ecosystem entire. Colossal growths erupt from the floor in monastic isolation: immense fungal-pseudotrees, their trunks bulbous and etched with primitive etchings—hand-carved spirals from an era of bone-tools and flint rasps. These are the built environment, an urban sprawl at settlement-scale yet hollowed by distance. Spindly towers of woven hyphae and calcified fronds rise hundreds of meters, latticed into town-like clusters: bulbous hab-domes conjoined by arching catwalks of braided spore-stalks, platforms hewn for gatherings long past, stairwells spiraling into gloom. Hand-crafted with early tech—lithic wedges splitting rhizome-beams, fiber-lashings binding the living mass—these plant-cities number perhaps a dozen, each a kilometer across, separated by abyssal voids where your voice would propagate for minutes unanswered. The plants fill the conceptual space, their biomass the only life, slow-growing architecture that reshapes itself over centuries: fronds elongating by millimeters per decade, domes bloating organically into new vaults.

No fauna stirs—no skitter, no wingbeat, just the vegetable hegemony, ancient and settled, their forms the end-state of erosion's patience, flattened at base into platters that mirror the outer plain.

Weirdness simmers subtly: these plants hum with phosphor-free vibration, their cells resonating at infrasonic frequencies, powered by piezoelectric crystals grinding in their depths—non-aqueous metabolism oxidizing silicates in cool silence turned symphony.

Sound erupts loud, omnipresent, amplified by the vault's parabolic hush: a thunderous chorus of creaks and booms, like tectonic murmurs made intimate. Colossal stalks contract in slow diaphragmatic waves, exhaling gusts that whoosh across voids; crystal walls chime with deep bass thrums as roots micromillimeter-shift, propagating cracks like distant artillery. The noise builds in patient cycles—hours of crescendo from a single frond's unfurling, then fades to hollow roar—never frantic, but relentless, a slow dynamic etched in decibels, gradients of pressure waxing over days.

Time dilates here. Changes crawl: a tower thickens by a fingerwidth yearly, salt-efflorescences migrate in glacial drifts, the vault's humidity (a scant vapor ghosting from mineral capillaries) nucleates fleeting rime that sublimes by dawnless noon. This is the oldest-stable-state, a sealed pocket eternity where the urban-plant weave endures, empty yet total, dark and loud in its vegetal hush. Breathe deep the cool void; listen as the architecture lives.
