Boulders the size of hovels lie tumbled in the bowl's corners, their surfaces polished to a glassy sheen, etched with faint, fractal runes that no storm has erased. The cirque itself is small—no more than a cathedral's nave in expanse, its walls curving inward to form a natural cloister perhaps fifty paces across at the widest, hemmed by sheer cliffs that funnel every gust into whispers.
