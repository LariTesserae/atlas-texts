The world here obeys slow rhythms. No sudden fluxes—no tempests within, no blooming nights—just equilibrium, the pocket as it has been since the last ice retreated, a balanced microcosm embedded in larger dryness, where surface rains sift down as occasional bounty but evaporate before flooding.
