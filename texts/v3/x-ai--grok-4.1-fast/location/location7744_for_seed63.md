The chamber breathes. It is a single vast hollow gnawed into the crown of a nameless peak, high enough that the air thins to a fevered rasp, yet the stone walls sweat heat from below—geothermal veins pulsing like buried arteries, radiating a dry, bone-scorching warmth that turns every breath into a sip of furnace air. No liquid water mars this place; moisture exists only as fleeting vapor ghosts rising from fissures, condensing briefly on jagged obsidian protrusions before evaporating into the stifling haze. The floor is a mosaic of fractured basalt and pumice, etched by eons of subtle gas erosion, its surfaces polished to a dull gleam where ancient flows have settled into equilibrium, unyielding and eternal.

Darkness reigns, thick and absolute, pierced only by the faintest flickers: scattered phosphorescent nodules embedded in the vaulted ceiling, like dying stars trapped in mineral flesh, casting erratic blue-green glimmers that illuminate nothing fully. The space measures no more than a cathedral's nave—perhaps fifty paces across at its widest, hemmed by sheer walls that curve inward to a domed roof forty spans overhead—yet it feels intimately claustrophobic, for there is no empty air here. Every surface pulses with life, saturated to the point of suffocation.

They are everywhere. The fauna dominates, an overwhelming tide of writhing forms that claim every inch: the walls undulate with mats of vermiform crawlers, translucent bodies segmented like heated glass, their countless legs a hypnotic ripple as they grind minerals into chyme for sustenance. Clinging to ceilings and ledges are leathery sacs, gravid with broods of scuttling hatchlings—fist-sized arthropods with mandibles that click mutely, their shells iridescent in the gloom, adapted to this chemosynthetic hell where no sun-fueled green has ever taken root. The floor heaves underfoot with burrowing swarms, pale grubs the length of forearms erupting and submerging in ceaseless cycles of predation, devouring shed carapaces and weaker kin. No plants interrupt this menagerie; vegetation is a rumor—perhaps a few threadlike fungal hyphae probing cracks, feeding on the heat and sulfurous exhalations, but they are ghosts amid the biomass, overwhelmed by the carnivorous churn.

The air thrums with subdued activity, mostly quiet save for the intimate symphony of survival: a velvet hush broken by the papery rasp of chitin on stone, the wet pop of bursting prey-sacs, the faint wheeze of bellows-lungs inflating in the heat. No roars or cries pierce the stillness; these are creatures of vibration and chemosignal, their world a silent frenzy where bodies entwine, rend, and reform. Predation cycles eternally—grubs ambushing crawlers, sacs disgorging young that scatter to feast on the fallen—keeping the density absolute, a living fur that leaves no crevice barren.

Strangeness seeps from the seams. Some fauna defy form: translucent swimmers that aren't—pseudopods gliding through the vapor without need of liquid, phasing briefly into solidity to snatch prey. Others glow internally with heat-lantern organs, lures that mimic the chamber's rare phosphors, drawing victims into maws that unfold like mineral flowers. The erosion here tells of deep time: formations balanced in their decay, fluted columns of anhydrite worn smooth by gas currents, pits where sinkholes have stabilized into perfect funnels for cascading debris—and fauna.

Scattered amid this press are echoes of intrusion: faint carvings on a basaltic pillar, eroded glyphs like spiraling claws, perhaps the work of long-vanished makers who scratched warnings or worship into the rock before fleeing the rising tide of life. A single crumbling archway of dressed stone spans a fissure, its blocks half-buried under crawler mats, home now to a nest of pulsating sacs. No tools remain, no mechanisms hum; this is nature reclaimed, techless and primal.

The chamber evolves moment by moment: vapors curl thicker from a new vent, drawing swarms to gorge on fresh minerals; a sac ruptures overhead, raining hatchlings that skitter into the melee below. Heat builds, shadows dance, the packed masses shift in their endless, quiet war. Stand still too long, and you become part of it—claimed, consumed, eternal.
