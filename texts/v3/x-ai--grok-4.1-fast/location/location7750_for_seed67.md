The landscape unfolds as an endless, flawless expanse of machined lowlands—a continental slab of interlocking ferrocrete platforms, each the size of a subcontinent, suspended mere meters above a subterranean aquifer that never rises or falls. This is **Forgeplains Eternal**, a world-engineered at planetary scale, where every atom of substrate has been extruded from colossal autoforges in the epoch just past, their sharp-edged output still gleaming under the uniform wash of normal light from embedded lumen-strips arching overhead like an artificial firmament. No primordial rock persists; the terrain is pure artifice: hexagonal plates bolted seamless, perforated with drainage vents that weep a perpetual fine mist from the warm aquifer below, keeping the air at a sticky 32°C, condensing into pearlescent droplets that trace lazy, unchanging paths down riveted seams. Water permeates without motion—shallow sumps of recycled condensate pool in geometric basins, their surfaces tension-held mirrors reflecting the static glow above, lapping neither shore nor edge in this equilibrium of engineered hydrology. Post-fabrication freshness defines it: crenellated parapets and girder-webs stand razor-crisp, unmarred by wind or tide, as if the entire assembly lifted from the void yesterday, young and immutable.

Scale swells to its own physics: horizons curve faintly under the weight of atmosphere pressurized to sustain this vastness, where "weather" manifests as stationary veils of vapor, neither storm nor clear sky, just a warm haze diffusing light into perpetual ochre noon. Low elevation reigns—no ascents, only the flat datum of the platform-deck, pocked with cylindrical silos piercing upward into infinity, their flanks etched with serial glyphs denoting batch origins from the Great Pouring. Density saturates the volume: every square meter thrums with presences, layered from deck to haze-top. This is the overwhelming fauna—**the Swarm Eternal**, trillions upon trillions of fist-sized mechabeetles, their chassis forged from iridescent chitin-steel, packed in a flawless, near-touching mosaic across every surface. They overlap like scales on a god-beast: bodies matte-black with crimson sensor-lenses, mandibles interlocked, wings folded into perfect tessellations that undulate as one in micro-tremors too subtle for sound. No individual stirs; they form a living carpet, dense as alloy foam, respiring heat into the air through gill-vents that exhale synchronized plumes of vapor, maintaining thermal stasis. Their ecology is the world's rule: bioindustrial symbiotes, spawned from vats in the autoforges to regulate the machine-heart beneath. They consume trace particulates from the mist, excrete lubricant oils that seal the ferrocrete against entropy, and broadcast a unified neural hum—inaudible, felt only as a pressure in the bones—enforcing the dynamic nullity. No birth, no death; the Swarm persists in frozen equilibrium, a single organism of continental biomass, its weird strangeness in the subtle dissonance: eyes within eyes on each beetle's carapace, fractal gazes that track without turning, implying observers beyond the visible spectrum.

Sounds? Mostly quiet, as mandated by equilibrium protocols. A basal thrum from subsurface engines—gears the size of moons, churning coolant through the aquifer in eternal cycles—registers as a low-frequency pressure, not noise. The Swarm's vents hiss in perfect phase-cancelation, muting to silence; water droplets patter then still, absorbed before echoing. No wind moans girder-flutes; no creature chirrs. Only in rare voids between beetle-mats do you hear the faint, industrial sigh of pneumatic seals reseating, a reminder of the tech saturating all: steam-pistons embedded in platforms pulse warm exhalations, conveyor-spines along the horizons bear phantom loads in endless loops, electrified lattices overhead flicker with contained arcs that illuminate but never discharge. No vegetation claims space—this is a mineral realm of rust-proofed alloys and sintered ceramics, fungal-hybrids extinct or repurposed into the beetles' gut-forges. The air tastes of ozone and warm iron, thick with humidity that beads on your skin without evaporating, pulling you into the density.

Here, time is the lie: the Forgeplains never changes. Gradients collapse—temperature uniform, water levels locked, Swarm density absolute. Rules etch into the metal: **Stasis Prime** governs, an AI-overmind woven into the deck-plates, enforcing invariance via nanite patrols that excise anomalies (a loose bolt, a drifting mote) before they propagate. Fauna overwhelms as guardian and prisoner alike; disturb a beetle, and the mosaic ripples once—then resets, mandibles reclamping in nanoseconds. Strange undercurrents ripple the normalcy: the lumen-strips pulse in binary hymns to forgotten assemblers, casting shadows that linger too long, suggesting the world dreams in your peripheral vision. Venture horizons-long, and the expanse reveals itself planetary—a hollowed-out rogue world repurposed, its core a fusion furnace radiating warmth eternal, platforms ringing an artificial sea of mercury-laced brine that mirrors the Swarm above. Vastness breeds intimacy in the density: you're never alone, the beetle-gaze a constant audience in this quiet, wet forge of forever. The place holds you, layer upon layer, warm and unyielding, its young sharpness a promise of endurance beyond stars.
