The air within is a broth of steam and salt, thick as syrup, pressing inward with the weight of unborn oceans; every breath scalds the lungs, draws moisture from flesh until skin prickles and gleams. Elevation devours context here: beyond the geode's flawless facets, hurricane gales claw at infinity, but inside reigns a breathless hush, broken only by the faintest *plink* of droplets detaching, the merest *hiss* of vapor kissing crystal.

No root dares this kiln, no leaf unfurls; life-as-known withers at the threshold. Instead, the surfaces shimmer with mineral rime—hoarfrost of sulfur and arsenic, fractal lattices of quartz precipitated in an instant, glowing faintly under normal light that bends oddly at the edges, as if the pocket resents illumination. The floor, a dimpled basin no larger than a palm, cradles a meniscus of water: not cool marsh trickle, but a living boil, 98 degrees Celsius, roiling from below where tectonic whim forces plumes of gas through invisible fissures. Bubbles rise in lazy defiance of gravity, which here warps like wet clay—stronger at the center, feebler near walls, so objects teeter on invisible slopes, roll uphill into corners.

And then the fauna: overwhelming, inexorable, a density that saturates every crevice, every facet, every droplet. They are not beasts of fur or scale but *fractals of hunger*, hot-adapted horrors that pulse and swarm in their millions, bodies the size of grains layered upon grains until the space thrums with near-touching masses. Scaled things with too many legs skitter across walls, mandibles clicking inaudibly as they harvest mineral salts, phasing briefly through glass when frenzy overtakes—reality frays here, permits such trespasses; a limb might extrude from a neighbor's flank, borrow a mandible, retract with a *pop* too quiet for ears. Winged motes, iridescent with heat-shimmer, dart in hypnotic flocks, colliding without injury, merging plumes into chimeric blurs that split apart moments later, identities dissolving and reforming. In the water's heart, pseudopods writhe—blind, gelatinous knots that engulf siblings whole, extruding them moments later as distended twins, mass conserved but form multiplied. Predation cycles ceaselessly: a larger crawler impales a flyer on a needle-tooth, absorbs its glow, swells until it bursts into a dozen juveniles, each wielding a sliver of obsidian as primitive tool—hand-sharpened edges for scraping rime, flung in territorial arcs that curve midair due to the gravity-knots. No nests, no territories endure; the pocket is one organism, its rules etched in thermal flux: survive the boil, exploit the phase-shift, propagate through fracture. Births happen in explosive sprays, deaths in silent implosions, bodies recycling into the steam before outlines fade.

Dynamics churn without clamor: the water level rises and falls in micro-tides driven by distant quakes, condensing mists that birth new fractals from spores exhaled by the dying. A central geyser-pimple pulses every 47 seconds—*hiss-plink-throb*—flinging droplets that arc impossibly slow, adhering to walls where they nourish the swarms. Weirdness peaks at the seams: peer too long into a facet and reflections duplicate laterally, infinite crawlers staring back from impossible angles; touch a wall and fingers *stretch*, elongating by millimeters before snapping back, leaving phantom itches. Time stutters here—watch a predator cycle and it repeats the third lunge twice, or skips the regurgitation, birthing malformed young that phase into the stone and emerge elsewhere, altered. The heat amplifies it all, a forge of entropy where sharpness endures: edges honed by birth-pangs of the mountain itself remain lethal, slicing vapor-trails into visible wakes.

This geode-pocket endures as a secret ventricle of the peak, untouched by hand or artifice, its silence a lie masking the frenzy within—intimate, eternal, alive with the raw insistence of a reality unspooling at the seams. Step wrong, and the overwhelming presses you into its rhythm; linger, and the weirdness claims your edges first.
