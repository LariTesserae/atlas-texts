You squeeze through a jagged rent in the sheer obsidian face of the world-spire—a needle of raw basalt thrust three miles skyward, where eagles fear to thermal and clouds congeal into rime before they dare ascend. The crevice seals behind you like a wound knitting, leaving no trace but a whisper of displaced air. This is no cave born of eons; it's an embryonic pouch, a pocket womb excavated and willed into half-life by hands that gripped flint knives and braided gut-thongs not a generation past. Scale collapses here: the space measures no more than an armspan across, yet it heaves with the insistence of a greater world compressed, every inch contested, saturated, no voids permitted.

Heat assaults first—tropical furnace breath, 38 degrees Celsius in the marrow, rising from fissures where the spire's core simmers on unquenched magma dreams. Sweat beads instant on your skin, mingling with the omnipresent wet. Water is the pulse: not pooled but emergent, condensing from a perpetual fog that beads on every surface and refuses to fall true. Walls weep mercury-slick rivulets of mineral-heavy brine, tasting of blood-iron and nascent salt; they trickle in defiance of gravity, spiraling upward to feed hanging bladder-vines that swell and burst in rhythmic gouts, spraying mist that refracts the dim. The floor—if floor it be—is a quivering membrane of precipitating stone, raw geology birthing itself in real time: calcium lattices extruding from the brine like coral polyps in fast-forward, filigreeing into lacework platforms that crackle and reform underfoot. Erosion in reverse; here, form accretes from chaos, primitives' crude chisels guiding the mineral rain into basins and struts, but the process overruns their intent, stone-fingers probing like blind fetuses.

Lushness chokes the air—vegetation at saturation, 90% coverage in this thimble-realm, no bare rock endures. Vines like braided intestines festoon the vault, hand-woven from thorn-ferns and lashed with sinew to sapling struts; they pulse with sap, leaves broad as palms overlapping in a verdant quilt, edges curling wetly to capture every photon. Fern-fronds unfurl from slits chipped in the walls, their spores glowing faint foxfire in the gloom—light level hovering at crepuscular haze, sourced from phosphorescent molds smeared by finger into recesses, and bioluminescent polyps that bud on vine-tips like tribal lamps, flickering erratically. Dimness breeds intimacy; shadows pool thick as oil, hiding the weave until your eyes adjust to the greenish pall.

This is total built: no natural substrate survives the primitives' frenzy. Every ledge a hand-planed shelf of greywacke, every drip-trough gouged by antler adze; vine-lattices are lashed scaffolds supporting clay orbs that capture condensing fog, funneled through bamboo aqueducts split with stone wedges. Tech is Paleolithic poetry—flint-flaked basins, bone awls pinning woven reed filters, ochre-smeared glyphs invoking growth-demons on the curving walls. Yet it's embryonic, unfinished: chisel marks weep fresh powder, half-formed gargoyles of stone leer incomplete from corners, their eyes still milky with birthing brine.

Reality frays at the seams—weirdness absolute, the weave of existence unraveling like poorly spun thread. Time stutter-loops in corners: a droplet detaches from a vine, arcs down, reverses midfall, rejoins its source with a wet *smack* that echoes wrong. Gravity welters—step wrong, and your foot sinks through solid lattice into a softer phase, emerging damp in a neighboring frond. Mirrors of warped space open in the fog: peer into a vine-hollow, glimpse infinite spires receding into hot infinity, then blink and it's a dragonfly's compound eye staring back. Sounds amplify to cacophony, loudscape at fever pitch: *drip-drip-CRASH* of bursting bladders rebounding in Doppler warps; vines *creak-thrash* as they overgrow their lashings in minutes, tearing free with fibrous *riiiips*; geothermal *hiss-gurgle* from below harmonizes with the mineral *crunch-pop* of floor-lattices self-assembling, all underscored by a low reality-hum, like cosmic strings snapping. No silence; the pocket roars intimate, vibrations rattling your teeth.

Dynamic churns ceaseless: an ecosystem in miniature frenzy, processes layered and predatory. Vines duel for light-pools, tendrils strangling rivals with audible *cracks*, sap-blood mingling in the brine to birth fungal blooms that carpet struts overnight. Water cycles manic—condense, swell, burst, infiltrate, re-emerge as geysers from pressure cracks. Rare fauna haunts the din: one translucent beetle-kin, fist-sized, scuttles rarely across the ceiling on sucker-feet, mandibles *clacking* like castanets as it predates glowing polyps; its shed carapace pulses alone in a niche, legs twitching in aborted resurrection. Another life flickers—a slime-worm, pearl-bodied, undulates through vine-weave once per hour, trailing fertility that sparks fern-sprouts. No herds, no flocks; presences solitary, opportunistic, their cycles synced to the pocket's manic beat.

The rules here defy: matter remembers intent—neglect a carved basin, it dissolves back to brine; whisper growth-words (half-remembered from primitive shamans), and lattices thicken under your palm. Air thickens to soup in heat, breath labored, but lungs adapt or drown in humidity. Exit is caprice: the entry-rent phases open only when brine levels crest, forcing immersion through a drowning birth-canal. This hidden forge-of-worlds, forged by flint and fraying sanity, coheres by collective hallucination—step too hard, doubt its edges, and the pocket might unspool, spilling lush ruin down the spire's abyss. It is becoming, insistent, alive in its confinement; stay, and it claims you as substrate.
