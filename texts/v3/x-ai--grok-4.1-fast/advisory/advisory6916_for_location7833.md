### Preparation Guide for Visiting the Phytomantic Basin

Visiting this basin is like stepping into the fevered hallucination of a photosynthetic god— a living, writhing city of hyper-evolved plants where reality itself is a suggestion, not a rule. As an ordinary human multiverse traveler (fit, experienced, but no superpowers or innate magic), expect sensory overload: bone-rattling noise, spatial loops that turn straight lines into mazes, time-warps that make minutes stretch into hours or vice versa, constant damp heat sapping your strength, and an inexorable pull toward "merger" with the biomass. Fauna is negligible (no bugs, beasts, or predators), but the plants *are* the ecology, society, and threat—they sense heat/movement, grow toward it aggressively, regenerate impossibly, and reshape the environment mid-step. Stability is a lie; everything shifts, phases, or surges. Survival hinges on isolation from the green tide: stay cool, quiet, dry, and disorienting to plants while anchoring your sanity and physics.

Plan for **short visits only** (under 2 hours initial; build up tolerance). Use multiversal portal tech for quick in/out—mark your exit point obsessively, as space folds. Budget for **high failure risk**: 70% chance of disorientation/assimilation on first trip without prep. Cost: Mid-tier multiverse kit (~5,000 creds). Below is exhaustive prep by category.

#### 1. **Environmental Protection (Heat, Humidity, Water Hazards)**
   - **Temperature/Humidity**: 85°F+ blood-warm vapor bath. Sweat evaporates poorly; hypothermia risk from constant wetting despite heat.
     - Wear a **full-body PhaseFab nanosuit** (multiversal standard: impermeable to water/vapor, thermoregulating to 72°F internal, self-cleaning). Matte black/non-reflective to minimize light absorption. Integrated cooling loops (powered by kinetic/microfusion cell, 48hr runtime).
     - Backup: **Personal climate bubble** (shoulder-mounted drone, 10ft radius, projects dry 70°F air shield via ionic repulsion). Repels mist/dew; vents excess moisture.
     - Avoid cotton/synthetics that wick/absorb—plants "smell" organic moisture.
   - **Water Management**: Everywhere slick, pooling, surging. Sinkholes/mists can drown or erode footing.
     - **GripMax boots** (vibro-textured soles, auto-adjust treads for moss/resin/quartz; magnetic/electro-adhesion for verticals).
     - **Hydro-repulsor field** (wristband emitter: creates 1-inch personal dry zone, pushes water droplets away; synergizes with climate bubble).
     - Carry **desal purifier pod** (processes ambient vapor if needed, but prefer pre-packed H2O rations—local water carries bioluminescent spores that induce fractal hallucinations).
