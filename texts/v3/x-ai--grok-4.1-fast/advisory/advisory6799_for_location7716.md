### Physical Preparation: Acclimating to the Zenith's Cruel Equilibrium

Visiting the City of Fractured Pinnacles demands treating your body as a fragile intruder into a realm that rejects organic frailty. Altitude sickness hits fast: expect cerebral edema within hours without prep, compounded by the dry rasp of ozone-laced wind that desiccates lungs and skin like a perpetual sirocco.

- **Pre-Travel Acclimatization**: Spend 2-4 weeks in a hyperbaric chamber simulating 8,000-10,000m elevations (higher than any Earth peak), with 10-15% oxygen mix and <20% humidity. Follow with high-altitude training hikes on sheer exposure routes (e.g., Trango Towers replicas or VR vertigo sims). Hydrate aggressively—aim for 6-8L/day pre-trip with electrolyte packs laced with desiccants to mimic the mineral tang. Test for hypoxia tolerance; if your SpO2 drops below 80% at rest, abort or gene-therapize RBC production.
  
- **Immunizations and Augments**: No pathogens here, but inoculate against exotic mineral pneumoconiosis (silica/quartzite dust analogs). Consider temporary cyberware: subdermal oxygen reservoirs (e.g., micro-alveoli implants releasing O2 on demand) and humidity membranes for nasal cavities to combat mucosal cracking. Epoxy-skin sealant for micro-abrasions from wind-sculpted particulates.

- **Fitness Baseline**: Peak VO2 max (60+ ml/kg/min), core strength for belay hangs (deadhang 2min+ on 20mm edges), and proprioceptive training (balance boards on uneven, warping sim-rigs). Train for "exposure fatigue"—prolonged vertigo drills where horizons curve falsely via goggles.
