### Overview: Understanding the Heartpump Nexus
Visiting the Heartpump Nexus feels like plunging into the fossilized heart of a drowned machine-god—a pitch-black, syrup-thick abyss where industrial decay has fused with abyssal biology into an eldritch, self-perpetuating organism. Motion is sluggish, like swimming in weighted molasses; darkness swallows you after arm's length except for faint bioluminescent flickers; silence muffles everything to whispers, broken by hydraulic sighs and subsonic crustal groans. Machinery pulses ceaselessly—pistons churning ores, dynamos humming, currents looping in slow gyres—while subtle weirdness (hallucinogenic water, twitching alloys, glyph-vapors) gnaws at your sanity. As an ordinary human, unprotected exposure means death in seconds: hypothermia, drowning, crushing pressure, or disorienting madness. Survival demands total encapsulation, redundant life support, and hyper-specialized gear. Plan for 1-4 hour dives max; longer risks suit fatigue or brine corrosion. Budget: $500K+ for custom multiverse-grade kit (scavenge or fabricate via traveler networks).
