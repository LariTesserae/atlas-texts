### Preparation Guide for Visiting the Obsidian Spire Chamber

Visiting this chamber—a freshly calved, reality-fraying maelstrom of submerged geological fury—is not an adventure; it's a survival ordeal that will test every limit of human physiology, psychology, and sanity. Imagine plunging into the heart of a volcanic birth pangs crossed with a Lovecraftian fever dream: chest-deep in a blood-warm, ink-shot mercury that defies up/down, battered by sideways water-walls, deafened by eternal apocalypse-roar, while the ground twitches like a living wound and your own future echoes mock you from the fluid. An ordinary human (no superpowers, just grit and gear) has a slim chance of enduring 10-30 minutes without prep; unprepared, you'd drown in disorientation, shredded by rocks, or unraveled by warping physics in seconds. **Priority: Short reconnaissance only. Have an emergency recall beacon prepped—test it in sims mimicking 2.7 turbulence and 2.6 reality shear.**

Preparation breaks into **essentials** (must-haves for bare survival), **optimals** (to function beyond panic), and **exotics** (multiverse-sourced if accessible, for deeper exploration). Budget for redundancy—everything fails in flux. Train in hyperbaric chambers, zero-g sims, sensory deprivation tanks, and chaos VR approximating the sound/light/turbulence combo. Physical conditioning: 6 months of ultra-endurance (free-diving, rock-climbing, cold/hot immersion) to handle the "nurturing fever" warmth clashing with razor basalt and gravity hiccups.

     | Backup: Portable gill-implants or aqualung mouthpiece with auto-deploy hood. | Biocompatible, 30-min emergency. | For breaches; fluid erodes little but clogs filters fast. |

   - **Training**: 100+ hours free-diving in warm, turbid, high-flow pools with artificial currents (e.g., Red Bull Cliff Diving sims + wave machines). Practice "flux breaths"—slow exhales against bubble-drag.

   - **Primary Threat**: Sparse density (vast emptiness), reconfiguring chamber, fauna nibbles (dartlings/eel).

#### Mission Protocol
1. **Entry**: Beam-in at spire base (avoid flux-rifts). Anchor immediately.
2. **Duration**: 15-min max first trip. Objectives: Sample phosphor-weed, scan fossils, log warps.
3. **Exit**: Beacon if >5-min disorientation, suit breach, or "regret smell" (ozone cue).
4. **Post-Visit**: Debrief in quiet—expect nightmares, time-lag jetlag. Quarantine for fluid residue (mutagenic?).
