### Physical Preparation: Gear and Attire

- **Primary Suit**: A lightweight, fully sealed hazmat-style biosuit rated for extreme humidity (IP68+ waterproofing) and biohazards (Level C or higher). Sealable gloves, hood, and booties essential—zippers or magnetic seals that self-clean. Include anti-microbial silver nanoparticle lining to fend off synthetic bacteria from dissolving corpses. - **Head and Face Protection**: Full-face visor with anti-fog HEPA filtration (P100 filters for midges, spores, and mist). Integrated HUD for augmented reality overlays: light amplification, fauna mapping, and vital monitoring. Polarized lenses to cut biolum glare from aquamarine filaments.

- **Respiratory**: Powered air-purifying respirator (PAPR) backpack unit. The thin alpine air (1.7 high, ~70% Earth sea-level pressure) simulates 4,000-5,000m elevation—expect mild hypoxia and dizziness. Supplement with 21% O2 boost from chamber moss (sparse 0.6 vegetation), but carry a portable O2 concentrator or mini-tank for 30-60 minutes. Filters handle ionized salts and enzymatic vapors.
