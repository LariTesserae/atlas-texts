### Preparing for the Underswarm: A Traveler's Guide for Ordinary Humans

Visiting the Underswarm feels like stepping into the planet's still-beating heart—a colossal, breathing void where time forgets to march and the boundary between self and surrounding blurs into a hypnotic hum. Imagine vast chambers dwarfing cathedrals, their jagged ceilings lost in stratified gloom, lit only by synchronized blue pulses from glowing crystals that make shadows dance like living ink. The air clings warm and dry, tasting of blood and brimstone, while the ground thrums with subsonic life: carpets of writhing tubes, herds of gliding torpedoes, and distant tripods that *call* without sound. It's serene equilibrium masking peril—sharp rocks that slice careless skin, creatures blind but attuned to your every vibration, and a weird psychic pull that whispers *stay, join, synchronize*. An ordinary human (no cyberware, no innate multiversal gifts) will feel dwarfed, disoriented, and inexorably drawn in. Survival hinges on isolation: from the sharpness, the dryness, the dark, the *hum*. Prepare to be a ghost in the machine of resonance, observing without resonating. Underestimate it, and you'll phase-lock into the swarm, your nerves singing in eternal chorus.

Expect a 3-7 day expedition (subjective; time dilation may stretch surface years). Entry via surface shafts or fissures demands initial descent gear (grapples, pitons), but the Underswarm proper requires hyper-specialized kit. Budget for custom fabrication if sourcing from multiversal traders—nothing off-the-shelf from Earth analogs works here. Total prep cost: high (tech-heavy), but reusable for similar deep-void biomes.
