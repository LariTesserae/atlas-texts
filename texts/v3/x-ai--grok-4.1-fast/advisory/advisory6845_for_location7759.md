### Preparation Guide for Visiting the Mycelial Supercontinent

Visiting this living, fungal-engineered expanse—let's call it the Mycelial Sheet for shorthand—feels like stepping onto the skin of a colossal, breathing organism that's only half-formed. It's not hostile like a predator world, but indifferent and subtly invasive: a hot, humid incubator where everything pulses with microbial ambition, and human fragility stands out like a misplaced spark. As an ordinary multiverse traveler (no superpowers, just grit and gear), your prep needs to prioritize **heat tolerance**, **biological isolation**, **self-sufficiency**, and **mental endurance** against the creeping sense of geological infancy. Expect disorientation from the scale—kilometers of near-identical terrain warping time into a slow, fleshy crawl. Plan for 1-7 day excursions max; longer requires a mobile habitat. Here's a breakdown:

#### 1. **Clothing and Personal Protection (Seal Yourself In)**
   - **Full-body environmental suit**: Opt for a lightweight, phase-change material (PCM) hazmat suit rated for 100°F+ with high humidity (e.g., Gore-Tex Pro or multiverse equivalent like adaptive nanoweave). It should be breathable yet impermeable to spores, biofilms, and volatiles—think Level 4 NBC (nuclear/biological/chemical) with positive-pressure HEPA filtration. Helmets with polarized visors to cut mirage glare and prismatic dew-shines; integrated cooling vests circulating ice-water or thermoelectric chillers.
     - Why? Sweat evaporates fast but leaves salt; suits wick moisture internally and prevent "fungal logic" infiltration (spores colonizing fabrics/skin, inducing subtle hallucinations or lethargy from geosmin/ozone overload).
     - Add sealed gloves/boots with vibro-grips for the gelatinous yield—surfaces rebound, so avoid slips on pseudopods or dew-traps.
   - **Layering**: Moisture-wicking base (merino/synthetic), UV-protective midlayer, outer shell with anti-microbial silver threading. Backup: Disposable Tyvek over-suits for high-spore zones (twilight/night releases).
   - **UV/Solar**: Broad-spectrum sunblock (SPF 100+ reapply hourly), lip balm, and neck gaiters. The relentless sun bakes through thin air—no ozone layer feels "uniform blue" deceptive.

#### 2. **Hydration, Nutrition, and Electrolytes (Beat the Solar Furnace)**
   - **Water**: Carry 10-15 liters/person/day in insulated bladder-packs with purification straws (UV/electrolysis for microbial soup). The air's humidity teases but doesn't hydrate—dew-traps are spore-laden traps.
     - Tech: Atmospheric water generators (pull 5L/day from humid air) or solar stills. Test all water: boils or filters catch fungal cysts.
   - **Electrolytes/Salts**: High-sodium tabs (e.g., LMNT or homemade: salt/sugar/potassium mix) every 30-60 min. Humidity accelerates salt loss; expect cramps/headaches without.
   - **Food**: High-calorie, low-water compact rations (freeze-dried meals, energy gels, nuts). Avoid fresh produce—vegan/MRE-style to dodge allergies. Caloric intake: 4,000+/day from heat expenditure.
     - Supplements: Multivitamins, B-complex (for geosmin stress), antihistamines (ozone irritation).
   - **Pro Tip**: Time meals for dawn/dusk; midday "bake" suppresses appetite but ramps dehydration.

#### 3. **Mobility and Navigation (Cross the Living Desert)**
   - **Footwear/Terrain**: High-traction boots with puncture-resistant soles (terrain cracks into plates, yields like cartilage). Trekking poles with sensors for probing subsurface rhizomes—avoid stepping on throbbing veins (peristaltic surprises).
     - Vehicle: Lightweight rover or e-bike with fat tires for fibrous flats/domes (20-30m hills). Solar-charged, dust-proof; dust devils scatter spores.
   - **Navigation**: GPS jammed by biogenic interference? Use inertial trackers, star-compasses (clear nights), or drone scouts. Mark domes/ridges as waypoints— they're sparse landmarks in 80% emptiness.
     - Maps: Pre-scan orbital imagery; on-site, hyperspectral drone for mycelial "roads" (rhizome threads).
     - Pace: 2-3 km/hr walking; mirages spawn phantom spires—trust instruments over eyes.
   - **Distances**: Supercontinent scale—pack for 50km loops; resupply drones for longer.

#### 4. **Shelter and Rest (Claim a Dome, Briefly)**
   - **Habitat**: Inflatable geodesic dome (3-4 person) with mylar insulation, AC unit (thermoelectric/solar), and airlock. Anchor to fungal crust (it grips like velcro).
     - Why not camp on-surface? Elastic rebound + night dew = soggy, spore-infested bed. Elevate cots 1m off-ground.
   - **Night Setup**: Twilight spore-mists saccharine-thick—seal tight. Dewfall carves micro-canyons; position leeward of domes for thermals.
   - **Duration**: Sleep in 4-hr shifts; biological "clockwork" (groans, plinks) disrupts deep rest.

#### 5. **Health and Medical Kit (Counter the Invisible Empire)**
   - **Core Kit**: Broad-spectrum antifungals (fluconazole/amphotericin), antibiotics (for bacterial biofilms), steroids (swelling from volatiles). Epinephrine auto-injector (anaphylaxis risk from lichen pollen).
     - Bio-monitors: Wearable O2/CO2 sensors, spore counters, EEG for "fungal logics" (drowsiness/hallucinations from inhaled yeast).
   - **Hazards Table**:
     | Hazard | Symptoms | Mitigation |
     |--------|----------|------------|
     | Heatstroke | Dizziness, nausea | Cooling packs, shade every 30min |
     | Spore Inhalation | Cough, mind-wander (slow-time feels) | N95+ masks, nasal rinses |
     | Skin Breach | Weeping slurry infection | Wound sealant, no cutting surfaces |
     | Ozone/Geosmin | Eye/throat burn, earthy "high" | Eye drops, activated charcoal |
     | Elastic Terrain | Sprains from rebound | Padded joints, slow steps |
   - **Vaccines/Immuno**: Pre-boost fungal allergies (if multiverse clinic available); probiotics to crowd out locals.
   - **Evac**: Beacon with 1-hr drone pickup—don't push symptoms.

#### 6. **Tools and Equipment (Interact Without Disturbing)**
   - **Essentials**: Multitool (no blades—use probes), headlamp (prismatic nights glow), binoculars (spot distant throbs), audio recorder (capture creaks for study).
     - Science Kit: Petri dishes, swab kits, portable microscope (document growth—ethics: minimal sampling).
     - Power: Solar blankets (endless sun), hand-crank backups.
   - **Comms**: Long-range radio (thermals interfere); log video for multiverse share.
   - **Ethics/Leave-No-Trace**: No fires (flammable volatiles), minimal footprints (mycelium heals but track mud). Take only pics/data.

#### 7. **Psychological and Temporal Prep (Endure the Slow Pulse)**
   - **Mindset**: Isolation amplifies subtlety—vast quietude echoes internally, fungal "patience" warps your urgency into futility. It's meditative horror: a world building itself sans you.
     - Prep: Meditation apps for heat-induced irritability; podcasts for sound-masking (drip-schlurp gets hypnotic).
     - Group Travel: Solo risky (cabin fever from infinity); 2-4 people with defined roles.
   - **Time Dilation**: Human days feel eternal—schedule micro-goals (e.g., "reach next dome by noon").
   - **Exit Strategy**: Set hard timers; the Sheet "claims" lingerers psychologically (dreams of expansion).

#### Sample 24-Hour Itinerary (Day 1 Scout)
- **Dawn (coolest)**: Suit up, hydrate, drone-map 10km radius.
- **Midday**: Short traverses (dome-to-dome), sample air/soil.
- **Twilight**: Shelter, log data, spore-watch.
- **Night**: Monitor throbs, rest.

**Total Kit Weight**: 20-30kg/person (backpack + rover). Cost: Mid-tier multiverse survival (~5k credits). Intuition: Thrilling yet humbling—like hiking a giant mushroom's scalp in a steam room. You'll leave salty, spore-scented, with a profound respect for microbial eternity. Safe travels—don't let it grow on you.
