It's not a place to "enter" like a cave; it's a microscopic kiln of entropy, thrumming with fractal horrors in breathless silence. Your body won't fit without radical alteration. The hush amplifies horror—every *plink-hiss-throb* echoing in your skull amid overwhelming density. Survival means becoming a ghost-observer: shrunken, shielded, stabilized. Linger too long, and the pocket claims you, unraveling your form into its rhythm.
