### Essential Preparations for Visiting the Ferrokrill Forge Chamber

The space feels like swimming inside a deafening, vibrating engine block: sharp-as-glass rocks everywhere, hydrothermal vents spewing heat/chemicals, and a haze of thumb-sized critters clacking, whumping, and zreeeing at industrial volumes, capable of latching onto you with electric jolts and "integrating" mineral-rich flesh into their nutrient cycles.

Visiting means total immersion in dense life (they're *everywhere*, brushing like a solid fog), lethal sharpness, sensory overload, and slow-time weirdness where hours drag like days. Survival odds without prep: minutes, via drowning, hypothermia, lacerations, swarm suffocation, or bioelectric paralysis. With prep, you could explore for hours/days. Here's a comprehensive guide, prioritized by threat level. Budget: 5,000-50,000 credits across multiversal markets (tech from aqua-worlds like Rapture analogs or deep-sea outposts; bio-mods from biotech hubs).

Projects a breathable air shell sustained by an internal O2 recycler (nano-algae tanks last 12 hours).    - **Extreme Option: Temporary Gill Implant**. Bioprinted neck slits (dissolvable after 48 hours) from merfolk medics. Allows direct brine breathing but risks mineral buildup in lungs (counter with chelation tabs). Risky for norms—hallucinations from ferrous tang reported.
   - **Duration Tip**: Prep for 24-72 hours minimum. The chamber's "diurnal pulses" sync to light shafts; enter at "dawn" (max light) for visibility.
