### Physical Protection and Environmental Suit
As an ordinary human, the primary killer here is the **intense, unrelenting heat**—a constant 2.7 forge-glow that radiates like being inside a kiln at 800–1200°C (1470–2200°F) ambient, with hotter vents belching metallic steam. Your skin would blister in seconds unprotected, sweat evaporates instantly in the arid plasma air (no humidity, just superheated silicates that taste like hot copper on your tongue), leading to hyperthermia, organ failure, and hallucinations within minutes. Vision swims from heat haze, and the 2.4-bright unfiltered sunlight shaft acts like a laser, potentially causing instant retinal burns or snow blindness.

**Essential Gear:**
- **Full-body hazmat/exploration suit rated for extreme thermal environments**, e.g., a modified NASA EMU or industrial furnace suit upgraded with:
  - Multi-layer insulation: Outer aerogel/reflective Mylar shell (mirrors 99% radiant heat), middle phase-change material lining (absorbs/melts at 60°C to wick heat), inner liquid cooling garment (circulates ice water via backpack reservoir).
  - Self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) with 4–6 hour oxygen supply, plus CO2 scrubbers. The air is thin (high-elevation compression at 2.1, like 8,000m+), arid plasma (ionized, metallic tang), and laced with toxic vapors (copper, iron, ozone)—no filtering mask suffices; you need sealed positive-pressure.
  - Built-in hydration system: 5L insulated bladder with electrolyte tabs, sipping tube inside helmet. Dehydration hits fast—no water cycle here, so carry extra for vapor extraction if tech allows (but don't rely on it).
  - Sealed boots with thick vibram soles and thermal barriers; the floor is fracturing basalt flexing like membrane, hot enough to melt rubber.
- **Helmet/visor**: Polarized, UV/IR-filtering ballistic glass with HUD overlay (temperature, O2 levels, vitals). Anti-fog coatings essential against heat-mirages and sizzling glass droplets.
- **Backup cooling**: Portable phase-change vest inserts and a small dry-ice pellet dispenser for emergency chill bursts.
- **Total weight**: 40–60kg; train for mountaineering with load beforehand.

**Test in sim**: Spend days in a 200°C dry-heat chamber with gear to acclimate; simulate metallic steam with aerosolized iron filings.
