### Overview of the Visit
Visiting this volcanic basin—let's call it the Eternal Thrum for shorthand—will immerse you in a primordial hellscape that's equal parts geothermal spa, biblical plague, and frozen diorama. Imagine a vast, shallow crater that's brand new from a volcanic event: razor-sharp black obsidian and basalt everywhere, ankle-deep warm mineral water seeping constantly, 85°F heat with 100% humidity turning the air into a perpetual steam room sweat-lodge. Light is perpetual twilight under fog and insect clouds, sound is a wall of unrelenting noise (think chainsaw orchestra mixed with a million dripping faucets and animal shrieks, non-stop), and life is a hyper-dense crush of Earth-like animals (huge swarms of bugs, bats, dogs, rodents) brushing against everything in endless motion. A primitive stone-age city of thousands of stacked basalt huts sprawls across it, built by shadowy humans mid-activity.

The killer feature: **total stasis**. Nothing changes. Animals don't eat/kill/die/birth; rocks don't erode; buildings don't collapse; humans don't react. Time is "frozen equilibrium"—you'll feel it immediately, like wading into cosmic molasses where your thoughts slow and motions arrest into the "eternal now." As an ordinary human, you'll be overwhelmed sensorily before the stasis grips you. Expect disorientation, exhaustion, potential infection/irritation from the bio-crush, cuts from glass-sharp rocks, and psychological unraveling from the sameness. Goal: Survive 1-2 hours max, observe, extract. Prep assumes you have multiverse travel tech (e.g., a portal beacon for exit), but you're "ordinary" so no superpowers—just practical gear.

### 1. Clothing and Physical Protection (Seal Yourself In)
This place chews through exposed skin like a grinder. Sharp rocks lacerate feet/legs; water soaks everything (ferrous minerals stain and irritate); fauna crawls/bites/brushes constantly (insects pierce fabric; larger animals shoulder you).

- **Base Layer**: Moisture-wicking synthetic long underwear (merino wool or CoolMax) to manage sweat without chafing. Avoid cotton—it retains moisture and mildews instantly.
- **Mid Layer**: Lightweight, breathable long-sleeve shirt and pants (nylon/spandex, e.g., fishing or tactical gear like Frogg Toggs). Seal wrists/ankles with elastic cuffs or tape.
- **Outer Shell**: Full-body rainsuit or hazmat-style coverall (Tyvek or Gore-Tex, waterproof/breathable). Add a hood with drawstring. Imperative: **insect-proof mosquito netting suit** over everything—head net with bite-proof mesh (0.5mm holes max), extending to gloves. Test for full enclosure; bugs are thumbnail-sized and relentless.
- **Footwear**: High-ankle mountaineering boots with aggressive Vibram soles for grip on slick basalt/plinths (obsidian shatters like glass—expect micro-cuts). Neoprene booties underneath for water immersion. Gaiters to mid-thigh to block water/insects/crevices.
- **Hands/Head**: Puncture-resistant gloves (leather-palm nitrile). Balaclava under netting. Wide-brim hat or helmet with brim to shed drips.
- **Extras**: Knee/elbow pads for crawling/slipping. Duct tape rolls for quick repairs/seals.

Pack light (20-30L backpack, waterproofed). Total weight: <15lbs to avoid fatigue in heat.

### 2. Protection Against Fauna and Biohazards
Density is insane—think Times Square at rush hour, but all animals. Insects form "humming veils" dimming light; bats/rodents/dogs swarm paths/rooftops. Stasis means no attacks (no hunger/aggression), but physical contact transfers filth/parasites. Microbial crusts could harbor extremophiles.

- **Repellents**: DEET 100% (permethrin-treat all clothes pre-trip). Picaridin for skin. Citronella useless here—too humid/volatile.
- **Barriers**: As above (netting/suit). Add a **face veil/shield** (plexiglass mosquito mask) with anti-fog coating.
- **Respiratory**: **Full-face respirator or PAPR (Powered Air-Purifying Respirator)** with P100 filters for miasma, chitin dust, steam. Bugs fly into mouths/noses; acrid forge smoke and hydrothermal gases (H2S possible) irritate lungs. Backup: N95 masks x10.
- **Medical Kit**:
  | Item | Quantity | Why |
  |------|----------|-----|
  | Broad-spectrum antibiotics (doxycycline, cipro) | 10-day course | Microbial infections from cuts/water. |
  | Antihistamines (Benadryl), hydrocortisone cream | Ample | Bug bites, allergic reactions. |
  | Tetanus booster (pre-trip), wound antiseptic (iodine wipes) | Full kit | Sharp rocks = puncture risks. |
  | Electrolyte tabs, Imodium | 20+ | Diarrhea from mineral water. |
  | Epinephrine auto-injector | 2 | Anaphylaxis from unknown bugs/microbes. |
  | Painkillers (ibuprofen), anti-nausea (meclizine) | High dose | Overload headaches, motion sickness from din. |

Vaccinate pre-trip: Tetanus, hep A/B (waterborne), rabies (dog/bat contact).

### 3. Sensory Protection (Combat Overload)
- **Noise (2.6 intensity—deafening drone)**: Custom-molded earplugs (30-40dB NRR) under **over-ear noise-canceling headphones** (e.g., Bose QC45 on max ANC). Loop a 20-min playlist of white noise or silence to buffer. Without, it'll vibrate your skull and induce vertigo/anxiety in minutes.
- **Light (1.2 haze—dim twilight)**: **LED headlamp** (1000+ lumens, red mode for night vision) + waist/chest floodlights. Fog scatters light—use tight beams. Backup batteries (lithium, waterproof).
- **Smell/Taste**: Respirator filters handle sulfur/mud/decay (scavenger feces everywhere).

### 4. Hydration, Nutrition, and Heat Management
Constant 85°F/100% RH = hyperthermia risk fast. Sweat loss: 2L/hour.

- **Water**: 4L in insulated bladder (with purification tablets/straw—boil if possible, but no fire). Drink electrolytes hourly (Nuun tabs). No local water—ferrous/toxic.
- **Food**: High-energy gels/bars (caffeine-laced for alertness). Avoid solids—noise/din kill appetite.
- **Cooling**: Cooling towel (soak in carried water), neck gaiter. Monitor core temp with forehead thermometer.

### 5. Navigation, Mobility, and Tools
Alleys are "choked slits," paths "slick with moisture," baffles divide districts. No maps—use GPS wristwatch (e.g., Garmin) with offline topo (pre-download basin coords).

- **Mobility**: Trekking poles (carbon, with carbide tips for basalt grip). Compass + altimeter.
- **Tools** (primitive-friendly, no metals to avoid anachronism):
  - Multi-tool (Leatherman, but stone-wrapped if interacting).
  - Paracord 100ft, carabiners for climbing stacks.
  - Flint/ferro rod for emergency signal (not fire—too wet/smoky).
- **Documentation**: GoPro (waterproof, chest-mounted) for hands-free recording. Stasis might glitch electronics—hardened case.

### 6. Psychological and Temporal Preparation (The Stasis Trap)
This is the real horror: "Your own motions arrested... woven into the dense... unending now." Sensory equilibrium warps time perception—you'll feel eternity in minutes. Intuition: It's a reality-anchor where gradients (motion, change) flatten; your brain syncs to the "thrum."

- **Mental Prep**:
  - Meditate daily pre-trip: Focus on breath amid noise (use the din recordings online—simulate with fans/bug zappers/dog barks).
  - Set micro-goals: "Walk 10min to plaza, observe 5min, return."
  - Affirmation: "I am transient; my time flows independently."
- **Anti-Stasis Tech** (ordinary multiverse hacks):
  - **Personal Beacon**: Wrist-mounted timer/portal anchor (e.g., quantum tether device—standard traveler gear). Set auto-extract after 60min.
  - Stimulants: Caffeine pills, modafinil (low-dose) to fight mental slowdown.
  - Grounding Object: Pocket talisman (photo of home) to remind of external time.
- **Exit Plan**: Mark entry point with glowsticks/chemical markers. Never go deep—stick to edges. If stasis grips (limbs heavy, thoughts loop), activate beacon verbally/shake.
