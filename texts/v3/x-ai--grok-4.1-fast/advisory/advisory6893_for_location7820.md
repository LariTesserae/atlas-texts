### Preparation Guide for Visiting the Unyielding Forge

As an ordinary human multiverse traveler—fit, trained, but without innate superhuman abilities—you're stepping into a meticulously engineered hellscape: a frozen, roaring, entropic void masquerading as an industrial cathedral. This isn't a hike or a dive; it's a brief, high-risk immersion into a pocket of manufactured stasis where physics bends to reject change, and human frailty is the only variable. **Do not visit without full preparation; unprepared exposure means death by hypothermia, barotrauma, sensory overload, or psychological fracture within minutes.** Plan for a visit of **no more than 10-15 minutes** maximum—enough to witness, not to linger. Longer risks "rapport" with the place: heartbeat entrainment, causality glitches imprinting on your mind, or spores integrating into your gear.

Intuitively, standing there feels like being shrink-wrapped inside a perpetually climaxing factory explosion: bone-deep cold knifing your core, a roar that vibrates your eyeballs and drowns your soul, machines looming like indifferent gods in shadowless glare, faint green lichen whispering "stay forever." It's intimate terror—sparse enough to walk freely amid the giants, but the stasis claims you subtly. Prepare as if assaulting an alien reactor core at the edge of reality.

#### 1. **Physical and Medical Preparation (Pre-Travel Health Check)**
   - **Baseline Fitness**: Be in peak condition—VO2 max >50 ml/kg/min, no cardiac issues, eustachian tube function verified. Get a full medical scan for hypoxia susceptibility (high-altitude ghosts linger here). Acclimatize to -50°C cold chambers and 120dB noise simulators for 48 hours prior.
   - **Vaccinations/Immunos/Inoculations**: No known pathogens, but inoculate against heavy-metal volatilization from euvein (mercury/cadmium analogs). Broad-spectrum nanites for plasma radical neutralization. Anti-spore prophylaxis: ingest 500mg aerogel-bound fullerene suppressants 24h prior—they'll orbit and neutralize airborne euvein propagules.
   - **Psych Eval**: Screen for claustrophobia, sensory processing disorders, or temporal disorientation (e.g., jet lag PTSD). Practice 30-min sessions in a "stasis simulator": uniform cold (-80°C), white noise at 130dB, monochromatic light, with looping video of unchanging machinery. Use microdosing of nootropics (e.g., 5mg phenylpiracetam) to sharpen focus against weirdness.
   - **Travel Sickness Kit**: Dimenhydrinate (50mg) + scopolamine patch for phantom hypoxia/vertigo from altitude seal-memory. IV saline pre-load (1L) to counter xenon desiccation.

#### 3. **Entry and Navigation Protocol**
   - **Approach**: Verify hatch integrity—seamless pressure-seal at 28k m altitude means megastructure flank climb or portal-drop. Use vacuum-rated drone scout first: confirm iris-shutter cycles (0.5s open/close).
   - **Ingress**:
     1. Suit-check: All seals green, thermal loop primed, ANC online.
     2. Breach: Palm remote hatch-override (multiversal decryptor probe); step through in <3s—cold shock hits instantly.
     3. Immediate: Anchor tether to inner hatch-lip. HUD-lock hatch vector (40m straight-shot return).
     4. Orient: Face entry quadrant. Machines sparse—six forges perimeter, central conveyors/gears. Avoid cables (infinity-loops snag suits).
   - **Inside Rules**:
     | Action | Allowed? | Why/How |
     |--------|----------|----------|
     | Touch Machines | NO | Causality snapback lacerates gloves; phasing teeth clip fingers. Observe 5m min. |
     | Sample Euvein | Micro-only (vacuum syringe) | Spores orbit eternally—suit filters catch most, but quarantine gear post-visit. |
     | Speak/Shout | Useless | 140dB drowns all; subvocal log thoughts via HUD. |
     | Sit/Lean | NO | Stasis claims—stand dynamic to fight entrainment. Walk counter-clockwise helix to mimic conveyor psychology. |
     | Duration | ≤10min | Timer alarm escalates: vibrate at 5min, strobe at 10min. Exit at beep. |
   - **Weirdness Mitigations**:
     - Heartbeat sync: HUD monitors BPM (target 60-80 vs. roar's ~17Hz modulation)—beta-blockers (propranolol 20mg patch) pre-loaded.
     - Shadow prefiguring: Train peripheral vision drills; don't chase "ghost sparks."
     - Breath vortices: Disable suit vents; they become haunting trails.

#### 4. **Emergency and Exit Procedures**
   - **Egress**: Reverse ingress—tether-reel to hatch, signal iris-open (suit-mounted laser cutter backup). If stuck (1% causality glitch risk), deploy shaped-charge breacher (nano-foam expander).
   - **Hazards Tiered Response**:

   - **Decon Post-Visit**: Full suit autoclave (200°C plasma arc). Quarantine 72h for metal volatilization blood tests. Psych debrief: EMDR for roar-PTSD.

#### 5. **Logistics and Mindset**
   - **Team**: Solo risky—bring tethered partner at hatch. Multiverse gate on standby for extract.
   - **Cost/Time**: Prep 1 week, visit 1h round-trip. Budget: 100k credits (suit rental dominant).
   - **Why Go?**: Witness god-industry's stasis—profound, but prepare to flee its baptism. Intuition: You'll emerge shivering, ears humming, tasting metal, forever changed—**the roar never fully fades.**

Pack light, suit up tight, enter quick, witness, egress. The Forge endures; you won't if you don't prepare. Safe travels, traveler.
