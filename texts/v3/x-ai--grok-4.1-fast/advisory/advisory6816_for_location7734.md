### Overview: Understanding the Visit
Visiting this pocket—a fist-sized void in a nascent mountain throat, jammed full of a defying, hyper-dense oceanlet at hypoxic heights—will feel like plunging into the raw birth canal of a geologic god. You're not exploring a cave or diving a reef; this is a self-contained maelstrom of embryonic stone, perpetual submersion, and physics-bending weirdness, where water thrashes like a living beast, crystals erupt and shatter in seconds, and a deafening roar obliterates your sanity. An ordinary human lasts minutes here at best: the chill numbs you, turbulence slams you, noise vibrates your skull, and the chaos could trap you as "next artifact." Preparation isn't optional—it's survival engineering. Expect 5-10 minutes max inside before hypoxia, disorientation, or a vortex claims you. Goal: Witness the spectacle (prismatic light on birthing rocks, spectral algae, primitive runes) without becoming sediment. Prep time: 2-4 weeks minimum, blending mountaineering, cave diving, and extremophile survival.

### Physical Conditioning and Health Prep
Your body must endure high-altitude hypoxia (air thins to whispers), bone-chilling 1.2 immersion (like glacial fog turned liquid, ~4-6°C/39-43°F), and G-forces from 2.7 turbulence (slams equivalent to whitewater rapids in a blender).
- **Altitude Acclimation**: Spend 1-2 weeks at 4,000-6,000m (13,000-20,000ft) equivalents (e.g., Bolivian Andes or Kilimanjaro base camps). Train with hypoxic tents or masks to boost red blood cell count. Get a full medical: EKG, bloodwork for anemia/heart issues. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy pre-trip to preload oxygen.
- **Cold Tolerance**: Ice baths daily (progress to 30min at 4°C), neoprene immersion training. Build fat reserves with high-calorie diet (keto-heavy for hypothermia resistance). Train breath-holds in cold water to simulate meniscus air-sips.
- **Strength/Endurance**: HIIT with resistance (weighted vest climbs), core for turbulence, grip strength for quartz prongs. Yoga for flexibility in tight squeezes. Aim for VO2 max >50ml/kg/min.
- **Inoculations/Vitals**: Tetanus booster (raw stone cuts). Antibiotics for potential infection from isopod scratches or relic scrapers. Carry personal AED if cardiac history.

- **Dive Primary**: Closed-circuit rebreather (CCR) like rEvo or Optimum Inspiration (O2 scrubbers for 30-60min). Why CCR? Open-circuit bubbles disrupt weird vortices; zero-bubble stealth avoids feeding chaos. Trimix gas (21/35 He/N2) for narcosis resistance at ~10-20m equivalent depth/pressure.
