#### 1. **Physiological Baseline and Medical Prep (Pre-Travel Health Optimization)**
   - **Dehydration Countermeasures**: This air is a desiccant sponge—your lungs, eyes, and skin will lose 2-5% body water per hour via evaporation and hygroscopic salts. Get a full medical eval: baseline bloodwork for electrolytes, renal function. Pre-hydrate with IV saline (2L over 4 hours) and oral osmolyte solutions (e.g., glycerol-based drinks). Pack auto-injectors for hyaluronic acid (skin moisture lock) and desmopressin nasal spray (reduces urine output). Train for "dry drowning" symptoms: use a home dehydration chamber to simulate 10% RH at 0°C.
   - **Cold Adaptation**: Ambient ~0°C dry cold bites deeper than wet; hypothermia onset in 30-60 min without insulation. Acclimatize in a -5°C cold room for 3 sessions/week. Stock vasoconstrictor creams (e.g., capsaicin-based) for extremities.
   - **Respiratory Prep**: Air is sparse (likely <15% O2 partial pressure due to "near-nothingness"), mineral-laden, and alkaline from calcites—expect coughing, mucosal damage. Pulmonary function test; avoid if COPD/asthma. Prophylactic mucolytics (e.g., guaifenesin) and albuterol inhalers.
   - **Psychoacoustic Conditioning**: Silence amplifies heartbeat to "thunder"; city's vibrations sync to it, inducing dissociation. Practice sensory deprivation tanks with binaural beats mimicking peristaltic pulses (0.1-1Hz infrasound). Anti-anxiety: microdose propranolol for pulse suppression. Hypnosis audio: "You are observer, not substrate."
   - **Vaccines/Antibiotics**: Unknown microbiology—broad-spectrum prophylactic (e.g., azithromycin + antifungals like voriconazole for chitin-derived spores). Tetanus booster; tetanus-like risk from adhesive sampling wounds.

#### 2. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) – Full-Body Sealing**

Seal with magnetic neck/ wrist/ankle closures + positive-pressure hood. Weight: 8kg; test for 24hr wear.
   - **Gloves/Booties**: Kevlar-reinforced, non-porous nitrile outer with electrostatic-dissipative lining (prevents static adhesion to fractal vents). Boots: Vibram soles with micro-spikes for regolith grip; no laces (magnetic straps).
   - **Headgear**: Full-faceplate helmet (polycarbonate, anti-fog gold coating) integrated with suit. Avoid fabric helmets—frays into spore traps.
   - **Adhesion Mitigation**: Coat exterior in PTFE (Teflon) spray + talc powder. Carry solvent wipes (isopropyl alcohol) for instant release if sampled (walls "remember" chemistry for minutes).
   - **Thermal Regulation**: Phase-change material vest (melts at 15°C, absorbs body heat). Battery-powered heaters in torso/limbs (lithium-thionyl chloride cells, 48hr life).
