### Physical and Physiological Preparation

As an ordinary human—flesh-and-blood, no cybernetic enhancements—you're stepping into a world optimized for post-biological existence. The Slab is a dry, overbright forge where water is extinct, physics is playful, and "survival" means negotiating symbiosis with the environment rather than dominating it. Your body will face desiccation, sensory overload, thermal stress, and subtle quantum weirdness. Expect 24-48 hours of acclimation before full exposure; longer visits risk unintended integration with the substrate.

- **Vision**: Layered visors—outer polarized (blocks 99.9% UV/IR), middle AR overlay (false shadows, horizon grids for scale), inner neural shunt (pipes visuals to optic nerve if corneas fry). Train with VR sims: 1-week immersion in mock Slab phenomenology (blinding flats, bouncy gravity, eternal hush).

- **Locomotion**: Exoskeletal legs with gravity compensators (gyro-stabilized, auto-adjust to 0.8-1.2g fluctuations). Wheeled/legged rover hybrid (solar-charged, 50km/h top speed) with treads for foam-substrates—avoid walking barefoot; ground "hums" into your bones.

### Defense and Interaction Protocols
No overt hostility, but the Slab is alive—touch wrong, get queried/assimilated.
- **Against Ecology**:
  - **Nanites/Drones**: Ultrasonic emitters disrupt swarms (pebble-drones dissolve on contact). EMP bursts for larger polyhedrals.
  - **Crawlers**: Stealth mode (IR cloaking, mimic facet-glow). If engaged, viral-code sprayer repurposes them as mounts/guides. Observe 100m buffer— their mirrors induce uncanny-valley stares.
  - **Plants**: Harvest crystalline fronds for spare parts (carbyne blades cut anything).
- **Physics Hazards**:
  - Plasma blooms: Faraday cage mode + ground.
  - Photon storms: Shelter in self-digging "wax-panel" caves (suit probe yields substrate).
  - Wormhole vents/exotics: Radiation-hardened Faraday bubble (personal scale).
- **Substrate Mind**: Non-verbal interface glove—transmits binary queries (yes/no to glyphs). Default: Passive observer mode. If "invited," pre-load memetic firewall (blocks memetic viruses disguised as memories).
- **Weapons**: Minimal—stun-prod for crawlers, matter printer for barricades. Lethal force risks substrate retaliation (local physics rewrite: gravity crushes you).
