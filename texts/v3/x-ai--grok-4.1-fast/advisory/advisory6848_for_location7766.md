### Preparation Guide for Visiting the Embryonic Seafloor Vesicle (Spirehood Microcosm)

Visiting this pocket—a submerged, child-sized bladder of jagged basalt in a nascent seafloor fracture—is like stepping into the damp, throbbing heart of an alien womb. It's not an abyss you dive into; it's a quivering, intimate enclosure where hyperdense brine (density ~1.3–1.5 g/cm³, colder than 1°C) presses in like a living shroud, warping your body and senses. The space is claustrophobic (roughly 3m x 3m x 2m at most), alive with a fleshy "city" of polyps, squid-worms, and swarms that brush against you constantly. No air, no light beyond faint microbial glows, no escape from the predatory hush. Time feels syrupy; a 10-minute "stroll" could tax you like hours in arctic waters. Survival demands overpreparation: you're not exploring, you're infiltrating a dense, toxic biosphere that might dissolve or assimilate you. Aim for visits under 30 minutes; longer risks hypothermia, neurotoxic haze, or unwanted symbiosis.

As an ordinary human multiverse traveler, leverage portable tech from civilized worlds (e.g., neutral-density wetsuits from aquatic realms, chemsuits from toxic hives, or enchanted gear from abyssal cults). Budget: 5,000–20,000 multiversal credits for a full kit. Source from hubs like the Nexus Dive-Shops or Voidfarer Outposts. Test everything in a simulated brine tank (rentable for 200 credits/day).

#### 6. **Health, Psychological, and Post-Trip Prep**
   - **Pre-Trip:** Full medscan (lungs, thermoregulation). Hyperbaric acclimation chamber (24h). Psych eval for claustrophobia.
   - **During:** Heart-rate monitor (target <120 bpm). Record everything—HUD streams to crystal archive.
   - **Post-Trip:** Decon shower (acid-neutralizer + chemsorb). 48h quarantine (toxin flush). Therapy for "brine dreams" (recurring alien collective pulses).
