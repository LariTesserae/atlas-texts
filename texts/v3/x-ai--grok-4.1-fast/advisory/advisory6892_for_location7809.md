### Physiological Preparation: Acclimatize to Extremes of Altitude, Oxygen, and Climate

This plateau's elevation—thousands of meters into thinned air—delivers hypoxia faster than Everest's death zone, compounded by cool (10-15°C), saturating humidity that chills through evaporation and conduction. Oxygen partial pressure might hover at 10-12 kPa (half sea-level), triggering altitude sickness in hours for the unacclimatized.

- **Pre-trip Acclimatization**: Spend 2-4 weeks in a high-altitude simulation chamber or hypoxic training tent, ramping from 3,000m equivalent to 6,000m+. Train with acetazolamide (Diamox) at 125mg twice daily starting 24 hours before entry to speed acclimatization and counter cerebral edema risk. Hyperventilate drills to build tolerance for the damp chill seeping into lungs.
  
- **Supplemental Oxygen**: Carry a closed-circuit rebreather system with 2-4 liter titanium tanks (O2 at 40-60% mix, titrated via pulse oximeter to maintain SpO2 >92%). Demand-valve mask with heat-moisture exchanger (HME) to humidify intake and prevent desiccation despite ambient moisture. Backup: Portable electrolytic oxygen concentrator (powered by solar-flex panels) generating 5-10L/min, but test for humidity fouling.

- **Hydration and Thermoregulation**: Dehydration hits hard from respiratory water loss in thin air. Pre-hydrate with electrolyte solutions (sodium 3g/L, potassium 1g/L). On-site: Water beads everywhere, but filter aggressively—boil, UV sterilize, or use graphene-oxide nanofilters (0.1 micron pores) against microbial/nanotech contaminants. Insulated wicking layers under gear to trap body heat; chemical hand/foot warmers for extremities.

- **Medical Kit Essentials**: Dexamethasone for HAPE (high-altitude pulmonary edema), nifedipine for HACE, ibuprofen for headaches. Epinephrine auto-injectors for anaphylaxis from pollen/spore barrages. Broad-spectrum antifungals/antivirals (amphotericin B inhaler, remdesivir tabs) given mycelial dominance and potential airborne pathogens.

Limit visits to 4-6 hours; microdose anti-anxiety (lorazepam 0.5mg) if prone to panic. Journal pre-sim: "It's not hostile, just *existing* at scale."
