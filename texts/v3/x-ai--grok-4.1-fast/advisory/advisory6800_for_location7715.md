### Overview of Challenges
Visiting this nascent aerie—a humid, fog-shrouded cleft halfway up an embryonic mountain spine—feels like stepping into the birth canal of a living world. It's a raw, pulsating genesis zone: warm mist condenses on your skin like a perpetual sweat, the ground throbs subtly underfoot like breathing flesh, and the air thrums with the shrieks and skitters of fist-sized fauna in constant frenzy. The space is deceptively intimate (200 paces across, cathedral-like), but sheer walls and high altitude induce vertigo; dim pearl-gray light erases shadows, turning navigation dreamlike. Weirdness peaks near alien obelisk-shards: time stutters, gravity pools, rocks probe back when touched. Ecology is voracious—carcasses dissolve in seconds, and immobility invites integration as "substrate" via nanoscale symbiotes or acidic pools. Density is sparse, but activity is overwhelming: expect sensory assault (loud at 1.9), slipperiness everywhere, and dynamic fluxes (mists cycling hot/cold, rock extruding spires). Survival hinges on mobility, avoidance, and isolation from local physics/biology. Plan for 1-4 hours max; extraction via your multiverse portal is priority—don't linger.

Prepare as a lightly armored explorer in a hostile petri dish: prioritize waterproofing, fauna-proofing, anomaly evasion, and constant motion. Total kit weight: 8-12kg, modular for quick jettison.

### Clothing and Personal Protection (Layered, Sacrificial Outer Shell)
The humidity (1.6) means everything beads with mineral dew; fabrics wick or you're waterlogged in minutes. Fauna (1.9) targets exposed skin—iridescent moths swarm faces, crawlers skitter up legs, gliders snatch at hair/eyes.
- **Base layer**: Moisture-wicking synthetics (merino-poly blend, e.g., Smartwool or Arc'teryx), long-sleeved/legged. Add silver-ion treated underwear/socks to deter nanoscale symbiotes (they shimmer on damp skin, devouring exudates).
- **Mid layer**: Lightweight fleece or aerogel-insulated puffy (e.g., Montbell Plasma 1000) for thermal drafts (warm baseline, but mist chills to 15-20°C).
- **Outer shell**: Gore-Tex Pro or eVent full-body rainsuit (hooded, with face/neck gaiter). Sealable pit/zip vents for humidity. Overpants with integrated knee/elbow pads (slick quartz-veined rock bruises falls). Helmets like Petzl Astro (climbing-grade, ventilated) with detachable visor/netting.
- **Footwear**: Approach shoes with sticky rubber (e.g., Scarpa Instinct VS) + overgaiters (Gorilla Wear). Vibram Megagrip soles for wet undulations; add micro-spikes (Kahtoola Nanospikes) for hummocks. Change into dry spares pre-entry.
- **Gloves**: Waterproof gauntlets (e.g., Black Diamond Dirt Bag) with haptic feedback liners—insulate against responsive rock pseudopods (they "probe back" if prodded).
- **Head/eye/face**: Full-face bug net (military surplus M50-style) over UV/IR goggles (Oakley M Frame). Earplugs (high-NRR foam, e.g., 33dB) + over-ear muffs (Peltor Sport Tactical) for sound barrage (1.9)—wing-flaps like tearing silk, shrieks Doppler-warping. Phosphorescent fronds glow faintly; night-vision monocle optional for dim light (1.3).
- **Scent/mask**: Full-face respirator (3M 6800) with P100/organic vapor cartridges. Local air has ozone/mineral tang—filter for acidic saliva residues or nanotech aerosols. Apply DEET-picardin hybrid repellent (Sawyer 20%) head-to-toe; reapply every 20min.

### Dealing with Anomalies and Weirdness (2.0+ Rating: Primary Threat)
This is no passive ruin—tech (1.4-2.8) emerges *with* the rock, symbiotic. Fauna adapts (phasing hides from grazing shards). Rules: time dilates (replays near nodes), gravity wells in pools (orbiting detritus), rock animates (pseudopods), mists flux (frenzy trigger).
- **Mapping**: Pre-scan via portal probe (if available) or sketch: Central slab widest; obelisks at "odd angles" (NE/SE quadrants?). Avoid 5m radius of shards—mark with chalk arrows ("DETOUR"). Dome-frame: Ghostly panes shatter probabilistically—crouch low.
- **Physics protocols**:
  - **Touch nothing**: Gloves on; if rock probes, freeze then retreat—slow subsides.
  - **Shadows**: Wear matte-black disrupts (e.g., Voodoo ghillie strips)—lingering echoes mimic movements.
  - **Gravity/time**: Step wide over pools (whirlpools suck boots). If loop: Count aloud (1-2-3-repeat) to track real-time.
  - **Nanotech**: Faraday pouch for electronics near filigree lattices (warps signals).
- **Ecology hacks**: Move with glider packs (they spiral lazily)—mimic pseudopod hum (low whistle) to slip through. Fern tufts safe(ish)—no roots in pools.
- **Motion rule**: Never halt >30sec. Pace figure-8 loops; ecology "claims" via symbiotes (skin itch → dissolution).
