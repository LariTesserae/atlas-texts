### Overview of Challenges
Visiting this suspended plateau—a hyper-engineered, glitch-riddled megastructure floating in an unstable sky—will feel like stepping into a vast, whispering machine that's slowly dreaming itself apart. As an ordinary human multiverse traveler (fit, resourceful, with access to compact high-tech gear but no innate superpowers), you'll face relentless brightness that sears the eyes, a pervasive chill-dampness that soaks through layers, unnatural quiet amplifying your isolation, teeming bio-mechanical fauna that treats you as scenery, and reality-warping anomalies that twist space, time, shadows, and perception. Movement is glacial and deliberate; haste invites mishaps amid the dense layering of catwalks, tunnels, and phasing structures. Survival hinges on blending into the slow, patient rhythm: insulate against the cold mist, shield your senses from overload, anchor yourself against glitches, and carry redundancies for everything. Plan for 24-72 hours initially, with extraction contingencies. Total prep weight: aim for 15-20kg in a modular exosuit backpack.

Fauna density at 2.0 means constant brushes: cat-sized mechavores (rust-nibblers, mildly aggressive if cornered), glowing-winged servitors (darting flocks, eye-searing), fiber-optic hounds (pack prowlers, ignore humans unless provoked). They birth via vats/glitches, saturating crevices.

- **Bio-Shield:** Exosuit with electrostatic skin (zaps crawlers). Limb-mounted vibro-knife (vibrates at glitch-freq to disrupt circuits/flesh hybrids). Avoid kills—numbers rebound unnaturally.

- **Behavioral Rules:** Move at 1.4 pace (amble, no running). Exosuits blend you in; mimic pedestrian processions. If duplicated (rare human glitch), stay calm—anchor collapses it. No feeding; hydro-vines are for O2, not snacks.
