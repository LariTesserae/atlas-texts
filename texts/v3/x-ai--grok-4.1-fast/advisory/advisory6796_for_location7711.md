### Physiological Preparation

Oxygen partial pressure is ~5-7% of sea level, inducing immediate hypoxia, cerebral edema, and unconsciousness in under a minute. Temperatures hover at -5°C to 0°C with hypersonic winds (-100 km/h gusts laden with silicate dust that scour flesh like sandblasting). No free water exists—any liquid evaporates instantly, accelerating dehydration to fatal levels in hours. Psychoactive volatiles in the plant-exhaled oxygen will trigger hallucinations, paranoia, and synaptic overload, potentially permanent. Time dilation near spars (1.5-2x subjective time stretch) risks disorientation, stranding you in looped perceptions.
