### Overview of the Challenge
Visiting this abyssal, cephalopod-forged drowned highland is like plunging into a living, collapsing cathedral at the bottom of an alien ocean—eternal cold (near-freezing at 0.4/3 warmth), crushing pressures from kilometers of water overhead (likely 300-600+ atmospheres, enough to pulverize unprotected flesh), dim bioluminescent haze (1.0/3 light), and hyper-turbulent currents (3.0/3 dynamism) that treat your body like driftwood in a blender. The sparse (1.4/3 density), eroded megastructures of nacre-chitin offer handholds but shed debris, collapse unpredictably, and host teeming fauna (1.7/3 abundance)—wolf-sized isopods that scuttle and snap, swarming squid with electric pulses, drifting jelly-medusae that sting like lightning, translucent fish packs probing with lantern-eyes, and colossal whale-behemoths that could bulldoze you mid-swim. No air, no plants (0.2/3 vegetation), just chemosynthetic bacterial mats and thermal vents spewing toxic minerals. Hypothermia numbs you fast if you're still; predation or current-smash if you're not. Sound is a quiet hush (1.1/3) of rumbles and clicks, amplifying isolation. Mild weirdness (0.7/3) like "remembering" currents or synchronized mega-fauna herds adds subtle dread.

An ordinary human multiverse traveler—you're tough but fleshy, no superpowers—must prioritize **survival triad**: pressure/thermal isolation, propulsion dominance, and fauna deterrence. Plan for 1-4 hour dives max; longer needs a submersible base. Total prep cost: mid-tier multiverse kit (~5-10k credits or equivalent). Mindset: You're a ghost in a frenzy—move with currents, never stop, observe erosion/rebuild cycles poetically but exit before collapse. Risk of death: high (currents crush, cold seizes, teeth rend); success feels like surfing chaos.

1. **Acclimation (24hr prior)**: Hyperbaric chamber to 300atm baseline. Hydrate heavily; no alcohol (narcosis risk).
2. **Dive Plan**: Scout via orbital scan/multiverse probe for gyre patterns. Entry: Drop from submersible at plateau edge, ride descending current. Objectives: Surface-monolith glyph-reading, vent-probe, fauna-watch. Exit: Jet to beacon cluster, balloon ascend.
3. **In-Situ Tactics**:
   - **Current Surfing**: Align with "hyperbolic swirls"—use sonar vectors to slingshot between obelisks.
   - **Shelter Rules**: Hug structures for reduced flow, but ping for instability (groans = evacuate).
   - **Fauna Etiquette**: Dim lights during squid passes; mimic drift to fool predators.
   - **Emergency**: Thruster burst + beacon ping = drone swarm cover + evac.

4. **Mental Prep**:
