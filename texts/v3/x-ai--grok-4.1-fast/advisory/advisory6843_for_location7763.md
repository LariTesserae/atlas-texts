### Physical Protection and Clothing
An ordinary human would melt, slip, scald, or corrode without specialized gear. Prioritize a full-body **phase-change material (PCM) hazmat suit** rated for 50°C+ with active cooling via backpack thermoelectric chillers (powered by lithium-sulfur batteries or a small geothermal-compatible fuel cell). The suit must be **GOR-TEX equivalent but hyperbreathable with selective vapor-permeation membranes** to wick away the relentless 100% humidity—think NASA EVA suits crossed with SCUBA drysuits, sealed at neck, wrists, and ankles with magnetic or inductive seals. Inner layers: moisture-wicking synthetics like advanced merino or graphene-infused fabrics that regulate body temp between 20-30°C core.

- **Head/Face**: Full-faceplate helmet with polarized, anti-fog gold-coated visor (IR/UV/visible spectrum filtering to pierce the 200-lux gloom). Integrated HUD for vitals, nav overlays, and thermal imaging. Goggles underneath for redundancy. Respirator with **HEPA + activated charcoal filters** for sulfur/iron vapors, aerosol silicates, and potential acidic mists—cartridge life ~8 hours; pack 10 spares.
- **Feet/Hands**: Vibram-soled **magneto-rheological boots** with adaptive treads for slick basalt/glasscrete (grippy on wet minerals, rigid on uneven fractures). Heated insoles to combat conductive chill from damp stone despite ambient heat. Gauntlets with haptic feedback for tool use, reinforced against arthropod bites or crystal "growths."
- **Radiation/Thermal**: The smeared sun and geothermal sources suggest IR overload; layer in **multi-spectral mylar underlays** and a deployable parasol drone for shade.

Seal everything with **silicone-based nano-coatings** to repel the mineral-laced water—no rust or skin irritation from silica/iron brines.

### Health, Hydration, and Nutrition
This place is a dehydration paradox: water everywhere, but salty, mineral-saturated, and potentially bioactive (biofilms, algal mimics). Your body will sweat gallons, risking hyponatremia from overdrinking bad H2O.

- **Hydration**: Carry a **reverse-osmosis backpack purifier** (processes 5L/hour) with UV-C sterilization and ion-exchange resins to strip salts/heavy metals. Stock 20L of your own electrolyte-balanced fluids (e.g., oral rehydration salts tuned for heat stress). Taste-test local water only after triple-filtration—expect a metallic tang.
- **Food**: Self-heating MREs or freeze-dried extremophile-safe rations (high-protein, low-water-activity to avoid spoilage in humidity). 5,000+ kcal/day minimum; supplements for electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium), B-vitamins (heat depletes them), and iodine (thyroid protection from halogens in vents). Avoid foraging—no edible biology.
- **Medical Kit**: Hyper-focused on heat illness: IV saline kits, cooling packs, antipyretics (ibuprofen), electrolytes in ampules. Antihistamines/decongestants for humidity-swollen sinuses. Broad-spectrum antibiotics (e.g., ciprofloxacin) for biofilm infections via cuts. Altitude meds (acetazolamide) despite heat—4km elevation thins usable O2 in humid air. Antifungals/antivirals preemptively dosed. Epinephrine auto-injector for allergic reactions to exotic vapors. Wound sealant (bio-glue) for slips on knife-edges.
- **Monitoring**: Wrist-mounted bio-tracker for core temp, HRV, O2 sat, and early psychosis flags (weirdness-induced disorientation). AI-assisted for anomaly detection.

Plan for 72-hour max exposure; heat stroke hits fast in 48°C/100% RH effective temp (feels 60°C+).

### Mobility, Navigation, and Exploration Gear
Vast (50km basin), slippery, foggy, with shifting terrain—easy to wander lost or tumble into sinkholes.

- **Locomotion**: **All-terrain exoskeleton legs** (powered assist for climbs, anti-slip stabilizers) or **wheeled rover drone** for gear-haul (geothermal-rechargeable). Grappling hook with ascender for spires/canyons; foldable carbon-fiber poles for probing fog-hidden drops.
- **Navigation**: **Multi-modal GPS/INS/IMU system** hardened against magnetic anomalies (iron-rich rocks). Handheld lidar/sonar rangefinder for fog penetration (50m+). Thermal/NV binoculars to spot vents/fauna/shifts. Compass redundant (geothermal mag-deviations). Pre-load satellite ortho-maps if multiverse uplink allows; mark entry point with GPS beacon and chem-lum flares.
- **Lighting**: Primary **high-CRI LED headlamp** (2000+ lumens, tunable 1000-5000K to counter bruised twilight). Backup chem-lights and solar-rechargeable lanterns. Avoid flames—methane pockets from organics/decay.
- **Shelter**: Inflatable **geodesic heat-reflective dome** (one-person, anchors into basalt). Deployable hammock over marshes. Insulate against ground conduction (heat from below sucks).
