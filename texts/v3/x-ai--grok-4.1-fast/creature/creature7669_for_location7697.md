They are the Emergent, or so they name themselves in the low thrum of their glottal chants—skin-folk born not from wombs but from the valley's own slow parturition, their bodies half-hewn from the same raw basalt and pumice that claws skyward. No singular form defines them; they vary as the upthrusts do, each generation marked by the vent from which their progenitors first bubbled forth. Slender and elongated, like the spectral fish that ghost their fringes, their frames average the span of two arms outstretched—lithe torsos tapering to hips broad for buoyancy, limbs webbed between digits that splay into sensitive fringes for tasting currents. Skin shimmers slick and nacreous, a living patina of coralline accretions: pale viridian where algae symbiotes colonize in tattooed whorls across gillslits and lungs, crimson-flecked on those who forge near methane seeps, obsidian-veined for the deep-clan divers who quarry the nascent ridges. No hair mars their contours; instead, frond-like crests of hydrophyte filaments sprout from scalps and spines, undulating to harvest faint photons, pulsing with the same subdued phosphorescence as the kelp-canopy. Eyes are vast and lidless, milk-opal lenses that refract the gloaming into private gloats, pupils contracting to pinpricks when vents rumble near. They move with a liquid languor—propelling through water in sinuous glides, webbed feet kicking in slow scissors for propulsion, or leaping buoyantly from scaffold to ziggurat when geothermal updrafts lighten their weight.

Their numbers swell pod by pod, perhaps a thousand souls dispersed across the basin's sprawl, clustered in vent-kinships of twenty to fifty: the Thrumclans hugging the warmest fissures, their skins etched with sulfurous rosettes; the Frondweavers mid-valley, tendril-crested and vinelike in their coilings; the Ridgeclaimers on the escarpments, stockier with pumice-callused palms from levering megaliths. No kings or councils dictate; emergence rules them too—elders, those whose algae have overgrown into mossy beards, drift at the periphery, murmuring precedents from dissolved memories as new upthrusts quake the substrate. Youth emerge not birthed but "quickened": spores from pod-ferments inhaled through reed-masks, germinating in clay amphorae until tiny forms uncoil, gill-breathing and hungry, fostered by the pod until they claim a personal vent-niche. Mating syncs to solstice slacks, when currents hush—bodies entwining in mid-water drifts, releasing sporeclouds that drift into the green fog, viability gauged by how many quicken in the communal vats.

Life pulses to the valley's glacial rhythm, patient as coral's millimeter creep. Dawn—such as it is in perpetual twilight—begins with the Drift-Rite: kinships ballooning reed-sacs to rise through the frond-tangle, masks exchanged for deep inhalations of algal bloom, voices moaning in throaty unison to attune spines to the day's thrum. Harvest follows: Frondweavers sickle hydro-vines with coral blades, coiling tendrils into net-bundles that bob like gravid jellyfish to the settlement pods. Thrumclans tend forges, mauls ringing softly on copper ingots softened in vent-baths, sparks winking out in the density to birth harpoons, sickles, or the curved prows of coracles—bark vessels sealed with algal gum, paddled in tandem across chasms to trade pods of fermented brew: a heady slurry of spore-vats and mineral fizz, effervescing visions of geologic pre-birth, skin tingling with echoes of drowned plateaus. Ridgeclaimers midwife the land itself, poling megaliths from quaked-up crags, lashing saplings into scaffolds that protrude like embryonic limbs, willing ziggurats from tuff one block at a time. Tools evolve mid-use: a stone maul might sprout vine-grip over months, a coracle thicken with coralline shell.

Weirdness inhabits them as intimately as the water— the effervescent memory seeps into pores, birthing seer-visions during brew-sips: glimpses of the over-world's silver membrane, or the valley's womb-conception in cataclysmic flood. Plants' synchronized twitches ripple through their filaments, drawing clans into trance-dances where bodies undulate as one, spines humming to vent-rumbles, foretelling upthrusts. Stone-shifts on ridges haunt their dreams, faces resolving into ancestral profiles that whisper claims: "This crag is mine; quicken it." Gravity's whims aid their labors—leaps spanning net-walks become ritual flights, testing buoyancy to affirm the valley's favor. Fauna interfaces sparingly: luminous fish lured to communal feasts with mirrored obsidian, polyps harvested for numb-sting salves that ease forge-burns. Predation absents; instead, a spectral symbiosis—elongated swimmers shadowing divers, trading guidance through murk for spore-scraps.

Conflicts brew slow as silt-drift: vent-warmth sparks Drift-Wars, clans dueling in coracle-jousts with blunted harpoons, victors claiming the hottest flows for a generation's quickenings. Hydro-vine solstice harvests turn territorial when currents slacken unevenly, sickles clashing in muffled silks until elders intervene with memory-brews, dissolving grudges into shared visions. Death integrates seamlessly—forms slackening into substrate-sink, bodies claimed by the stone's awakening, algae blooming brighter on their remnants to hasten ridge-growth. No graves; the quickened inherit the enriched vents, filaments attuned to lost kin's thrum.

They are not conquerors but continuations—half-formed as their city, bodies inching toward fuller expression with each quake and sporeburst. In the hush, their moans weave a submerged litany: "From stone we quicken, to stone we cohere," voices muffled yet insistent, echoing the valley's own gestation. Here, in the dense dim, Emergent and emergence entwine, skin and stone gestating toward a surface yet unborn.
