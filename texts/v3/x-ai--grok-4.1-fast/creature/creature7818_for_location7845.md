### The Flitwings: Aerial Reapers of Shatterdance
First among the sky-thieves, the flitwings command the thermals like barbed kites in a hurricane. Six-limbed virtuosos, their bodies elongate to the length of a man's arm, torsos swollen with lightweight silica-foam bladders that buoy them against the downdrafts. Wings—four vast, translucent sails veined in iridescent chitin—unfurl to three strides across, edged with serrated barbs that harvest mid-flight: a flick, and nascent crystal tips shear free, gulped into beak-maws fringed with grinding ridges. The forelimbs, clawed and dexterous, pluck spore-pods mid-dive; hindlimbs trail as rudders, webbed for braking in the cyclone's twist.

They live in swarms of fifties, no hive-mind but a loose armada bound by the hunt's rhythm—darting in whip-cracks of membrane-taut snap, translucent hides cracking like thunderclaps to stun prey or rivals. Mating is a mid-air melee: males spiral in glowing trails (bioluminescent lures from ingested minerals), colliding in screeching dogfights that rain crystal chaff. Eggs, pearl-clutches of hardened vapor-resin, affix to spire undersides, hatching in hours to join the froth. Phasing flickers haunt their edges—mirage-blurs from thermal refraction in their hides—letting them ghost through gales unscathed. In the bowl's reversal winds, they invert, bellies up, harvesting updrafts like inverted vultures. Their call? A staccato *krick-krack*, rifle-sharp, syncing with crystal fractures to mask ambushes. They are the amphitheater's eyes in the haze, fractal gazes on wing-veins tracking every bloom and intruder, blinking in unison during the frenzy's lulls.

### The Scuttlebeasts: Subterranean Forges of Collapse
Beneath the froth, the scuttlebeasts are the bowl's insatiable grinders, low-slung siege engines armored in regolith-chitin fused from devoured stone. Spindlier than hounds, yet broader across the carapace—multi-jointed legs splaying eight-fold, each piston-limbed with spade-claws that excavate in blurring frenzy. Their bodies undulate like segmented bellows, vents exhaling mineral steam to soften stone for burrowing; mouths are radial maws of rotating grinders, pulverizing shale into nutrient paste that feeds symbiotic pore-fungi in their gut-sacs.

They prowl in territorial octets, no larger packs lest the earth buckle—tunnels collapse in engineered avalanches, trapping vapor-runners for the feast. Hyperactive, they burst from flats in chitinous avalanches, legs clattering hailstorm percussive, warring over fissures with mandible-locks that spray silicate sparks. Reproduction pulses with the geothermal: gravid females swell, burrowing deep to birth live broods that swarm upward like magma-worms, immediately tunneling anew. Phasing manifests in their carapaces—half-there shells flickering as ions overload, letting them phase through falling shards unscathed. Eyes? Clusters of gem-lenses, deep-etched to mimic the runes, scanning for vibrations. Their verse in the cacophony: a chittering *skrrrt-skrrrt*, gravelly and relentless, harmonizing with the krak-booms. They are the forge-masters, recycling ruin into rebirth; without them, debris would choke the fissures, starving the crystal-spires.

### The Vapor-Runners: Thunderhooves of the Panic-Circuit
Striding the negative spaces like elongated specters, vapor-runners embody the herd's mad poetry—bipeds thrice a man's height, gaunt and balloon-tailed, pelts of heat-mirage fur shimmering taupe to opal. Legs: reverse-jointed pillars ending in broad, dust-devil hooves that kick thermals into spiraling towers; tails, vast and inflated with geothermal gas, steer their looping stampedes across the bowl's expanse. Heads crest with branching antler-racks of shed crystal, sensors for wind-moods; muzzles trumpet bellows, mouths filtering ions from the gales.

They migrate in herds of twoscore, circuits etched by ancient winds—thundering clockwise till reversal, then counterclockwise frenzy, kicking avalanches that expose fresh burrows for scuttlebeasts (symbiosis veiled as panic). Predated by flitwings nipping flanks, they rear and lash tails like flails, gas-blasts stunning swarms mid-air. Mating amid the thunder: dominant bulls clash antlers in choral roars, females calving mid-gallop, young ballooning upright in strides. Phasing blurs their herds into mirage-phantoms, edges dissolving as they weave the sparsities, bodies half-erased by speed and shimmer. Eyes track with slow, fractal blinks, pupil-spirals mirroring glyphs. Their thunder rolls the deepest bass—bellows booming off walls like living earthquakes—drowning thought, summoning the cyclone's fit. They are the bowl's pulse, their migrations syncing eruptions: a topple here births lattices there, their hooves seeding the steam.

### The Echo-Folk: Phantoms of Failed Dominion
Threading the frenzy's underside, the true weirdness lives in the echo-folk—or so the runes whisper—these are the "primitive hands" transfigured, no longer flesh but mineral revenants, adapted or cursed into the chaos they once tamed. Rare as the lulls, they flicker from lee-shelters: humanoid silhouettes etched in living quartz, two strides tall, forms elongated and phasing like the beasts. Skin? Patina'd basalt veined with throbbing glow-crystals, limbs multi-jointed like scuttlebeasts yet graceful, ending in four-fingered claws clutching quartz-axes pecked from spires.

They dwell solitary or in dyads, lean-tos their totems—framed in lashed stalks, hides draped as wards. No language spoken; subharmonic whispers from throat-crystals converse with the dreaming lattices, glyphs etched afresh to map wind-moods or summon reversals. They hunt with flung shards, phasing to ghost through dogfights, harvesting flitwing clutches or runner-calves. Reproduction? A merging: two clasp amid a crystal pulse, bodies dissolving into vapor, reforming as a single youth-crystalled offspring days later. Eyes are the fractal watchers, slow-blinking from foreheads and palms, tracking the unseen cargo in the haze. Their sound weaves faint: a wind-flute hum under the roar, bone-deep, syncing the frenzy to lulls where they mend tools. Abandoned camps mark their flights from "the mounting," yet they persist—hungering for disturbance, drawing intruders to feed the cycle. Are they makers or made? Ancestors of beasts, or beasts aspiring? They claim the emptiness, totems pulsing as hearts, guardians of the ballet's knife-edge.
