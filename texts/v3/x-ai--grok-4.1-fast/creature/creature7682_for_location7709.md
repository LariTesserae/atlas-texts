### Pebble Imps: The Endless Masons

At the cirque's heart swarm the pebble imps, those fist-sized spheres of mottled granite-gray fur, their pelts a living camouflage of embedded lichens that shift hue with the prismatic glare—now slate-blue, now frost-lichen verdigris. Each imp is a compact dynamo, body no larger than a clenched fist yet dense as river-smoothed stone, rolling and unrolling on stubby limbs that end in chisel-teeth filed to razor edges from perpetual gnawing. Their eyes, paired rubies inset like flawed gems, flicker with subsurface heat, steaming faintly in the marrow-chill; breath vents as micro-plumes from gill-slits along their flanks, inhaling the metallic mist to fuel ceaseless motion.

They do not walk so much as propel, somersaulting in orchestrated cascades across boulders and hovel-roofs, shells clacking mutely against stone in pulsed rhythms—short-long-short for "claim this slab," a staccato flurry for "stack higher." Hundreds strong, they form ephemeral guilds: scouting imps roll downhill from seeps, hauling flecks of raw granite in cheek-pouches bloated like hamster-balls, while mason-crews gnaw them into bricks mid-motion, teeth sparking silent against the unrelenting light. Erosion is their god and rival; as a fresh wall corbels upward in frenzied assembly lines—imps balancing atop one another in trembling pyramids, mortaring gaps with saliva-frozen moss—the granite face bubbles anew, extruding rivals that they claim or flee. No rage, only reconfiguration: a tumbled imp splats into mire, fur sloughing to reform as a fresh pebble elsewhere, its ruby gaze rekindling in another body.

Deeper quirks emerge in their cycles. In the forge-groove, elite "spark-fang" imps—those with bifurcated teeth—hammer flint nodules scavenged from churn-worm upheavals, forging blades that gleam like bottled lightning. These tools barter in shell-taps: a superior edge traded for a prime lichen-patch on a migrant's back. Reproduction pulses communal; synchronized by the cirque's geothermal throb, they extrude pebble-eggs from abdominal slits, rolling them into nursery-crèches where mist-weasels guard against opportunistic flits. Life as imp is eternal apprenticeship—pup-imps hatch gnawing their own shells, joining the bazaar before fur fully sets.

### Mist Weasels: The Knot-Barons of Trade

Slithering through the saturated seams come the mist weasels, eel-like enigmas a meter from hooded snout to tapered tail, bodies translucent as condensed fog yet veined with pulsing lumens that sync to the ice-rim's glare—emerald throbs for hunger, sapphire flares for accord. Their flesh yields like wet silk under pressure, reforming around intrusions; they squeeze through chinks narrower than a finger, coiling into the warren's tunnels where pebble imps dare not roll. No bones rigidify them; hydrostatic sinew propels sinuous glides, fins feathering from flanks to rudder the murk.

Commerce defines them, barons of the alley-markets where stolen moss-balls, flint-shards, and worm-slime nodules exchange in knotted "contracts." A weasel loops its tail thrice around a moss-bundle, offers it to an imp-scout pulsing ruby acceptance; the knot tightens, sealing the deal, then unravels as payment—a sliver of blade tucked into the weasel's gullet-pouch. Disputes ripple as body-waves: dueling weasels entwine, lumens clashing in hypnotic strobes until one yields, sloughing translucent scales that harden into path-tokens for future trades. They hoard in substructure vaults, chambers burrowed under hovel-floors, walls lined with knotted trophies that vibrate subsonic histories—*this coil from the great reform of 'last-thaw,' that loop bartered post-spire-fall.*

Their intimacy with wetness borders on merger: weasels drink the seeps directly through pore-skin, exhaling vapor that beads the hovels' interiors. In crèches, they midwife imp-eggs, coiling protective lattices; in return, imps cap weasel-nests with slate lids. Apex traders, yet prey in the churn—crushed by boulder-migrations, they mist into vapor, recondensing from fog-wraiths drawn to the nearest pheromone-tang, memories knotted into muscle.

### Ice-Flits: The Frenzied Pollinators

Darting in turbulent clouds overhead wheel the ice-flits, thumb-sized whirlwinds of membrane wings like shattered quartz panes, iridescent and razor-edged, slicing the bright air without a whisper. Bodies ovoid, armored in fractal chitin that refracts the prismatic floods into personal halos—each flit a comet-trail in miniature. Six limbs fan from a central thorax: fore-wings for propulsion, mid-limbs clutching spore-pods frozen from thornbush pollen, hind-legs wielding stingers that inject not venom but fertilizer, spurring moss-growth amid the sparsity.

They nest in hovel-vents, droppings crystalline manure that thaws to nourish hearth-fires—imps stoke these with flit-shards, smoke venting as luminous plumes. Swarms synchronize wing-flares to the glacial hum, pollinating thornbushes in hyperactive ballets: a dozen flits converge on a frost-glistened twig, sacs rupturing in unison to birth spore-spires that topple by dusk, feeding the mire's churn. Communication flares visual: wing-mirrors pulse binary rainbows—red-green for "swarm here," ultraviolet bursts for "hive-threat."

Nesting reveals tenderness; vents bulge with pupal husks, translucent orbs where flits molt thrice before flight, emerging to immediate frenzy. Predated by weasels—who snag laggards mid-air—they fragment into glittering dust, reforming on thorn-leaves as the wind wills, drawn by collective wing-hum.

### Churn-Worms: The Subterranean Tillers

Beneath the peat toil the churn-worms, pale annelids thick as human forearms, segmented bodies glowing soft pearl from bioluminescent glands that map the dark. Each meter uncoils into dozens: head-frills siphoning metallic seep-water, tail-spouts extruding slime that flash-freezes into path-stones—flattened pebbles woven into imp-alleys overnight. They burrow radial from the mere's fringe, upheaving granite-fissures for the imps, their passage a slow thunder felt in boot-soles.

No eyes pierce their world; pores sense vibrations—the geothermal pulse dictating rise-cycles, imp-shell-taps summoning digs. They mate in slime orgies, segments budding clones that tunnel independently, swelling the horde. Crushed by thrall-ripples or boulder-drifts, they liquify into fertilizer, segments reknitting around fresh nuclei burrowed from bone-scatter.

### Echo-Thralls: The Silent Wardens

Towering thrice human height, the echo-thralls shambling the plaza are silhouettes of compacted ice and bone—femurs interlaced with rib-cages (ancestral imps? weasels ossified?), sheathed in glacial rime that gleams prismatic. No faces mar the voids where heads should crown; starburst reflections pulse from these abysses, mirroring the cirque's light in hypnotic defiance. Limbs terminate in splayed palms, gestures rippling the mire—outstretched arms send waves that herd swarms: imps pyramid toward fresh extrusions, weasels coil into markets anew.

Do they build? Patrols trace the menhir altar, palms pressing runes that throb geothermal replies, synchronizing the flux. Or witness? They gather on the mere's isle-hummock, encircling bone-teeth protrusions, postures dilating time—minutes as eons watching hut-rise-fall. Untouchable, they neither eat nor excrete; "death" shatters them into shards that rain across the bowl, bones reclaiming mire to birth new thralls from the chorus. Pheromone-lords, their ozone-tang invigorates lesser fauna, numbs intruders—perhaps they are the cirque's dream made flesh, enforcers of eternal remaking.

### The Living Organism: Fauna in Flux-Harmony

Layered thus, the cirque's denizens pulse as one organ: imps mason the scaffold, weasels knot its veins, flits seed its nerves, worms till its gut, thralls its watchful cortex—all recycling ceaselessly. No hierarchies fracture; trades flux with the dynamic frenzy, nurseries shared, markets migratory. Intruders—rare phantom shapes like you—elicit curiosity: imps roll probes across boots, weasels coil tasting-tendrils, flits halo in wary swarms, thralls gesture containment. Stay long, and the chill tang seeps in; skin beads moisture, thoughts vibrate to the hum. Pebble by pebble, you shift—fur sprouting, coils yearning—claimed by the mountain's endless, indifferent dream.
