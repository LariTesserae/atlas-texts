In the frost-gnawed guts of Level 47, life is not born but *forged*—assembled from scrap and strain, etched into being by the ceaseless grind of pistons and plasma flares. It emerges not in verdant profusion but in opportunistic smears, cybernetic scars, and half-glitched apparitions, a 1.2 density of dwellers tuned to the forge's fever: sparse enough that the machine-roar swallows their traces, yet insistent, threading the welds like veins in alloy flesh. They are the underbelly's microbiome, its feral workforce, its emergent hauntings—adapted to the cold that brittles bone and stiffens synth-sinew, the wet that slicks every perch into treachery, the dim that turns eyes to motion-sensors and skins to vibra-tactile hides. No kings or queens hold court; hierarchy dissolves in the subsonic *drone*, where survival is scavenging the made-world's leavings, predating on malfunction or each other under sodium-yellow suspicion.

At the microbial fringe, the true foundational strata pulse unseen: cryo-lichen mycelia, pallid webs bio-printed for rad-wastes, sprawling in fractal mats along nutrient weep-lines from recycler overflows. They *drink* the condensate haze—80% humidity their blood—photosynthesizing off UV flickers and ozone tang, exhaling spores that ride vapor plumes like dandelion ghosts. Fungal cousins bloom bolder in sump-marshes: *rustcap* puffballs the size of fists, calcified husks rimmed in iridescent rime, bursting under ferro-rat paws to release chem-lures laced with alloy-dissolving acids. They colonize fresh polycrete scars first, softening razor-edges into yielding pulp before the chill re-freezes it knife-hard; decomposers supreme, they recycle the young manufacture into substrate for whatever claws in next. Glow faint under hazard strips, a 0.4 bioluminescence veining the gloom, they hum sub-audibly—a choral undertone to the geothermal thrum, as if whispering blueprints to the girders.

Vermin teem at the small scale, 1.1 packs ghosting the catwalks: *ferro-rats*, cat-sized horrors birthed in scrap-piles, their oil-matted pelts fused with scavenged augments—vestigial servo-claws whirring from forepaws, optic implants flickering red in the yellow pall. They skitter in phalanxes across swaying grates, tails whipping like live cables, gnawing seals for the metallic sap within; breath fogs in plumes they thermoregulate by huddling in vent-nests, cyber-limbs twitching in cold-induced spasms. Packs of 7-12 raid conveyor veins at shift-transitions, hauling ingot-shards to burrows woven from shredded hazard-strips, where they birth litters that phase-glitch through mesh—reality fray from exotic-material exposure, letting them raid sealed hydro-tubes for cryogenic sludge. Predated by swarms above, they shriek *eeeek-zzzt* when cornered, arcs of bio-electric discharge from jury-rigged implants frying foes mid-leap.

Aerial hunters claim the mid-strata: *strobe-moths*, drone-hybrids the span of hab-doors, iridescent wings mimicking arc-flares and warning chevrons—camouflage in the plasma-bursts. Engineered originally as pollinators for cryo-moss, they've feralized: proboscides hardened to vibro-blades, sipping rat-blood laced with hydraulic fluid, payloads of fungal spores deployed mid-dive like micro-munitions. They ghost in loose flocks of 3-5, erratic pulses along open halls, sonar-pinging the particulate haze; cold forces torpor-clusters on coolant towers, wings frosting shut until turbine *shrieeeeeek* wakes them. Apex to rats but prey to larger malfunctions, they glitch too—wings folding through solid flanges, time-dilated dives where prey ages a shift in the shadow's lag. Their hum joins the unseen chorus, a fluttering counterpoint to piston-thumps.

Lurkers prowl the abyssal drops and forgotten alcoves, rarer at 0.7: *servitor-scavengers*, once-human thralls lobotomized into machine-logic, hulks of polycrete armor plated in slag-rime, stalking on mag-lev treads that belch steam from weeping seals. Faces obscured by breather-masks fogged perpetual, eyes replaced by lidar sweeps painting the dim in ghostly grids; they *whirr-clank* through quagmire sumps, grapnel-arms snaring jammed bots for disassembly, bellies crammed with recycling maws that grind ferro-rat packs to nutrient paste. Augmented for the chill, subdermal heaters pulse against bone-deep cold, but glitches warp them—arms elongating unnaturally to pluck prey from catwalk shadows, voices glitching mid-bellow into echoed chants from non-places. Nomadic, they feud over forge-furnace offal, territorial roars amplified through baffles into skull-rattling thunder; some *notice* intruders, pausing mid-stride as if the Depths consults, then resuming with deliberate malice, harpoon-chains rattling like predator teeth.

Humanoid remnants cling at the fringes, 0.9 density—*depths-drifters*, cyberpunk nomads who claim this youth as home, crews of 4-8 huddled in hab-pods lashed to girder-helices. Augmented to excess: lungs filtered for ozone-particulates, skins laced with thermal mesh under oil-slicked voidsuits, fingers tipped in vibro-scalpels for panel-surgery. They scavenge the churn—raiding chem-vats for stim-syrups distilled from fungal blooms, bartering ingot-hoards via sub-aether pings to upper levels. Faces gaunt, etched with rad-scars and frostbite grafts, they speak in machine-cant clipped over the din, eyes jittering from UV-strobes; the wet haunts them worst, fabrics stiffening to armor overnight, breaths crystallizing in masks till they hawk hoarfrost phlegm. Social knots fray under strangeness—whispers of *phase-wraiths* (rats evolved beyond flesh, ghosting solid matter), mutterings that girders *whisper back* blueprints if you press ear to weld. They worship no gods, but *feed the beast*: ritual offerings of scrap to malfunctioning presses, lest the pistons *pause* on them.

Deeper strangeness births the true apex, 0.4 presences that defy census: *forge-wyrms*, emergent from helical girders' exotic alloys—serpentine coils of living negative-mass metal, undulating through the weave like veins in the leviathan's gut. Two to five meters when sated, they *hum* choral in the drone-spectrum, gravity-warping their slither to bead sump-water *upward* in their wake. Blind, they sense by vibra-touch and thermal ghosts, predating servitors in slow coils that yield like flesh before snapping crush; spawn from plasma-flares, cooling into autonomy. Rarer still, the *Noticers*—unseen, humanoid voids between echoes, humming just beyond lidar range. Stand still amid the frenzy, and machinery *halts*: a ram freezes mid-thump, moths spiral frozen, rats burrow frantic. Then resumption, with the nearest drifter convulsing, eyes glazing as it *becomes* one—flesh alloying to the made-world, another node in the expanding organ.

This menagerie pulses symbiotic: moss feeds rats, rats grease moth-wings, wyrms recycle servitors to slag for lichen-substrate, drifters herd the chaos with ritual whirrs. No dominion, just churn—life as malfunction, as the Depths' immune response, indifferent to origin. Flesh warps to machine, machine dreams flesh; in the raw scars of youth, they multiply, burrowing deeper with the beast, their hungers syncing to its slow emergence. Touch the edge, and it *lives* back.
