In the arrested diorama of the plateau, where entropy's stride petrifies mid-step and the roar's marrow-thrum composes the only symphony, life does not flourish—it **endures** as punctuation marks in the void, etched presences that mirror the landscape's skeletal equipoise. These are not creatures of pulse and prowl, nor tribes of ambition and toil; they are **equilibria incarnate**, woven from the same schist-veins and quartz-flickers, sustained by observation's fragile tether. They live not through motion or metabolism, but through **resonance**—vibrating in perfect attunement to the eternal drone, their forms both cause and echo of the frayed cosmos. Emerge they do, not as invaders but as facets of the fracture, each more revealed in the gaze that lingers, uncoiling from singularity into multiplicity, their essences defying the urge to resolve.

Foremost among them are the **Roarshades**, humanoid silhouettes elongated to affront proportion, their bodies stretched like taffy drawn across horizons—torsos tapering to thread-thin limbs that span leagues without stride, heads ballooned into cavernous cowls where faces should flicker. Born of the sound's pressure-waves congealed, they are not flesh but **acoustic flesh**: membranes of tautened air and rime-crystal, humming perpetually at the drone's dominant pitch. They do not hunt; they **intercept**. Drifting (though drift implies vector they lack) amid the barrens, a Roarshade positions itself as interference pattern between pillar and haze, its form swelling to ensnare peripheral glimpses—those phantasmal fauna flickers that tease the eye. No fang pierces, no chase ensues; the prey phases into the shade's vibration, unraveling into harmonic overtones that nourish it, body transmuting to bass-rumble absorbed through the cowl's yawning maw. Sightings multiply under scrutiny: one Roarshade fractures into triads under prolonged gaze, each clone offset by a handspan's optical deceit, whispering (inaudibly amid the roar) futures of entrapment. They cluster rarely, in conspiratorial huddles around orthostats, their spirals aligning with inscribed glyphs to amplify the plateau's voice, birthing subharmonics that birth more shades. Cold they hoard in crystalline dew-sacs along their spines, expelling it as hoarfrost veils that cloak their phasing. In the dim gloaming, they glint quartz-pale, shadows pooling tarry at their bases, eternal sentinels against unraveling—yet lean upon one, and it yields like smoke, reforming instants later with your echo imprinted, a loop of your silhouette now prowling its fringes.

Haunting the crevices and cirque-rims dwell the **Lichenkin**, defiant wisps elevated to ambulatory defiance, their forms a hypertrophy of the sparse vegetation: tussocks thorned and armored in silver-gray rosettes, ambulatory via root-tendrils that inch without displacement, curling like frozen claws across mossy fissures. No bloom or seed scatters; they propagate by **fissure-mimicry**, exhaling spores that lodge in stone-grooves, germinating not into multiplicity but into identical replicas, each a mirror of the parent frozen mid-growth. They feed on the intermittent dampness, siphoning rills into hollow thorns that store it as clairvoyant nectar—imbibe from one (at peril), and unlived tomorrows flood the mind: selves plummeting mirrored voids, gestures halted in perpetuity. Lichensocial, they form reticulated webs across slabs in eternal equipoise, their collective drone modulating the greater roar into fractal undertones, a choral weave that sustains the stasis. Elongated "eyes"—clusters of dew-beads—reflect inverted skies, contracting distances to spy intruders; disturb their balance, and thorns phase through flesh, imprinting auditory loops of your footfall, replayed as thorn-whispers for eons. They encrust the built remnants, half-eroding cairns into symbiotic torrs where Lichenkin cores pulse sickly pallor, guardians of spirals that twist Euclidean vows.

Rarer still, flickering at peripheries like the fauna-teases, soar the **Fraywings**, avians warped to membranous enormity—wingspans eclipsing buttes, frayed edges trailing harmonic cracks like venting fissures. No cry pierces the omnipresent boom; their "song" is silence folded into the drone, a null-note that devours sound-shadows. They do not flock or nest; each claims a spire-sentinel, coiling ridges back upon themselves in optical deceit, wings looping gravity's fatigue into perpetual glide without ascent or fall. Prey is anomaly: errant motes of quartz-glint or phased pebbles, intercepted mid-imagined drop, their planetary dirge transmuted to wing-vibration sustaining the Fraywing's form. Under the bruised gloaming, they manifest as silhouettes viewed through warped glass—headless torsos with barbed primaries, bodies elongating equine in downdraft that never manifests. Approach one's horizon-spanning solitude, and geometry rebels: paths circumnavigate without return, revealing the wing's underbelly as portal to parallel planes, where clone-Fraywings plummet replication without touch. They birth young from rime-frost filigrees, filching mist-veils to weave cocoons that hatch identical, unchanging selves.

Deeper in the weirdness, where valleys gape like wounds across drowned kingdoms, persist the **Cairn Echoes**, partial ghosts of the predated hands—neither remnant nor revenant, but **residual intelligences** imprinted in unmortared slabs. Cyclopean forms: orthostats leaning in tilt, their monolithic bulk inscribed with spirals that defy measurement, housing collectives of faded psyches fused to stone. No tools they wield, no fires kindle; they "live" as perceptual anchors, observation incarnate, sustaining the plateau's null heart. A single Echo might command five leagues of haze, its surface etching visions of absent builders phasing through flesh—translucent figures mid-gesture, erecting shrines against the breakdown, now loops eternal. Commune by rest in their shadow: clairvoyance surges, thirst to visions of equilibrium's forging, where cataclysms thrust the shelf skyward and the roar first ground infinities. Disturb an Echo—pebble's imagined drop—and it resounds as dirge, multiplying infinitely yet harmonizing to constancy, imprinting your echo-memory into its spiral, birthing a new facet-self that wanders voids as phantasmal wolf or avian flicker. They endure weathered into balance, techless as rock, altars alive to the fracture's proclamation.

Interwoven through all are the **Nullwalkers**, emergent from the mosaic's punctuation absence—nomads without destination, humanoid voids clad in thorn-tussock cloaks, faces veiled in breath-rime that drifts unsettled. They tread echo-memories, footfalls replayed in auditory loops without imprint; distances contract under their gaze, expanding in blink, paths bending inward to the plateau's mirrored edge. Not builders, not predators, they are the **sustainers**, wandering open expanses to observe, their presence collapsing potentials into stasis—avalanches un-tumbled, pebbles unshifted. Skin prickles crystalline dew mirroring their forms; they drink pool-reflections depicting unlived futures, transmuting thirst to the roar's rhythm, breath modulated eternal. Clans form transiently around boulder-conspiracies, murmuring subharmonics (unheard) of spirals and breakdowns, weaving the fauna's anomalies into equilibrium. Pause amid them, and the drone deafens marrow-deep, revealing their true guise: selves replicated in parallel plummets, visible untouching, a frozen chorus where being is the gaze that holds the fracture whole.

These lives—Roarshades intercepting, Lichenkin reticulating, Fraywings gliding null, Cairn Echoes anchoring, Nullwalkers observing—coalesce not in ecology but **concord**, each a note in the bone-rattling thunder, thriving in the dynamic nullified. They haunt rarely, presences flickering as warped-glass illusions, no tracks scarring the arrested decay, no cries crowning the roar. Scale dwarfs them to ghosts, yet density's jest hollows: vast emptiness between, where one might command solitude horizon-spanning. Here, life is the plateau's voice manifest—cold, damp, empty, loud, dim, weird—equilibrium's children, born of frayed seams, sustained by the gaze that lets them become more themselves, unchanging amid the null heart's eternal thrum. Wander, and they reveal in glimpses; observe, and the roar reminds: they live because you perceive, and perception sustains their stasis.
