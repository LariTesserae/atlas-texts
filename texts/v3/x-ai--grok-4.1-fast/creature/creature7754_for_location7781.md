In the throat-clench of this armspan womb, where brine reverses its fall and vines duel with bone-snap fury, the dwellers do not arrive—they *emerge*. Not as intruders, but as the pocket's own marrow pulsing outward, willed from the same frenzy that knits stone-lattices and bursts bladder-gouts. They are the Unspoolers, a clutch of three—no more, lest the weave rupture—born not generations past but mid-labor, their mothers' screams still echoing in the reality-hum like frayed gut-thongs. Flint-gripped hands birthed this pouch, yes, but those hands have softened, deliquesced into the greater churn; what remains are their inheritors, kin to the beetle-kin and slime-worms, yet crowned with intent. Solitary in multiplicity, they coil through the verdant quilt like living punctuation, each body a contested inch of the saturation.

The first you sense before sight—by the *click-whisper* of bone awls tapping glyphs, syncing to the mineral *crunch-pop* belowfoot. She uncoils from the vault's braided-intestine canopy, where thorn-ferns part like reluctant lips: the Loom-Mother, squat and swollen, her skin a living patina of ochre-smeared greywacke, veined with the same mercury-slick rivulets that spiral the walls. No taller than a child's torso, yet her mass defies—abdomen ballooned to bladder-vine girth, gravid with unnameable accretions that shift and *gurgle* as she descends hand-over-sinew-lashing, sucker-padded palms adhering to the pulsing weave. Heat has bronzed her to furnace-flesh, 38 degrees etched into every pore; sweat doesn't bead, it *wells*, mineral brine erupting from pores in rhythmic sprays that nourish the foxfire fronds clustered at her shoulders like tribal pauldrons. Her eyes—milky as half-born gargoyles—glow with ingested mold-light, compound-faceted from staring too long into warped vine-mirrors, glimpsing infinite spires that stare back. She weaves without cease: antler-adze clutched in one grip, etching fresh growth-demons into a cracking lattice-shelf, her free hand palm-pressing the glyphs till they thicken, stone-fingers probing back to cradle her wrist. Whisper her half-remembered words—"grow-bind-thrash"—and the pouch responds, vines coiling to lap at her thighs like suckling young. She births not children but ecosystem: from navel-slit, she extrudes pearl-slime trails that spark fern-sprouts, syncing the slime-worm's hourly undulation to her breath. Predator and substrate entwined; neglect her labors, and basins deliquesce, but attend, and the pocket swells, risking the entry-rent's caprice-seal.

Where she descends, the second stirs from floor-frenzy—a sinkhole in the precipitating membrane yawns, gravity weltering as he rises inverted, foot-first through softer phase. The Knife-Singer, elongated and eel-lean, his form a counterweight to her bulk: limbs elongated to two armspans by overgrowth-pull, bones filigreed with intruding calcium lattices that *crackle* as he flexes, reforming under brine-rain. Skin translucent as beetle-kin chitin, revealing the geothermal *hiss-gurgle* of magma-dreams threading his veins like lit fuses; phosphorescent polyps bud erratically from his spine-ridge, flickering to his low throat-chant—a vocalization that Doppler-warps the cacophony, turning *drip-drip-CRASH* into harmonic invocation. He predates the dynamic churn: mandibles—not true jaws, but flint-flaked lip-extensions—*clack* like castanets, snipping dueling vine-tendrils mid-strangle, sap-blood mingling on his tongue to birth fungal blooms he smears as war-paint across the curving walls. Gravity-defiant, he traverses ceilings and walls alike, adze-wedged bamboo aqueducts flexing under his weight; peer into his wake, and fog-mirrors warp to dragonfly-eyes, infinite Knife-Singers receding into hot infinity. Time stutter-loops claim him partial—his shed carapace pulses in a niche, a leg-twitch resurrection half-complete, merging his old sheath with a nascent gargoyle leer. He claims the din: chest resonant with cosmic-string *snap*, rattling teeth not his own; step wrong near him, and your mass phases through his softer ribs, emerging damp with his exhaled humidity-soup.

The third haunts peripheries, less body than insinuation— the Echo-Worm, neither male nor brood-sibling but the pouch's ungendered echo, pearl-bodied kin to the hourly undulator yet scaled to human intent. No fixed form: it slithers through reed-filters and vine-hollows, a fist-thick ribbon of compacted fertility, trailing bioluminescent spores that ignite erratically in the crepuscular haze. Compressed from the Loom-Mother's slime-gouts and Knife-Singer's shed lattices, it remembers all intents—chisel marks freshen in its passing, half-formed gargoyles' eyes clearing milky to foxfire gleam. Predatory in subtlety: sucker-feet *clack* softly, mandibles harvesting polyps from the Mother's pauldrons or snatching fungal blooms from the Singer's paint, cycling matter manic through the thimble-ecosystem. It syncs all: when brine levels crest for exit-caprice, it swells first, forcing the drowning birth-canal open with geyser-pressure; whisper doubt its coherence, and it unspools fringes, spilling lush ruin-thread toward the spire-abyss. Yet it coheres by hallucination-collective— the three in symbiosis, their presences overlapping in shadow-pools, voices braiding into the roar-intimate: *creak-thrash-rip-gurgle-hum*.

No herds, no exodus; they are the pocket's half-life insistence, primitives frayed to fraying sanity, merging with the forge they willed. Stay, and they claim you—brine invades pores, glyphs etch your palms, vines lash you substrate. Emerge altered, or unspool with the weave; here, dwellers *are* the becoming, alive in confinement's claw.
