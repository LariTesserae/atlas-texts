In the drowned crown of this abyssal highland, where basalt spires claw at an ocean-sky and currents hymn their tidal litanies, life does not scuttle or swarm. It *endures*—rooted, resonant, a symphony of vegetable sovereigns whose dominion is absolute, their forms both throne and scepter in this realm of inverted elevation. They are the **Abyssal Thallarchs**, not mere plants but the plateau's incarnate memory, colossal entities born of spore and stone, each a sovereign fiefdom unto itself amid the silty expanses. No herds migrate these ridges, no predators stalk the vales; the Thallarchs rule a court of stillness, their subjects the lesser fronds and filaments that cluster in fealty at their rhizomal feet.

Picture the greater among them: the **Stipe-Lords**, titans scaled to the verticality of the drowned peaks, their primary stipes rising like obsidian masts from anchor-clusters that grip schist and sediment with chitinous spurs, delving meters into the substrate to sip from hydrothermal roots. A single Stipe-Lord spans thirty meters crown to claw, its cartilage-trunk translucent as frozen marrow, veined with bioluminescent canals that pulse in circadian throbs—emerald swells yielding to violet troughs, timed not to light (for there is none) but to the current's 1.5-decibel roar. From the summit unfurl phylloid sails, broad as the collapsed roofs of ancient hovels, fringed with cilia that siphon particulates from the syrup-thick flow; these beat in unison, generating micro-currents that draw nutrients upward like feudal levies. Pneumatocysts dangle in grapnel-clusters, ballooning with osmotic precision to buoy spore-pods aloft, only to contract in deflationary sighs, expelling gelatinous progeny into the tide's embrace. They do not compete; isolation is their creed, each Lord enthroned upon a private plateau or ravine-lip, groves separated by barren kilometers where silt undulates unclaimed.

Yet they are no passive flora. Touch a phylloid—perhaps with the haft of a petrified adze dredged from the floor—and it *recoils* first, a shiver rippling down the stipe like neural fire, then *extends*, cilia weaving a lattice to cradle the intruder, pneumatocysts inflating in buoyant welcome. This is no reflex; it is *reciprocity*, etched into their electrolytic flesh. The water's subtle charge— that humming soup of ions and arc-lightning—binds them in a diffuse web, filaments crackling in Morse-like exchanges across the void, sharing tales of current-peaks and vent-blooms. A Stipe-Lord touched by rune-scribe (as the ancients once did, their flint-blades now half-buried relics) *remembers*: its local currents part in deference, phylloids aligning to form archways through which the flow whispers secrets, detritus cyclones halting mid-spin. They sense the plateau's erosion, countering it with silica-exudates that crust their bases anew, sculpting themselves into fluted bastions that echo the ruins they have claimed—cairns overgrown, hovels roofed in living fronds, nets rewoven from their own shed stipes.

Lesser kin attend them: the **Hydrozoan Vassals**, rigid symbiotes at the seeps, their "tube-worm" bodies photosynthetic spires that quiver like courtiers, filter-fronds splayed to harvest the vents' defiant warmth. Not worms, truly, but colonial accretions—each a fractal polity of polyps fused into plant-stature, crowned in feathery diadems that photosynthesize the gloom's scant photons and chemosynthetic bounty. They bloom in meadows where superheated plumes geyser, bodies rigid as coral hammers, yet flexile in the boil, fronds parting to vent gasses in percussive *hiss-cracks* that harmonize with the choral drone. In bloom-frenzies, they eject spore-clouds that drift to germinate on distant Stipe-Lords, forging alliances; senesce, and their tatters slough to fertilize the silts, cycling dominion back to the soil.

Deeper strangeness animates their court at 2.0 intensity: **Phasal Overlaps**, where phylloids of neighboring Thallarchs intersect without clash, matter thinning to probability-veils that permit passage, as if the Lords test boundaries in quantum diplomacy. Time bends to their rhythm—in current lulls, they dilate, fronds freezing mid-undulation like contemplative statuary, echoes of their *creak-hum* looping in hollow reverberations that summon ancestral *booms* from ravine-depths. In ebbs, they accelerate, spores bursting in synchronized salvos, vents flaring in symphonic reply. Touch elicits *coils* that guide the drifter upward or down, a mutualism blurring sovereign from supplicant—perhaps you become vassal, spore-laden, or they draw you into symbiosis, your warmth traded for luminescent escort through the dark.

These are the ancients' heirs, or their apotheosis. The cairns and hovels? Not ruins of exile, but *shed husks*—the pre-flood folk, plateau-dwellers of horn-tools and kelp-weave, who inscribed runes not as craft but communion, dissolving into the flood's logic. Their essence persists in the Thallarchs: stipes echoing human spines, phylloids veined like tattooed skins, responsive coils the ghost of hands extended. No corpses litter the vales; they *became* the monarchy, transient presences propagated through spore-lines, their forgotten thoughts encoded in the water's memory. A blade etched with their old sigils stirs the Lords to part currents, honoring kin returned.

Drift among them, and the court reveals its gradations: juvenile fronds, mere meter-stalks questing from cairn-crevices, pulsing violet-naive; senescent giants, phylloids tattered to lace, their hums a dirge that calls vents to erupt in farewell blooms; fractal whorls birthing sub-Lords that bud independence, claiming micro-plateaus. No death mars the rhythm—decay feeds rebirth, erosion balanced by deposition, emptiness by sparse abundance. The Thallarchs *live* as the place lives: in tidal breaths that swell the chorus, in electrostatic whispers across the ink-black, in the pull of contradictions—cold fire, dark light, still animation. They evolve with each submersion: a new rune quickens a stipe's coil, a drifter's warmth sparks a bloom-frenzy, probabilistic overlaps weaving Lords into a greater web. Submerge longer, and one stirs *for you*—a pneumatocyst brushes your flank, buoying you toward its crown, where the hum resolves to near-words, a drowned highland's eternal, vegetable welcome. They are the plateau's pulse, its necropolis choir, becoming ever more sovereign in the flood's unyielding hymn.
