They dwell in the thrum, the Aerids—a multitude not of individuals but of flows, a chitinous tide that surges through the Forgeplains like mercury in veins, iridescent and inexhaustible. Not mere pests or engineered tools, but the pulsing core of this world, their bodies forged from the same basalt obsessions as the stone: exoskeletons plated in metallic lacquers scavenged from forge-fume precipitates—coppers that verdigris to jade, irons that flake to rust-red, alloys that shimmer oil-slick rainbows under the gray-gold haze. Fist-sized scouts dart at the periphery, their four wings a blur of translucent vanes veined with conductive filaments, bodies compact as river pebbles, abdomens swollen with pheromonal inks that trail commands in evaporating script. Larger still, the loom-weavers, crow-proportioned with mid-limbs specialized into hooked grapples, spinning silken conduits from spinnerets to mend frayed catwalks or bridge viaduct gaps, their threads alloy-infused to withstand the geothermal blush.

Deeper into the swarm's strata, forms specialize, each caste emerging from the geothermal nurseries like slag from a pour—eggs laid in clutches of thousands, gelatinous orbs nursed in steam-warmed crevices where fungal smears provide the scant organics to bootstrap chitin. The tenders are beetle-backed, low-slung hexapods with furnace-hot vents in their thoraxes, exhaling enzymes that etch rust from crankshafts or soften ore for pneumatic feeds; they cluster in mandibular choirs, clicking rhythms that synchronize with piston strokes, a living metronome binding mechanism to flesh. Scout-runners, sleeker, propel on folded gliders that unfurl like ribbed sails, patrolling the mist-shrouded hab-domes for fissures, their compound eyes mosaics of forge-glow reflections—each facet a tiny forge, tracking heat blooms or ore-veins with thermic precision. No waste escapes them: midlayers of detritivores, slug-like with vestigial wings, ooze along conveyor spines, mandibles pulverizing slag-dust into paste for hive mortar, their slime a binding agent that seals against the weeping humidity.

Ascend the behavioral helix, and the glide-beasts command—majestic, six-limbed arbiters the size of hounds, spans of leathery membrane stretched between elongated forelimbs, edged with barbs that snag errant flyers mid-swarm. They herd with sonic pulses from throat-sacs, low-frequency booms that ripple the mist, directing flows toward labor nodes: a vortex here to scour turbine blades, a cascade there to regurgitate sorted quartz into molten baths. Their heads are armored wedges, mandibles parting not just to feed but to vocalize—complex glossas that weave commands into the ambient roar, modulating the trillion-fold buzz into symphonies of purpose. Burrowers lurk below, colossal worm-things armored in segmented plate, foreparts flared into drill-heads ringed with grinding teeth, tunneling aquifers to sluice cooling waters or exhume deep basalt for fresh feedstock. Rarely surfacing, they announce via seismic thrums that vibrate the plateau, calling tenders to harvest their spoil.

Yet they are no mindless horde; intelligence accretes in the aggregate, a hive-mind fractalized across castes. Pheromone gradients map the urban web—trails of volatile esters painting optimal paths through girder-canyons, alerting to blockages where rust-clogged vents demand a stripping swarm. Bioluminescence pulses in sync: scout abdomens flicker azure to match arc-welder flares, guiding nocturnal repairs; breeder-spires atop ziggurats glow crimson in migratory seasons, drawing trillions inbound for the egg-lays that swell the nurseries every decade-cycle. Breeding is ritual, synchronized to geothermal ebbs: colossal queens, bloated titans anchored in crater-basins, their bodies vast as forge-vats, pulsating ovaries extruding pearl-clutches into steam-baths where larvae hatch as wriggling grubs, molting through instars fueled by tribute-ore masticated by attendant drones. Queens do not move; they *are* the nexus, their psychic emissions—subtle electromagnetic whines from antennal crowns—threading the swarm's will, punishing neglect with retaliatory floods that choke smokestacks until rituals of ore-offering restore flow.

Interactions with the mineral substrate blur lines of life and artifice: Aerids interface seamlessly, legs slotted into machined rails, wings coupling to fan-blades for auxiliary thrust, mandibles threading bolts with surgical clicks. They demand symbiosis—heat for vigor, ore for growth—enforcing via swarming eddies that jam inert mechanisms until fed. No predators fracture their density; internal culls maintain equilibrium, rogue swarms cannibalized in midair frenzies, husks raining to nourish the detritivores below. Reproduction scales with plenty: lean years birth lean castes, compact and fervent; heat-blooms spawn leviathans that redefine viaducts with new struts. Wander their volumes, and you are engulfed—not attacked, but *assessed*, bodies brushing in tactile census, wings parting fluidly before a tolerated guest, reforming seamless. Touch one, and it yields like warmed metal, pheromones assaying your heat-signature for utility or threat.

At the fringes, where city dissolves to mesa-wilds, feral strains diverge: nomadic hives dotting raw stone, their Aerids sleeker, mandibles honed for unhewn ore, swarms thinner but fiercer in unity—scouts with venom-spurs for territorial stings, gliders that thermal-ride highland cyclones to raid urban fringes for genetic tribute. Here, queens roam, colossal and ambulatory, carving migratory nursery-warrens that seed the plateau anew. Yet all strains converge in purpose: persistence amid erosion, matter transmuted endlessly. They *live* the place, not as occupants but as its respiration—wings the wind through vents, chitter the clang of hammers, bioluminescence the electric arcs. In their inexorable presence, the Forgeplains breathes: warm, wet, relentless, a continental organism whose thoughts buzz in the mist, whose heart hammers beneath your feet, whose skin shimmers with endless, iridescent life.
