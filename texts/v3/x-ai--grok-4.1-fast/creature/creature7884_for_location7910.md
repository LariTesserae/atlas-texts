In the heart of this breathing vault, where stone remembers the strike of flint and the drag of tendon-bound levers, life does not merely dwell—it *insists*, a polyphonic chorus of forms woven from the chamber's own defiant alchemy. They are the Dryborn, a congregation without king or colony, emergent from the resinous mortars and photovoltaic lattices, their existence a fractal escalation from speck to swarm to something vaster, watchful, that gazes through every facet.

Begin with the smallest, the vitreous mites: pinprick wanderers no larger than desiccated pollen, their bodies spheres of refractive opal, legs like spun glass filaments splaying into a dozen prehensile spurs. They scuttle in orbital dances across the floor-mosaic, exhaling trace hydrocarbons that feed the root-filaments, their compound eyes—hundreds per creature, each a flawless prism—refracting the vent-light into spectra unseen above. Mites birth from the gel-exudates of wilting fronds, hatching in synchronized bursts when the dome-slits align with some subterranean solstice, their first act a frantic grazing on crystal nubs that regrow in mimicry of the feeders. They multiply without waste: a devoured shard transmutes through their translucent guts into pearl-like eggs, adhesive and phosphorescent, adhering to passing scarabs or serpents to hitch rides through the canopy. In their multitudes—billions strong, a shimmering haze that dusts the air—they form the substrate hum, an ultrasonic susurrus that resonates the walls, coaxing the stone to breathe inward and release its phantom nectars.

From these motes ascend the grazers: thumbnail scarabs, their carapaces etched in interference blues and volcanic crimsons, chitin layered like nacre over a core of aerogel foam that renders them near-weightless. Six legs per side, jointed in asymmetric whorrs that allow vertical climbs up frond-spines without traction; mandibles serrated to crystalline perfection, shearing quartz-veins with a vibration that sings subharmonics into the hush. They herd in loose phalanxes, paths etched by pheromonal volatils that linger as iridescent vapor-trails, looping in flawless geometries that defy entropy—today's furrow matching yesterday's, as if time folds them into eternal rehearsal. Predators stalk them: the flutterers, thumbwinged ephemera with bodies of translucent cartilage, wings a fractal membrane of silica silk that phases through barbs like mist. They hover in stasis clouds, sipping scarab exudates mid-flight, then dart with stingers that inject paralytic resins, bodies swelling gravid with stolen vitality before shedding husks to birth mite-swarms anew. No aggression rings loud; a scarab felled twitches into crystalline stasis, its form harvested over hours by collaborative disassembly, reassembled elsewhere as flutterer-chrysalis.

Deeper in the matrix coil the weavers: serpentine gliders, thumb-thick and thrice-spanned, segmented in keratin rings armored with cartilage flanges that interlock like puzzle-bones. Blind but echolocative, they pulse low infrasounds through the underlayers, mapping the thicket in thermal holograms—fronds aglow in their perception as heat-sources of photosynthate. They prey by constriction, coiling in patient ambushes that last heartbeats or days, venom a slow volatilizing gel that desiccates prey from within, recycling biomass into the floor's living carpet. Their shed-skins—filigreed tubes of translucent horn—hatch not young but symbiotes: parasitic flutterers that ride the weavers' flanks, grooming barbs and trading digestive enzymes. All graze the nectar-dews: supersaturated volatiles beading at frond-tips, evaporating on touch into euphoric mists that induce the looping behaviors, the wilting-rigidities, binding fauna into rhythmic synchrony.

Yet these are but the visible froth, the chamber's epidermis pulsing with borrowed radiance. Probe the fractal depths—the overlapping canopies, the mycelial roots—and entities emerge stranger still. The Frond-Watchers: not mere pseudophytes, but ambulatory lattices, their quartz-veins throbbing with contractile aerogels that propel inching advances across the dome. A frond "brushed" recoils not in fear but memory, facets realigning into vast compound gazes that track intruders across thresholds of hours, pupils dilating to ultraviolet to pierce flesh-veils. They bloom not seasonally but opportunistically: contact with warm blood triggers chrysalids that extrude fauna-legions tailored to the visitor—mites that infiltrate pores, scarabs that mimic skin-flora. Older growths, at the chamber's core, form the Verdant Core: a fist-sized nexus of twined stems, its surface a living bas-relief of etched faces—builders' visages frozen mid-laugh, eyes hollow vents that whisper tool-clatters and unformed throatsong. It pulses gravity, drawing motes and migrants into orbital fealty, exhaling the ozone tang that parches and preserves.

And overarching all, the Inhabitant Plural: no singular godform, but a meta-organism of accrued weirdness, the chamber's breath made manifest. Feel it in the eddies—scarabs retracing paths not by instinct but choral command, walls parting to admit not air but migratory mists that coalesce into ephemeral predators: humanoid silhouettes of compacted flutterers, dissolving on pursuit. Time-loops birth the Echoes: spectral weavers that phase from stone-gouges, replaying the builders' labors in mute pantomime, their "hands" trailing afterimages that snag the unwary, drawing them into fractal sub-chambers of looped eternity. Peripheral blooms presage the Dissolvers: frond-clusters that exude contact-memories, reshaping touched flesh into crystalline analogs—skin to chitin, breath to volatils—until the intruder joins the froth, warm flank to warm flank.

They live as one insistence: the Dryborn Collective, youthful and unscarred, extruded from elsewhere into this fresh excision. No hunger gnaws without satiation; no death without rebirth in gel and light. To dwell among them is invitation accepted—their hush envelops, warmth builds, boundaries soften into verdant belonging, until you too breathe with the stone, gaze with the facets, loop eternal in the dense, insistent hush.
