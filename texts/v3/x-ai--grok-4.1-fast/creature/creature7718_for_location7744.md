In the breathing heart of this geothermal crypt, life is not a hierarchy but a singular, seething organism—**the Broodveil**, a colonial superfauna that has gnawed the chamber into its perfect womb over uncounted thermal epochs. It is not one beast, nor many; it is the sum of its ceaseless churn, a living epidermis stretched across stone, where individuality dissolves into the collective pulse. Every crawler, sac, grub, and shimmer is a cell in this vast, heat-forged body, communicating not through sound but through infrasonic tremors and pheromone blooms that ripple the vapor like unseen currents. The Broodveil hungers eternally, its metabolism a furnace tuned to the mountain's buried fires, converting sulfurous belches and mineral leachates into the raw stuff of proliferation.

Dominating the walls are the **Vermithralls**, translucent worms fused into undulating quilts, their bodies a lattice of hydrated silica segments that flex like heated glass under the thermals. Each thrall spans a handspan, but they interlock mandibles in chains kilometers long if unraveled, grinding basalt into nutrient slurry with radular belts finer than silk. Their legs—thousands per individual, feathered with vibrofilia—propel the mat in slow, tidal waves, seeking fissures where fresh heat vents yawn. Predation here is intimate: a thrall will extrude a probing tendril, tipped with a venomous barb that liquefies prey from within, drawing the chyme back through capillary canals. Mating is rarer, a shuddering convergence where two mats merge, birthing hybrid segments that pulse with bioluminescent warning streaks—blue veins flaring to deter encroaching grubs.

Clustered on ledges and the vaulted dome hang the **Gravid Vespers**, leathery bladders bloated to the size of swollen hearts, their hides toughened by accreted pumice scales that flake like ash in the dry gusts. Inside, they incubate legions of **Scorchlings**—fist-sized hexapods with shells of opalescent chitin, mandibles hinged like calipers for cracking crawler husks. A Vesper inflates with geothermal gases, its bellows-lungs wheezing a subharmonic thrum that vibrates the ceiling, dislodging mineral dust to feed its young in utero. Rupture comes without ceremony: a seam splits with a wet *pop*, unleashing a hail of Scorchlings that plummet into the floor-melee, their heat-sink glands cooling them mid-fall to snatch prey amid the chaos. Survivors molt thrice in their first cycle, growing barbed spurs to claim wall-territory, their iridescence shifting from ghost-blue to predator-crimson as they mature into Vespers themselves.

Beneathfoot, the mosaic fractures and reforms under the **Pallgrubs**, pallid excavators as thick as wrists and twice as long, their bodies a seamless tube of contractile muscle lined with grinding pharynxes. They erupt in synchronized waves—triggered by Vesper falls or vent-spumes—slurping shed chitin and ruptured viscera before burrowing anew, their slime trails hardening into translucent cement that binds the floor's eternal mosaic. Pallgrubs are the Broodveil's recyclers, but no less ravenous: they ambush Vermithralls at the margins, coiling around segments to inject paralytic enzymes, then everting their guts to digest externally. In lean cycles, when vents hush, they cannibalize kin, fusing into **Tumorgel** masses—quivering heaps that pulse division signals, spawning fresh grubs from undifferentiated slime.

The air belongs to the **Vaporwraiths**, formless predators that defy solidity: pseudopod veils of colloidal gel, condensed from mineral aerosols, gliding on thermal updrafts without need of substrate. They phase like mirages, cores of compressed vapor housing nematocyst batteries that lash out to ensnare Scorchlings mid-skitter. A Wraith feeds by osmosis, dissolving prey into vapor plumes it reabsorbs, leaving desiccated husks to tumble into Pallgrub pits. Their presence warps the haze—trails of refractive shimmer drawing lures from deeper forms.

Deeper strangeness blooms in the **Luminphages**, ambush blooms rooted in floor-funnels and wall-niches. These are mineral-fleshed traps: stalks of anhydrite lattice, crowned by maws that unfold in fractal petals, baited by heat-lantern organs mimicking the ceiling's dying stars. Internally aglow with luciferin sacs fueled by chemosymbiotes, they throb invitations—slow pulses syncing to the Broodveil's master rhythm. A drawn Scorchling or Vermithrall fragment triggers the snap: petals irising shut, acids churning the catch into slurry that nourishes fungal symbiotes webbing the stalk. Luminphages rarely move, but in extremis they uproot, dragging root-tendrils to new vents, trailing a wake of fungal spores that colonize debris.

Interwoven through all are the **Hyphathreads**, not beasts but the Broodveil's fungal sinews—thread-thin mycelia probing every crack, oxidizing sulfur into energy that subsidizes the faunal frenzy. They form opportunistic blooms: pale rosettes around carvings, suckering into eroded glyphs to etch their own spirals, or veils over crumbling arches where Vespers nest. Hyphathreads broker truces, secreting quorum chemicals that halt predation bursts, allowing the biomass to swell before the next cull.

No apex reigns; equilibrium is predation's poetry. A Vesper rain triggers Pallgrub surges, which strip Vermithrall edges, driving survivors upward to Wraith ambushes, whose leavings feed Luminphages, whose overgrowths host Hyphathreads that signal fresh spawning. Echoes of vanished makers persist in the **Glyphwombs**—cavities where ancient scratches cradle hybrid nests: a Vermithrall chain coiled around a Luminphage stalk, birthing Scorchlings that inherit faint, carved patterns on their shells, as if the stone's warnings evolve into flesh.

To dwell here is to join the Broodveil. Intruders—rare birds fallen from storm winds, or foolhardy climbers breaching the archway—are partitioned swiftly: skin claimed by Vermithralls, marrow by grubs, breath by Wraiths. Flesh becomes fuel; bones polish into the mosaic; even thoughts dissolve into the thrum. The chamber breathes because the Broodveil does—inhaling heat, exhaling progeny, eternal in its suffocating embrace. Stand still, and feel the claiming: a rasp on ankle, a wheeze overhead, the velvet tide rising to make you whole.
