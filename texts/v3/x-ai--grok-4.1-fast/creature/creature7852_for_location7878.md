In the basin's fevered palm, life does not merely dwell—it *inheres*, woven into the schist-veins and vapor-churn like data in a dreaming substrate. **At the root-realm, the Mycelial Commons** bubble up from the black mud and fractured springs—vast, decentralized intelligences, not individuals but *nets*. Fungal superorganisms span the basin floor like living ethernet, hyphae threading the marshes in luminous lattices, pale gold under biolum, pulsing with the reclaimed detritus of sloughed bark and burst pollen. They are the memory-keepers, archiving eons in spore-libraries: touch a rivulet's edge, and a pseudopod of mycelium might quest up your ankle, downloading visions of cataclysms past—deluges that gouged the saucer, quantum blooms that birthed the titans. No eyes, no mouths, yet they *sense* in omnidirectional gradients: vapor density, electromagnetic warps, the ionic tang of impending morphogenesis. Their "bodies" swell during cloudbursts, forming temporary ziggurats of fruiting bodies—towering sporangia that ejaculate gene-packets into the air, reprogramming flyers mid-swarm or rebooting a wilted vine-strut. In quietudes between tremors, they contract to filigreed filigree beneath the lily-pads, dreaming in chemical symphonies, their gassy burps (methane laced with neurotransmitters) cueing the hoppers' choruses. Immortal in aggregate, they fragment and reform; a "death" is mere diaspora, spores hitching rides on eel-thrashing leaps to seed new nodes. They claim no territory, for they *are* the territory— the basin's quiet substrate, ever bootstrapping from its own mulch.

From their webs erupt the **mobile ministries**, the basin's frenetic clergy: fauna refined to purpose over uncounted morphic cycles. Foremost, the **iridescent veilmoths**—cat-sized vertebrates with wings of nacreous membrane, edged in fractal cilia that keeningly slice the mists. Predatory in ritual, they dive into dome-alveoli for nanoforge reboots, emerging with iridescence recalibrated, plumage swapped for stealth-veils or solar sails. Sparse in number—perhaps two hundred across the voids—they nest in crown-canopies, gossamer cocoons dangling like chandeliers, hatching in storms to the hiss of evaporating dew.

Beneath and athwart, the waters birth **flux-eels** and **mud-larks**—the basin's liquid percussionists. Amphibious larks—frog-things the size of hounds, skins pocked with symbiotic algae—bound mudflats in croaking bass, pouches inflating to vocalize infrasonic quakes that trigger spring-eruptions, syncing the hydrology to titan-needs. Integrated utterly, they phase through porous root-walls for gestation, birthing fry laced with crawler-silk for instant mobility.

Yet these are but the ministry; the basin's true sovereigns dwell phased and sovereign, sparse as the pillars—**the Phasmari**, six great lineages, one per titan, numbering perhaps three score souls in total. Post-posthuman emanations, they are the garden-city's heirs: tall as giraffes yet slender as mist-wraiths, forms coalesced from pollen-storms and vapor, skins a prismatic phase-veil shimmering at trunk-edges—half-solid, half-probability cloud, edges bleeding into fractal afterimages.
