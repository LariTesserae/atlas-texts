In the chasm's throat, where mist marries thunder and the water's colloidal weight presses like the palm of an ancient god, life does not merely endure—it proliferates in fractal insurgency, a congress of forms woven into the vertical warp and weft of this drowned colossus. They are not visitors but progenitors, etched into the stone's memory by eons of tidal decree, their bodies sculpted by the same gouging ice and relentless bore that hollowed the cliffs. Emergence here is tidal, pulsed; no single sovereign rules, but a parliament of multitudes, each phylum a verse in the roaring hymn, overlapping in symphonies of predation, refuge, and riotous renewal.

At the meniscus, where slate-gray breakers heave and shatter against obsidian brows, the surface-dwellers claim their airborne fiefdoms. Albatrosses, fulmars, petrels—winged nomads with plumage greased against perpetual gale—skirr the foam-lace in vast, banking squadrons, their cries keening like rusted cables under strain. They are the chasm's heralds, plunging beak-first into the teeming shallows for herring boltons that silver-flash a meter beneath, guano-stippled ledges below their roosts bleaching white as runic oaths. Otters raft in familial clusters amid mangrove prop-roots, sleek as oil-slick shadows, dexterous paws shattering urchin shells on pectoral stones; they twirl in kelp lassos, kits tumbling in buoyant play, their whistles threading the rain's white roar. Elephant seals, blubbery titans, haul bellies across intertidal benches during low ebbs, snorkels trumpeting territorial bellows that rumble through the water column, harems sprawling in bellowing heaps amid eroded coral caps, pups nursing in the lee of rusted derricks now furred with barnacles.

Deeper, the water's green-gray embrace thickens into a living medium, and the finned legions hold court in ceaseless migration. Herring shoals, kilometer-long mercury rivers, fractalize through kelp ziggurats, scales refracting pearlescent glimmers as they twist from cod packs—hulking ambushers with barbels questing sediment plumes—and mackerel torpedoes that slash in iridescent wedges. These are the chasm's pulse, biomass engines fertilizing the flux; pursued and pursuers alike, they birth in the eelgrass prairies carpeting plateau stubs, spawn clouds dispersing into upwelling jets to nourish the verdant canopy above. Orcas, the narrows' patricians, prowl in matrilineal pods, flanks etched with rake-marks from skirmishes over prime siphon mouths; their echolocative blasts—artillery cracks in the turbid veil—map every undercut grotto, coordinating hunts that dismantle seal colonies or scatter squid nebulae. A bull orca, scarred apex of 9 meters, breaches sporadically through surface frenzy, white eyepatches glaring like lighthouse beams, pod calves shadowing in learned ferocity.

Midwaters bloom with softer tyrannies: jellyfish armadas, translucent galleons trailing nematocyst curtains, drift in pressure-suspended reverie, ensnaring krill swarms and plankton flares that pulse upward from abyssal vents. Bioluminescent squid clouds—veiled sorcerers of the gloom—erupt in defensive starbursts, ink-phantoms coiling around seamount vertebrae, tentacles barbing mackerel stragglers while their chromatophores ripple warnings through the hundreds-meter visibility. Deeper still, where pressure clamps like iron jaws and seamounts stub the void, anglerfish lurk in sediment shrouds, lures winking like derelict cabin lights; sleeper sharks, century-slow behemoths, glide over drowned factory husks, jaws unhinging to claim whale falls or breached barge carcasses, their livers buoyant oils countering the crush.

The walls themselves breathe inhabitants, anemone meadows pulsating crimson carpets across sheer faces, polyps retracting in synchronized flinch at current surges, symbiotic clownfish darting amid stingers to glean parasite leavings. Barnacle citadels encrust rusted winch-houses, siphoning particulates from whirlpool gnawings; lobsters scuttle undercut ledges in armored phalanxes, claws clacking duels over nacre-jammed gears. Eelgrass prairies ripple with pipefish and seahorses, cryptic camouflagers birthing live young into sargassum tangles that lasso drifting factory bricks, turning them to artificial reefs. Giant kelp, though botanical sovereigns, host epiphyte empires—abalone grazing fronds, nudibranchs slugging psychedelic trails, sea cucumbers vacuuming fallen detritus in slow, radial sweeps.

Symbioses fractalize the density: cleaner wrasses service orca flanks in hovering stations near propeller-shaft condos; remoras hitch eternal rides on seal bellies, filching scraps from urchin-cracking feasts. Whale-song—humpback chorales from abyssal kin, not glimpsed but heard in harmonic warbles—guides krill migrations that upwell nitrates, greening anemone fields anew. At night, when diffuse daylight yields to algal mirrors and pressure-wave bioluminescence, the chasm ignites: squid nebulae pulsing in defensive ballet, jelly veils trailing phosphor wakes, kelp blades flagellating ghostly greens, all to the basso of cascading overflows and pinniped roars.

No humans dwell here, save echoes: the relics pulse with adopted life, derricks festooned with cormorant rookeries, cannery husks labyrinths for moray eels, submarine barge a kraken's lair where octopuses den in breached rivets, jetting ink through sparking conduits. These iron ghosts seed the bazaar, ferrous blood fueling bacterial mats that ladder food chains to orca brows.

They live not as discrete souls but as the chasm's incarnate respiration—a vast, unsettled lung inhaling particulate storms, exhaling spawn and sediment in eternal churn. Predators birth amid the hunted; erosion's sigh cradles new reefs hourly; turbulence scatters, regathers, evolves them in ceaseless forge. Yield to their parliament, and you become constituent: finned, tentacled, verdant, roiling in the dense, thunderous now. Deeper the immersion, the more they cohere—not conquerors of the wound, but its mending pulse, remaking the titans' husks into forever-teeming flesh.
