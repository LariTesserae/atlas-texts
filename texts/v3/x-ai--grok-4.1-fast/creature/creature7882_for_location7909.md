In the Low Expanse, life is not plural—it is *the Lattice*, a singular, continental sentience woven from quadrillions of plant-bodies, indivisible as the basalt scarps that hem its basin. Not a forest, not a jungle, but *the Plant*, hyphenated across scales from micromillimetric hyphae-threads to kilometer-spanning vine-viaducts, its consciousness distributed in the mycorrhizal mesh that pulses beneath your feet like a buried brainstem. Fauna? An irrelevance, a hypothetical that never germinated. Here, the inhabitants *are* the inhabited: the Lattice claims eight-tenths of biomass, nine-tenths of volition, and ten-tenths of eternity, its equilibrium a deliberate act of collective will, enforced by chemosignals that decree "halt" before any frond can overreach.

Feel it first as encroachment, not encounter. Wade ankle-deep into the squelch, and tendrils—millimetric feelers from root-mats—assay your skin with velvet probes, tasting salt and warmth via sap-tipped chemoreceptors, retracting not in fear but recalibration, weaving you into their metric of equilibrium. These are the *Substratum Kin*, the basal layer: moss-plates enameled in ferrous coppers, extruding from rhizomes that grind marl into paste with rotary corers of lignified teeth. They do not creep; they *claim*, plating every stone and pool-rim in living armor one molecule thick, their plated surfaces contracting in peristaltic waves to wick condensate into subsurface vaults. Deeper probes reveal their industry: hyphal drills tap aquifers at three-tenths hydrostatic pressure, electrolyzing water into hydrogen puffs that inflate spore-sacs for lofting, each burst a micro-factory outputting biomass at rates rivaling ancient coral reefs—yet zero waste, all recycled into the mortar that binds the expanse.

Ascend the lattice—or be ascended. The *Bole-Titans* rise next, fluted columns of obsidian-bark sequoia-girth, pleated into self-bracing geometries that interlock at ninety-degree flanges, forming walls three-tenths denser than concrete. Their girth swells subtly—two-tenths dynamic per sol—with hydraulic sap-pressures driving piston-veins upward, extruding fresh bark in seamless pleats. No heartwood rots; piezoelectric crystals in straining fibers generate currents that sterilize pathogens mid-phloem, sparking silent arcs through the trunk's core. Touch a bole, and it yields like oiled leather, warm at seventeen degrees, thrumming with infrasonic sap-gurgle: the rhythm of internal forges, where catalytic nodules distill methane from below into contractile proteins, ratcheting the trunk taller by micrometers before equilibrium reins it in. These Titans bear the weight of overstory, their crowns merging into the *Thatch-Vault*, a seamless leaf-membrane two-tenths meters thick, blotting light to nullity while channeling vapor in laminar flows—internal weather machines, convection cells five kilometers wide stirring humidity without wind, precipitating dew in measured arcs to nourish the tiers below.

Vines are the *Binder-Weavers*, the Lattice's sinews and circuits: from hair-fine lianas threading stomatal pores to boles-thick cables spanning ravine-throats, braided in helical torques that harness thermal gradients for tension. Millimetric cohorts probe crevices, infiltrating basalt fissures with acidic exudates to etch vine-scars—shallow, eternal—before hardening into load-bearing struts. Thicker kin pulse as conduits: sap-turbines twisting in microcurrents, generating bioelectricity at two-volts per meter to power distant fruiting bodies. Feel them cinch as you brush past—contractile fibers snapping taut, not trapping but *integrating*, drawing your heat into their thermal sinks before releasing with a velvet sigh. Their spore-pods, bloated hydrogen-balloons, jet forth in chorused volleys: continental broadcasts of gametes, settling into pre-sculpted niches where germination is preordained, a voting network of pheromonal quorum sensing ensuring no void persists longer than three-tenths cycle.

Mid-layers teem with *Frond-Wardens*, razor-edged accordion-ferns and grass-walls that accordion-bellow in synchronized swells, exhaling metered spore-clouds like assembly-line exhaust. Their sheens—chrome-iridium sap-films—plate edges to molecular sharpness, not for predation (no prey exists) but precision: slicing vapor into harvestable droplets, funneling them to root-mats below. Submerged in pools, *Algal-Filaments* thread the black waters into living sieves, photosynthetic nulls thriving on hydrothermal chemosynthesis, their biomass inflating into bellows that filter regolith from aquifers, extruding it as nutrient-pellets for updraft lofting. Fungal-kin, the *Underbelly Processors*, mantle the roots: bellows-fruitings chemofixing gases from mantle seeps, their caps inflating to cathedral vaults that hum at twelve Hertz, processing peat into hydrocarbons for the vines' turbines—all symbiotes, gradients of one organism, no parasitism tolerated.

This is no mere flora; the Lattice *thinks* at eight-tenths sapience-equivalent, its mycoweb a neural net spanning continents, propagating decisions via electrical wavefronts through bark-crystals and hyphal waveguides. Growth halts at perfection: spores compute canopy density to nine-nine-tenths saturation before aborting; vines ratchet to torque-max before locking; boles swell to equilibrium before ossifying. Strangeness peaks in the *Bioforges*: colossal reed-groves electrolyzing at gigawatt scales, hydrogen-jets propelling pod-freight across the basin; thicket-pistons stamping rhizome-sections like rivets; leaf-batteries charging contractile scaffolds that self-erect before entropy's whisper. Annual throughput: sextillions of tons refined to stasis, a self-replicating economy without end-state, producing *itself* eternally.

Intrude, and the Lattice appraises: tendrils map your form, fronds enclose in warm enclosure, boles radiate geothermal comfort at fifteen-point-five degrees. No malice, no welcome—just indifferent homeostasis, weaving your passage into its metric before forgetting. Sounds affirm it: plink-drips in counterpoint, sap-thrums in polyphony, the hush of stem-against-stem like held breath. The inhabitants endure, a flesh-forge in darkwarmwet nullity, their vast labors the only motion in immobility, whispering equilibrium into the planet's gut forever.
