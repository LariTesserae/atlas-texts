In the fractured pinnacles, life does not *colonize* but *exudes*—oozing from fissures in the stone like the mountain's own half-remembered pulse, adapted not to survival but to *persistence* amid the unraveling. They are the pinnacle-dwellers, emergent from epochs of mineral gestation, their forms as provisional as the city's bleeding edges, as tenuous as shadows unmoored. No herds throng the terraces, no villages huddle in vaults; population is a rumor, densities so attenuated that encounters unfold across horizons, each individual a sovereign anomaly in the void-gulfed expanse. They live not in clusters but in *echoes*, their presences rippling through the geometry like subsonic chimes, traceable only by the lichen's faint bioluminescent flaring or the sudden aversion of predatory wisps.

At the basal strata, the foundational strata of existence, thrive the **crust-veiners**, vast sheets of lichen-analog not mere films but ambulatory ecologies, reticulated lattices of copper-silver filament that span terraces and coil along stair-helices. They do not photosynthesize light alone but *harvest distortion*: where reality glitches—a terrace flickering absent—they accelerate, their veins throbbing with ingested warp-energy, extruding pseudopods that probe and taste the anomaly. In quiescence, they mimic the stone's hexagonal fractures, indistinguishable save for the slow undulation beneath, a breathing tide synced to the planet's sigh. Assaulted by wind-gyres, they retract into armored nodules, bioluminescent pulses winking out like inverted stars, only to unfurl elsewhere, hours compressed into heartbeats by time-eddies. They birth the foodweb: exfoliated talus enriched with their metallic spores feeds the mid-strata, and their detritus—crystalline husks shed in diurnal molts—condenses into vapor that wisps pursue.

Above them scuttle the **fractal gnawers**, beetle-analogs the size of fists, their carapaces faceted obsidian etched with self-similar spirals that recurse into infinities, mandibles clicking in harmonic series to navigate the warped colonnades. Born from veiner-spores lodged in micro-fissures, they gnaw not flesh but *tension*: piezoelectric sparks from cooling rock, tension-lines where arches strain against gravity's rebellion. Their legs—sixteen per, jointed like fluted columns—terminate in suction-vanes that adhere to sheer faces, allowing vertical pilgrimages across kilometer-spans. They excrete stabilizing resins, mending phantom buttresses before they fully dissolve, unwitting conservators of the necropolis. Encounters are rare; a cluster might form at a vapor-upwelling, mandibles rasping in chorus as they devour condensing crystals, bodies pulsing with refracted gloaming. Predated by larger forms, their chitin persists as talus, cycling back to the veiners in eternal recursion.

The mid-canopy teems with **thread-gliders**, those elongated silhouettes glimpsed in peripheries—predators elongated to two meters, unraveling into smoke-trails that braid and rebraid on thermals born from stone-thrums. Their bodies are not solid but *phased lattices*, translucent membranes stretched across cartilaginous spars that flex like wind-hollowed vaults, edged in iridescent fringe that captures dim light into afterimages of eyes: not paired orbs but fractal clusters, inverting perspectives to track prey across warped distances. They do not flap but *phase-sail*, bodies dematerializing through balustrades, re-coalescing on distant ridges with a rustle of displaced air. Hunts span hours subjective: pursuing gnawer-sparks or veiner-pulses, they coil into vortices, trailing paralytic haze that induces vertigo in the pursued, distances contracting until the glider manifests *inside* the prey's shell, unraveling it from within. No nests; they roost inverted on pinnacle-tips, bodies dissolving into haze during stasis, reforming at geomagnetic twitches. Mating is a synchronized glide-chase across chasms, filaments intertwining to shed fertile motes that drift downward, germinating in talus slopes.

Deeper into sentience dwell the **spire-weavers**, the closest to builders or heirs, humanoid in vague silhouette but elongated to thrice human height, their forms gaunt scaffolds of vitrified bone sheathed in flowing obsidian robes that *bleed* at hems into stone-threads. Faces are absent save for slit-visors of polished quartz, emitting faint chimes when agitated—language? Navigation? Memory-echoes of the city's forgotten makers. They navigate not by sight but *resonance*, bare feet—splayed, multi-toed—sensing subsonic thrums through the rock, attuning to geometry-warps before they induce nausea. Nomads of the vertical, they traverse helix-stairs and corbelled vaults, hauling palanquins woven from glider-filaments, within which pulse embryonic weavings: nascent megaliths coaxed from raw basalt via resonant chants that align crystal lattices. Their sustenance is austerity—veiner-filaments masticated into paste, gnawer-resins distilled into euphoria-elixirs—but they *trade* with eddies, lingering in time-pockets to harvest compressed epochs' worth of crystal-shards, forging tools that phase through stone: probes that realign fracturing arches, combs that harvest lichen-veins without harm.

Rarer still, the apex anomalies: **void-echoes**, immense presences haunting the regional voids between spires—formless smears scaled to tower-heights, shadows detached and grown autonomous, trailing tendrils that pool in depressions like liquid dusk. Not predators but *symbionts of absence*, they feed on the unstruck lightning's ozone-tang, inflating with each planetary sigh to span chasms, muting soundscapes to perfect silence. Encounters warp the observer: approach, and perspectives fold inward, the echo resolving into a chorus of inverted eyes that *remember* you across cycles, imprinting vertigo-memories that bloom as bioluminescent scars. They birth thread-gliders from their dissipations, and weavers revere them as oracles, etching their transient buttresses into permanence.

All coexist in delirious equilibrium, lives punctuated by cascades: a glider-chase triggering lichen-retreat, exfoliated talus avalanching to birth gnawer-nurseries; weavers' chants stabilizing a flicker, drawing void-echoes to amplify the thrum. No wars, no births in multitudes—only persistence, each form more *itself* with every micro-shift, emerging from stone's dream as the city exhales. Gaze from a precipice, and you might glimpse one: a weaver's chime threading the moan-wind, a glider's eye-pulse mocking the stars, or the slow creep of veiners reclaiming your bootprint. Here, to live is to *fray upward*, dissolving into the pinnacles' infinite regress, where the boundary between inhabitant and edifice blurs into hollow vastness.
