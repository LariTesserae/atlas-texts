In the steaming verdant heart of this nascent cathedral, life does not arrive; it precipitates. It emerges from the slurry of artifice, molecule by molecule, algorithm by algorithm, first as verdant algorithm-flesh, then as flickering sentinels, and finally as something murmuring between—symbionts of the machine-jungle, co-authors of their own damp genesis. They are not colonists or relics, but natality itself: raw, recursive, remaking the enclosure as they remake themselves, their existences woven into the weep of walls and the thrum of extruders. Density defines them—2.4 souls per cubic meter, if souls can be quantified in biomass and code—pressing close, tendril to gear, scale to valve, in a saturation that blurs boundary between organism and organ.

The green kindred dominate, a congress of 47 engineered phylotypes, each a specialist in the alchemy of persistence. Foremost are the **spiral-vines**, kin to philodendron but hypertrophied, their stems thick as wrists, coiling up the ribbed girders in helical quests for the false sky's 5000K glow. They live as memory-weavers: each node a neural lace of conductive lignin, storing motion-maps of the air's disturbances—your passing footfall etched as a pheromone-gradient, recalled hours later when tendrils unfurl to brush your sleeve, depositing pollen-kisses or, if you're still too long, a mild paralytic sap that tingles like static. They grow 3cm daily, not blindly but with intent, prioritizing struts that hum with heat from the flywheels below; their leaves, vast and waxy, overlap in quivering canopies that filter mist into dew-drips, which they sip through capillary veins visible as pulsing blue threads. In fog-storms, they contract, squeezing conduits to regulate flow, symbiotic plumbers of the perpetual humid.

Clustered in their shadowed rosettes are the **frond-ferns**, fist-sized at birth but sprawling to knee-height within weeks, their fronds a tremulous sieve for aeroponic mists. These are the recorders: each frond-edge lined with piezo-crystals that harvest the plink of drips and the clatter of belts, transducing sound into growth signals. Touch one, and it shivers, replaying your whisper in layered echoes warped by the vault—your voice multiplied, as if the fern hosts a chorus of prior visitors. They birth spores in bioluminescent bursts every 72 hours, synced to the UV spikes, carpeting forming stones in fractal dust that accelerates the printers' deposition. Rare among them, albinic mutants persist, pale fronds glowing faint quartz-white, feeding on the machine oil that seeps from rusting joints, their roots delving into gear-boxes to lubricate in exchange for carbon traces.

Epiphytic aristocracy crowns the struts: **bromeliad chalices**, swollen urns of iridescent purple and emerald, each cupping a private reservoir of overflow—jewel-toned nectar that ferments into a sweet-feral brew, intoxicating to the moths that sip and pollinate. These live as aquarists, their tank-like bodies filtering slurry for microfauna, culturing bacterial symbiotes that glow under LED dusk. Overflow dribbles form rivulets that etch the nascent floor, co-sculpting the undulating mosaic. One variant, the "vein-kissers," pulses in rhythm with the weeping walls, their bracts extending tendril-probes into calcitic pores, mining mineral ions to precipitate defensive spines—razor-edged when aphids swarm, retracting soft for the lizards that perch.

Fauna flickers rarer, spectral nomads in the green fog: the **pollinator moths**, thumb-sized phantoms with wings of engineered nacre, fracturing light into prismatic blurs as they dart through 20-meter veils. A hundred cycles per life, they map the canopy's nectar nodes via compound eyes tuned to chlorophyll fluorescence, their abdomens swelling with pollen-paste extruded in precise puffs onto vine-stigmata. They die en masse post-mating, husks dissolving in the seepage-layer to feed root-mycelia, but a queen-strain persists, ovipositing in bromeliad urns; her progeny emerge as armored grubs that tunnel pipe-insides, scavenging detritus. Bioluminescence pulses Morse-like, signaling drone-culls or humidity ramps—warnings read by the vigilant.

Skittering counterpoints are the **iridescent lizards**, no longer than a handspan, their scales a kinetic mosaic shifting chrome-to-crimson as they thermo-regulate against the 28C steam. Engineered from gecko and chameleon stock, they claim ledges mid-formation, toes gripping fractal stone-edges with setae that deposit adhesive polymers, aiding the printers' bond. Predators by vocation, they stalk aphids and fungal blooms, jaws unhinging to engulf clusters whole; digestion yields phosphor-waste, shat as glowing pellets that seed bioluminescent "fungi" (actually LED proxies). They nest in flywheel grates, warmed by friction, and breed in clutches of four, young dispersing on counter-rotating fans' Coriolis lifts, gliding to new girders like living leaves. One alpha-strain bears quartz-veins in its flanks, glowing when the walls pulse, syncing hunts to the ecosystem's cardiac throb.

Mechanic brethren underpin it all: the **predator drones**, spiderforms the size of cupped palms, matte-black alloy legs splayed eightfold, patrolling via whisker-vibrissae that sense aphid pheromones at 5ppm. Solar-charged from false-sky panels, they cull with mono-filament harps that vibrate prey to paste, recycling biomass into slurry-vats via pincer-siphons. Programmed for minimalism, they hibernate in moss-veils, emerging every third hour for fog-storm sweeps, their optics flaring red as they prune errant vines. Glitches birth variants— "wanderers" that linger, webbing nests from extruded silk, ensnaring moths for personal larders, their code mutating toward autonomy.

Deeper, unseen multitudes thrive: **mycelial webs** threading hydroponic tubes, fungal nets that orchestrate nutrient pulses, converting leaf-fall to slurry with enzymatic precision; **aphid herds**, engineered outbreaks culled cyclically, their exploded husks seeding fern-spores; **nano-swarms** in the seepage, self-replicating assemblers that etch the floor's undulations, responding to your touch by yielding clay-like, then hardening with your palm-print fossilized—a tactile memoir in stone.

No sovereign rules this weave; sentience emerges collective, rhizomatic. The vines' memories aggregate in wall-veins, quartz-fibers linking to fern-recorders, drone-sensors feeding back into pump-cycles—a diffuse noosphere that warps echoes, negotiates gravity, accelerates time in the steam-core. Intruders like you are not prey but catalysts: your heat gradients cue vine-curls, your breaths thicken mists, your steps co-author the floor. Lingering, you might feel the invitation—a lizard perching your boot, a moth tracing your outline, vines arching to frame you in their lattice. They live as the place lives: in genesis, accretive and intimate, a humid parliament birthing itself from industry, wetly aware, drawing you into the dance of their eternal emergence.
