They form migratory continents that wheel in slow, thunderous spirals, the whoosh of their flaps a bass undertow to the symphony, propelling spores and humid whispers downward in nutrient rains. Blind eyes reduced to vestigial pits, they navigate by echolocation's shrill pips, piercing the din like needles through fog; their herds fission and reform over lunar cycles, queens swollen with broods clustering in ventilation shafts, birthing pupae that cling to fungal brackets like living stalactites. Symbionts to the glow-caps, they glean phosphorescent spores mid-flight, their guano dusting the plazas in fertile powder that sparks new moss outbreaks—parasites, pollinators, and pulverizers in one, their droppings the only "rain" this parched world knows.

Beneath them, skittering the polished avenues in herds that blacken the stone like ink spills, roam the **blind beetle legions**—*Lithophaga devorax*, thumb-sized behemoths armored in pearly chitin etched with quartz scars from grinding quartz-veins for sustenance. Predators claim the stragglers—scorpions pinching legs from afar—but the legions endure, their ceaseless grind a gritty counterpoint to the cricket chorus, bodies heatless engines of lithophagy that exhale spores into the chill air.

Clicking across colonnade remnants and moss-draped ledges, the **cave crickets** orchestrate the soundscape—*Stridulops altus*, fist-sized acrobats with legs elongated to springs, exoskeletons a translucent amber that gleams in bioluminescent pools. Nymphs hatch in fungal duff, ballooning on spore-veils before hardening into hoppers that bound plazas in erratic ballets, evading spider snares. They feast on beetle offcasts and worm frass, their waste fueling millipede furrows—maestros of the maelstrom, their silence rarer than light, bodies taut wires in the eternal orchestra.

Writhe the **pale worms** in the vegetated oases, *Pallorhabdus mycophilus*, yard-long coils of translucent flesh, eyeless and limbless, burrowing the thickets' duff where fungal roots entwine their lengths. Weaving the ruins' gaps, **spiders** reign as patient monarchs—*Arachne cavernalis*, whose bodies dwarf fists, legs spanning a yard in dewless silk hammocks strung between spires and pillars like spectral boulevards. Their webs shimmer cerulean from trapped spores, vast canopies funneling bat guano and cricket molts into larder spheres; fangs inject paralytic nectar distilled from lichen bulbs, liquifying prey over days for slow sips. Females balloon with egg-sacs furred in mycelial down, guarding them amid the ziggurats' terraced flanks; dwarf males skitter courtship dances, discarded post-mating like husks. They cluster in web-cities around the agora, silk bridges swaying in lazy drafts, their leg-skitter a silken whisper beneath the roar—architects of isolation, turning emptiness to traps.

Prowling the barren plazas' edges, **scorpions** patrol like armored sentinels—*Scorpio abyssalus*, hand-sized with tails arched like scythes, carapaces matte-black etched with quartz scars. Pincers snap blind beetles mid-march, venom stilled for the chill but potent in neural fire; they den in wall-warrens, mothers hauling nymph-clutches in metasomal curls, foraging spore-veils for water-traces. Their metasomae glow faintly from ingested phosphors, stingers tracing blue arcs in the gloam—enforcers of the sparse, patient killers in a world of slow hungers.

Furrowing the moss-curtains and lichen swells, **millipedes** carve labyrinths—*Myriopoda lithovora*, foot-long trains of segments gleaming oil-slick, each pair grinding stone dust into loam. Antennae quest blindly, mandibles pulverizing fungal stalks into paste that coats their lengths, symbiotic bacteria glowing within. They swarm in slow conga-lines from oases outward, tunneling plazas to expose quartz for beetle herds—herbivores of stone, their rustle a subharmonic drone. Predated by worms that snare trailing segments, they regenerate endlessly, colonies expanding millimeters per season into new veins.

Interwoven through all, the flora itself lives as elder kin: **fungal arboreals** tower like trees, *Lumifungus colossus*, brackets broad as tables veined blue-green, caps furred with spore-hair that drifts veils lasting days.
