### The Crawlkin Veil
Dominating the walls are the **crawlkin**, hexapedal (sometimes octet, form fluxing with thermal gradients) scuttlers whose carapaces gleam like quenched obsidian, etched with self-repairing runes of arsenic frost that fluoresce under the pocket's bent light. ### The Vaporwings Chorus
Suspended in the syrup-thick air swarm the **vaporwings**, mote-sized flits whose bodies are heat-shimmer prisms: six vanes of quartz-nanofiber that refract light into hypnotic spirals, luring prey into collision-flocks. ### The Boilheart Pseudos
At the basin's core writhe the **boilheart pseudos**, the pocket's aqueous Id: amorphous blobs of thermo-gelatin, fist-nubbin size at maturity, composed of denatured proteins and supercritical water that phase-shifts at bubble interfaces. ### The Swarm's Overmind
No individual reigns; the Thermikines are one entity, quorum-sensed through hemolymph pheromones diffused in steam—decisions ripple as synchronized throbs: all crawlkin halt at geyser *hiss*, vaporwings align helices, pseudos extrude en masse.
