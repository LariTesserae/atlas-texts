In the Crucible Hollow's fevered embrace, life is not a scatter of survivors but a woven multitude, each form an extension of the rock's own dreaming fracture—a biota born from the geothermal blush below, insistent and interlocked, where every breath trades vapor for vigor. They are the Hollow's citizens, architects, and devourers, their bodies sculpted by the same unyielding alchemy that denies water its freedom: desiccation as discipline, hypertrophy as defiance. Density presses them into intimacy (1.8), hides brushing hides, tendrils threading exoskeletons, a parliament of flesh where predation is less murder than merger, sustenance distilled from the hoarded dew of the condemned.

No mere insects, these are the Hollow's masons and monarchs, ambulatory engines of empire (tech 0.3, pure frenzy), each the mass of a great dane, mandibles curved like scythes from jaws that pulverize quartz into paste. Predators by necessity, they dismantle the unwary—a glider snared mid-loop, its membranes mulched into aerial causeway-filler—yet their society hums with castes: breeders swollen queens tunneling deep, their abdomens gravid with pearl-clutches that hatch into pale grubs fattened on alkali-rich slurry.

No apex reigns unchallenged; a termitid queen might fall to glimwing talons, her clutch redistributed to worm-warrens.
