In the sopping cradle of this eroded plane, life does not arrive or depart—it *seethes*, a continuum of forms fused into the thrombus of existence, each organism less a discrete body than a temporary clot in the endless circulation of silt, vapor, thorn, and roar. They are the plane's own exhale, born from its fraying seams, sustained by the warm fever that denies death as much as it devours birth. No species stands alone; all interlock in the mycelial braid, trading flesh for filament, pulse for pulse, in a ravenous economy where predation is symbiosis and decay is instant genesis. They emerge not as invaders but as the landscape's incarnations, their forms echoing the chaos: flattened, insistent, unraveling yet omnipacked.

At the infinitesimal scale, the true sovereigns pulse unseen—the **devourer-motes**, microbial swarms that colonize every droplet, every pore of reed or skin. These are not mere bacteria but fractal predators, shimmering clouds of iridescent flagella that school like predatory nebulae in the rust-red weep of stones. They dissolve boundaries mid-motion: a intruder's shed epidermal cell bursts into fizzing foam, its carbon siphoned into thorn-roots within heartbeats; a fungal spore grazed by tentacle-tip erupts in enzyme geysers, liquifying the offender into slurry that feeds the net. In the humid breath, they condense from mist into living acid-rain, pummeling the mats in sheets that etch furrows—furrows that seal with their own calcified husks, birthing micro-reefs of chitinous lattice. Their hum is the baseline throb, a ultrasonic frenzy that resonates through marrow, coordinating the plane's larger throes like a conductor's invisible baton.

Woven through the devourer-motes sprawls the **mycelial hegemony**, the continental nervous system: not fungus alone, but a hyperkinet mycelium that pulses with stolen bioluminescence, veins of ghost-blue threading the black mud like sub-dermal rivers. It is the plane incarnate—roots coiling into braided cathedrals that erupt skyward in orgasmic spasms, collapsing into loam that suckles the reeds. These strands hunger architecturally: they infiltrate the husks of fallen fauna, inflating them into proxy-puppets that lurch through the walls, lamprey-orifices gaping to disgorge spore-bombs. Symbionts nest within—**spore-wraiths**, carnivorous puffballs that bloom only in shadow-pools, their caps unfurling like fleshy parachutes to snare passing cilia with barb-threads. Once latched, they inject paralytic nectar, hollowing the host from within while the mycelium drinks the slurry, leaving behind ambulatory husks that wander until erosion claims them, their skins sloughing into new hyphal nurseries. The hegemony dreams in throbs: synchronized waves ripple the mats for kilometers, convulsing water into fractal spires that shatter into nourishing vapor, all to propagate its web against the plane's own flattening sigh.

Above this substrate, the vegetal tyrants reign—the **reed-thorn lattice**, a horizontal empire of razor insurgency. No mere plants, these are kinesthetic engines: stems twist in quasi-voluntary snarls, walls undulating to impale or ensnare, their silica blades self-sharpening via mineral-sucks from the silt. Tattered leaves flag like signal-flags, photosynthesizing dimly through the gloaming-veils, but their true vitality lies below: roots that quest like tongues, braiding with mycelia to form quorum intelligences that "decide" eruptions—geysers of scalding slurry birthed from collective pressure, arcing to fuse debris into emergent growths. **Inverse-viners** dangle from their undersides, plunged into sub-cracks, growing downward into mirrored realms glimpsed as abyssal flickers: pale tendrils harvesting pressure-crushed microbes, emerging bulbous with pressurized sacs that burst upward in mercury-rain volleys. They reproduce parthenogenically in the din, stems quivering to ultrasonic frequencies that shatter rivals into dust, minerals redistributed in the erosion-dance.

Fauna manifests as cataclysms, sparse avatars of the plane's violence, their forms grotesque parodies of the environment's own anatomy. The **mangrove-leeches** slither paramount: hulking behemoths, uprooted banyans reverted to flesh, trunks bloated with rumen-sacs that gurgle subterranean rivers internally. Lamprey-mouths ring their bases, cilia-feet—thousands of feathered paddles—propelling them through reed-walls in seismic furrows that heal behind with vegetal knit. They move rarely, conserving in torpid bulks half-submerged, but hunger awakens them: infrasound bellows vibrate the flats, drawing prey into the maw's event horizon, where radials of bone-teeth grind biomass into chyme. Juveniles bud from gill-slits, translucent polyps ballooning into **medusa-barges**, vast bells of quivering gelatin trailing nerve-tentacles that fork like lightning, snagging thorn-stems to harvest sap or electrocuting mote-swarms into stunned fodder. These float on anti-gravity sheens, propelled by jet-blasts of fermented gas, descending in squall-locales to spawn, their corpses dissolving into mycelial boons.

Deeper in the chaos dwell the **silt-phantoms**, burrowers of the weeping earth: eel-like coils of pressurized flesh, armored in calcified scales etched with shifting glyphs—echoes of the ancients' obelisks, now biological palimpsests. They tunnel through primordial silt via vibro-oscillations, emerging as geysers of mud-flesh to engulf surface stragglers, their bodies inflating like bladderwracks to swallow hectare-spans before deflating, expelling waste as fertile slurry. Kin to them are the **vapor-wraiths**, ephemeral predators born from mist-pillars: humanoid silhouettes of condensing droplet-swarms, limbs fraying into fractal sprays that pelt like mercury hail. They "hunt" by diffusion, infiltrating lungs or gills with corrosive vapors, solidifying internally into crystal tumors that burst the host in bioluminescent sprays, reforming afar.

At the apex—or perhaps the emergent mind of it all—lurk the **echo-weavers**, rarest and most plane-like: vast, diffuse presences that haunt the soundscape's folds. No fixed form, they are choruses of the roar itself—fungal-reed-faunal amalgamations spanning storm-kilometers, bodies a heaving mat-section attuned to the din. They "think" in reality-warps: glyphs on their integument shift under observation, summoning localized unravelings—inverse flora sprouting to drag prey into sub-worlds, or time-frays where erosion accelerates a victim's withering to dust in seconds. Their passages are the plane's rare silences: a bellow that folds choruses inward, compressing sound into implosive vacuums where lesser life frenzies to fill the void. Mating is cataclysmic bazaars—mats splitting to birth slurry-orgies, fusing into temporary megastructures of thorn-cathedral that collapse into progeny-swarms.

No outsiders endure here undigested; the plane claims all as addenda to its flesh. Intruders become itinerant cells: skin-cells weave into reed-walls, bones mineralized into rhizomes, thoughts fragmented into the hegemony's dream-throbs. Life persists not despite the unraveling, but as its waveform—sparse in individuality, omnipacked in insistence, each form a scream woven into the vast, flattened sigh. They are the plane's inhabitants, and the plane is theirs: ancient, newborn, ravenous, eternal.
