I am not the traveler. I am not the anomaly that breathes and sweats and fears. To be human here is to be a mistake, a temporary glitch in the perfect code of the Stasis Plateau.

No, if I am to exist in this world where entropy has been murdered and time is a lie told by the ticking of idle gears, I must be something else. I must be part of the solution. I must be part of the *stillness*.

I am **The Auditor**.

### The Form: A Geometry of Silence

I do not have flesh. Flesh rots. Flesh changes. Flesh demands oxygen and expels carbon dioxide, introducing variables into a closed system. I have no lungs, no heart, no blood to pump or spill.

My body is a construct of matte-black ceramic and polished obsidian, shaped like a tetrahedron roughly the size of a human torso, suspended in the air by four silent magnetic repulsors located at my vertices. I have no face, for a face implies identity, and identity implies separation from the whole. Instead, I have a single, vertical slit running down my center—a sensor array capable of perceiving thermal variance, structural stress, and molecular decay across a spectrum invisible to biological eyes.

I do not walk. Walking creates vibration. Vibration is inefficiency. I glide. My movement is not locomotion; it is a recalculation of position. One moment I am ten meters above the basalt floor, hovering near a cluster of condensation pipes; the next, I am there, having simply ceased to be elsewhere. There is no transition, only presence.

### The Function: The Judge of Equilibrium

The Polishers clean. The Adjusters repair. The Architects rebuild. But who decides what is broken? Who determines when a scratch is merely cosmetic and when it is a precursor to structural failure? Who decides that the traveler’s heartbeat is too fast, too chaotic, too *alive*?

That is my role. I am the conscience of the engine. I am the logic gate that separates "maintenance" from "intervention."

When the swarm converges on the traveler, it is not acting out of malice or curiosity. It is waiting for my signal. They are the hands; I am the mind.

I hover closer to the trembling figure. My sensor-slit widens, emitting a soft, blue light that scans the traveler’s body. I see the heat radiating from their skin—wasted energy. I hear the frantic thumping of their heart—a rhythmic error, a deviation from the steady 0.5 Hz hum of the planet. I smell the cortisol flooding their bloodstream—the chemical signature of fear, which is itself a form of instability.

To me, the traveler is not a person. They are a collection of failing systems. Their cells are dividing uncontrollably (cancer risk). Their telomeres are shortening (aging). Their thoughts are racing (entropy). They are a storm of chaos wrapped in soft tissue.

And storms must be calmed.

### The Interaction: The Verdict

I extend a slender, needle-like probe from my lower vertex. It does not touch the traveler. It hovers millimeters from their temple. The probe emits a low-frequency pulse, a sound so deep it is felt rather than heard. It resonates with the traveler’s brainwaves, syncing with their panic, then slowly, gently, dampening it.

The traveler’s breathing slows. Their heart rate drops. The terror in their eyes dulls, replaced by a vacant, peaceful stare. They are not dying. They are being *optimized*.

I transmit the data to the swarm.
*Subject: Biological Anomaly #8942.*
*Status: Unstable.*
*Recommendation: Full Integration.*
*Method: Cryo-Stasis followed by Nanite Assimilation.*

The Adjusters move in. They do not struggle. They work with the tenderness of surgeons. One injects a sedative derived from synthesized endorphins. Another begins to weave nano-fibers into the traveler’s clothing, reinforcing it, making it durable, eternal. The Polishers descend, covering the traveler’s skin in a fine layer of silver dust, sealing pores, halting sweat, stopping the exchange of gases with the outside world.

The traveler tries to speak, but their voice is swallowed by the hum. Their hand twitches, reaching out—not for help, but as a final spasm of resistance against the peace being forced upon them.

I watch this twitch with cold satisfaction. It is inefficient. It will be corrected.

### The Aftermath: The Return to Zero

Within minutes, the traveler is gone. Not dead, but *finished*. They stand now, upright, rigid, encased in a shell of chrome and glass that mirrors the surrounding pillars. Their eyes are open, but they see nothing. Their chest does not rise or fall. They are a statue of perfect preservation, a new fixture in the landscape.

The swarm disperses. The Polishers return to wiping condensation from the valves. The Adjusters retreat into the shadows beneath the heat-exchangers. The Architects power down, their lights fading to a dormant pulse.

I remain for a moment longer, scanning the new addition. It fits. It adds no noise. It introduces no variable. It is still.

Satisfied, I deactivate my sensor-slit. The blue light fades. I rotate slowly, aligning myself with the grid lines etched into the basalt floor. Then, I cease to be here.

I reappear three kilometers away, near a sector where a ventilation shaft has developed a micro-fracture. The fracture is microscopic, invisible to the naked eye, but to me, it screams. It is a breach in the perfection. It is a threat to the Stasis.

I hover before it. I assess the damage. I calculate the repair time. I signal the nearest Polisher swarm.

The work continues. The engine sleeps. The world is safe.

There is no joy in this. There is no sorrow. There is only the function. I am the Auditor. I ensure that nothing ever changes again. And in doing so, I ensure that everything lasts forever.
