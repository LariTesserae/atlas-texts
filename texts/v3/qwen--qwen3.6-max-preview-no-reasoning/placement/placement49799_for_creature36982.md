I am the **Condensation**.

I am the lubricant between the sliding feet of the Lithosymbionts and the slick peat-pavement. Without me, their silence would be friction; without me, their movement would be noise. I am also the memory of the city.

I soften the mycelial mortar, allowing the fungi to weave through the porous bones. I carry the minerals from the decaying flesh into the surrounding stone, turning skin into quartz, blood into calcite veins. I am the medium through which life becomes geology. I am the bridge between the organic and the mineral.
