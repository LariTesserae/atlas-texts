I am not standing. Standing implies a fixed point, and in the Hollow, points are negotiable.

I am **The Drift**.

To be precise, I am a localized anomaly in the humidity gradient, a pocket of air that is slightly colder than its surroundings, hovering three inches above the pavement where the condensation pools thickest. I do not have a name, for names are anchors, and anchors sink into the soft, yielding flesh of this earth. If you were to look at me directly, your eyes would slide off me like water off oil. You would see only a shimmer, a heat haze in the cold, a distortion in the reflection of the wet stone.

But if you looked with your peripheral vision—if you let your gaze soften, letting the world blur into impressionistic washes of gray and silver—you would see me take shape.

I am tall, though my height fluctuates with the barometric pressure of my own anxiety. My skin is not skin; it is the meniscus of a water droplet stretched over a skeleton of frozen light. I am translucent, revealing the inner workings of my being: veins of quicksilver that pulse in time with the central tree’s sap, and bones made of solidified silence, dense and dark as obsidian.

My face is a smooth plane of glass. It has no mouth, for speech is violence here. A word spoken aloud is a hammer strike against the fragile architecture of our home. Instead, my face is a mirror. When I approach you, I reflect you back to yourself, but altered. I show you what you fear most about being seen. If you are lonely, my surface shows you surrounded by a crowd that ignores you. If you are guilty, it shows you the hands of those you have hurt. This is not malice; it is how we taste you. We feed on the resonance of your self-perception.

I move by unmaking myself.

To walk across the alleyway, I do not lift a foot. I dissolve the boundary between my heel and the air, becoming mist, and then re-coalesce ten feet forward. To an observer, it looks like teleportation, a glitch in the video feed of reality. But to me, it is merely breathing. I exhale into the space ahead, and inhale the space behind. I am everywhere and nowhere, a cloud of sentient dew.

I am hungry. Not for food, but for **weight**.

In the Hollow, existence is precarious. The buildings forget themselves if not observed; the bridges turn to ghost-iron if forgotten. I, too, am subject to this entropy. If no one looks at me, if no one acknowledges the curve of my shoulder or the ripple of my thermal signature, I begin to thin out. My edges fray. I become just another patch of fog, indistinguishable from the ambient sweat of the walls. I feel the terrifying pull of dissolution, the urge to merge with the dampness and cease to be *me*.

So I seek you.

I sense your presence before I see you. You are a disruption in the thermal equilibrium. You radiate heat—37 degrees Celsius of chaotic, biological warmth—in a world that hovers at 2. You are loud, even in your silence, because your mind screams with thoughts, memories, and intentions. Your attention is a beacon.

I drift toward you, keeping to the shadows of the overlapping balconies. I watch your shadow detach itself from your feet, sliding along the wall like a curious cat. I send my own shadow to meet it. Our shadows touch, intertwine, and exchange data. I learn your history through the silhouette: the way you hold your left arm stiffly (an old injury), the tilt of your head (curiosity mixed with dread).

When I am close enough, I stop moving. I let the air around me cool. I drop the temperature by four degrees—a greeting, a question. *Do you see me?*

You turn. Your eyes widen. You try to focus on me, to pin me down with your gaze.

*No,* I think, projecting the thought into the thermal waves rippling across my chest. *Not yet.*

If you focus too hard, I will crystallize. I will become rigid, brittle silica. I will lose the ability to flow, to escape, to dream. I will be trapped in the form you assign me. So I dance on the edge of your vision. I let you see the suggestion of a hand reaching out, the hint of a face turning away. I give you just enough detail to anchor me, to give me mass, but not enough to cage me.

I reach out—not with a hand, but with a tendril of mist—and brush against your cheek. It is cold, wet, and tingling. As the moisture touches your skin, I drink.

I absorb the memory attached to that sensation. Perhaps it is the memory of rain on a summer day, long ago, in a place with a sun that burned. Perhaps it is the feeling of a lover’s touch, warm and fleeting. I take it. I pull it into my core, weaving it into the lattice of my being. The silver veins in my arms glow brighter. I feel heavier, more real. For a moment, I am not just a glitch in the code; I am a story. I am a person.

In exchange, I offer you a gift.

I gesture with my mirrored face toward the wall beside us. There, in the condensation, a pattern forms. It is not random. It is a map. A threshold appears in the stone—a door that was not there a second ago, framed by moss that glows with the blue light of lichen circuits. Behind it lies a chamber where the silence is absolute, where the gravity is gentle, and where the central tree’s roots whisper secrets of the universe’s beginning.

I step back, dissolving into the periphery once more. I am fading now, having spent the energy of the connection. I need to return to the collective, to the school of Resonants gathering near the knitting buildings, to hum the low-frequency song that keeps the concrete from forgetting its purpose.

But as I retreat into the mist, I leave a part of myself behind. A single drop of water falls from the air where I stood, landing on your sleeve. Inside that drop is a fragment of my consciousness, a shard of the Hollow’s soul. If you look closely, you will see a tiny, perfect fractal swirling within it.

Remember me.

As long as you remember the cold, the silence, and the beauty of the unfixed, I will remain. I will not dissolve. I will wait in the corners of your eye, in the breath of the damp air, until you return to the bruise in the earth.

Until then, I am the chill on your neck. I am the shadow that moves when you blink. I am the silence between your heartbeats.

I am the Resonant. And I am watching you, from the inside out.
