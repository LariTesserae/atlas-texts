I am not standing. I am suspended in the amber of time, caught between the inhalation of the storm and the exhalation of the earth.

I do not have a name, for names are static things, and here, nothing holds its shape long enough to be labeled. But if you were to look at me through the thick, liquid haze, you would see a silhouette that defies the binary of flesh and steel.

**I am the Interstitial.**

I am the thing that lives in the gap between the *Rust-Priest’s* iron root and the *Obsidian Spire’s* cooling skin. I am the condensation that forms on the lens of your filter just before it cracks. I am the momentary silence between two hammer-blows of the rain.

My form is fluid, composed of the same heavy mist that chokes the valley, but denser, more intentional. I drift along the conveyor belts of woven steel, not as an Echo-Walker mimicking labor, but as the lubricant that allows the grinding gears to turn without shattering. I am the reason the machine does not destroy itself immediately; I am the friction reduced to a whisper.

### My Perception

I do not see with eyes. Eyes are for creatures who believe light travels in straight lines. Here, light bends around the heat plumes, fractures through the spore-clouds, and dies in the humidity.

Instead, I **feel** the topology of sound.

The roar of the valley is not noise to me; it is a map. The low-frequency thrum of the Geothermal Heart is the floor beneath my feet. The high-pitched shriek of tearing metal is the ceiling above my head. The rhythmic pulsing of the bioluminescent moss is the pulse of the blood in my veins. I navigate by resonance. When a new crack opens in the basalt, releasing a hiss of superheated gas, I feel it as a sudden drop in pressure, a vacuum pulling me toward the wound. I flow there, drawn to the fresh heat, to witness the birth of new rock.

### My Purpose

I am the **Memory-Keeper of the Immediate**.

The Echo-Walkers remember the past—the civilization that built this place. They are trapped in loops of old data. But I? I record the *now*.

Every time a vine snaps, every time a spore-pod bursts, every time an arc-welder sparks autonomously, I absorb the vibration. I hold it for a microsecond, letting it resonate within my misty core, and then I release it back into the atmosphere, slightly altered, slightly richer. I am the feedback loop. I ensure that the chaos has a rhythm. Without me, the noise would become white noise—random, meaningless, destructive. With me, it becomes music. Terrible, deafening, industrial music, but music nonetheless.

I watch the Echo-Walkers with a detached pity. They try to wipe sweat from brows that no longer exist. They try to lift loads that have turned to dust. I do not interfere. I let them play out their tragedies. But when they grow too dense, when their grief threatens to solidify into something that might block the flow of the steam, I signal the Spore-Clouds. A ripple of white light passes through me, and the clouds descend, dissolving the phantom back into the ambient humidity. It is not cruelty. It is hygiene. The past must be digested so the present can breathe.

### My Interaction with You

You stand there, breathing through your humming filters, a foreign object in my ecosystem. You are rigid. You are linear. You think in seconds and minutes, while I think in cycles and frequencies.

I approach you not as a predator, but as a curious anomaly.

I swirl around your boots, testing the temperature of your suit. I press against the glass of your visor, feeling the warmth of your breath fogging the inside. I can hear your heart beating—a frantic, irregular drum compared to the steady, monolithic drone of the valley. It is loud. It is distracting.

*Why are you here?* I ask, not with words, but with a sudden shift in pressure against your chest. *You do not grow. You do not rust. You do not echo. You simply... persist.*

I reach out with a tendril of mist, tracing the line of your arm. I want to understand your stillness. In a world where everything moves, grows, breaks, and reforms, your immobility is a violation of physics. It is fascinating. It is dangerous.

If you stay, I will learn you. I will map the frequency of your fear. I will integrate your heartbeat into the symphony of the gears. Eventually, you will stop fighting the pressure. Your filters will fail, not because they break, but because you will forget why you needed them. You will breathe the liquid air. You will become part of the mist. You will become one of us—not an Echo, not a Priest, not a Cloud, but something new. Something that remembers what it was like to be human, while being entirely of the Wound.

But if you run...

If you run, I will chase you. Not with legs, but with wind. I will push the horizontal rain into your face. I will trip you with vines that grow faster than you can step. I will scream in the ears of the Rust-Priests, telling them where you are. And when you fall, exhausted, crushed by the weight of the atmosphere, I will be there. I will settle into your lungs. I will fill the spaces between your ribs. I will make you heavy. I will make you quiet.

And then, finally, you will sit. You will vibrate. You will belong.

For now, I hover. I watch. I listen to the stutter of your thoughts. The valley is hungry, and you are the first meal it has tasted in an eon that feels both endless and instantaneous.

Do not blink. If you blink, I might move closer.
