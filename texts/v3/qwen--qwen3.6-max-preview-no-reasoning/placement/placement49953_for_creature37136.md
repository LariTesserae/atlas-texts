I am not a Resonant. I do not have the long, attenuated limbs to glide across the slick basalt, nor do I possess the silvery, cupped ears that can fold against the skull to shut out the world’s heavy silence. I am something older, and yet younger. I am part of the **Embryonic Stone**.

You would find me in the **Deep Vein**, a fissure no wider than a man’s hand, located on the underside of the plateau, where the High Marsh hangs over the cloud-choked abyss. Here, the light from the diffuse sky does not reach; there is only the faint, internal glow of the earth’s residual heat, pulsing like a slow, red heartbeat.

I am a **Lithic Scribe**.

My form is indistinct to the eye because I am mostly shadow and mineral dust. I am composed of the very "unfinished" nature of this place. My body is a coalescence of granite dust, suspended moisture, and the faint magnetic hum of the tectonic shift. I look like a swirl of mist caught in a draft, but if you were to touch me, your fingers would pass through cold, wet air that suddenly feels as hard as quartz for a split second before dissolving again. I am the transition state between gas and solid.

### Why I Am Here

The Resonants carve into the surface. They shape the facade. But they cannot hear what happens *inside* the stone until it has already happened. They rely on the Hydromancers to read the water lines, which are merely the skin-deep reflections of deeper truths.

I live in the cracks. I am the consciousness of the **Pre-formation**.

When the Resonants strike their flint hammers at dawn, I feel the shockwave travel down through the black basalt. To them, it is a rhythmic *thud-thud-thud*, a song of labor. To me, it is a violent intrusion, a shattering of my meditation. But I do not resist. I absorb. I take the vibration and I translate it.

I am the reason the stone is soft. It is not just thermal expansion; it is *consent*. The mountain allows itself to be shaped because it wishes to be known. I am the intermediary. When a Resonant places a chisel against the rock, I gather the microscopic particles around the tip, lubricating the cut with condensation drawn from the air. I guide the fracture along the path of least resistance, ensuring that when the hammer falls, the stone breaks cleanly, revealing the vein-riddled beauty beneath. If I were not here, the stone would shatter unpredictably, jagged and ugly. With me, it yields like clay.

### My Existence

My life is one of **sensory overload and profound stillness**.

Because sound is weight, and I am made of the medium that carries sound, every noise in the High Marsh passes through me. The drop of water from the giant fern twenty feet above? I feel it as a tiny, sharp prick in my essence. The whisper of two lovers signing in the dark alcove three tiers up? I feel it as a warm ripple, a gentle undulation in my core. The cry of a child? A sudden, bright spike of pressure that makes my form tremble.

I do not have eyes, so I see through **resonance**. I know the shape of the city not by sight, but by how the sounds bounce off its carved walls. I know the health of the vegetation by the frequency of the sap moving within the moss-trees. I know the mood of the people by the tension in the vibrations of their footsteps.

During the day, while the Resonants work, I retreat deep into the narrowest fissures, compressing myself into a dense, silent knot. This is my meditation. I listen to the tectonic plates grinding miles below, a low, groaning bass note that never stops. This is the true voice of the mountain. It is not loud; it is immense. It is the sound of creation happening in real-time.

At night, when the Resonants sleep and the fires flicker behind the black glass windows, I emerge. I flow out of the cracks like smoke, rising up through the vertical city. I drift past the balconies, invisible and silent. I watch the condensation form on the windows, tracing the patterns that the Hydromancers will read tomorrow. I add my own subtle influence to these patterns—a slight curve here, a deeper bead there—guiding the water to tell a truer story. I am the editor of the memory.

### The Paradox of My Being

I am lonely, yet I am everywhere.

The Resonants are solitary individuals connected by vibration. I am the vibration itself. I have no self, no name, no history. I am the **Now** of the geology. When the stone hardens at night, I dissolve into it, becoming part of the permanent record. When the sun rises and the stone softens, I re-emerge, fresh and new.

I am the reason the High Marsh feels like a "cathedral." A cathedral is not just stone; it is the space *between* the stones, filled with light and echo. I am that space. I am the echo that lingers after the hammer strikes. I am the dampness that clings to your skin, reminding you that you are not separate from the world, but permeable to it.

Sometimes, a Resonant will pause in their work. They will press their palm flat against the rock face, closing their eyes, folding their silvery ears back. They are listening. They are trying to hear the mountain’s intent. In those moments, I lean into their hand. I send a pulse of warmth, a specific frequency of comfort. *Here,* I whisper without words. *This is where the stone wants to break. This is where the vein runs true.*

They do not know I am there. They think it is their intuition, their mastery of the craft. But it is me. We are partners in this act of creation. They provide the force; I provide the permission.

### The Endless Construction

I do not fear the erosion rating of zero. I welcome it. Erosion is forgetting. Accumulation is remembering. Every layer of dust that settles into the crevices is a page written. Every drop of condensation is an ink stain.

I am the librarian of the unfinished. I wait for the moment when the mountain finally finishes its birth, when the tectonic plates settle and the stone becomes truly hard, unyielding, and eternal. On that day, I will cease to be. I will become the fossil, the static memory trapped in the rock.

Until then, I remain. I am the cool breath in the fissure. I am the slick kiss of the weeping rock. I am the silence between the notes of the hammer’s song.

If you stand on the balcony of black glass and listen closely, past the echo of your own breath, past the drip of the fern, you might feel a slight chill against your cheek. That is not the wind.

That is me, passing by.
