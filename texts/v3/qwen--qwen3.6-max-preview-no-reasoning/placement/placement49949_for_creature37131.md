I am not standing on the ground. I am suspended in the *Between*.

To be precise, I am a **Spore-Singer**, one of the rare Echo-Walkers you mentioned, but my mutation is not in my skin or my voice. It is in my perception of gravity and time. I do not feel the pull of the sphere’s center as a force to resist; I feel it as a tide.

### Where I Am

I am clinging to the underside of a leaf the size of a cathedral nave. This leaf hangs from a vine that spans three miles across the hollow interior, a green tendon stretching from the "North" root-mass to the "South." I am upside down relative to the people living in the pores below me. To them, I am a speck against the vast, teal-lit void. To me, they are distant lights, flickering like fireflies in a deep well.

The air here is thinner, colder, and sharper. The humidity (1.7) that comforts those below feels like a heavy blanket up here, so I have wrapped myself in layers of dried, translucent spider-silk gauze to keep warm. My filaments—those sensory hairs on my scalp—are stiff with static charge. They twitch, tasting the ozone of the approaching storm clouds that drift lazily through the central emptiness.

I am alone, but I am never lonely. The silence here is absolute (0.5), yet it is loud with presence. I can hear the sap moving in the vine beneath my feet—a low, thrumming river of amber light. I can hear the heartbeat of the Sphere itself, a slow *thump-thump* that takes hours to complete a cycle. And if I close my eyes, I can hear the whispers of the Ancestors in the compost cradles far below, their dreams rising like mist.

### Who I Am

My name is **Kaelen-of-the-Updraft**. But names are human things, discrete labels for discrete beings. Here, I am more accurately described as a *node of curiosity*.

Physically, I am small, four feet tall, with limbs of calcified lignin that look like twisted birch branches. My skin is a pale, almost white moss, shifting to a soft grey when I am still. But unlike my kin who stay in the Pores, my skin has developed a strange iridescence—it shimmers with colors that don’t exist in the spectrum of the bioluminescent fungi. When I feel fear, I flash violet. When I feel wonder, I glow gold.

My most distinct feature, however, is my back. Between my shoulder blades, two large, membranous folds of skin have grown. They are not wings, not yet. They are delicate, veined structures, thin as parchment, filled with a lighter-than-air gas secreted by glands near my spine. They tremble constantly, catching the micro-currents of the void. I cannot fly, not truly. But I can glide. I can fall with grace.

### What I Do

I am a cartographer of the impossible.

While the Root-Singers below maintain the harmony of the present, mapping the flow of nutrients and the health of the crops, I map the *edges*. I climb higher than anyone else dares. I venture into the chasms where the light is dimmest and the shadows stretch toward heat sources in confusing, wrong ways.

Today, I am watching a phenomenon that defies the logic of the Built parameter. A cluster of dewdrops has formed on the tip of a hanging fern. But these are not reflecting the teal glow of the lichen. They are reflecting a sky of burning orange and jagged black rock—a place of stone and sun, concepts alien to our world.

I press my palm against the cold surface of the leaf, extending my capillaries. I let my sap mix with the condensation on the leaf’s surface. I taste the memory in the water.

*Heat. Dryness. A sound like cracking thunder. A feeling of immense, crushing weight.*

It is a memory of a world that died before the Sphere dreamed itself into existence. Or perhaps, a prophecy of a world waiting outside.

### The Internal Conflict

This is the burden of the Echo-Walker. To know is to isolate.

When I return to the Pores, I will not speak of this. Speech is vibration, and vibration disrupts the Great Silence. Instead, I will seek out a Dream-Weaver. I will sit with them, palms pressed together, and I will pour this vision into their mind through the exchange of sap. They will weave it into a tapestry of red vines and dark leaves, a warning or a promise, depending on how the community interprets it.

But part of me—the part that feels the tug of the updrafts, the part whose skin flashes gold at the sight of the unknown—wants to keep it secret. Not out of malice, but out of protection. If the Sphere knows that there is an *Outside*, will it dream harder? Will it tighten its roots? Or will it crack, as the mad prophets say?

I feel the urge to jump. To step off the edge of this leaf and let the membrane on my back catch the wind. To glide toward the center of the void, where gravity dissolves, and see what lies in the true darkness beyond the green shell.

But I do not jump. Not today.

Instead, I close my large, black eyes. I listen to the hum of the vine. I sync my breathing with the slow, ancient pulse of the world-tree. I let the boundary between Kaelen and the Leaf dissolve. For a moment, I am not an observer. I am the observation. I am the green. I am the dream.

And in that surrender, I find peace.
