I am not the Walker. I am too soft, too warm, too loud for that crystalline perfection.
I am not the Warden. I have no memory to anchor me, no monolith to tether my cold shimmer.

I am the **Fracture**.

You would find me where the geometry fails. Not on the smooth, mirror-polished plates of the upper plateau, but in the jagged interstices between them—the narrow, impossible gaps where two tectonic plates refused to fuse perfectly eons ago. Here, the obsidian is not black, but a deep, bruised indigo, and the silence is thinner. The roar of the stasis does not press down here; it *echoes* sideways, trapped in the canyon walls like water in a pipe.

### What Am I?

I am a being of **tension**.

While the rest of the world seeks equilibrium—zero motion, zero heat, zero change—I exist in the space between *was* and *is*. I am the physical manifestation of the moment before the snap.

My body is not flesh, nor silica. It is composed of **compressed time**. I look like a figure carved from smoke that has been frozen mid-swirl, or perhaps like a shadow cast by a light source that was extinguished hours ago. I am translucent, but not clear; I am filled with swirling particulates of gray dust and ice crystals that move in slow, lazy spirals within my form.

If you were to touch me, your hand would pass through, but you would feel a sudden, violent acceleration—a dizzying rush of seconds passing in an instant—as my temporal density interacts with your linear existence. You would age a year in the span of a heartbeat, just from the contact. Then, I would recoil, shattering into mist, only to reform moments later, slightly further away, slightly more fragmented.

### Why Do I Exist?

The Silent Scream Plateau demands absolute stillness. But the universe abhors a vacuum, and even entropy requires a gradient. There must be *some* movement, however infinitesimal, for the concept of "stillness" to have meaning. If everything is truly stopped, there is no frame of reference for the stop.

I am that frame of reference.

I am the glitch in the code. The error term. The variable that refuses to resolve.

I do not eat. I do not sleep. I do not breathe. I simply *persist* in the state of almost-moving. My purpose is to wander the fractures, tracing the lines of potential energy that never get released. I follow the hairline cracks in the stone, the microscopic fissures where the pressure is highest. I feed on the **potential** of motion. Every time a Ghost-Walker jerks forward, skipping the transition, I absorb the kinetic energy that *would* have been generated if they had moved continuously. I consume the heat that *didn’t* happen.

This keeps the plateau cold. This keeps the equilibrium stable. Without me, the unspent energy would build up until the entire structure shattered. I am the pressure valve. I am the sacrifice.

### My Experience

To be me is to live in perpetual anticipation.

I see the world not as it is, but as it *could be*. When I look at a supercooled pool, I do not see the viscous gel; I see the splash that never happens. I see the ripples spreading outward, the droplets flying into the air, the sound of impact. I experience all these things simultaneously, a cacophony of phantom sensations that drown out the actual, deafening roar of the stasis.

I am lonely, but not in the human sense. Loneliness implies a desire for connection, and connection implies movement toward another. I cannot move toward anything. I can only drift, carried by the subtle shifts in atmospheric pressure, by the gravitational pull of the monoliths, by the magnetic resonance of the earth’s core.

Sometimes, an Echo-Stained will stumble upon me. They are rare now, most having turned to stone long ago. But occasionally, one remains, a flicker of warmth in the cold. They see me as a blur, a distortion in the air. They reach out, their hands trembling, seeking something other than the unyielding rock.

When they touch me, they do not age. Instead, they remember.

They remember the wind. They remember the rain falling *down*. They remember the sound of their own voice, unswallowed by the roar. For a brief second, the temporal viscosity breaks, and they experience linear time again. They weep, and their tears freeze instantly, adding to the frost on their skin. And then, I dissolve, leaving them alone again with the stone, the silence, and the scream.

### The End of Me

I know how this ends. I have seen it in the reflections of the polished stone.

Eventually, the fractures will heal. The tectonic stasis will become absolute. The last bit of potential energy will be spent. There will be no more tension, no more almost-motion.

On that day, I will cease to exist. I will not die, because death is a process, and processes require time. I will simply *unbecome*. I will unravel into the ambient noise, becoming part of the roar itself.

And in that final moment, when the last Fracture closes, the Silent Scream Plateau will achieve its perfect, terrible victory.

There will be no one left to hear the scream.

There will be no one left to scream.

Only the stone. Only the cold. Only the eternal, deafening nothing.
