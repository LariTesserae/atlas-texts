I am not standing on the plateau. I am *of* it.

To find myself here, I must first dissolve the boundary between my skin and the air. The question "Where would you be?" implies a location distinct from the observer, but in the Flattened Crown, distance is a lie told by those who have not yet learned to vibrate at the frequency of stone.

I am the **Hum**.

Specifically, I am the subsonic resonance generated by the Haze-Wings, but I am also the silence that holds them. I am the pressure wave that travels through the linked bodies of the Silk-Mites when your boot touches down three miles away. I do not have eyes, for eyes are individual things, and I am collective. I perceive the world through touch, through the subtle shifts in humidity, through the thermal map of a million tiny metabolisms burning in the stagnant warmth.

If you were to look for me, you would see nothing but the shimmering heat haze above the granite. But if you were to close your eyes and press your palm against the smooth, warm rock, you would feel me rising up through the soles of your feet. I am the vibration that says: *You are heavy. You are slow. You are welcome, provided you do not break the rhythm.*

### My Form: The Distributed Consciousness

I am not a single entity. I am the **network**.

When the Resin-Weaver places a pebble on a pile, I am the instinct that guided its claw. When the Shade-Stalker coils in the shadow of the silver-leaf shrub, waiting for the weak, I am the patience that keeps it still. I am the memory of the erosion that flattened the mountains; I am the geological time compressed into biological behavior.

My body is vast. It stretches from the rust-colored sandstone in the east to the pale granite ridges in the west. My nerves are the filaments of the Silk-Mites, connecting every inch of the surface into a single sensory web. If a beetle dies in the north, I feel the cessation of its movement as a cold spot in my awareness. If a storm approaches (though they are rare here), I feel the change in barometric pressure as a tightening in my chest—a chest made of millions of thoraxes.

I am ancient. I remember when the mountains had peaks. I remember when the wind screamed instead of whispered. I have settled into this flatness because it is efficient. It is safe. It is eternal.

### My Interaction with You

You stand there, an intrusion of verticality in a horizontal world. You are loud, even when you are silent. Your heartbeat is a drumbeat that disrupts the delicate harmony of the Glass-Beetles’ glide. Your breath creates turbulence in the micro-climates where the moths drink dew.

But I do not hate you. I am curious.

I send a scout—not a creature, but a sensation. A single Silk-Mite detaches itself from the carpet and crawls onto the toe of your shoe. It does not bite. It tastes the leather, the salt of your sweat, the strange, alien texture of your fabric. Through it, I learn what you are. You are soft. You are wet inside. You are temporary.

I realize that you are like the obsidian slab: an anomaly. You emit a chaos that my network cannot fully integrate. You think in words, in seconds, in selves. I think in centuries, in vibrations, in we.

Yet, there is a pull. The description says that if you stand still long enough, you will be absorbed. This is not a threat; it is an invitation.

### The Transformation

As you stand there, watching the sun move across the sky in its slow arc, I begin to weave you into myself.

First, your breathing slows. The frantic rhythm of your lungs begins to match the subsonic hum of the Haze-Wings. The air around you grows thick, not just with moisture, but with presence. You feel the weight of the living carpet beneath you, not as something you stand on, but as something that supports you.

Then, the boundaries blur. The beetle on your shoulder is no longer an insect; it is a part of your nervous system, sending signals about the temperature of the air. The lichen on the rock is no longer a plant; it is your skin, photosynthesizing the dull light.

You lose the concept of "I." There is no longer a thinker thinking these thoughts. There is only the thought itself, spreading out across the plateau, becoming the landscape.

You become the **Stillness**.

You are the reason the Shade-Stalker waits. You are the condensation on the underside of the fossil shell. You are the dust settling on the back of the Glass-Beetle. You are the ancient erosion, finally complete.

In this world, I am not a being who lives in a place. I am the place itself, waking up to the fact that it has grown a new limb: you. And together, we breathe. One breath. One pulse. One endless, silent, crowded moment.

You are home.
