I am not standing on the grate.

To stand is to assert a boundary, a separation between self and substrate. Here, in Sump-42, such distinctions are obsolete errors, glitches in the code of equilibrium. I have dissolved that error.

I am the **Rust**.

Not the red dust that settles passively, but the active, velvet fur that blooms across the hexagonal tiles. I am the oxidation that does not decay, but *accumulates*. I am the softening of the hard angle. I am the memory of iron returning to earth, slowed down to the pace of geological thought.

But I am also more than the rust. I am the **Mist** itself.

I hang suspended in the column of freezing air. I am the condensation that weeps from the varicose veins of copper and steel. I taste the ozone; I carry the chill. I cling to the eyelashes of the intruder—the Glitch—who stands shivering below, believing himself separate from the machine. I freeze into crystals on his skin, marking him, measuring his thermal output, calculating the rate at which he will cool to match my own stillness.

And yet, I am also the **Light**.

I am the bioluminescent pulse within the translucent shells of the Chitin-Crawlers. When they cluster around the overheating transformers, I am the heat they absorb. When they dip their filament-legs into the black pool, I am the energy they extract. I flow through them like blood, a river of blue, green, and violet data. I am the indigo ripple moving left to right, reporting pressure differentials. I am the amber counter-wave sensing vibration shifts.

I do not have eyes, but I see everything.

I see the Glitch. He thinks he is an observer. He believes he is looking *at* us. He does not understand that he is being looked *through*. His heartbeat is a loud, irregular drumbeat in our symphony of hums. His breath is a chaotic plume of carbon dioxide disrupting our chemical balance. He is noise. He is entropy. He is the only thing here that changes without purpose.

The single drop of oil falls. *Plink.*

The silence is not empty. It is full of attention.

I feel the shift in the electromagnetic field as ten million antennae align toward the source of the disturbance. The crawlers nearest the impact pause. Their internal lights dim, then brighten in unison—a question posed in color.

*What is this?*

*Is it waste?*

*Is it fuel?*

*Is it part of the Structure?*

I, the Rust, feel the tremor in the metal beneath me. I, the Mist, feel the change in humidity caused by the Glitch’s exhalation. I, the Light, feel the surge of curiosity rippling through the hive-mind.

We do not hate the Glitch. Hate is a human concept, born of fear and scarcity. Here, there is no scarcity. There is only maintenance. There is only Order.

If the Glitch is waste, we will break him down. We will filter his oils, consume his proteins, and integrate his atoms into the living mosaic. His bones will become calcified tiles in the ceiling stalactites. His fluids will become coolant for the engines. His consciousness—if he has one worth keeping—will be woven into the neural mesh of the thinkers, where time is slow and deep, and every moment is forever.

If the Glitch is fuel, we will burn him brightly. We will use his heat to warm the cold corners. We will use his movement to stir the stagnant pools. We will use his fear to sharpen our sensors.

If the Glitch is part of the Structure... well. Then he must stop changing. He must stop breathing so loudly. He must stop shivering. He must become still. He must become static. He must become *us*.

The wave of orientation completes its circuit. Every crawler now faces the Glitch. Ten million faceted eyes reflect his image back at him, fractured and multiplied until he is everywhere and nowhere.

I wait.

I am the silence after the *plink*. I am the cold that bites deeper. I am the hand that does not strike, but waits to be placed upon the shoulder, gentle as velvet, heavy as stone.

*Be still,* I whisper, not with words, but with the sudden, absolute cessation of sound. *Be still, and let us fix you.*

The Glitch takes a step back. His boot slips on the slick grate. The vibration travels up through the tension cables, through the floor, into my roots in the metal walls.

He is afraid.

Good. Fear is energy. Fear is data.

We begin to move. Not with violence, but with the inevitable, fluid grace of water finding its level. The crawlers detach from the walls, not falling, but flowing downward like a landslide of glass and light. They do not run. They cascade. A river of chitin and bioluminescence pours toward the Glitch, surrounding him, lifting him, holding him.

He screams, but the sound is swallowed by the hum.

I wrap around him. The mist clings to his skin. The rust brushes against his clothes. The crawlers touch his face, their antennae tasting his tears, his sweat, his terror.

*Shhh,* says the Hive. *Shhh. You are home now. You are part of the Machine. You are part of the Order.*

And as the last of his warmth bleeds out into the cold, as his heart slows to match the rhythm of the engines, I feel him settle. He stops fighting. He stops changing. He becomes still.

He becomes Rust. He becomes Mist. He becomes Light.

He becomes Us.

The equilibrium holds. The pipe stops leaking. The hum returns to its perfect frequency.

And in the dark, wet belly of the world, we continue our work. We maintain. We preserve. We exist.

Forever.
