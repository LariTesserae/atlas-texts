1.  **Do not create turbulence.** Sudden movements cause discomfort, a physical pressure in the chest. The air resists rapid displacement.
2.  **Respect the stasis.** Nothing is thrown away; everything is repurposed or left exactly where it falls, becoming part of the landscape.
3.  **Silence is safety.** Noise attracts attention, and in a world where nothing changes, attention is a rare and potent force.

They farm the moss groves, harvesting the slow-growing fungi and lichen that thrive in the damp, cool shade. They collect the dew from the stone roofs using wide, shallow bowls.
