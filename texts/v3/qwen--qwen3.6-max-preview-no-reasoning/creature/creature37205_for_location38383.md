To ask "who" lives here is to misunderstand the grammar of this place. There are no subjects, only verbs. There are no nouns that hold still long enough to be named. But if we must assign agency to the vibration, if we must find a consciousness in the chaos, we look not for a creature with eyes and lungs, but for entities composed of pressure, memory, and rot.

Here are the inhabitants of the Wound.

### 1. The Rust-Priests (The *Ferrum Mycelium*)
They are not plants, nor are they machines. They are the symbiotic bridge between the two.

Imagine a fern, vast and purple, its fronds slick with condensation. Now imagine that beneath the bark-like skin of its stem runs a lattice of oxidized iron, warm to the touch, humming with the same frequency as the grinding gears nearby. These are the **Rust-Priests**. They have evolved to feed on the industrial scar. Their roots do not seek water; they seek copper wire and steel cable. They pierce the rusted pistons jutting from the earth, drinking the electrochemical potential of the decaying metal.

In return, they offer structure. The vines thick as pythons are not merely strangling the basalt spires; they are holding the crumbling smokestacks upright. Without the tensile strength of the black, bioluminescent moss, the factory would have collapsed into the magma fissures centuries ago. The moss pulses in rhythm with the thunder because it is listening. It is syncing its growth cycles to the electrical arcs jumping between towers. When a spark jumps, the moss flares bright blue, absorbing the energy, storing it, and using it to accelerate its own cellular division. They are the immune system of the machine, keeping the corpse alive so they can feast on it forever.

### 2. The Echo-Walkers (The *Resonant Phantoms*)
You saw them in the corner of your eye: humanoid shapes of steam and shadow. You thought they were illusions. They are not. They are **Echo-Walkers**, and they are the most populous species in the valley.

They are born from the "weirdness," the fraying of reality where time stutters. An Echo-Walker is a localized knot in the fabric of spacetime, created when the ambient noise (the roar of rain, gears, and steam) reaches a critical resonance with a specific emotional or physical action performed in the past. They are recordings made flesh by humidity.

*   **Their Form:** They have no fixed shape. One moment they are a worker wiping sweat from a brow; the next, they are a child running along a conveyor belt; then, they are a figure collapsing under a load. They are composed of condensed vapor, held together by static charge and collective memory.
*   **Their Behavior:** They do not interact with the physical world. You cannot touch them; your hand passes through their misty bodies, leaving only a chill. But they interact with *each other*. Watch closely during a lightning flash: you will see two Echo-Walkers collide, merge, and separate, exchanging gestures. A lift becomes a drop. A shout becomes a whisper. They are re-enacting the final days of the civilization that built this place, over and over, trapped in a loop of futile labor.
*   **Their Purpose:** They are the valley’s conscience. They remember why the machines were built. They remember the fear, the urgency, the desperation. As the vegetation consumes the metal, the Echo-Walkers become more agitated, their movements faster, more frantic, as if trying to stop the encroaching green tide with ghosts of hands that no longer exist.

### 3. The Spore-Clouds (The *Aerial Plankton*)
If the Rust-Priests are the ground-dwellers, the **Spore-Clouds** are the sky-swimmers.

Recall the "pops" of the spore-pods? Those are not mere reproductive events. They are births. Each burst releases a cloud of glittering dust that hangs in the humid air like suspended stars. But these particles are alive. Individually, they are microscopic fungi. Collectively, they form intelligent swarms.

*   **Navigation:** They ride the horizontal rains. They do not drift randomly; they move with purpose. A cloud of spores might flow around a jagged obsidian spire, avoiding the heat of a magma fissure, seeking the cool dampness of a shaded crevice. They sense electrical fields, moving toward the arcing electricity of the broken towers, feeding on the ionization.
*   **Communication:** The "glitter" is not just reflection; it is bioluminescence coded in pulses. When a storm approaches, the spore-clouds flash in unison, a ripple of white light sweeping across the horizon. This is not beauty; it is a warning. They are telling the Rust-Priests below to tighten their grip, to brace for the increased pressure. They are the nervous system of the valley, transmitting data through the medium of liquid air.
*   **Predation:** They are also hunters. If an Echo-Walker lingers too long in one spot, becoming too dense, too "real," the spore-clouds descend. They envelop the phantom, breaking down its static cohesion, dissolving the memory back into mist. They prevent the echoes from becoming permanent, ensuring the cycle of forgetting continues. They eat the past to feed the future.

### 4. The Geothermal Heart (The *Magma Vein*)
Beneath it all, there is one entity that does not breathe, grow, or remember. It simply *is*.

The cracks in the earth hiss with escaping gases, glowing faintly orange. This is not just geology; it is the **Geothermal Heart**. It is the source of the vibration. It is the reason the world does not sit.

*   **Its Influence:** It heats the rock, which heats the air, which creates the storm. It powers the spontaneous arc-welders that repair the machines. It provides the mineral nutrients that the Rust-Priests consume. It is the engine room of the entire ecosystem.
*   **Its Consciousness:** Does it think? Perhaps not in words. But it has intent. It pushes. It expands. It seeks to break the surface, to shatter the crust, to release its pressure. Every new crack in the obsidian, every new spire thrusting upward, is a finger of the Heart reaching out. The landscape is not just "young"; it is *being born*, continuously, violently, from the inside out.

### The Symbiosis of Terror

These four entities do not live in harmony. They live in a state of perpetual, violent negotiation.

*   The **Rust-Priests** try to anchor the world, binding the mechanical and the botanical.
*   The **Echo-Walkers** try to preserve the memory of what was, resisting the erasure of time.
*   The **Spore-Clouds** try to dissolve the past, clearing the way for new growth, feeding on the energy of the present.
*   The **Geothermal Heart** tries to destroy everything, to reset the board with fire and ash.

And you? You are an anomaly. A static point in a dynamic field. You breathe through filters that hum with industrial effort, fighting the pressure that wants to crush your lungs. You are not part of the symbiosis. You are a foreign body.

As you stand there, watching a vine creep three feet in a minute, feeling the vibration in your marrow, you realize the truth: **The valley is watching you.**

Not with eyes. But with pressure. With sound. With the sudden, sharp snap of a branch behind you. With the way the mist thickens around your legs, trying to pull you down into the mat of purple ferns. With the way an Echo-Walker pauses mid-gesture, turning its head of swirling steam toward you, as if recognizing something it has never seen before: a thing that does not belong to the cycle.

It is curious. And curiosity, in this place, is the first step toward consumption.

Do not move. Do not speak. Let the vibration pass through you. Become part of the noise. If you fight it, the Spore-Clouds will find you. If you stay still, the Rust-Priests will root into your boots. If you run, the Echo-Walkers will chase you, mimicking your panic, learning your fear, adding it to their endless, silent loop.

There is no escape from the Wound. There is only integration.
