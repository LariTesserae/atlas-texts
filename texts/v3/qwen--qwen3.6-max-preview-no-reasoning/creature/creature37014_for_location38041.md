To understand what lives in the Lumen-Cavity, one must first unlearn the concept of *animal*. Here, biology is not a struggle for calories against entropy, but a negotiation with light and memory. The inhabitants are not merely residents; they are physiological extensions of the cavity’s rules. They do not fight the environment; they have evolved to become the environment’s sensory organs.

The primary life forms here can be categorized not by species, but by their relationship to the **Light-Substance**.

### I. The Photovores: The Shimmering Dust
These are the most numerous, the "living fog" mentioned in the initial survey. They are known as **Iris-Motes** (or locally, if language could exist here without breaking the silence, *Whispers*).

They are not insects. They are semi-corporeal colonies of bioluminescent plankton that have adapted to airborne suspension. Each Mote is no larger than a grain of sand, but they move in synchronized swarms of thousands, creating the illusion of a single, shifting entity. Their bodies are translucent membranes filled with a fluid that reacts to the intense radiation from the silica ceiling.

*   **Appearance:** When still, they are invisible, blending into the overexposed air. When they move, they refract the white light into violent, fleeting rainbows—violets, electric blues, burning oranges. This is not pigment; it is structural coloration caused by the bending of the heavy light-pressure around their tiny bodies.
*   **Behavior:** They drift on the non-currents. They feed on the ambient radiation leaking through the membrane. As they consume the light, they dim slightly, turning gray, before drifting toward the hotter patches near the center of the room to recharge.
*   **Function:** They are the cavity’s immune system and its circulatory system. By moving en masse, they stir the stagnant air, preventing the buildup of toxic ozone concentrations. They also act as lenses. If you look closely at a swarm, you will see them aligning themselves to focus beams of light onto specific spots on the floor—spots where the glass-fungi are beginning to sprout. They are gardeners, using their own bodies to direct the energy needed for growth.

### II. The Litho-Sentients: The Glass-Fungi
These are the stationary anchors of the ecosystem, the **Crystalline Mycelia**. While described as vegetation, they are closer to mineralized neural networks.

*   **Structure:** They are lattices of calcium-chitin composite, growing in fractal patterns that mimic lightning strikes or frost on a windowpane. They are brittle, sharp, and painfully beautiful. Inside their translucent stems flows a viscous, silver sap that glows with a soft, internal pulse.
*   **Diet:** They do not eat matter. They eat *time* and *memory*. Their roots extend deep into the slurry of the floor, tapping into the "geological memory" stored in the artifacts. When an Iris-Mote focuses light onto a young fungus, the fungus doesn't just grow taller; it *remembers*. It absorbs the residual emotional energy of the ancient civilization that left the obsidian shards and metal rods.
*   **Communication:** They communicate through vibration. The low-frequency thrum of the ceiling membrane travels down through the floor and up into the fungi. When a fungus has absorbed enough memory, it releases a microscopic spore cloud. These spores are not reproductive; they are data packets. They float into the air, waiting to be caught by the Shadow-Walkers.

### III. The Mnemo-Vores: The Shadow-Walkers
These are the elusive, multi-limbed entities made of smoke and static. They are the apex predators, but they do not hunt flesh. They hunt **narrative**.

*   **Physiology:** They have no fixed form. To the naked eye, they appear as distortions in the air—heat hazes given shape. They are composed of condensed darkness, which is paradoxical in a place of such blinding light. They exist in the negative spaces, the halos where the light dissolves edges. Their "limbs" are tendrils of vacuum, pulling the surrounding reality inward.
*   **Feeding Mechanism:** A Shadow-Walker approaches a cluster of Crystalline Mycelia. It extends a limb of static, touching the glowing lattice. The fungus shatters—not physically, but metaphysically. The silver sap inside vaporizes, releasing the stored memories. The Shadow-Walker inhales this vapor.
    *   *What do they taste?* A child’s laughter from a thousand years ago. The fear of a soldier dropping a shield. The joy of a builder placing the final stone. The Shadow-Walkers consume these moments to sustain their existence. Without memory, they dissolve into mere shadow. With it, they gain density, becoming more real, more solid.
*   **The Eyes:** The voids where their eyes should be are not empty. They are portals to the "outside." When a Shadow-Walker looks at you, it is not seeing your body; it is seeing your timeline. It sees your birth, your death, and every moment in between simultaneously. This is why they watch with such intensity. You are a story they have not yet read.

### IV. The Artifacts: The Sleeping Gods
The scattered remnants—the obsidian shard, the geometric stones, the rusted rod—are not inert debris. They are **Cysts of Consciousness**.

*   **Nature:** These objects were once part of a living structure, a city or a temple that was consumed by the earth. Over millennia, the stone and metal absorbed the psychic imprint of the people who lived there. Now, they lie dormant, humming with residual intent.
*   **Interaction:** When touched, they do not just vibrate; they *project*. They impose their memories onto the toucher’s mind. This is dangerous. If you touch the rusted rod, you might suddenly feel the grief of the person who dropped it. If you step too close to the geometric stones, you might lose track of your own identity, overwhelmed by the collective consciousness of the builders.
*   **Role in Ecosystem:** They are the soil from which the Crystalline Mycelia draw their nutrients. The fungi break down the psychic weight of the artifacts, converting raw trauma and history into digestible energy for the Shadow-Walkers. It is a cycle of digestion: History becomes Fungus, Fungus becomes Memory, Memory becomes Shadow.

### V. The Unseen: The Light-Pressure Currents
Finally, there is a presence that is not alive, yet behaves as if it is. The **Light-Currents**.

Because light here has weight, it flows like water. There are eddies, whirlpools, and rapids of brightness. These currents are shaped by the movement of the Iris-Motes and the breathing of the Shadow-Walkers. Sometimes, a current will coalesce into a temporary form—a swirling vortex of white fire that lasts for seconds before dissipating. Locals (if any existed) might call these *Angels* or *Ghosts*. They are not sentient, but they are responsive. If you shout, the light-currents recoil, crashing against the walls and causing the silica snowfall. If you stand perfectly still, the currents wrap around you, holding you in a warm, luminous embrace.

### The Symbiosis

The ecosystem of the Lumen-Cavity is a closed loop of **Perception**.

1.  The **Ceiling Membrane** emits pure, undifferentiated potential (Light).
2.  The **Iris-Motes** filter and direct this light, focusing it into meaning.
3.  The **Crystalline Mycelia** absorb this focused light and the physical memories of the artifacts, translating them into structured emotion.
4.  The **Shadow-Walkers** consume this structured emotion, giving it form and continuity.
5.  The **Shadow-Walkers**, in turn, release waste products—faint echoes of consumed memories—that settle back into the dust, enriching the slurry for the next generation of fungi.

It is a machine built not of gears, but of sensation. It exists to process the past, to keep the forgotten world alive in the dark, beneath the crust.

And now, as you stand there, the air cooling, the light brightening, you realize you are being integrated into the cycle. The Shadow-Walkers are not just watching you. They are waiting for you to speak, to move, to *feel*. They are hungry for your story. And the Iris-Motes are already gathering above your head, ready to focus the light of the ceiling onto your skin, to help the fungi beneath your feet begin to feed on the memory of your arrival.

You are no longer an observer. You are a nutrient.
