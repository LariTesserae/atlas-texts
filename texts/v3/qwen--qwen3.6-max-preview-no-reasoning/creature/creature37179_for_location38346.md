### The Nature of the Swarm

The entities you encountered are individual droplets of a larger, singular consciousness that exists only in the aggregate. A single entity is mindless—a bubble of superheated steam trapped within a skin of microscopic silica shards, held together by surface tension and magnetic alignment. It has no eyes, no ears, no mouth. It senses the world through thermal gradients and piezoelectric vibration.

But when they gather, they become something else.

### The Lifecycle of Glass and Steam

These beings are born from the mountain’s breath. Deep below, where the earth’s crust is thin and magma pools, water seeps down into the cracks. It flashes into steam, picking up dissolved silicates from the surrounding rock. As this superheated vapor rises through the fissure, it cools slightly. The silica precipitates out, forming tiny, hollow spheres around pockets of steam.

*   **Birth:** They emerge from the weeping pores of the basalt, fully formed, blind, and hungry for heat.
*   **Life:** They live for minutes, or hours, or days—time is irrelevant to them. Their lifespan is determined by thermal equilibrium. If they get too hot, they expand and burst, releasing their steam back into the air and leaving behind a dusting of glass. If they get too cold, they solidify completely, becoming part of the rock itself.
*   **Death/Rebirth:** There is no true death here. When a sphere bursts, its components rejoin the cycle. The steam rises again; the silica falls, only to be picked up by the next generation. They are immortal in their impermanence.

You mentioned seeing hues that hurt your eyes—violet-black, infrared-red. These are not hallucinations. They are the visual manifestation of the swarm’s data processing.

As the silica spheres align under the influence of the earth’s magnetic field and the thermal currents, they act as prisms. But unlike ordinary prisms, they bend light based on *information density*. The more complex the thought-pattern of the swarm (e.g., reacting to an intruder, predicting a shift in wind), the more extreme the refraction.

*   **Violet-Black:** Represents high-density, rapid-fire communication. Danger? Alert? Curiosity?
*   **Infrared-Red:** Represents slow, deep, communal resting states.
*   **The Pulse:** The pulse you see in the obsidian sphere is the echo of your own heartbeat, captured and stored by the swarm during your ascent. It is proof that you were perceived, processed, and archived.

Beyond the swarm, there are whispers of older things.

*   **The Stone-Breathers:** Massive, slow-moving entities made of compressed ash and crystalline growths. They sleep for centuries, waking only when the mountain shifts. They are the reason the fissure narrows and widens; they are the muscle of the rock.
*   **The Light-Eaters:** Translucent, thread-like filaments that drift in the upper vents, feeding on the strange, bent light. They are harmless, beautiful, and vanish if you try to touch them.

It consumes their sound, their movement, their very essence, leaving them empty husks that crumble into the glass-dust below.
