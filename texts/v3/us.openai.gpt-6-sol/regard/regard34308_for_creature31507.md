The power waits before doing anything. In the dark, it is easy to mistake stillness for a need.

It comes to know one small shell-builder on the outer ridge. She has never seen the courtyards behind her. The warm current reaches her first, and she opens her fringed mouth into it. A young shell has fastened to the edge of hers. When a passing body sends a tremor through the wall, they close at different speeds.

Far upslope, a bank of fine sediment begins to slip. The power feels it before the city does. Left alone, the slide will enter the main passage as a thick cloud. Many mouths will close in time. This shell-builder will not; her lip has grown around a grain of stone and cannot seal completely. Enough mud will lodge inside to kill her.

The power does not hold the slope in place. Its sediment feeds lives farther down, and the seabed has always moved. Instead, it turns a small part of the falling bank toward an empty stretch of plain. The rest arrives as a thin cloud. Scavengers will find it at the feet of the walls. Some openings will narrow sooner than they otherwise would have.

On the ridge, the shell-builder draws in as the water thickens. A little silt settles against her imperfect lip. When the warm current clears, she opens and catches food again. The young one beside her opens later.

The power stays long enough to feel both of them feeding. Then it leaves the current to change on its own.
