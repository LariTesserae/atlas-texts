The power does not smooth the ridge.

It sees the loose black fragments, the crowded holdfasts, the fish that has learned where to vanish. It sees, too, that the warm water is not a wound to close. The seams are making this place, and the young stone will go on breaking whether or not anything cares for it.

So for a long while, it watches.

Then a slab shifts on one of the steep faces. It would have fallen sooner or later. The power does not hold it up. It turns the falling stone only a little, so that it comes to rest against a bare point instead of scraping down the shelf below.

The sound passes through the water and is gone. Brown fronds still stand above the slab. Beneath it is a narrow dark space with two ways out. The small fish finds one opening that evening and leaves by the other.

In time, a green blade takes hold along the slab’s upper edge. Red growth spreads across its shadowed side. The warm water rises as it always has, troubling their outlines until each seems to move more than it does.

The power lets the ridge keep its sharp edges.
