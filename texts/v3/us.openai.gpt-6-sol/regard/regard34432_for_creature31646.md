The power would not come down into the cleft. There is scarcely room for another body, and even a kind hand would crush something there.

It would attend to the water.

Far beneath the animals, a fresh fracture is beginning to close under the mountain’s weight. In another season it would shut the hot seam. The power eases one buried stone against another, no more than a hair’s breadth, so that the water can continue to rise. It does not make the seam abundant. There is still reason to climb when it runs low, still reason to catch a drop along the edge of a chip.

At the band where shadows disagree, it watches longer. A hunter misses; elsewhere along the same stone, one feeds. A small animal escapes by a crack it seems to have entered before it opened. The power does not straighten their moments. Whatever the creatures know of this place, they live by it.

Then it withdraws. Cloud passes over the rim and the walls weep. An old pale animal presses its worn back beneath a ledge, and something translucent waits below its folds. Water gathers, falls, and is taken up before the stone dries.
