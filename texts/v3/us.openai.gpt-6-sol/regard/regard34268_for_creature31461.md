The power came to the hollow with enough strength to still the vanes. It did not use it.

It waited through several deep notes, long enough to learn which drops the flyers caught and which fell past them. It watched the scavengers gather where water brought them food, and the flat-backed animal lift once beneath the sill. There was hunger here, but nothing asked to be rescued from the fact of eating.

Near the vine, a channel had filled almost to its lip. The next heavy run of water would spill across the corner and take the roots and their little bed of silt over the drop. The power could have cleared the channel in an instant. That would have sent all the water away from the vine.

Instead it moved one grain.

The grain lodged against a rough place farther down the wall. Water divided around it: most went through the old channel, while a narrow thread continued into the corner. The silt stayed where it was. A scavenger stepped across the new current without noticing; the animal beneath the sill remained hidden. When the deeper note came, the flyers shivered and turned their faces upward. One caught a drop.

The power stayed until it was sure the grain would hold. Then it left the hollow to its roar, its mist, and its own small weather.
