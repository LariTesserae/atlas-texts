The power would not still the water. The creature eats because the mountain sheds itself, and shelters because the water leaves minerals behind. To make the notch safe would be to empty it of the life it knows.

It would wait through a clear day, long enough to see the strips spread over the pale floor. Then it would feel a fracture behind the three wedged stones: a flat piece nearly free, large enough to close the creature’s opening when it fell.

The power would touch only that fracture. Not to hold the stone in place, but to let it break tonight instead of later, while the strips were withdrawn. The current would carry most of it away. One thin piece would lodge above the cleft, making a small lee where the weed could keep its hold.

By morning, the roar would be unchanged. Grit would still drift from the seam; the new ledge would last no longer than ledges do. The creature might draw the thin piece closer, or leave it where it lay.

The power would leave the knot untouched. Someone had tied it for a reason now lost, and the creature had found its own use for the stones beneath it. It would leave the narrow passage narrow, too. Help need not turn every closed place into a door.

When the strips emerged again, they would test the new edge, then reach into the current. The power would stay just long enough to see that they could withdraw freely. After that, it would go.
