The power watched until the light changed.

It learned which trails led to food, which cracks sheltered the pale animals, and where the hunter waited when its shadow no longer frightened anything. It heard the track give its low note in the wind. It saw that the falling grit fed the hollow, and that the hollow was kept open by creatures who ate there.

One bolt beneath the toothed strip had begun to work loose. In a strong wind the strip would fall across the dent, covering its grooves. The power pressed the bolt back into its old seat.

It did not clean the dust away. It did not lift the pale animal from the hunter’s path, or still the hunter’s forelegs. By evening, some of the morning’s trails had vanished and others had been worn into the stone.

The wind came up after dark. The strip held and hummed. Grit fell through the teeth, and below it, in the shallow hollow, the small feet went on moving.
