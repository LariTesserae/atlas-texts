The power came to the court and did not enter it at first.

It watched a crab wait while a pale hunter occupied the passage ahead. It watched the crab turn back, and a grazer cross the same passage unharmed. Nothing here was safe for long, but much of it was possible. The power understood that difference.

It could have stopped the mortar from wearing away. That would have closed the seams. It could have raised a wall against the dark below, and changed the slow current the fish held themselves within. It left the court as it was.

Far beneath the last course visible from the parapet, one stone had begun to shift. Its weight had settled onto the edge of the stone below it. In time, the two would have moved together, and the courses above them after that. The power went down where no inhabitant of the court could follow and pressed a finger against the lower stone.

It moved only enough to bear its share again. Silt drifted from the joint and fell into the dark.

Above, the warm water rose through the floor at the same slow rate. The crab worked a grain from the chisel mark and carried it into its seam. After a while, the hunter reached into the passage and found nothing there.

The power stayed until the disturbed silt had passed below the court. Then it let go.
