The power comes to the ridge prepared to help. It finds nowhere to put its hands.

It could widen the basin, giving the fish room to turn. But the narrowness keeps the larger hunters out. It could strengthen the shells in their fractures, and the snapping hunters would go hungry. It could hold the seep open forever, though its pauses and breakages have made places for the film to grow.

So it waits through a turn of the shoal. It watches a transparent animal fold itself thin and survive the passing silver bodies. It watches another fail. The loss hurts, but it is not a mistake in the workings of the place.

Near the seep, the large fish passes over the stone. Dark branches appear in the film behind it. The power can read no message there, and does not give the marks one. It lets them fade.

There may come a day when something from beyond the ridge threatens to crush it without feeding or being fed: a falling weight, a careless machine. The power would turn that aside if it could. It would not make the basin safe from its own inhabitants.

For now, it remains near enough to notice and far enough away to leave the water undisturbed. The valve thumps. A shell scrapes the first soft edge from new stone, and the film begins again.
