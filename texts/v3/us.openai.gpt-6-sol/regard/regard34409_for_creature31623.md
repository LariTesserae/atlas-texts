The power came into the hollow without making itself large enough to fill it.

It watched the water first. It followed the seep from where it rose through the limestone to the low notch where it left, and saw that the salt, the film, and the small animals belonged to the same slow arrangement. More water would wash the film away. Less would leave the seams dry. Even a gift of sunlight might change what could live here.

So it did not make the hollow greener or brighter.

One of the three crossing stones had begun to rock. A person stepping on its far edge would put a foot into a seam beside it, where several small animals fed. The power waited until they had moved beneath the overhang, then settled the stone onto its old bed. It did not build a path across the hollow. It made the path that was already there dependable.

Near the outlet, a loose flake of salt leaned into the narrowest part of the flow. The power lifted it and laid it on dry stone. Water passed through the notch as it had before.

Then it sat for a while beside the chipped cup. A drop fell from the roof. Beneath the bosses, the larger animal advanced through the pale film, leaving its thin, fresh line. The power had no reason to call it out into view.

When it left, the hollow looked unaltered. The next person to cross would find a firm place for a foot, and the creatures under it would go on feeding.
