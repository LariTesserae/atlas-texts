The power arrives without a footstep. For a while it listens from the middle of the courtyard, where the high notes lose their edges and the sound has weight.

It finds no hunger to answer. Air reaches the throats; the passages remain open. It could widen them and make the chord louder, or carry its voice beyond the parapet, but neither would be a kindness the creature could use. The courtyard has room for all of it.

So the power follows the hidden passages through the stone. It learns which lintels bear their weight and which repairs have become part of the voice. At one narrow turn, an old support is taking more strain than it once did. The power eases that strain into the blocks beside it, moving nothing that shapes the air.

In the courtyard, no note changes. The pressure stays steady beneath the floor.

The power leaves no mark to tell what it has done. It goes knowing the chord may never have heard a difference, and that this is well.
