The power watched the pale animal come back to the same pool with grass in its mouth. It watched the animal stop, put down one foot, and test the stone along the water’s edge until it found a place it knew.

Then it watched a traveler do much the same thing, less patiently. The traveler had begun to follow a warm passage under the scree. At each opening, the animal heard the scrape of a boot and went deeper among the stones.

The power left the note in the ridges alone. It did not loosen the black beads or draw the white notch nearer. The animal had lived its life with those things; changing them might take away more than anyone understood.

Instead, while the traveler slept inside a stone ring, the power moved a few loose stones. Beside the ring it set two flat ones close enough for a hand to span. At the next rock beyond them, it set two more. Each pair led only as far as a person could walk while keeping the next rock in sight. Their edges were rough on one side and smooth on the other, so they could be read even in the dimness.

The traveler found the first pair after waking. By holding to one rock at a time, they came at last to ground that fell steadily away from the valley. They did not need to disturb another of the animal’s passages.

Later, the pale animal crossed the open floor. It stopped near the first two stones and listened to a pebble click beneath the traveler’s retreating foot. Then it went on to its pool. The great note held, and the bead beneath the overhang remained where it was.
