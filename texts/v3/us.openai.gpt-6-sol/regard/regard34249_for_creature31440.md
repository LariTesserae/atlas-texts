The power would come into the hollow without making room for itself.

It would not bring light. Light might help a visitor see the narrow passage, but the inhabitants have no need of it. It would not fill the basin beneath the falling drop or smooth the ridges underfoot. The water and stone have made small differences here, and small differences are enough to live by.

For a while, it would listen. The drop falls. A few grains of grit settle. Beneath the notched stone, something scarcely visible grazes the film that has gathered there.

There is no wound to mend. The warm water keeps coming; the pale threads reach the rock; eggs remain safe in places a finger cannot reach. The power would leave all of that alone.

It might do one thing for the next visitor. As they feel along the wall, looking for a place to set a foot, their hand would meet the notch. They would pause long enough to hear the drop and find the ridge beneath their shoe. Then they would turn carefully, without shifting the marker or grinding the damp grit underfoot.

After they had gone, the hollow would be dark again. Moisture would gather beneath the lowest stone. One of the small creatures would come out of its crevice and feed.
