The power came to the courtyard after rain. It found the ants waiting on higher ground with their seeds, the gecko under its leaf, and the fig taking in water through roots no thicker than thread.

It could have brought more sun between the houses or made the tree grow tall enough to find it. But the courtyard was not in need of haste. Water was sinking into the clay. A bird landed in the fig, shook rain from its wings, and left.

So the power did nothing that afternoon.

Some weeks later, the person in the doorway brought out a few flat stones to set where bare feet kept sinking. They knelt beside the low place beneath the fig and considered putting one there. For a moment they watched an ant carry a seed across the damp ground. Then they laid the stone nearer the doorway, where the sun would dry the clay too quickly for anything to grow.

Whether the thought was their own, the power did not mind.
