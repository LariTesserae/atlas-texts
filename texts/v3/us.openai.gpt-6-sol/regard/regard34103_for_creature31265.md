The power watched the plateau for a long time before it understood that the birds’ missed strikes were not mistakes, and that the dark creatures’ distant answers did not always come from a distance. It watched a grazer pause where a passage had closed years ago. It watched the young press against their mothers and learn to cross ground that sometimes remembered a different shape.

There was much it could have changed. It could have made the hollows keep their places, given the birds dry wings, or taught every root to hold fast. But the hollows sheltered the dark creatures, the wet wings made the birds wait and watch, and roots that never parted would leave no new space for the stalks. The plateau was difficult to live on. It was also alive in ways the power did not wish to undo.

So it did very little.

In the oldest reaches, where the surface could seem firm and fail beneath a family, it gave the roots one more way to move. Just before a false stretch yielded, a faint tremor passed through the mat—too slight for a bird on its stalk to notice, but clear to a grazer with its belly near the ground. The first young to feel it drew back without knowing why. Their mothers drew back with them. In time, they came to know that tremor as they knew the pull of a turning crown.

The power did not close the uncertain places. Small lives passed through their clear, empty shapes; water found its old passages; the dark creatures still surfaced where there seemed to be no room for them. Birds still caught grazers, and sometimes struck only water. Stalks stopped turning and stood for years before their leaves went with the wind.

Once, beside a passage that had grown shut, the old grazer felt the new tremor underfoot. It waited, as it often did. Then it turned and took another route through the roots, one that had not been there when it was young.

The power stayed a while longer, listening to the scattered thudding. The stalks almost found a common rhythm. One crown shuddered and turned the other way. Nothing followed it into step, and the plateau went on.
