The power came when a storm was still beyond the distant ranges. It felt the old stone hold the day’s heat, heard the note moving through the passages beneath it, and saw how the small ones paused with their forefeet spread to listen.

It could have raised walls against the wind. But the wind carried rain into the seams, scattered seed, and gave warning before it became dangerous. It could have filled every basin. But the shallow pools warmed in the sun, and the rooted places had grown around water that came and went. The plateau was not waiting to be made gentle.

So the power waited too.

One of the small ones was crossing toward a shelter, following the stones through a cloud that had dropped suddenly onto the summit. A marker had fallen. It lay close to its place, but from where the creature crouched, the next upright stone was hidden by a shallow rise. The note under the rock had begun to deepen.

The power set the fallen marker upright. It pressed its base into the crack with no more force than the creature would have used, leaving it a little crooked, as the others were.

The small one reached it, touched it with a forefoot, and went on. By the time rain struck the plateau, it was inside the shelter beside the carved bowl. Water began to tick into it. Outside, the wind crossed the open stone as it always had, and the marker stood where it could be found again.
