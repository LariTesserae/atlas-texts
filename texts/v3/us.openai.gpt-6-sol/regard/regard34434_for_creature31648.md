The power would not open the lowest door.

It would first spend a long time listening where the walls met. The pumps knocked in patterns that changed as new rooms joined the city. The small ceramic clicks followed those changes, setting passages open and closing them again. Whenever the apertures overhead released bubbles, the work stopped. The collector pulled the bubbles away; the needle marked *absence*; the work began once more.

There was no reason, so far as the power could tell, to forbid the apertures. They might serve some part of the city it could not see. Instead, it touched the water immediately beneath them.

The next time they opened, the roar came as before. But the bubbles entered a clear sleeve of flowing water that had not been there a moment earlier. The sleeve carried them to the collector without letting them scatter through the courtyard. The needle stirred, then settled. Along the walls, the seals continued their slow adjustments.

The power stayed for a while to make certain. A plate turned; a conduit crossed the gap and joined the far wall. The new sleeve held through each change of current. When it had held through many openings of the apertures, the power left it in place, with a little room to bend as the city grew.

Beside the drain, the grass bowed in the altered flow and rose again. The power did nothing more to it.

No plate turned toward its visitor. Nothing in the courtyard gave thanks. Above, another chamber took its measure of water and passed the rest along, and the city went on finding where its edges met.
