The power does not lift the mountain.

It comes first to the rim, where the black stone is breaking, and waits through several flashes of pale mineral. It watches the small clear bodies gather before each seam opens. It watches the larger creature hold its limbs in a closing crack until the bubbles free it. Then it goes to the broken fourth wall.

There is a block the current has turned many times without setting down. The power puts one finger against it. For a moment the block stays still, though the water races on both sides. The creature stretches between the walls and the rim. Its chips spread; its searching limbs touch the block, then the course beneath it. When the power takes its finger away, the current turns the block again. This time it settles where the creature has been feeling for it.

The power waits. A seam opens below the wall. Three small bodies slip into its brightness, take what they can, and come out. The rock closes. Another seam opens farther off, and the creature is already moving toward it.

There is no need to make the basin still. The creature knows this water; the small ones live by the mountain’s brief openings. But one pale seam near the fourth wall has begun to close faster than the others. When it next flashes open, a small body enters and does not return.

The power places its hand on the stone. It cannot hold the mountain open without holding it, in some measure, apart. So it leaves the seam no wider than it was. It only slows the last instant of closing, until the dark knot and then the clear tail pass through.

The hand leaves no mark. The crack closes, and the grinding roar goes on.

Later, the larger creature finds the new block in place. It folds a length of itself inside the fourth narrow enclosure and rests there while the current runs over its chips. At the next flash, it reaches into the stone. The power remains at the rim long enough to see its limbs come free.
