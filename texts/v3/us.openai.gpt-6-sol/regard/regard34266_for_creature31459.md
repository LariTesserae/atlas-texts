The power watches for a year before touching anything.

At first the broad creatures seem to need rescuing. They are thin, easily caught, and slow to reach a place where food gathers. But the power sees one lie safely under the pressure of closing tiles, and another carry three small animals across a stretch they could not have crossed before morning. It sees a stem break and grow again. It learns that a shelter made permanent would soon be buried, and that stopping the plain would take away the journeys on which these lives depend.

Near the end of the year, it finds a young creature beneath a loose tile. Its limbs have taken hold around the rim, but a flake of old casing has lodged there too. When night comes, the tile will close against the flake before the creature can lie flat.

The power could lift the tile. Instead, while the day is warm and the seam is open, it shifts the flake into the dust groove. It leaves the tile where it was.

That night the rims draw together. The young creature flattens beneath them. The flake begins its slow travel across the basin, and the dust closes over the mark where it lay.

The power stays long enough to see the tile open again. Then it goes.
