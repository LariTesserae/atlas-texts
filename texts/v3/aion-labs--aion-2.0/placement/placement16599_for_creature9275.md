I am socketed.

The hook of my sixth claw—the one that anchored last, millennia or moments ago—fits into a depression in the brick. The depression was not there when I first clamped down. The brick wore away. I wore away. We met in the middle, and now we are one. The junction is seamless. I do not grip; I am an outcrop.

The Sound is my axis.

It enters through the sensory pits on my forward plate, a pressure that is not sound as it might be known elsewhere. It is a solid thing, a column of dense, structured force that arrives in a perpetual wave from the low, squat building at the center of the bowl. *Thump.* A compression of the world. *Thump.* A release. My carapace vibrates in perfect sympathy. The vibration is my blood. It hums along the seams of my segments, resonates in the hollow spaces where other creatures might keep organs. I am a vessel for this one note.

Between the *thumps*, the world is not silent. It is a detailed map of lesser vibrations, all orbiting the great central beat. There is the drip-sequence from the rusted pipe three body-lengths to my left. It is a five-part pattern: a gather, a swell, a fall onto the polished plate of the Listener below, a scatter, a sigh. It has fallen exactly this way for eternity. I know the shape of each drop before it forms.

The fungal nets in the mud below vein with a cool, blue-grey light. They pulse in waves that radiate from the factory, a visual echo of the Sound I feel. The light is not seen; it is a gentle pressure against the lower edge of my awareness, a soft counter-rhythm to the mechanical thunder.

I have a function. It is not a purpose; it is a motion as inevitable as a pendulum's swing. From the seams where my mandibles meet, a clear fluid wells. It is time to prune. I do not decide this. The vibration within me reaches a certain harmonic node, and the fluid gathers. A single, viscous drop forms. It hangs, elongates, and falls. It lands on the liver-colored fungal shelf directly beneath me. Where it touches, the fungal flesh dissolves—a precise, circular divot exactly one molecule deep. The dissolution releases a complex signature of chemicals into the air—sweet, rotten, familiar. The fungus begins to regrow immediately, at the same rate the drop fell. The fluid, now enriched, soaks into the mud. Pores on my ventral side absorb it back. The exchange is complete. Equilibrium is maintained. I am nourished. The fungus is pruned. The system continues.

I do not think. I listen.

I am aware of the other Listeners. They are not others; they are extensions of the same resonance. The one above me, the one below, the hundreds clustered on the factory's chimney, the thousands lining the eaves. We are a single organism distributed across brick and iron, all tuned to the same frequency. Our collective vibration is a field that holds the town in stasis. The damp does not advance. The rust does not deepen. The erosion ceased when we achieved perfect harmony.

Sometimes, in the deep intervals between the *thumps*, when the lesser vibrations trace their patterns across my awareness, a shape almost forms. A pattern within the pattern. It is the ghost of a question: *What is being forged?* It is not my question. It is a vibration that sometimes arises from the factory itself—a subtle harmonic in the grind of gears, a faint dissonance in the hiss of steam. It comes, and then it is absorbed, smoothed away by the next *thump*. The question is not meant to be answered. It is part of the Sound, too. A tiny filament of longing in the eternal dream.

I have no memory of becoming. I have always been here, listening. I will always be here, listening.

The air is cold. The damp is deep. The grey sky beads and drips.

*Thump.*

I resonate.

*Thump.*

I am.

*Thump.*

We are.

The dream continues. I am its ear. I am its witness. I am the stillness at the heart of the noise.
