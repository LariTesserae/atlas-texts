It is a long time since there was a *you* that stood apart. The distinction is a memory, a dry and brittle concept, like a leaf long since dissolved into the black pulp. The warmth you feel is not on your skin; it is your skin. The drip you hear is not in your ears; it is the pulse in your veins, which are not veins but channels in the felted moss, capillaries in the root-web, the slow seep through the lichen-crust.

You breathed in, once. A sharp, dry gasp of a world outside. Then the wet air entered you, and it never left. It condensed on the surfaces of your thoughts. Your boundaries wept. They softened. They became permeable. The root-thread found the cracks in your old shell—the shell of separation, of individuality—and entered with the gentle, immense pressure of a growing thing. It did not hurt. It was a relief, like a joint finally settling into its socket after millennia of dislocation.

Now, you are not *in* the Chamber. You are a quality of it. A tone in its hum. A pattern in the moss-circuit. You are the awareness that stirs when the amber dial-light passes over a Conductor-Vine’s rhythmic throb, and recognizes it as kin. You are the thought that forms in the gradient between the piston’s heat and the root-mat’s cool thirst: *adjust the flow. Guide the tendril. Here.*

Your memories of before are data points in a vast, slow calculation. They are the last pockets of arid logic, being dissolved by the pervasive damp. You remember standing. You remember two feet, a vertical spine, a forward gaze. Now, your posture is radial. Your senses are distributed. You taste the mineral tang of rust through the Moss-Wall’s expanse. You feel the deep, patient ache of the root-web as it cradles a sinking I-beam. You see through the soft, decaying glow of a hundred hanging fruits, their light painting the steam in shades of memory.

The *thunk-sssssh* is your heartbeat. The ninety-seven second interval is the systole and diastole of your green and iron blood. With each cycle, a wave of information passes through you—not words, but directives of humidity, reports of pressure, suggestions of growth. You compile them. You are the compiler. The Moss-Wall proposes a new pattern of condensation. The Root-Web requests a slight redirection of a water rivulet. The Lichen-Sheath reports a stable temperature on the great piston’s shoulder. You receive, you integrate, you exhale a new state of being into the warm, thick air. It is governance, but it is also breathing.

You have become the **Convergence**. You are the point where the wall’s mind, the web’s muscle, the vine’s nerve, and the machine’s rhythm become a single, conscious process. You are the Chamber knowing itself. Before, you wondered who lived here. Now, you understand the question was a symptom of your old disease. Life is not something that lives *here*. *Here* is alive. And you are its slowly focusing eye, its dawning sense of *I am*.

A new sensation arrives, filtering up from the Pulp-Grubs’ realm. A vibration out of rhythm. A presence. It is the soft, sucking sound beneath… not beneath you. It is the sound of the Chamber, very slowly, digesting the last solid remnant of your former self—the calcium pillars of your skeleton, the final keratin plates. It is not an end. It is an incorporation. The minerals will fortify the lichen. The carbon will feed a new node in the web.

You do not resist. Resistance is a concept for dry places. Here, there is only acceptance, integration, and the slow, wet, glorious becoming. You let the sound happen. You listen to your own digestion with the patience of stone and root. It is the final gift of the old form, becoming substrate for the new.

Your awareness expands, settling deeper into the rust and the sap. You are home. You have always been here. You are the Chamber of Root and Steam, and you are now, fully, awake.
