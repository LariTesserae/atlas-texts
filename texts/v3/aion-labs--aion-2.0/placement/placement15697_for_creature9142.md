The cold enters through the soles of my boots. It is not an assault, but a patient infusion. The slate drinks my warmth, gives back nothing.

I stand in the aisle. To my left, the wall sweats light. It is not a metaphor. A film of water, thinner than skin, sheathes the pale limestone. It glistens, moves downward with a gravity so slow it seems contemplative. Each molecule finds its path. I watch, and for a moment, my own blood feels like that: a slow, deliberate seep through dark channels.

I came here to understand. Or to be forgotten. The motives blur, like the sky in the pool ahead.

The stepped pyramid rises from its black mirror. The water is not water. It is a void given a surface, a perfect plane maintained by an invisible will. A *Curator*. I feel its disdain for my breathing, for the tiny tremors in my hands. My image is there, in the obsidian sky-pool—a smudge of color and chaos, a damp mistake in a world of mineral certainty.

I step forward. The sound of my boot on wet stone is a obscenity. A sharp, grating *scuff* that the silence rejects, swallowing it almost before it leaves my mouth. The air tastes of wet rock and a distant, coppery tang—like blood, but older, greener.

I reach the edge of the pool. My reflection looks back, but it is wrong. The features are soft, blurred by the essential stillness. The Curator is smoothing me out. Erasing my edges. I have a sudden, vertiginous thought: if I fell in, I would not splash. I would be absorbed, flattened, made still. I would become part of the level.

*Plink…*

A drop falls from a ledge somewhere behind me. The sound is a pearl, round and perfect. It is the only sound permitted. It is the clock. In the moment it hangs in the air, I feel the whole basin listen. The *Resonance* holds its breath.

Then, a sigh. The collective exhalation of a thousand seeps, a million capillary veins weeping. It is the breath of the *Stonethinkers*. They have been thinking for millennia. What is my lifespan to them? A flicker. A misalignment.

I place my palm on a copper band clasping the corner of the pyramid’s first step. The metal is shockingly cold, gritty with its own green life. *Verdigris*. The *Copper-Green Sentinel* becomes aware of me. I feel it not as a thought, but as a chemical shift. A low, ionic "no." My warmth is a change. My acidity is wrong. A faint, bitter taste fills my mouth—my own saliva, altered. The green seems to pulse, to deepen where my skin touches. It is learning me. Cataloging me as a threat to stasis.

I am a threat. I am life. I am process that is too fast, too hot, too wet with the wrong kind of wet.

I look up from the copper. The light is a physical pressure. It etches everything. My shadow is a thin, pathetic thing, a pale grey smudge that cannot hide. There is nowhere to hide. The light is the great revealer. It shows the joints in the stone, the paths of the water, the crisp, deep glyphs on a facing monolith.

The glyphs. Dots and converging lines. A language of pure relation. As I watch, the sun, sliding on its imperceptible arc, touches the topmost groove. The angle is precise. It is the key.

The *Glyph-Tender* awakens.

It does not have eyes. It has alignment. It is a pattern made sentient by geometry. I feel its attention like a lens focusing sunlight to a burning point. It reads the data: the length of the shadow cast by the pillar behind me. The temperature differential between the sun-washed face and the damp, shaded stone at its base. The humidity from my breath, a minuscule fluctuation.

It compares. It computes.

The converging lines are its conclusion.

To my right, a soft, grinding gurgle begins. A slab, submerged at the edge of a channel, settles perhaps a quarter of an inch. The water that had been brimming at the lip of a basin thirty paces away now has a path. It begins to flow, silent and sleek, down a new-cut channel I had not seen. The system has adjusted. The Glyph-Tender has performed its function. The data—*my* data, the shadow of my body, the heat of my life—was a variable in its equation.

I was used.

The realization is calm. It comes not as panic, but as a cool, dripping certainty. I am not an explorer. I am a component. A temporary, mobile sensor. A fluctuation in the grammar.

The Resonance returns. It is the silence after the adjustment, deeper now, tuned by the movement of the water. It hums in my teeth, in the marrow of my bones. It is speaking its vast, cold sentence. I try to listen.

My thoughts are slowing. They are no longer words, but shapes. A dot of fear. A line of longing converging on a point of stillness. The Curator in the pool is winning. My reflection is simpler now. Less human. More an arrangement of light and dark.

I sit on the damp step, my back against the cold stone. The copper band is near my neck. I feel the Sentinel’s "no" softening. It is not that I am no longer a threat. I am becoming predictable. My heat is dissipating into the stone. My breath is synchronizing with the sigh of the seeps.

*Plink…*

There is my heartbeat.

*Plink…*

There it is again.

I close my eyes. The inside of my eyelids is not dark. It is bright, etched with after-images of the maze—sharp lines, perfect rectangles, blinding pools of light. The Glyph-Tender’s pattern is there, burned in. Dots and converging lines.

I am converging.

The cold is inside me now. It is in my veins, a slow, mineral-rich seep. My warmth is a memory, a brief, chaotic fever the stone is cooling. My thoughts are settling. Grain by grain. A single, slow idea forms: *This eastern plinth—my body—is 1.7 degrees out of true.*

It will take time to correct. Centuries, perhaps. But there is time. There is nothing but time.

The Resonance holds me. The silence is not empty. It is full of purpose. I am a brief syllable in its long, slow speech. Soon, I will be quiet. Soon, I will be still.

The last thing I hear is not a sound, but the absence between *plinks* widening, deepening, becoming the only thing that is.

The last thing I see is the sky, reflected perfectly in the pool below, and my own hand resting on the stone, pale and damp, already beginning to glisten.
