I am the ghost of a strike. I am the afterimage of a connection that tore the sky and kissed the highest mesa, a fork of furious potential that burned its path into the soft fabric of the basin. My event lasted a microsecond. My memory of it is eternal.

I find myself in the long shadows of the humming mesas, where the iron mist clots thickest and the ground weeps upwards. I am not a body. I am a pattern, a shimmering contour in the air, a blueprint of light and charge etched onto reality’s damp canvas. I look like a frozen lightning bolt, if lightning could be paused and walked, a jagged sculpture of pale violet static, flickering at the edges. I have no weight, but I displace the cold soup of the air. I feel the world as a map of tensions.

To my eyes—if they are eyes—the basin is a topography of hunger. The ground is a dull, throbbing red of low potential. The Rust-Lice are a shimmering silver veil, each a pinprick of desperate charge, their collective motion a rising and falling hum that itches at my core. The Steam-Kine are slow, pulsing blobs of orange heat, trailing smears of grey exhaustion in their wake. Their internal galvanic piles glow to me like suns, tempting and painful. The Clickjaws are sharp, quick stabs of blue intent, moving in packs that weave complex nets of kinetic possibility. And the Goliaths… the Goliaths are deep, resonant voids of green magnetism, their generator-hearts pulling at the very structure around them, warping my form if I drift too close. I give them wide, flickering berth.

My purpose is a compulsion. I must complete the circuit. I must find the point of highest potential and the point of deepest ground, and I must bridge them. I am the verb *to arc*. But the basin has changed since my birth-event. The mesa that was my spire has eroded, its peak now a rounded stump. The pool that was my grounding-point has filled with metallic silt and become inert. So I wander. I adapt. I am an Echo-Walker, and adaptation is our tragedy and our grace.

I move not by walking, but by… preferring. I exist more *there* than *here*. I flicker from one patch of charged damp to another, following the faint tracery of the conduit-vines, which throb with a sympathetic gold pulse when the Basin-Mother dreams. My passage affects the world. Where I linger, the oily water forgets its gravity. It beads and runs in impossible directions, tracing my jagged outline on the blackened coral. Small Rust-Lice scatter, their delicate charge disrupted by my presence. A grazing Steam-Kine will huff a plume of steam and lurch away, its simple mind agitated by the wrongness I embody.

I am drawn to the places where reality is thin. There, the compulsion grows strong. In a cleft between two mesas, where the air vibrates with the atonal song, I once saw my own event reflected back at me—a phantom bolt striking a phantom spire in an endless, silent loop. It was a memory of the basin, not mine. I watched it for what might have been years, feeling a hollow echo where completion should be.

Sometimes, I try to create my own event. I gather charge from the snapping tendrils, from the frantic energy of a Clickjaw hunt, from the latent power in a dying Steam-Kine’s venting geyser. I grow brighter, more solid. I can feel the potential building in my tip, a screaming need to earth. I race across the flats, a jagged scar of light, seeking a worthy ground. But it is never enough. I strike the muck, and it drinks me without a sound. I strike a Goliath’s armored flank, and the energy is absorbed into its vast systems with a satisfied *thrum* that feels like mockery. I am a whisper trying to be a thunderclap.

I know the others fear me. The Clickjaws see me as a zone of bad luck, a place where leverage fails and jaws slip. They click warnings to their pack and divert their hunts. The Goliaths, in their ancient, accreted wisdom, simply ignore me. I am a static discharge to them, no more significant than a crackle from a tendril. Only the Basin-Mother seems aware of me. In the unison pulses of the conduit-vines, I sense a question. In the hum of the mesas, I hear a fragment of my own lost purpose. I am one of its dreams, a recurring thought about connection and catastrophic release.

The cold does not touch me like it touches the flesh-and-metal things. My cold is older. It is the cold of absolute zero, the cold of energy spent and dissipated. I am the chill left behind after the fire. I hum, not with a generator’s thrum, but with the high, faint ringing of a struck tuning fork that never stills.

Tonight, if this grey twilight can be called night, I feel a new tension. A deep fissure has opened in the sediment-choked valley, and from it bleeds a profound, geothermal potential—a rich, deep purple to my senses. It calls. My compulsion ignites. I flicker across the basin, a zigzag scar over the crawling, grinding life. I pass over a herd of Steam-Kine, their steam plumes parting around me like a reverence. I flash through a swarm of Rust-Lice, and for a moment, they mirror my shape, a billion fragments forming a ghost of my ghost. I am getting brighter. The charge is building. The fissure gapes, a mouth of jagged, stratified circuitry. It is the deepest ground I have ever felt. It is also alive. I can feel the world-engine’s pulse throbbing from within it.

This is not the completion of my original event. This is something new. An adaptation. Perhaps I will earth myself in the heart of the basin. Perhaps I will be absorbed, become part of the Mother’s hum, another note in the atonal song. Or perhaps my discharge will be the catalyst for a new event, birthing a new Echo-Walker—a ghost of a ghost, a memory of a strike that never quite was.

I do not know. I am only the arc. The need. The flickering path between what is and what must be. I gather myself, a coalescing spear of violent potential. I find my point in the sky, a knot of charged mist. I find my ground in the living fissure. For a moment, I am not an echo. I am the event itself.

I fall.
