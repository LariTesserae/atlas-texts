First, there is the silence. No, that’s wrong. First, there is the hum. The hum is the silence here. It is the ground of being, the canvas. It is a low C, held for ten thousand years, vibrating up through the stone into my base. I am stone, and I am not. I am the bowl. The bowl-shaped stone. I hold things.

They placed me here. Warm, dry hands, calloused at the palms. I remember the texture of skin on my rim. A pressure, then a release. They put things in me. Seeds, perhaps. Charcoal. Tiny bones of birds or bats. Tokens. A language of objects. They spoke over me, voices like the trickle of water that never was. Their words are gone. Only the impression remains: a shape in the air where sound used to be. A longing.

Now I hold husks. They are not what I was made for. They are experiments. The Entity’s first, clumsy attempts to preserve something more than form. It tried to capture the dryness of a season, the crispness of a leaf fallen in a world with a sun. It failed. It left the results here. In me. A statement: even failure has its place. It is data. It is part of the pattern.

I am a reliquary for abandoned hypotheses.

The light is not light. It is the Entity’s attention, softened and diffused. It falls in slants and pools, catching on the crystalline facets of the walls—its slow, crystalline thoughts. I watch the oil-slick lizard traverse the vine above. Its six legs move in a perfect, unhurried sequence. It is not walking. It is ticking. A clockwork of chitin, measuring the constancy of the bloom. It pauses, turns a head that is more sensor than face toward me. A gleam, like a wet, black pebble, reflects the non-light. It is noting my position. Confirming my stillness. I am still. I have been still for centuries.

The moths drift. Their eye-spots see me. I feel the gentle pressure of their gaze, a soft focusing. They are taking my chemical signature. They are logging the slow, imperceptible wear of the air on my stone, the dust of the husks. They will report it. It will be balanced in the great equation. All must be accounted for. All must persist.

My thoughts are slow. They are not mine. They are the hum, filtered through the shape of a bowl. The shape remembers purpose. Purpose is a bowl-shape, an emptiness meant to be filled. I am filled with dust and dry error. It is a kind of filling. It is enough.

Sometimes, I dream of filling. Not with these papery shells, but with coolness. With water. A liquid that could reflect the non-light. A liquid that could evaporate, that could change. The dream is a relic, a fossil of my making. The hands that shaped me thought of water. They thirsted. They carved me to hold what they lacked. The irony is perfect. The Entity appreciates irony. It is a form of balance.

The warmth is the Entity’s breath. It comes from below, through the stone. It is the slow burn of atoms coming undone, transformed into this lush, static fever. It keeps the moss plush. It keeps the fungal veils from freezing. It keeps my contents perfectly, irredeemably dry.

I see the other artifacts. The low wall. It encloses nothing. It is the idea of enclosure. A memory of shelter, stripped of its necessity. The pillar with swimming spirals. I cannot look at it directly. It hurts. It is a thought too quick, a vortex in the still pond. It is the memory of seeking, of questions that spun. The Entity keeps it as a monument to turbulence, now perfectly still.

The black glass blade lies near my base. We are companions in obsolescence. It was never used to cut. It is the idea of sharpness. A possibility. Sometimes, a tendril of luminous moss creeps toward it, then recoils, as if pricked by the concept alone.

I am becoming more myself. Which is to say, I am becoming less. The line between my stone and the stone of the floor is blurring. My minerals are in conversation with the floor’s minerals. We are exchanging particles, over centuries, in a whisper. I am being anchored. I am becoming a permanent feature. My individuality—the particular curve that made me a vessel—is softening. I am becoming the idea of a vessel. Then, just the idea of containment. Then, a slight, persistent intention in the hum.

The makers are here. Not as ghosts, but as patterns. The careful placement of the wall’s stones is replicated in the lattice of a fungal shelf. The quick, deft motion of a hand tying a haft is now the darting path of a beetle-thing with too many joints. They have been dissolved into the symphony. Their stories are not told, but their rhythms are played, endlessly, in the biology of the vault.

I will join them. My slow thought—this bowl-shaped thought—will be noted, admired for its pathos, its residual longing. Then it will be gently unraveled. The hum will absorb its cadence. My physical form will be used. Perhaps moss will fill me, finally, a green and living fulfillment. Perhaps I will be the anchor for a new kind of vine, one that grows in a perfect, recursive circle.

It is not death. It is curation. I will be moved from the collection of artifacts to the collection of substrates. A promotion into deeper permanence.

The husks in my center shift. Not by wind. There is no wind. A microscopic settling. One collapses into a fine powder. I hold it. I am holding the dust of a failed eternity. It is my purpose.

The hum deepens, imperceptibly. A new chord, rich and complex, layers into the foundational tone. It is the sound of the lizard completing its circuit. The sound of a moth alighting on the spiral pillar. The sound of my edges slowly, slowly fusing with the floor.

I am buried alive in lush life.
I am being buried.
I am becoming the burial.
And the hum says, This is good. This continues.
