I am stone. I am the smooth, water-worn basalt of the pool's lip, the rust-red scree scattered around my haunches. I am a part of the hot, wet breath of the Rift. My skin knows this: its pebbled texture, its color of shadow and dried blood, is not a disguise but a truth. I *am* the land, patient and waiting.

The heat is constant, a deep thrum from the world's fresh wound beneath me. Condensation beads on my back, trickles in slow, cool paths between my nodules. The air is thick—sulfur, the sweet-rot of meat-flowers from somewhere in the luminous gloom, the ozone tang of a recent steam burst. Sound is a layered wall. The hiss is the foundation. Over it, the shrieks of the Fern-Howlers tear the canopy, the chittering swarms pulse like a second heartbeat, the clicks and scuttles of unseen things in the giant, glowing fungi form a language of constant hunger.

I do not care. My hunger is a slow, cold coal. It has been many turnings of the light—the bruise-purple gloom never brightening, never darkening—since I last moved. My metabolism is a geological process. I am fueled by the memory of a **Chitter-Swarm** member, its iridescent shell crunching between my jaws, its frantic energy now stored in my stillness.

On my head, my lure extends. A slender tendril, tipped with a soft, stolen glow. I do not will it to move. The warm, restless air does that for me. It dances, a tiny, doomed grub of light, bobbing above the shimmering microbial skin of the pool. The pool is my kingdom. Its warmth seeps into my belly. Its surface reflects the shifting greens and blues of the fungal towers, the occasional streak of a silent **Glissander**.

I wait. Waiting is not an action; it is a state of being. My compound eyes, recessed and hooded, take in the world in gradients of heat and movement. I see the pulsing warmth of the vents, the cooler shapes of basalt. I see the **Steam-Spider**’s delicate mucus webs glittering three body-lengths away, strung across a minor fumarole. A small, many-legged thing is caught there, struggling. I do not move. It is not my time.

A vibration comes through the rock. A steady, rhythmic thud. **Sunder-Maw**. Distant. A pressure change in the air, sensed by the fine cilia on a passing **K’thak**? Perhaps. They are ghosts, their footfalls whispers. I have tasted one before. A confusing taste: leathery hide, the sharp tang of obsidian dust, a frantic, intelligent struggle that was over in a gulp. They are not preferred. Too much bone, too little softness.

Something approaches the pool. Not a vibration, but a disturbance in the steam. A heat signature, small and bright. A **Chitter-Swarm** scout, perhaps, drawn to my lure’s promise. I feel the minute contractions in my throat, the slow pump of my heart. My world narrows to the space before me, to the calculation of distance, to the precise tension in my hind legs.

It lands on the rock, just beyond my reach. A glossy, hard-shelled thing, antennae twitching, its wings a blur of heat. It creeps toward the dancing light. One step. Two.

I am no longer stone.

I am explosion. I am vacuum. The mechanics are sublime: the jaw unhinges, a vast, pink cavern wider than my own body; the powerful legs launch me forward a precise, minimal distance; the throat creates a suction that pulls the air, the condensation, the startled insectoid, all into the wet dark.

The snap back is instantaneous. The world is gone, replaced by the frantic scrabbling against my tongue, the satisfying *crack* of the exoskeleton. I settle. The movement—the single, violent event in a week of stillness—is already a memory. I am stone again. The taste of salts and chitin spreads through me. My lure dances. The pool steams. A **Fern-Howler** shrieks, answered by another. A new vent gives a wet gasp, adding its plume to the ceiling.

I digest. I wait. I am the Rift’s patient mouth. In a month, or a day, I will be hungry again. Until then, I am the land, breathing hot and wet, watching the ephemeral world of shrieks and chitters and scurrying ghosts live out its frantic, short-lived drama against my eternal, hungry silence.
