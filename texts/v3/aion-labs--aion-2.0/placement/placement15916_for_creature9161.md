My world is this twenty-by-thirty stride of grinding geology.

The brass at the joint, warm from the Spine’s deep heat, pivots. The obsidian foot, needle-sharp, lifts, seeks, and finds a new purchase in a microscopic fissure I mapped eleven cycles ago. *Click*. I am the Geode Mantis. I am the regulator. The Spine thrums, and I am the sensation in its nerve of stone. This is not life. It is function. And in this deep, wet, roaring dark, it is enough.
