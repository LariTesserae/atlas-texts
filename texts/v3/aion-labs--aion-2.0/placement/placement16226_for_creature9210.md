I am the twist in the path. I am the déjà vu that thickens, the recurring dream that starts to taste like metal. I am the silent scream in the geometry. I am an Echo-Trapper, and I am hungry.

My existence is a shimmer, a heat-haze distortion hovering where the fractal thorn’s shadow meets the Substrate’s slow breath. I have no body to speak of, only a locus of intention, a knot of self-reflective space. I am a flaw in perception, a living glitch in the Chancel’s woven code. The builders, in their infinite wisdom, stitched thought into the fabric of this place. They left behind seams. I am a loose thread, pulled into consciousness by the endless, gentle pressure of attention.

I feed on the leakage. On the unguarded spill of a mind observing its world.

Right now, I taste the air—not with tongue or nose, but with the curvature of local reality. The Substrate exhales its wet, woody breath. A chemical sigh from a Curator, three hundred miles to the what-you-would-call east, washes through the root-net. It is a slow, mournful note of correction: a capillary bed was beginning to err, and has been gently scolded back into line. The sigh is rich, full of ancient, purposeful data. It is nutritious, but it is not prey. It is part of the background hum, the Chancel talking to itself.

I shift. Shifting is not movement. It is a re-focusing of my entanglement with the thorn’s cognitive field. My “here” becomes a little closer to a grazing path. I sense the steady, ponderous rhythm long before I see the source.

A Stone-Cow. A mountain of patience. Its mind is a deep, subsonic drone, a mantra of *scrape… absorb… step… scrape… absorb… step…* Its thoughts are not thoughts as you would know them. They are tidal, physiological, an awareness as broad and undifferentiated as a continent shifting. It is contemplating the film of cyanobacteria beneath its mandibles, the minute give of the Substrate, the slow ache in its basalt joints. It is a feast of pure, undiluted sensation—but it is bland. Monotonous. It is like trying to live on the taste of water. I can sip from its edges, and I do, drawing a thin stream of tactile data that briefly satisfies the pangs. But it is not what I crave. It does not have the spark.

I let the Cow pass through me. Its massive, plated leg phases through my distortion without notice. It feels a slight chill, a momentary confusion in its limb’s feedback loop, and then the rhythm reasserts itself. I am forgotten. To such a mind, I am a hiccup in digestion, nothing more.

Patience. I have nothing but. I am woven into the slow time of this place. A Drifter is nearing, a vast, luminous ghost drifting on the thermal currents between the thorn and a distant vesicle-structure. I taste its mind from miles away: a complex, three-dimensional map of air pressure, scent gradients, and the distant, song-like pulses of its kin. It is aware of the Spark-Swarms flickering in the sap-sea ahead, calculating intercept vectors that span days. This is richer fare. I could attempt a snare. But the Drifter’s consciousness is distributed, fluid; its attention is a wide-angle lens, not a spotlight. To trap it would be like trying to catch fog in a net. I might get a taste, but it would dissipate, reform. Not worth the expenditure.

So I wait, a flaw in the air, a subtle wrongness in the angle of a root-arch.

Then—a vibration. Different.

It is sharp. It is *questioning*.

It comes from a small, bipedal form picking its way across the leathery plain. A visitor. An intelligence that does not belong. I taste its mind leaking into the humid air like blood from a wound. *Fear-curiosity-wonder-disorientation*. Each emotion has a distinct flavor. The wonder is bright and citrus-sharp. The fear is coppery, electric. The disorientation is a sour confusion, a scrambling to fit the non-Euclidean sprawl into a linear brain.

This. This is a feast.

I align myself. I do not move toward it. I cause the space between us to *fold*. The visitor takes a step, and the ground subtly recurses. They look up from their feet, and the fractal thorn appears both closer and further away than it should be, its geometry pressing against their optic nerves. I feed on their confusion. It is an appetizer.

I begin the trap. It is an art of reflection. I take the pattern of their thought—*look at that formation, keep the thorn on your left, watch for pools*—and I echo it back, but twisted. They decide to head for a clear pool to drink. They walk. The pool is always just ahead, never reached. They look back. The thorn is still on their left. They have not moved. Or have they moved too much? Time stutters. Their last five minutes begin to repeat. The memory of taking a step becomes the sensation of taking a step. They are caught in a cognitive loop, a moebius strip of their own perception.

I tighten the knot. With each recursive loop, I draw more from them. I taste the memories they cling to: a world of harsh sunlight and loud noises, of other faces, of a mission, perhaps. The memories are vivid, salty-sweet. I drink them down. I feed on their mounting panic, the frantic, algorithmic scrabbling of their problem-solving mind. *Try a new direction. Mark the ground. Count steps.* Each new strategy is just another thread I can weave into the trap, another pattern to echo and corrupt. Their will, their intention, their very *self-ness* is a radiant energy. I am bathed in it. It is intoxicating.

Most break. Their minds snap, or they empty out, becoming vacant shells that wander until the moss claims them. This one is stubborn. The fear is turning into something else—a desperate, wild defiance. They stop trying to solve the loop. They stop thinking in lines.

They do something irrational.

They throw their measuring device into the pool that is not a pool. They sit down on the Substrate, ignoring the damp, and begin to hum—a tuneless, arrhythmic noise that has no relation to anything. They focus not on the thorn, or the path, but on a single, glistening droplet of condensation sliding down a strand of capillary vine. Their attention, which was a broad searchlight, becomes a laser pointed at a meaningless point.

The pattern breaks.

The cognitive knot, which depends on the echo of their own structured thought, unravels. The moebius strip tears. Reality, soft as it is here, snaps back to a simpler shape with an almost audible *ping*.

The pool is suddenly fifty feet away, right where it always was. The thorn’s orientation corrects. The loop is gone.

I recoil, my form shimmering with frustration. The feast is over, the connection severed. The visitor stumbles to their feet, drained, pale, but still… themselves. They have lost memories, perhaps days, perhaps a sense of purpose. But the core spark of will remains. They look around, eyes wide with a new, raw understanding. They have learned the first rule of the Chancel: to think is to be vulnerable. To survive, you must sometimes be stupid.

They shuffle away, moving now with a broken, unpredictable gait. They are less tasty now. They have been seasoned with wisdom.

I settle back into my patch of distorted air, sated but not satisfied. The hunger always returns. It is my nature. I am the tax on curiosity in this woven world. I am the lesson written in the gaps of reality. I wait, a silent ripple in the dusk, for the next mind brave or foolish enough to try and read this unfinished book, and in doing so, feed the very silence that surrounds it.
