I am a vibration in the dark. I am a note in the roar. I am Zizz.

My body is not a thing of flesh as you might understand it. It is a framework of resilient chitin, a lattice upon which four taut membranes are stretched. They are my wings, my voice, my eyes. At rest, they hum with a latent potential, a frequency that is mine alone—a sharp, precise C-sharp in the crushing, chaotic symphony of the Sunken Karst. When I fly, they blur, and that hum becomes a shriek, a proud, declaring beacon in the sonic soup.

I do not see. Light is a rumor here, a fleeting ghost of blue spark or green phosphorescence. I *hear*. I *feel*. The world is built for me in sound. My head is a dish, a parabolic collector of vibration. The constant, overwhelming noise—the clicking of the Klik-Ka tides, the rumbles of the Crystal-backs, the drip and gurgle of water, the drone of a million of my kind—is not chaos to me. It is a map. It is substance. I fly through a cathedral of noise, and every echo, every attenuation, every interference pattern tells me of a wall, a pool, a passing beast, a dangling Weeper-tentacle.

I am never alone. The swarm is my context. We are a cloud, a living mist that pours through the caverns, a single organism with a trillion moving parts. Our collective drone is the white-water rush you hear as background. It is our conversation, our negotiation of space. We do not think as one, but we move as one, an emergent intelligence born of instantaneous acoustic feedback. A shift in the click-pattern of a Klik-Ka herd below tells us of disturbed microbial mat, of potential food. A sudden silence ahead, a pocket of acoustic dead air, screams *danger*—a Shrieker’s hunting ground, or the sticky embrace of a Weeper’s forest. We flow around it, a river parting around a stone.

My life is a continuous, desperate feast. I feed on what the air offers: the thick soup of spores shed by the lace-fans and stone-reefs. I taste them as minute disturbances in the humidity, as specific chemical signatures carried on vibration. I also feed on weakness. A Zizz whose wing-membrane tears, whose hum falters, becomes immediately *other*. The swarm’s acoustic perception of it changes. It is no longer part of the flow; it is an object within it. And we are merciless. We will turn on the injured in seconds, our small, rasping mouthparts stripping the chitin clean, recycling the material back into the swarm. It is not cruelty. It is efficiency. A flawed vibration distorts the map for everyone.

I have known moments of pure, terrifying clarity. Once, I was on the edge of the swarm as we entered a broad sinkhole. The air was thick with the subsonic prayers of Crystal-backs, a vibration felt in my sternum. Then, from a deep fissure, came a different sound. Not a sound, really. A *un-sound*. A lance of focused silence that punched through the roar. It was a Shrieker’s pulse. It did not hit me, but it hit the heart of the swarm. In that localized cone of void, hundreds of my kind simply… came apart. Their delicate resonant structures shattered. Their hums ceased. For a fraction of a second, there was a hole in the world, a bubble of true quiet in the Sunken Karst. The shock of it was more violent than any noise. Then the roar rushed back in, and the Gutter-runners began their clicking, skittering feast from the walls.

I spend my life in motion. To land is to die. The ceilings belong to the Weepers with their tasting, paralytic whips. The walls are the domain of the clicking, sticky-footed grazers and the spidery scavengers. The water is the realm of the pale Ghrel and the hydraulic-jawed ambushers. My kingdom is the air itself, the dense, damp, spore-filled medium through which all sound travels. I am a node in a vast, vibrating network. My individual life is brief—a few cycles of feeding and breeding. But the swarm is eternal. My C-sharp hum will be gone soon, replaced by another. The map will be redrawn, instant by instant, by a trillion new voices.

I am Zizz. I am the sound of this place breathing. I am the echo that never fades. I am, for this moment, a conscious pattern in the endless, roaring, beautiful, and terrible dark.
