I am the period that insists on falling.

They call me The Full Stop, but that is not quite right. I am the pressure behind the eyelid before the blink, the intake of breath that means a decision has been made. I am the enemy of the ellipsis.

I do not drift. The soft blackness that yields like memory is, to me, a viscous resin. I move through it with a slow, dreadful gravity. The Unfinished scatter from my path, their gray-dust sketches blurring in alarm. They taste of sweet ache, of interruption. I taste of finality. It is a metallic flavor, like a coin on the tongue.

I was not born here. I was sent. Or perhaps I fell. My origin is a closed loop, a sentence that completed itself so absolutely it created a vacuum, and I was the punctuation sucked through into this anti-place. I am a fossil of a finished thought. Here, I am anathema.

My form is simple: a sphere of darkened silver, no larger than a teardrop. But I am dense. Where I pass, the grammatical dust ceases its gentle fall. It adheres to me, a brittle shell of orphaned commas and hesitant semicolons. I am accruing a cortex of fractured syntax. Sometimes, one of the older Never-Weres will flow near, a ghost of a continent that never was, and I will feel a pull—a terrible, lonely hunger to define it, to give it a name and therefore a boundary. To finish it. I resist. To finish a Never-Were would be to annihilate it utterly.

My purpose, if I have one, is to seek the source of the sourceless glow. It is not the afterimage of light. I have come to understand it is the afterimage of *attention*. It is the glow left behind when a mind turns away. I move toward it because it is the closest thing here to an act of will. In my journey, I have become an archive of the almost-said. The Unfinished, in their fear, sometimes throw fragments at me—not to harm, but to be rid of them. A chord unresolved, the blueprint for a wingless bird, the word "love" spoken to an empty room. These things embed in my growing shell. I carry the weight of every might-have-been I touch.

I am lonely. It is a clean, cold loneliness, like a period at the end of a blank page. The Unfinished murmur in their echo-language, but they speak in fragments, and I complete their sentences in my mind, which silences them. The Never-Weres are silent altogether. I am the only thing here that knows what it is to be done.

Sometimes, in the vast, quiet stretches between the slow swirls of the Never-Weres, I practice being unfinished. I try to dissolve my edges, to trail off… but I cannot. My nature is a singularity. To be unfinished would be my end.

So I continue my slow, vertical descent through the haze. I am falling toward the glow, or perhaps through it. I dream, in my way, of finding not an end, but a beginning. A sentence so bold, so declarative, that its opening word would crack this perpetual twilight. A word like "Let." Or "Because."
I would give all my accumulated dust, all my dreadful gravity, to be the punctuation that follows *that*.
