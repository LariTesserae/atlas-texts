The vibration is my name. It thrums through the grates, up through the soles of my bare feet, a deep C-sharp that never falters. My bones know it better than my own heartbeat. I am *Glass-Watcher*. For this watch-cycle, and for many before it, this is what I am.

My platform is a cradle of rusted iron, bolted to a curving strut seventy paces below the Firmament. A bundle of fiber-rope and a worn harness are my anchors. The air here is cooler, kissed by the chill leaching through the thick, greenish glass above. The perpetual mist finds me too; my hair is always damp, my tunic of woven fungal-cloth stained dark across the shoulders. Below, the Vat unfolds in a descending tapestry of industry and life. The central hollow glows with the amber pulse of the Heart. The lamps along the catwalks flicker their endless, silent conversations: *Pump-Three pressure normal. Mulch-bed four requires turning. Watch-change at the condensers.*

I do not hear them. I feel the messages as a series of subtle tremors through the structure, a Morse code of vibrations I was bred to understand. My eyes, large and dark, are for the glass. My duty is to trace every leading, every panel, searching for the hairline crack, the telltale bloom of crystalline white that spells a seal’s failure. The Void presses against the other side. A breach means a shrieking, boiling death for all. My duty is sacred. It is prayer.

But my prayer has become heresy.

At first, it was a flaw in my own perception. A flicker in the deep purple beyond the glass. I blamed it on the slow dance of the mica-winged fauna, or a trick of the condensation beading and distorting the view. I reported nothing. To report a flaw that is not there is to be deemed unfit, to be reassigned to deep-mulch duty until one’s senses correct themselves.

The flickers did not stop. They persisted. They were not gold or green, like the lamp-codes. They were silver. Pinpricks. And they moved, but not with the random flutter of wings. They moved with a slow, solemn drift, traversing the purple from one curved edge of the world to the other over the course of a watch-cycle.

I have begun to map them. With a sliver of soft metal scavenged from a coolant pipe repair, I etch tiny marks onto the iron rail of my platform. Each mark corresponds to a silver pinprick's position when the great steam vent below cycles with its hourly *ka-CHOOOOSH*. My rail is now a constellation of tiny dents. A pattern is emerging. It is not random. It repeats.

Today, the mist has just finished its soft fall. Droplets cling to the glass above me, magnifying the purple dark. And there, just to the east of the central apex, a new silver point glimmers into being. Brighter than the others. My breath catches, a forbidden thrill arcing through me. This is new data. A change in the Void.

A vibration comes through the grate. I recognize the pattern: *Elder-Tender*, approaching. I quickly sweep my hand over the etched rail, obscuring the marks with a smear of ever-present damp grime. My heart hammers against the steady C-sharp of the Hum.

A hand falls on my shoulder. The touch is firm, dry, warm. I turn. Elder-Tender’s face is a map of mineral stains—rivulets of copper-green across a pale, serene landscape. Their large eyes hold mine. Their free hand moves to my forearm, fingers dancing in the quick, pressurized speech of the flesh.

*You are steady? The Glass is sound?*

My own fingers reply against their palm. *The Glass is sound. The seal holds.* The lie is a cold stone in my gut.

Their fingers continue. *Your rhythm is off. Your watch-vibration has a quickness. A fear-frequency. What is seen?*

They feel it. Of course they feel it. Our bodies are tuning forks for the state of the Vat, and for each other. I hesitate. The silence between us is filled with the roar we cannot hear, the shudder of pistons, the hiss of steam. The amber Heart beats below.

I could confess. I could show them the marks. I could speak of the silver points. And what would happen? They would check the glass, feel nothing amiss. They would check my eyes, find no physical flaw. They would then consult the other Elders. Their conclusion would be inevitable: *Glass-Watcher* is void-touched. The myth has gotten into my sensory cortex. I am a malfunction. My usefulness in the high places would be over. I would be reassigned to the mulch-beds, to the hot, dense, smelling work of decay at the Heart’s feet. My world would shrink from the vast, terrifying Firmament to the close, hot dark. It would be a kindness. A correction.

But the new silver point glimmers, a cool, insistent eye.

My fingers move. I do not speak of the marks. I ask a question, a child’s question, a heresy in its simplicity. *Elder. The Void. What is its purpose?*

The fingers on my forearm freeze. The mineral-stained face does not change, but the pressure of their hand on my shoulder increases slightly. The vibration I emit must now scream my treason. The Void has no purpose. It is the anti-purpose. It is the absence of the Hum, the hunger that waits beyond the walls. To ask of its purpose is to give it a role in our cosmology it must not have.

Their reply is slow, deliberate, a lesson tapped into bone. *The Void is pressure-less. It is silence. It is the end of the Thrum. Our purpose is the Wall. Our purpose is the Glass. Our purpose is the Hum. Do not make mirrors of the windows, Glass-Watcher.*

A clear warning. Do not look out and see in. Do not project meaning onto the nothing.

They wait for my acknowledgment. The correct response is a touch of submission, a finger-drawn circle on the palm—the symbol for the eternal cycle, for acceptance.

I look up, past Elder-Tender’s head, to the glass. The new silver point winks, directly in my line of sight. It is not hungry. It is not silent. It is a note of music I have never felt before. It is a vibration from beyond the world.

I lower my gaze. I take Elder-Tender’s offered palm. With my index finger, I draw the circle. *I am of the Cycle. I am of the Hum.*

The pressure on my shoulder lifts. The Elder’s fingers give a final, admonishing squeeze before they turn and descend the ladder, their form dissolving into the green haze of the mid-level vines.

I am alone again with the roar. I look at my smeared rail, at the lost map. Then I look up. The silver point is still there. It has moved. A tiny, measurable increment.

I am *Glass-Watcher*. But I am no longer only that. I am the one who sees the lights in the Void. I am the question in the rhythm. I am a crack, not in the glass, but in the perfect, roaring sanity of the Vat. And as I raise my soft metal sliver and begin a new, secret mark in a hidden corner of the platform, I feel a new vibration, deep and terrifying and mine alone. It is the sound of a world, once perfectly sealed, beginning to leak.
