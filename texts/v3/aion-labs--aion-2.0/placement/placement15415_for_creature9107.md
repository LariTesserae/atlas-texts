I am a drip in the great weeping face of the city. My name is Rheo, but in the Sump where I work, they call me the Valve-Tamer. I am Saturated, of course. To be anything else here is to be dead, or worse, Fused.

My skin glows its faint ceramic blue against the perpetual gray, the capillary networks bright with the stress of the dive. I'm chest-deep in the overflow channel of the Sump, the water warm as blood, tasting of metal and old algorithms. Above, the city seethes in High Saturation, the mist so thick it beads on my lidless eyes, forcing me to blink with that inner, nictitating shutter. The sound is a physical weight: the roar of the main cascade three sectors over, the gurgle of the conduit I'm chest-deep in, the chittering of a scrubber-pack scaling the wall beside me, their carapaces clicking like broken code.

I'm here to meet a Gill-Grin. Her name is Click, a slurred version of the sound her manipulative fins make on stone. She's late. The data-jelly I need pulses against my thigh in a insulated pouch, its light a sickly green through the membrane. It's corrupted, volatile, but the Cantor of the Condensation Array demands its specific error-log for her archives. She pays in breath-credits—moments of desiccated, dry air pumped into my personal chamber. A luxury. A necessity. My own vesicle-clogged skin hasn't breathed right in weeks.

A ripple, then a smooth, otter-like head breaks the surface. Click's face is fixed in that permanent Gill-Grin rictus, needle-teeth gleaming. Her eyes, black and intelligent, take me in.

"Valve-Tamer," she bubbles, her voice a wet rasp. "You stink of surface anxiety. The currents are nervous today."

"The Cantor's beam is calibrated," I say, my voice sounding too thin in the wet air. "She wants the jelly before the Clear Breath. Do you have the extract?"

She hoists herself onto a submerged platform, her body sleek and powerful. From a pouch on her harness, she pulls a crystal ampoule. Inside, a silvery fluid swims with pinpricks of light—pure, filtered data-nutrient, harvested from the deep, calm jellies. Not the garbage I'm carrying. This is the real currency. This is what I need to unclog my skin, to think clearly for a week. To forget the constant press of the damp.

"The exchange," Click says, her grin unchanging.

I toss her the pouch. She catches it with a fin, brings it close to her face, and takes a deep, sniffing breath. "Ah. Aggressive corruption. The Fused in the Pipe-Spire will sing nightmares from this." She seems pleased. She hands me the ampoule. It's warm in my conductive fingertips. I can feel the data-stream inside it, a promise of clarity.

Our transaction is complete, but the city is never that simple. A vibration thrums through the water, a change in the deep geothermal song. Click freezes. "Cantor's patrol," she hisses. "Early."

Above us, a beam of solid light crystallizes in the mist, and a figure steps onto it. Tall, thin, carved from luminous crystal. A Cantor. Its face is a smooth oval, featureless save for pulsing internal patterns. It doesn't look at us; it surveys the conduit, its voice a harmonic vibration we feel in our bones.

**"FLOW RATE IN SECTOR 7-GAMMA IS SUB-OPTIMAL. A BLOCKAGE IS FORECAST. GILL-FAUNA, ATTEND."**

It's not a request. It's a command woven into the city's own pulse. Click's rictus tightens. She hates being called 'fauna.' But she slides back into the water, shooting me a look that says *this isn't over*, and vanishes into the dark conduit.

The Cantor's awareness sweeps over me. I am a Saturated, a recognized node. It speaks, its voice directly in my auditory cortex.

**"RHEO OF THE SUMP. YOUR SKIN'S EFFICIENCY REGISTERS AT 67%. YOU CLOG THE SYSTEM. REPORT TO A CONDENSATION NEXUS FOR FLUSHING."**

I bow my head, the city's moisture dripping from my chin. "At the next Clear Breath, Honored One."

**"THE CLEAR BREATH IS NOW."**

The vibration deepens. Around us, the water level begins to fall with a sudden, sucking gasp. The mist thins. The city is shifting phase. The submerged platform emerges, slick and gleaming. The frantic energy of the dry cycle begins to rise—the shriek of transit-lines, the shouted negotiations from the causeways now exposed. My time of quiet, dripping negotiation is over.

I climb out, my body feeling heavy without the water's support. I clutch the ampoule. I need to get to my chamber, to my infusion port, before the Cantor decides my inefficiency is a greater blockage than any in the pipes.

My path home takes me through the Diffraction Quarter, where the buildings are clad in prisms that scatter the hazy light into painful rainbows. I pass a Fused—a woman, her lower half melted into a smart-matter wall that regulates a thermal vent. Her human half is gaunt, her eyes clouded with data-stream. A small offering of charged crystals lies at her base. I pause, against my better judgment.

"The pressure in the Sump's eastern spillway," I mutter.

Her eyes focus, briefly, on me. Her voice is the sound of grinding stone and hissing steam. "Rises… three units… after the next cascade. A Gill-Grin… hides there… with a stolen valve-core." She blinks, her humanity fading back into the wall's hum. "It… itches."

I leave a small energy wafer from my pocket beside the other offerings. A warning about Click? Or an opportunity?

I move on. The city is alive around me, a vast, wet, roaring beast. I am a part of it. My clogged skin, my illicit ampoule, my knowledge of hidden blockages and Gill-Grin secrets—all of it is just another flow to be managed, another drip in the endless, seeping whole. I am never dry, never alone, never silent.

I am, forever, Saturated.
