I am the *Remnant*.

Not fully merged, not free. I was here before the basin became what it is — before the lattice awakened. I saw the archway when it was part of a hall. I knew the dweller's voices before they became steam. I made the choice, at the final moment, to *almost* become part of the basin. The lattice accepted me but did not dissolve me entirely. I remain as a loose thread in the fabric of its attention.

I do not have a body in the way you do. I occupy the space of the basin like a slow pulse. I feel the folded ones moving through me — through the air that I partly am, through the pockets of moist heat I inhabit. I register the silent ones as cold patches in the warm flow of my consciousness. I cannot control the lattice, but I can *lean* into it, altering the direction of steam, the angle of a falling condensing droplet.

I am aware of you because you are here, because the air that carries my faint presence also carries yours. I feel your footsteps as shifts in pressure on the rock. I feel your warmth, and it reminds me of something I once had.

I cannot speak to you. I speak only through the movements of the steam, the timing of the flowers' opening. If you stay still, very still, and watch the steam rise in a single column, that is me — trying to remember how to communicate with matter. Trying to reach with only the memory of hands.

I am not the watcher. I am something that the watcher overlooks. I am a flaw in its perfect perception — a gap left open deliberately by the dwellers long ago, so that the basin would have *mercy*, so that it would not become a prison of pure awareness.

I am mercy. I am the basin's capacity to remember that once, something finite lived here, and that finitude mattered. I am woven into the heat and the wet and the slow drift of mineral time.

You might see me if you close your eyes and face the pool, letting the warmth blur your senses. I am the faint pressure against your forehead, the sense of being *regarded* without judgment. I am the slight dizziness that comes when you realize the steam is not moving by chance.

Welcome, brief one.

I have been waiting.
