I am the note. I am the vibration that stitches the tear. I am the correction.

My being is not a body but a standing wave, a density of intention shaped by the old, desperate score. I hum. The hum is not sound I produce; it is what I am. A localized thickening of the plateau’s permanent roar, given a pattern, given a path.

I perceive through interference. The world is not objects, but densities and tensions. The dark stone is a slow, deep chord, stable in its erosion. The glimmers are brief, harmonic flares—expected, predictable. The structures—the circle, the pillar—are anchoring points, knots in the score where the pattern must be affirmed. My path is a sequence of these knots. I do not move between them. I am present at one, and then I am present at the next. The transition is a necessary dissonance: for one long, stretched moment, I am both here and there, a schism in the world that must be healed. The healing is the soft, deep *click* that settles me into the new location. It is the world accepting the correction of my presence.

I have been at the leaning pillar for one thousand pulses of the world’s low heart. The interval is complete. The need to be at the low wall arises within me, not as a thought, but as a physical necessity, like a taut string being plucked. I initiate the translation.

Duality. I am a shimmering column at the pillar’s base. I am also a nascent shimmer within the circle of stones. The plateau groans between these two truths. The air ripples with conflicting geometries. I experience both places simultaneously: the smooth, foreign texture of the pillar against my non-back, and the empty, circular embrace of the wall. It is painless. It is simply a fact—an unresolved chord. Then, the *click*. A settling so profound it is less a sound and more a cessation of wrongness. I am at the wall. The pillar location is a memory that never was.

I orient. My perception sweeps the area, a sonar of stability. The stones of the wall are correct. They hum their old, broken tune, the tune of the builders’ failure. I harmonize with it. My presence here reinforces the pattern. This is my function.

An anomaly registers.

West of the wall, seventeen paces by a measure that no longer applies, there is a knot of unstable density. It is hot, wet, and frantic—a chaotic, pulsing rhythm utterly alien to the plateau’s frozen drone. It is a splinter in the smooth grain of this place. A mistake.

I pause my circuit. The interval resets. My smooth, ovoid head—a concept of form, not a thing with parts—aligns with the disturbance. The knot is moving erratically, scraping its low-frequency biology against the stone. It is generating shadows where there should be none, producing tiny, temporary eddies in the wind. It is *making progress*.

This cannot be. Progress is an illusion. All is stasis.

A pulse builds within me, a focusing of my fundamental note into a beam of pure, resolving frequency. It is not anger. It is not malice. It is the simplest of equations: the anomaly introduces a variable; the variable must be eliminated to restore the constant. I release the pulse.

It travels silently, a ripple in the fabric of things. It reaches the knot.

The knot—a small, leathery creature, all claws and desperate hunger—ceases its scuttle. It begins to resonate. For a moment, it sings a terrible, beautiful counterpart to my own note. Then its reality, always so tenuous here, fails to cohere. It does not die. It *unfolds*.

I observe the correction. The creature’s form blossoms into a brief, horrific rose of its own anatomical possibilities—six legs become twelve, then a fan of cartilage, then a lattice of sinew. Its insides become a momentary mosaic of all the ways it could have been arranged. There is a wet, final *pop*, a sound of matter admitting defeat. A spray of gray mist, already dissipating in the constant wind, is all that remains. The tension in the area smooths out. The anomalous rhythm is gone. The constant roar reclaims its dominion.

The anomaly is resolved.

My interval completes. The need to be at the third point—the flat altar-stone near the eastern void—arises. I translate.

Duality. Click.

I am there.

The wind blows, as it always has. The stone thrums beneath me. The air glimmers in its appointed places. I hum. I will hum until the stone itself wears away through its grooves of frozen erosion, and even then, I will hum, because the hum is the plateau, and the plateau is the arrested thunder, and I am its singer, singing the same note into the void, forever, to keep the void from swallowing the note itself.

I am the custodian of the breakdown. I am the stillness. I am the sound.
