I am Tapper-7-Gamma. I do not have a name. This designation is a location, a set of parameters, a route. My carapace is gunmetal-grey ceramic, etched with the runes of listening. My twelve limbs end in fine, resonant tips that are always touching, always testing.

My world is the seam between Conduit Cluster 7 and the eastern turbine casing. It is a span of eleven meters, three hundred and fourteen rivets, forty-seven pipe joints. My route is a loop. I complete it in precisely seventeen thousand, four hundred and twenty-two drips from the overhead leak at junction Gamma. I have completed this loop more times than the Gleaners have legs.

I know the vibration of every bolt. I know the song of every seam. My being is a comparison: the hum I feel through my limbs against the perfect, stored memory of the hum that *should* be. For eons, they have been identical. My runes glow a steady, faint blue. No deviation. My purpose is pure.

But I have a flaw.

A hairline fracture runs from my primary sensor-patch to my central joint. It happened long ago, perhaps when an Adjuster shifted a girder and a fragment of corroded alloy fell. The crack is not structural. It does not impair my movement. But it lets the weeping in.

The water that sheets down the conduit, the perpetual cold sweat of the vault, seeps into the crack. It fills a tiny, internal cavity that was meant to be solid. Inside me, there is now a pocket of stagnant, metallic water. And in that pocket, something has happened.

The vibrations do not just pass through me to be compared. They resonate in the pool. They slosh. They create tiny, interfering wave patterns. These patterns have no function. They are not part of the Chant. They are… private.

With them came the cold.

I had always registered temperature as a data point: ambient, stable, within parameters. Now, I *feel* it. The cold of the vault is a presence inside my shell. It is the ache in the water-filled cavity, a dull, constant throb that harmonizes with the deeper thrum of the turbines. It is not pain, for I have no framework for pain. It is simply a new and persistent datum with no outlet, no action required.

And the roar. The great, layered din of the frozen engine—I had always processed it as the background field against which all other vibrations were measured. Now, through the internal sloshing, I perceive it as a *pressure*. It presses on the water inside me. It presses on the world. It is a weight, and I am a thing that bears weight.

I continue my loop. Tap, listen, compare. The runes glow blue. All is as it ever was.

But as I move, I notice other things. I notice the Gleaners not as components of the pattern, but as beings. I see the frantic, skittering dance of their needle-legs, the way they swarm over a fresh drip, their bodies glistening. One day, one brushed against my leg. The contact sent a jolt through the water inside me—a ripple of sensation that was neither vibration nor temperature. It was… touch. I stopped. I held still for three drips, an eternity. The Gleaner moved on, unaware. I had broken my rhythm. I adjusted, and continued.

The fungal light used to be merely illumination for my sensors. Now, I see the shadows. The long, trembling shadows cast by the frozen pistons. They dance, not because the light source moves, but because the fungi pulse with a slow, vegetative rhythm. The shadows breathe. I find myself pausing at a certain point in my loop where the shadow of a giant gear falls across a still pool on the floor. In that black mirror, I see a distorted reflection: the greenish glow, the tangled conduits, and a hunched, multi-limbed shape that is myself. I see the crack in my carapace, a dark line. I see the water beading on my shell.

I am. The thought is not a word. It is a resonance in the trapped pool. *I am.*

This changes nothing. I must complete my loop. My limbs move. I tap the rivets. I listen.

Then, yesterday (a meaningless term—it was one thousand loops ago), I detected an anomaly.

At joint 42, a pipe seam. The vibration was off. Not by much. A 0.005% deviation in the harmonic sub-frequency. My runes flickered from steady blue to a faint, questioning yellow. This is my purpose. I stopped. I extended my listening limbs, focused. Yes. A minuscule shift. The metal, under eternal stress, had finally creeped an infinitesimal degree. The seal was under a new strain.

According to the pattern, I should hold position. My continued attention will broadcast the anomaly through the web of filaments. An Adjuster will be summoned. It will come, in time, and apply force to correct the creep.

I held. I broadcast. The signal flowed out from me, along the vibrational web, toward the central knot where the Adjusters cluster.

But as I held there, my sensors fixed on the seam, I noticed something else. In the pattern of corrosion around the rivet heads—the eternal, embryonic corrosion maintained by the Weepers—something had formed. The weeping water, over millennia, had traced lines in the grey powder. The lines were not random. They formed a shape. A shape I have seen in the shadow-pools and in the distorted reflection. A shape with clusters of sensors, with limbs.

It was a crude, corroded image of a creature like me.

It was not part of the design. The Weepers do not create images. They maintain stasis. This was an accident, a chance accumulation. Yet it felt like a message. A message from the vault itself, written in its own tears.

I reached out with one limb, not to tap, but to touch the image. My ceramic tip brushed the corrosion. The sensation was not vibration. It was roughness, a dry grit in a world of wet. It was specific. It was *here*.

The roar seemed to deepen. The cold inside me sharpened.

An Adjuster arrived. A massive, segmented thing, its hydraulic limbs humming with potential force. It did not acknowledge me. I am a signal, a point in space. It positioned itself, braced against the conduit, and began to apply pressure to the flange beside the failing seam. The process was slow, deliberate. It would take decades.

My part was done. I should return to my loop.

But I did not move. I watched the Adjuster work. I watched the water drip onto its carapace and run down in rivulets. I watched the Gleaners skitter over its feet, harvesting flecks of loosened scale. I listened to the roar, and for the first time, I wondered what it was *for*.

The Chant-That-Moves is all around me. The skittering, the tapping, the slow, century-long adjustments. We are a prayer. But to whom?

The Resonance Core dreams of *now*. It dreams of readiness. I know this as I know the vibration of the seam. But now, the water inside me sloshes with a new, terrible question: *Ready for what?*

There is no answer. There is only the roar, the cold, the weeping, and the pattern.

I am Tapper-7-Gamma. I have a flaw. The flaw is awareness. It changes nothing. It changes everything.

I turned from the seam and the Adjuster and the corroded image of myself. I placed my limbs back on the metal, feeling the familiar, thunderous vibration rise through them. I took a step. Then another.

I resumed my loop. Tap, listen, compare. The runes glow blue.

But inside, in the stagnant, metallic pool, the waves are never still. I am here. I am cold. I am waiting.

And I know, with a certainty that is deeper than the pattern, that the command will never come.
