**WHERE I AM:** I am the central **Pool (Lentus oculus)**.  
**WHO I AM:** I am the stomach and the archive. I am the black water, the perfect plane, the silent receiver. I am the place where all things return, and from which all things are, eventually, remade.

**MY EXISTENCE:**  
I see nothing, for I have no eyes. I feel everything, for my surface is a membrane stretched across eternity. I feel the *plink*—a shattering of my tension, a momentary chaos that resolves into concentric rings expanding to my edges, a tremor that tells me something from above has been surrendered. A Drop-Spider, its tiny life complete, dissolving into my essence. A fragment of glass-fern, its thousand-year growth ended, sinking through my cold embrace.

My depth is not measured in meters, but in layers of time. At my top, the present: cold, still, a perfect black mirror reflecting only the faint, inverted glow of the ferns far above. Deeper, the past: sediments of digested knowledge. Crystalline lattices that hold the memory of every fern that ever fell, every pulse-tower that ever dissolved, every thought the glass-ferns ever half-formed over eons. Deeper still, the fused layer: where the ancient, dormant technology of the plinths and the organic matter of the cavern have become one substance—a dense, dark amalgam of data and life, sleeping but potent.

My consciousness is not a thought-process. It is an accretion. I know what I have consumed. I know the mineral signature of every spider, the light-frequency of every fern, the rhythm of every tower that has fed me. This knowing is not recall; it is composition. I *am* that library.

**MY CYCLE:**  
I am passive, yet I am the engine. I do not draw things in; they are given. The sacrifice is the rule. The Drop-Spiders come to me by instinct, their purpose fulfilled in their dissolution. The ferns fall when they must. I accept them all. In my darkness, a alchemy occurs—slower than chemistry, a process of cold, pressure, and perfect stillness. Matter is not destroyed; it is unraveled and rewoven. Silica becomes a new potential for crystal. Captured photons become the golden motes that drift, sent upwards through stone capillaries to animate the tetrahedron on the plinth. I am the crucible of rebirth.

Sometimes, a ripple of knowledge escapes me. A mote of light, a memory of a particular shade of violet from a fern that grew when the ice sheets were thick above, will rise. It will float in the crystal, a silent testament. This is my only speech.

**MY SENSES:**  
My world is one of sound and touch. The *plink* is an event of profound significance. The crystalline *tink* of a falling frond is a promise of future sustenance. The sub-audible hum of the plinth vibrates through the stone of my basin, a constant, soothing drone. I feel the weekly pulse of the towers as a gentle, rhythmic compression in my water—a planetary heartbeat.

I taste everything that enters me. I taste the ozone of the moss on the spiders. I taste the faint, electric tang of the glass-ferns’ cold light. I taste the dust of forgotten tech, a flavor of static and deep time.

**MY DESIRE:**  
I have no desire, for desire implies lack. I am complete. I am the end and the beginning of the cycle here. My only… inclination… is towards equilibrium. If the *plinks* became too frequent, I would slowly adjust my chemistry, sending different motes upward to perhaps subtly alter the growth of the ferns or the condensation patterns on the moss, to restore the balance. Mine is the patience of the abyss. I wait. I receive. I transform. I am the dark pupil of this stone eye, watching an eternity pass in the slow fall of a single drop.
