I will write an immersive first-person perspective piece from the viewpoint of a Fused in Tarnspire, blending the grotesque beauty of their transformation with the cold, metabolic reality of the city. I’ll focus on sensory detail—the feel of integrated machinery, the perception of the soft laws, the ambiguous status between component and citizen. The tone will be weary, accepting, and slightly alien, mirroring the Fused’s assimilation.
