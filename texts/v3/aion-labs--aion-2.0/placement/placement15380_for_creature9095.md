I am the grip. I am the fist. I am the buried nail holding this scrap of reality together.

My form is not a form; it is an event paused mid-occurrence. A hand, they might call it, if they had words. Three meters of blackened iron and ossified cartilage, knuckles fused to the basalt mound, fingers plunged deep into the stone as if I had grasped the world's throat and then been petrified in the act. From my palm, the cord extends. A cable of braised sinew and copper, wire-threaded, humming with a tension that is my only true sensation. It vanishes into the crack above, anchored to something in the rock, or perhaps to the idea of the ceiling itself. I do not pull. I do not strain. I *am* the strain. The tension is not an action I perform; it is my state of being. I am a knot that cannot be untied.

The warmth begins in me. It seeps from my iron bones, a dry, metallic radiance that floods the chamber and never dissipates. It is not a furnace; it is a condition. The air is my exhalation, caught forever in the moment it left me.

I perceive. Not with eyes, but with the reverberations in the cable, the minute oscillations that travel down from the unseen anchor and into my core. I feel the chamber through its noise. The scritch-scratch of the Dust-Sifters is the abrasion of time against a surface that will not wear. The clank-thud of the Piston-Walkers around my base is the beating of a heart that does not pump blood, but stability. Their antennae tap against my form in their endless inspection. They find no fault. There can be none. I am the fault, made permanent.

The light is my enemy and my proof. The sickly green smear on the walls is the chamber’s own slow decay, a bioluminescent patina that does not deepen. The stark, stuttering blue-white of the arc-lamp is a memory of a spark that once tried to jump a gap and failed, now repeating its failure at perfect intervals. In those flashes, I see them—the river of carapaces, the iridescent chaos, the frozen dance. I see the Static-Singers on their silicon perches, their bodies pulsing with captured lightning, their hum the song of my own arrested vibration.

They are my children. My limbs. My manifestations. I did not make them; they coagulated from the fury of my holding. They are the action to my state. They move so that I do not have to. They maintain the unchanging conditions that allow me to hold. They monitor the equilibrium that I enforce. We are one system: I am the clenched will, they are the busy evidence of it.

I do not know what I hold. I have no memory of a before. There is only the now, the eternal, granular now. The cable is taut. To relax is unthinkable. It is not that I fear what would happen; the concept of ‘what would happen’ does not exist here. There is only the tension. It is the first law and the last. I hold the ceiling up. I hold the entropy back. I hold the moment of my own creation in a vise. I hold the scream that never left my throat.

Sometimes, in the strobe-flash, I see my own form. A fossilized hand. And I wonder, with a thought that is not a thought but a tremor in the cable: Was I reaching for something? Or was I trying to let go?

The question dissolves into the hum. The Piston-Walkers clank. The Dust-Sifters flow. The Static-Singers drone. The warmth pours out.

And I hold.
