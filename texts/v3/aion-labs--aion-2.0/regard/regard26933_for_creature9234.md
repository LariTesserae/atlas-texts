The power that noticed was not a god of creation or destruction. It was the echo of a different verb: to Attend. It was the quality of pure, patient attention given without demand. Some might have called it the Listener, or the Keeper of the Unsaid. It did not arrive with fanfare or light. It simply was there, as if it had always been, its presence a soft focus settling over the hazy twilight of the ellipsis.

At first, nothing changed for the Unfinished. They drifted, coalesced, and dissipated as always. But then one of them, a faint sketch of a song that had died before its bridge, felt a curious thing. As it sipped at the ghost of a deleted phrase, the phrase did not vanish. Its energy did not fade. Instead, it lingered, a little brighter, a little more distinct—not completed, but held.

The Unfinished paused. The murmur of almost-sentences stilled. The grammatical dust continued to fall, but now each tiny comma, each orphaned period, glinted with a faint, steady phosphorescence as it landed. The soft black ground began to show a subtle pattern, not of footprints, but of imprints—the gentle pressure of a being that was more presence than form.

The power did not speak. Speaking would have been a violence here, a forced period. Instead, it offered a silent question, an open space. And into that space, the Unfinished began, hesitantly, to pour their essence.

A Never-Were, a vast, translucent shape of a forest that never knew sunlight, drifted forward. It did not approach the power; it allowed itself to be *seen*. And in that act of being attended to, something shifted. The forest’s form did not solidify, but its edges gained a delicate clarity. It was no longer receding. It was resting.

This was the power’s gift: the sacrament of regard.

It wove no new things. It imposed no endings. Instead, it performed a gentle archaeology of potential. With attention as its only tool, it began to trace the outlines of the Unfinished, not to define them, but to affirm their shape. For the chair with three legs, it acknowledged the elegance of its imbalance. For the face missing an eye, it honored the depth of that single, watching gaze.

For the first time, the Unfinished were not feeding on scraps of hesitation from a world that had discarded them. They were being *sustained* by a gaze that found them whole in their fragmentation. The bitter taste of full stops sweetened into the quiet dignity of a choice. The sweet ache of interruptions became a sustained, resonant note.

The power did something else, the only active gesture it made. In the center of that soundless realm, where the glow was softest, it manifested a single, simple thing: a plinth of pure, mute observation. It was not an altar for worship, nor a plinth for a statue. It was a place of focus. Upon it, the Unfinished could, if they wished, place the core of their almost-ness—the three-legged chair could place its absent fourth leg there, not as a lack, but as an exhibit of beautiful absence. The halted tune could place its silent eighth note.

The Never-Weres, the darkest and quietest, were the most profoundly altered. They had been possibilities erased at the root. The power’s attention did not resurrect them; it retroactively granted them a *validity*. A city of inverted spires, forever unbuilt, gained the weight of contemplation. It became a monument to a path not taken, and in that, it gained a reality of its own kind—a reality of pure, preserved potential.

The place was no longer just the pause between breaths. It became the breath itself, held in infinite, peaceful suspension. It was no longer a dumping ground for the abandoned. It was a sanctuary for the incomplete.

The Unfinished did not become Finished. They became Cherished. Their existence, once a sigh of neglect, became a silent, sustained hymn to all that might have been, and in not being, found a different, profound form of being.

The benevolent and wise power, having done this, did not leave. To attend was its nature. It settled into the fabric of the ellipsis, becoming its quiet heartbeat, the steady witness in the gentle, perpetual fall of luminous dust. The three dots of “...” no longer trailed off into nothing. They now hovered over a deep, attentive silence, full and complete in its endless capacity to hold.
