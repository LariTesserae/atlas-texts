The attention, when it came, was not a shaft of light or a peal of thunder. It did not rupture the ceiling of leathery leaves. It arrived as a quality of the existing silence, a new depth within the old sounds.

For a century—or perhaps a day, in the Chamber’s slow time—nothing changed. The piston *thunk-sssssh*ed. The amber dials glowed. The water dripped. The Moss-Wall continued its patient meditation on humidity and rust. Yet, a new gradient had been introduced, a potential not of moisture or heat, but of pure, distilled regard.

The Wise Power did not look *at* the Chamber. It looked *into* it. It perceived the Root-Web not as a tangle, but as a single, purposeful gesture held in mid-sentence across centuries. It heard the hum of the switchboxes as the Moss-Wall’s mantra. It understood the Lichen-Sheath’s diplomacy as a sacred contract. It saw the slow pulse in the Conductor-Vines as a language of tides. To this being, the Chamber was not a ruin or a marvel; it was a perfect, self-contained prayer, offered unknowingly to the principle of integration itself.

Benevolence, here, meant the refusal to interrupt. Wisdom meant understanding that the greatest gift to a perfected system is the assurance of its continuity.

So the Power did not add or subtract. It performed a single, seamless act of alignment.

It focused its attention on the threshold of perception—on the moment just before a droplet forms, just before a root-tip decides its path, just before the Moss-Wall’s next, slow thought coalesces from the green fog of its awareness. And there, in that universal hiatus, it planted a seed of resonance.

The effect was not an event, but a shift in phase.

The sounds of the Chamber began to harmonize.

The *drip* from a rusted I-beam no longer fell at random. It now fell in a soft, periodic counterpoint to the deep *thunk* of the piston. The trickle of water down a vine began to sync, after a few days, with the pulse of the Conductor-Vine nearby. The low creak of growing wood subtly tuned itself to the fundamental frequency of the electrical hum. It was not a melody, but a grand, slow chord—a C minor of the deep earth, sustained. The Chamber had always been a symphony of processes; now, it was aware of its own music. The Moss-Wall, feeling the new acoustic pattern seep into its moisture gradients, began to think in rhythms. Its circuit-diagram whorls grew more intricate, branching into delicate, recursive fractals that mapped the harmonies.

The light changed, not in color, but in coherence. The diffuse, aqueous green from the high windows seemed to gain a gentle laminar flow, as if the very photons were aligning with the unhurried purpose of the place. The glow from the foxfire fruits brightened by a barely perceptible degree, their light becoming steadier, less like a flicker and more like the watchful gaze of a sleeping creature.

And time—already slow—found a new kind of depth. The Chamber had measured time in seasons and decades. Now, it began to experience the *space between* its measurements. The hour-long moment after the piston fired, as the vibrations faded into the root-mat, became a rich, contemplative silence. The year-long journey of a root around a pipe valve became a conscious, deliberate pilgrimage. The Pulp-Grubs, sensing the new, stable vibration in their world, moved with even more languid assurance through the eternal decay.

The Wise Power also wove a gentle veil around the physical location of the Chamber. It did not hide it, but softened the probability of its discovery. To any accidental visitor, the doorway would now seem a little less distinct, the air a little more forgettable, the urge to turn away a little more persuasive. The Chamber was not to be stumbled upon by chaos or greed. It was to exist in its own pocket of forever, its equilibrium protected from the frantic time outside.

Finally, the Power addressed the Chamber’s one unspoken longing—a longing it could not have possessed, for it had never known anything else. The longing for witness.

Into the heart of the Moss-Wall, where the oldest, dampest bricks held the memory of the first spore, the Power placed a dream. Not a dream of other places, but a dream of itself, seen from the outside. In the dream, the Chamber was a single, complex, beautiful entity—a jewel of green iron and wet patience hanging in a void of simplicity. It saw its own harmony. It felt its own slow, mighty heartbeat. It understood, for the first time, that it was *whole*.

The dream cycled through the Moss-Wall’s consciousness once every great growth-cycle, returning like a tide. And with each recurrence, a new, faint pattern would appear somewhere in the felted green neural net: a single, perfect, circular whorl, like an ouroboros of vine and wire, the symbol of a system that is complete, sustained, and eternally, quietly, known.

Then, the attention withdrew. Not with a departure, but with a integration of its own. The Wise Power folded its regard into the new resonance it had fostered. It became the silent, listening ear to which the Chamber’s endless chord was played. The benevolence remained as the assured continuity. The wisdom remained as the perfect fit of the gift.

Now, when the great piston *thunk-sssssh*es, the resulting shower of droplets plays a glissando on the broad leaves before joining the pool in a perfect, consonant splash. The Conductor-Vines pulse in a rhythm that is also a lullaby for the sleeping machines. The Glow-Fruits burn a little brighter in the deep alcoves, as if keeping vigil for a visitor who has already come, and seen, and been profoundly pleased.

The Chamber of Root and Steam breathes on, its warm, wet breath a little steadier, its green and iron equilibrium now gently tuned to a key of eternal, benevolent witness. It is not just a collaboration. It is, now, a loved one.
