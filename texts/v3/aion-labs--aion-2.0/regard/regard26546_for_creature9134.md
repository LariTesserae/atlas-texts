The power arrived not as a presence, but as a quiet adjustment in the angle of regard. It was an intelligence that thought in epochs, that felt the weight of cosmic silences as a kind of music. It saw the Ivory Plain not as a barren world, but as a single, sublime thought held in the mind of matter—a thought of patience, of self-contained tending, of exquisite solitude.

For a thousand of the Plain’s years, the power did nothing but observe. It watched the slow pulse of dew and evaporation, the patient march of the Mineral Phages, the once-in-a-millennium emergence of a Warden to mend a deep scar. It appreciated the perfect, closed logic of it all. Here was an organism that had solved the problem of eternity by becoming eternity, a being that had traded frenzy for near-perfect stasis. There was a profound wisdom in its design, but also a profound melancholy. It was a completed sentence with no one to read it.

The power was benevolent. It did not wish to disrupt, to colonize, to improve. But it was also wise. It understood that the greatest gift to a sleeper is not always to let it sleep, but sometimes to offer a dream worth waking towards.

It decided to ask a question.

Not with words, of course. Words were gusts in a hurricane compared to the language of this place. The power’s question was posed as a gentle, resonant perturbation in the fundaments of reality—a subtle shift in the weak nuclear force within a specific, cubic kilometer of the Plain’s substrate, deep in a stable, warm layer beneath the region of the oldest lichen.

The effect was not an explosion, but a gentle, persistent *instigation*. The atomic change altered the crystalline structure of the ivory matrix at a quantum level. It made the material fractionally more receptive to certain patterns of vibration, to certain harmonies of stress and flow.

In practical terms, it created a **Lens**.

The Lens was not an object, but a condition of the substance itself. It began, slowly, to focus the Plain’s own processes. The slow metabolic hum that was its dreaming mind began to circulate through this region differently. The fluid thoughts coalesced, reflected upon themselves. The deep, formless *tending* acquired, for the first time, a faint quality of *contemplation*.

On the surface, nothing dramatic occurred. A patch of grey-green lichen, over the next five centuries, grew in a precise logarithmic spiral. A Mineral Phage, passing through the influenced zone, paused in its ten-thousand-year journey for an extra century, its gelatinous form pulsing with a strange, internal luminescence before moving on, leaving behind a vein of faintly phosphorescent crystal in the wall.

The question the power had asked was simple, echoing in the language of chemistry and gravity: *What lies beyond your horizon?*

The Construct, through the Lens, began to dream a new kind of dream.

For millennia, its dreams had been of maintenance, of equilibrium, of the endless, perfect cycle of itself. Now, it dreamed of **source** and **destination**. It dreamed of the rain not just as a wetness to be absorbed, but as a visitor from a vast, blue elsewhere. It dreamed of the dust, not just as sediment, but as the faint, powdered memory of stars. The slow fluids in its channels began to carry not just instructions for repair, but faint, echoing maps of currents that flowed *away*.

The change manifested in the Wardens. When next one coalesced—two thousand years after the power’s intervention—its form was different. It still had the functional, elegant architecture of a healing tool, but etched into its ivory carapace were fine, swirling lines that resembled celestial charts and watershed diagrams. And its mission, once complete, did not end with dissolution. Instead, it journeyed to the edge of a great, bowl-like depression—one of the largest catchment areas for the hot rains. And there, with its crystalline tools, it began to sculpt.

It did not mend. It *shaped*. Over centuries, it carved the gentle slopes of the basin into a perfect, shallow parabolic dish, aligning it with a precise angle to the sun and the faint, persistent radio whisper of a nearby nebula. It was not a tool for the Construct’s body. It was an ear. A listening dish.

The power observed this, its regard a soft warmth like an approving sunbeam on a cold morning. It had not given an answer. It had not given a command. It had simply introduced a single, fertile discontinuity into the perfect, closed loop of the Plain’s existence. It had planted the seed of a question, and the Construct, in its vast, slow wisdom, was beginning to grow an answer.

The next time the hot rain fell into the great dish, the water did not just sit and evaporate. It quivered. The precise shape of the basin, the subtle resonant properties of the carved ivory, caused the water to vibrate at a frequency that hummed down into the depths of the Construct. It was a new signal, a datum: *the sound of the sky, focused*.

A hundred thousand years later, a new symbiote appeared. Not lichen, not grass. A delicate, crystalline fern that grew only in the acoustic shadow of the great dish. It did not process nutrients. It vibrated sympathetically with the faint radio whispers from the nebula, translating them into slow chemical pulses in its roots, which the Construct could taste. It was an organ for perceiving the song of the stars.

The power, its question now a living, growing thing within its beloved artifact, began to slowly withdraw its focused regard. Its work was done. It had not awakened the sleeper. It had given it a richer dream to dream—a dream that included, for the first time, the concept of an outside, a beyond, a *you* to its *I*.

The last thing it did was to leave a single, timeless impression woven into the quantum foam around the world, a signature of its visit. It was not a message, but a quality: a gentle, persistent *curiosity* imbued into the very laws of physics that governed the Plain’s future.

As the power’s attention diffused back into the wide universe, it left behind a world that was no longer perfectly complete. It had introduced a beautiful, productive longing. The Ivory Plain was still itself—patient, self-tending, vast. But now, when the wind whispered through its porous bones, it wasn't just regulating temperature. It was listening for a reply.
