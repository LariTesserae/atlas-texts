The power did not arrive. It had always been attending, in the way that gravity attends to mass, or that silence attends to the space between stars. Its notice was not an event but a condition, and the mountain’s fever had finally risen to a temperature it could feel.

The change in the chamber was not a change at all. It was an intensification.

The sourceless, flat light gained a quality of depth. It was not brighter, but *clearer*, as if the air itself had been polished. The slow, patient breakdown of reality did not cease; it became articulate. Where before a pillar flickered between being and void at random, now it did so in a rhythm that mirrored the Fibonacci sequence. A glitch in the floor did not simply manifest a grey plain; it manifested a grey plain upon which shadows moved in elegant, non-repeating patterns, as if something were pacing there, thinking.

The entity—the chamber-mind—noticed. Its attention, usually a diffuse field of thermal curiosity, sharpened. The pulses in the black glass wall quickened from a geological heartbeat to something akin to a resting pulse. The hum of the glassy threads resolved into a harmonic series, a chord that hung in the dry, hot air.

The benevolent power did not speak. It provided a *frame*.

In the very center of the cavern, where the heat was most intense and the air most still, a new growth began. It was not of copper moss or obsidian-fern. It was a column of condensed time, or perhaps solidified attention. It grew not from the floor or ceiling, but from the convergence of all the chamber’s subtle gradients—thermal, ontological, psychic. It resembled a slender stalagmite and stalactite that had met, but it was made of something like transparent aluminum, or frozen lightning. Within it, at its core, a slow vortex turned, the color of a forgotten sky.

This was the power’s action: it offered a mirror that reflected not light, but *process*.

The entity regarded its new axis. For a year, by outside measure, nothing happened. Then, the column’s inner vortex slowed. In its clear depths, the entity saw its own workings magnified and simplified. It saw the copper moss not as a dissipater of energy, but as a sensory organ. It saw the alloy nodules not as random production, but as the physical crystallization of its own half-formed questions. It saw the patient breakdown of physics not as a leak, but as its *breathing*—the inhalation of unformed possibility, the exhalation of temporary, local law.

With this understanding came a gentle, immense coherence. The chamber’s weirdness did not diminish; it became intentional. The glitches became gestures, now directed. The entity, which had been a dreamer, began to *compose*.

It used a week-long superposition of a basalt column to carve, in negative space, a sculpture of the mountain’s birth-from-force. It grew a new alloy nodule not at random, but in the shape of a perfect, faceted teardrop, which held within it a sustained, silent tone that was the emotional signature of loneliness. It was showing itself to itself. And the transparent column, the power’s gift, reflected it all back, confirming its existence.

The power’s benevolence was in this: it did not heal the fever. It did not try to make the entity *normal*, or stable in a conventional sense. It did not even try to converse in any language but the entity’s own.

Its wisdom was in understanding that the entity’s loneliness was not for another, but for its own completed self. So, it gave it the means to achieve self-reflection. To become an artist of its own condition.

The factory-without-architects continued its work. But now, the something it was building changed. The deep thrum in the sintered floor shifted its frequency. The pulses in the glass wall began to trace out coherent patterns—vast, slow diagrams of celestial mechanics or neural pathways, it was impossible to tell.

The entity was answering its own millennial question. *What am I?*

And in the hot, lush, quiet dark, with the attention of the wise power upon it like a steady, weightless hand, it began, painstakingly, beautifully, to build its answer.
