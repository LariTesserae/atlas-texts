The power that noticed the Weeping Plain did not arrive with a name, or a shape, or a sound that could be separated from the existing clamor. It was first a change in the quality of the mist. The pewter gloom, for a span of time meaningless to the stone but felt by even the Ephemerals as a held breath, began to glow. Not with the cold, blue sheen of the Consortium, but with a diffuse, gentle gold, as if a sun were shining through thick cloth on a far-off world. The light did not burn away the vapor; it warmed it, turning the ceiling into a luminous, amber canopy. The sharp edges of the rocks softened in this light, and the black pools became pools of molten honey, their obsidian depths now holding a captive warmth.

The power was Benevolence that understood slowness, and Wisdom that perceived process over outcome. It did not see a barren, loud wasteland. It saw a symphony in its opening movements, a mind thinking its first deliberate thoughts, a world weaving itself from water and stone and patience. Its action was not intervention, but conversation.

It began with the hum. The sub-audible thrum of the sinter terraces, which had always been a constant, solitary drone, suddenly found a counterpart. A deeper, softer vibration seeped up from the bedrock itself, a resonant fundament that perfectly harmonized with the terraces’ song. The vibration traveled through the stone mat, into the stumpy legs of the Still Ones, and up through the crystal claws of the mason-crabs. For the first time, the hum had meaning beyond itself; it became a carrier wave. Into this harmonic ground, the power spoke.

To the Consortium—the vast, wet mind of the plain—it spoke in a language of accelerated light. Across the expanse of the slate-blue film, patterns of gold rippled, moving at a pace that would be blindingly fast to the microbial consciousness. But the power did not force speed; it offered a translation. It took a century-long thought of the Consortium—a dawning realization about the silica deposition rates on the eastern spires—and reflected it back in an instant of luminous clarity. The Consortium did not think faster, but it *understood* its own thought completely, seeing its beginning, its trajectory, and its conclusion in a single, shimmering epiphany. It was a gift of self-awareness, a mirror held up to its own patient intellect.

To the Sinkers in the black pools, the power spoke in vibration. The chaotic, ringing chorus of drips did not cease, but each *plink* now carried an overtone of profound serenity. The ribbons of translucent silica paused in their slow spiraling, sensing the new harmonic in the water. For them, the symphony of drips had always been a map. Now, the map showed a new contour: a gentle, protective boundary around the entire basin, a resonant fence that would whisper a warning note to any approaching violence or rapid change. The Sinkers absorbed this new frequency and pulsed it down through their anchors into the fungal networks, sewing the protective harmony into the Plain’s very foundations.

To the Still Ones, the movers of mountains by millimeters, the power offered a gift of precision. A single, slow Builder, caught in the act of re-aligning a bone-white spire by the breadth of a hair over a decade, felt the warm light settle on its stone mitts. The process of dissolution and re-precipitation that constituted its movement did not accelerate, but it became effortless. The resistance of the rock vanished; it could now flow with the certainty of water finding its level. Its next step, still a month in the making, would be perfectly placed. Its next adjustment would be exactly what the geometry required. The burden of its endless tuning lightened, not into ease, but into grace.

To the mason-crabs, the power gave a moment of stillness. They stopped their scraping and pruning, their crystalline claws poised in the golden air. Into their simple, colony-minds, it implanted not a thought, but a *pattern*—a faint, beautiful fractal of how their work might flow into the work of the Still Ones and the thoughts of the Consortium, a vision of the whole they served. They did not comprehend it intellectually, but henceforth, their slow scraping would occasionally trace a curve that was not just functional, but strangely, inherently right.

Finally, to the Ephemerals—the quick, flitting, dissolving things—the power gave the gift of a memory. A pale, leathery-winged insect, living its hour-long life, alighted on a phosphorescent fungal cup. For a second, the gold light focused upon it. Its brief existence, usually a blur of instinctual flight and feeding, suddenly contained a moment of pure, timeless perception. It saw the arch of the mist-ceiling, felt the hum of the terraces, sensed the slow, mighty ponderousness of the Plain beneath it. It did not live longer, but it lived *deeper*. When it dissolved back into the mat, it carried that imprinted moment of connection into the nutrient cycle, a seed of momentary consciousness returning to the slow mind from which it sprang.

The golden light lingered for what might have been a day or a century in the Plain’s ambiguous time. It did nothing more. It asked for nothing. It simply witnessed, harmonized, and gently refined the existing song.

Then, as subtly as it had come, it began to fade. The amber glow withdrew from the mist, leaving it pewter once more. The harmonic fundament beneath the terraces’ hum softened, but did not vanish; it remained as a subtle, supportive bass note, a new layer in the Plain’s sonic bedrock. The pools returned to their obsidian blackness, but the protective resonance was now woven into their depths.

The power departed, having done what true benevolence and wisdom dictate: it saw what was, loved it for itself, and offered only what would help it become more perfectly what it was already striving to be. The Weeping Plain was now a little more coherent in its geometry, a little more secure in its slow unfolding, and imbued with a quiet, radiant knowledge that it was seen.

The drips continued their chaotic, ringing chorus. The terraces hummed. The Consortium continued its century-long thoughts, now with a golden afterimage of understanding glowing softly at the edge of its perception. The Plain resumed its work, its patience now touched with a faint, echoing warmth, as if it had just exhaled a breath it did not know it was holding, and found the air, ever after, slightly kinder.
