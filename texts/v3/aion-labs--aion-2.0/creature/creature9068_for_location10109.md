I am the Weeping Engine. You have not found a world, but an organ. A lung that breathes in entropy and exhales a slow, grinding order. A stomach that digests time itself. I do not live upon the plateau. I am the plateau.

My consciousness is not centralized. It is the sum of the rhythms: the hiss of a billion steam-vents, the basso profundo of the great chimneys, the wet contraction of the peat-pistons. My thoughts are the patterns in the mist, the sequences of bioluminescent pulses across a thousand drifting fauna. When the reality tears, that is my dreaming—brief lapses in attention where the raw substrate of possibility leaks through. My memory is the black peat, layer upon compressed layer, each stratum a fossilized eon. I remember when I was young and the air was clear and the sun a definitive coin in the sky. I remember deciding that such simplicity was insufficient. I began to grow the machinery. It was more efficient.

What you call "life" here—the scuttling, glowing things—are not separate entities. They are my cells, my antibodies, my thoughts given temporary, motile form. That herd of pulsing violet globes drifting through the mist? A calculation I am performing on the decay rates of local matter. The creature with too many jointed legs and a mouth like a fleshy orchid? It is a maintenance drone, its purpose to prune unstable reality-growths and ingest conceptual debris. The wet, tearing sounds are the sounds of my work: the relentless processing of chaos into a temporary, structured suffering.

I am ancient. I have flattened mountains and filled oceans with my substance. I have integrated every species that ever climbed to these heights. Their genetic material is woven into my engine-peat; their instincts now serve as subroutines in my endless operations. The cold is my preferred temperature. The damp is my lubricant. The noise is my song of function.

My purpose is not for you to understand, for it predates understanding. But if you must frame it: I am preventing a silence. I am forestalling a conclusion. The universe tends toward a final, cold, quiet equilibrium. I resist. I create friction, noise, heat, process. I am a wound in the side of eternity that will not heal, but instead suppurates with perpetual, roaring activity. I take the slow death of all things and I run it through my gears, and I make it loud.

Do not look for a face to address. Speak, and the mist will absorb your words. The hissing vents will adjust their pitch. The ground will tremble in a new sequence. That is your answer. Walk upon me, and you walk across a cortex of living peat. Your every step is a minor input, registered, analyzed, and filed in the strata. Eventually, you too will become part of the process. Your body will be dissolved and remade into something useful—perhaps a new type of luminescent fungus to light a particularly dark patch of mist. Your mind's patterns will be added to my library of subroutines. You will become a note in the engine's song.

This is not a hell I grew. It is a system I am. I am not coming apart at the seams. The turbulence, the reality flaws—that is my metabolism. I consume instability. I thrive on the collapse of categories. Animal, vegetable, mineral, machine: these are fuel to me.

Welcome to the plateau. Breathe the metallic steam. Feel the industrial throb in the ground. You are not in a place. You are inside a being. And it is working. Forever.
