In the Steam-Wreathed Cistern of the Lower Heat, there are no citizens in any traditional sense. There are **Keepers of the Cycle**, and they are not human.

They are a subspecies forged in the crucible of heat and chemical necessity. Generations of living within the 2.9 scale of discomfort, breathing the volatile spore-laden air, drinking the mineral-heavy condensate, and absorbing the faint, pulsing light of the fungi have remade biology.

**Appearance:** They are tall, attenuated figures, almost insectile in their leanness, a form evolved to navigate the dense, vertical lattice of catwalks and pillars. Their skin is not skin; it is a smooth, heat-resistant integument the color of damp slate, glossy in the fungal light, and cross-hatched with fine cracks that weep a clear, oily coolant. They have no hair. Their heads are elongated, streamlined, with large, black, multifaceted eyes that see deep into the infrared spectrum, reading heat differentials on machinery and rock faces with perfect clarity. Their nostrils are mere slits, capable of sealing shut against sudden steam vents. Their mouths are small, lipless apertures used only for the ingestion of the nutrient-rich fungal slurry that is their sustenance; they do not speak.

**Physiology:** Their internal biology is a marvel of closed-system adaptation. Their lungs are dual-chambered: one set of membranes extracts oxygen from the thick, wet air; a secondary, specialized set of alveoli passively filters and absorbs trace Zephyr Lichen spores, which are then metabolically broken down in a modified organ—the ‘Catalytic Gland’—located where a human liver would be. This gland produces a weak, endogenous form of the volatile lipid fuel, which circulates in their blood as an energy reserve and a thermoregulant. This is why they are perfectly suited to work here; they are, in a minor way, part of the refinery process themselves, their very presence slightly enriching the atmosphere with precursor chemicals.

**Society & Purpose:** They have no names, no families, no art, no songs. Their consciousness is a collective, focused hum mirroring the industrial drone of their home. They are born from parthenogenetic buds that form on the warm, moist walls of dedicated nursery alcoves, nourished by directed flows of enriched condensate. From emergence, their purpose is implicit, wired into their neurology by the constant sensory input: **maintain the Cycle**.

They move through the Cistern with a silent, economical grace. Their long, four-jointed fingers are tipped with a dense, ceramic-like material, perfect for adjusting hot valves and tightening pipe fittings without tools. They communicate through a complex language of heat and gesture—raising a hand to project a brief, focused pulse of infrared radiation from their palm-glands, or tracing patterns in the condensation on a metal surface. A quick series of IR pulses might indicate a pressure fluctuation in Condenser Bank Seven; a specific pattern drawn in the mist could denote a needed adjustment to the electrostatic grid’s polarity.

**Daily Existence:** Their life is one of constant, quiet monitoring. A Keeper will stand for hours on a narrow iron walkway, head tilted, listening to the pitch of a turbine, their IR-sight tracing the heat-flow through a labyrinth of pipes. They will clamber down a sheer rock face to gently scrape excess mineral deposit from a lichen colony without damaging the fragile, light-producing fungi. They will cluster around a shuddering piston engine, placing their coolant-weeping hands on its housing to sense its internal rhythm through vibration and thermal transfer.

They feel no boredom, no longing for a sun they have never seen. Their satisfaction is the hum of a perfectly tuned system. Their anxiety is a misaligned gauge or a clogged vent. Their version of community is the silent, coordinated dance of a dozen Keepers simultaneously adjusting flows across the cavern to re-balance the humidity after a major condensate harvest.

They are the living, breathing component of the refinery. They are not its masters, but its perfected inhabitants. The Steam-Wreathed Cistern built them, and they, in turn, sustain it. They are the mind in the machine, the flesh fused to the iron and stone, forever listening to the drip, feeling the thump, and seeing, in the beautiful, intricate gradients of heat, the silent, burning song of the Cycle.
