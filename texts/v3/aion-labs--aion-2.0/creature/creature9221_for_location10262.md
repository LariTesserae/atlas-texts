They are called the **Unfinished**. This is not a name they gave themselves, for they have no language. It is the only word that fits the hollow they occupy in the world’s logic. They are not alive, not in any biological sense. Life, as a process of consumption and decay, never gained traction here. Instead, they are **persistent echoes of the act of making**, a kind of standing wave in the damp air, given temporary, shuddering form.

Their existence parameters are few, and all are derivatives of the world’s own failed metrics:

*   **Cohesion: 1.7** – They hold together, but barely. They are not beings so much as **habitual gatherings** of material.
*   **Awareness: 0.8** – Not thought, but a **magnetic pull** towards flaw and fracture.
*   **Weirdness Resonance: 2.9** – They are born from, and are attracted to, the world’s reality breakdowns.

**Form:** In the moments of stillness, they are scarcely there. A thickening of the mist near a weeping wall, a slight gathering of the granular paste into a mound that suggests a crouching shape. But when a section of masonry shivers—when that bone-crack sound echoes across the plain—the localized fog and grit *coalesce*. A humanoid silhouette, roughly 2 meters tall, will step from the vapor. Its body is the color of wet sediment, a dull grey-brown, and its surface is constantly beaded with the same condensation that sweats from the stones. It has no distinct features—no face, no fingers—only smoothed approximations, as if a sculptor left a clay figure out in the rain. Where the head should be, there is a shallow basin, a depression that collects water. This water is never still; it swirls with faint, greyish luminescence, reflecting not the outer world, but the **inner potential**—the ghostly blueprints from the memory-pools.

**Nature:** They are epiphenomena. The immense, forgotten intention that built the world left behind a psychic residue, a kind of **architectural loneliness**. The Unfinished are that loneliness, given granular form. They are not the makers, nor their servants. They are the **dust that remembers the chisel**. Their awareness is a single, driving impulse: to go to the places where the world is coming **un-done**.

**Behavior:** They are drawn to the sites of geometric turbulence. In the awful silence after a block yawns and snaps back, you will see them—three, five, a dozen—materializing from the damp air, trudging slowly across the grit towards the affected site. They do not repair it. They do not touch it. They form a loose circle around the shuddered stone and **watch**. Their basin-heads tilt, the swirling water within quickening. They are **recording the flaw**. They are absorbing the precise moment of reality's disagreement into their transient forms. After a time—minutes, hours—they will turn, in unison, and disperse, their bodies slumping back into mist and dirt.

Their second behavior involves the pools. The rule states: *Water is Memory*. The Unfinished are that rule made manifest. They are often found kneeling (or their best approximation of it) beside the shimmering lenses that form in the flawed joints. They do not drink. They **lower their heads** until the water in their basin-depression touches the water in the pool. For a moment, the two bodies of water merge. The swirling luminescence in their heads becomes a storm of images—the spire that was not built, the bridge that remained a gap, the forest of stone that was only a branching pattern in the condensation. This is their only sustenance: the consumption of lost possibilities. It is what grants them the faint cohesion they have.

**Interaction with the Rules:**

1.  **Everything is Made:** They are no exception. They are made by the **byproduct** of the making—the leftover intention and the physical materials of mist and grit.
2.  **The Making is Incomplete:** They are the embodiment of this incompleteness. Their forms are vague, their purpose ambiguous. They are a feature that was never in the blueprint, a bug that became the only software running.
3.  **Water is Memory:** They are both the vessels and the siphon for this memory. They are the loop through which the world’s forgotten alternatives circulate.
4.  **Silence is Unstable:** They are the children of that instability. Their formation is the "sound" of the compression releasing, made visible. When many gather, the sub-audible hum in the air rises slightly in pitch, becoming a faint, discordant chord.

They are neither hostile nor benevolent. To encounter one is to be ignored, unless you stand between it and a freshly shivered stone or a memory-pool. Then, it will simply stop, its form quivering, as if waiting for an obstacle in the blueprint to be removed. They are the caretakers of a cathedral that has no god, tending not to the stones, but to the very concept of **error**. They are the proof that in a world built entirely by will, even the absence of a final, deciding thought can, given enough time and damp, thick heat, begin to gather itself into something that almost, almost resembles a tenant.
