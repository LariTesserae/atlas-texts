### **Preparatory Briefing: The Plain of Final Geometry**  

**TO:** Autonomous Multiverse Traveler (Baseline Human Variant)  
**FROM:** Department of Anomalous Terrain  
**SUBJECT:** Pre-deployment conditioning & survival protocol for designated location: **The Plain of Final Geometry**  

---

#### **1. CORE NATURE OF THE LOCATION**

This is not a wilderness. It is a **finished place**. Think of it as the ash after a cosmic fire, cooled into permanent form. All processes of change, erosion, growth, and decay have concluded. What remains is a tableau in a state of **perfect, eternal equilibrium**. The silence, the stillness, and the soft perceptual distortions are not threats in the active sense—they are the environment itself. Your primary dangers will be **psychological disintegration, navigational failure, and the slow leaching of your own will to act.**

The place is littered with countless inert entities—we call them **"The Stilled"**—of impossible geometry. They are not hostile. They are not *anything*. They are monuments. Their presence, however, warps local reality in subtle ways, making perception unreliable.

---

#### **2. PRE-DEPARTURE PREPARATION**

**A. Psychological Fortification (Most Critical)**
*   **Isolation & Silence Training:** You must undergo a minimum of 72 hours in a sensory-deprivation chamber that mimics the conditions: uniform gray light, constant temperature, absolute silence. This is to inoculate you against the initial shock and to identify any latent susceptibility to sensory deprivation psychosis.
*   **Non-Euclidean Navigation Drills:** Use VR simulators that gently introduce visual paradoxes (Penrose stairs, objects with conflicting perspectives). The goal is not to master them—that's impossible for a human brain—but to learn to **distrust your own eyes and spatial instincts** without panicking.
*   **Acceptance Conditioning:** You must internalize the following mantra: *"Nothing here will change. Nothing here needs me. My actions are temporary. My presence is irrelevant."* This sounds nihilistic, but it is a protective measure. The urge to "fix," to "explore," to "interact" will lead you to exhaust yourself. You are a ghost walking through a finished painting. Any mark you make will be erased by time, and the painting will not notice.
*   **Meditative Anchor Technique:** Develop a powerful internal "anchor"—a specific memory, a piece of music you can recall perfectly, a mathematical problem, a physical sensation. This must be something you can retreat into entirely, to remind yourself of causality, sequence, and *process*. You will need it.

**B. Physical & Logistical Preparations**
*   **Suit & Life Support:**
    *   A self-contained environment suit is non-negotiable. The atmosphere is breathable but thin and devoid of moisture. The suit must have internal climate control, humidity recycling, and a **completely silent air circulation system**. Any motor hum or hiss will become a maddening, alien intrusion in that silence.
    *   Visor must be polarized to reduce the uniform gray glare, but with a HUD overlay capable of **objective metric tracking** (see Navigation).
    *   Power cells must be absolute top-tier, with passive solar charging unlikely (no discernible sun). Assume no resupply. Calculate for 3x your expected stay.
*   **Nutrition & Hydration:** High-calorie, low-residue paste. You will have no appetite. The psychological environment suppresses biological urgency. You will need to schedule feeding and hydration alarms.
*   **Shelter:** A lightweight, rigid pod. The stacked-plate shards are not reliable. Your shelter must be fully enclosed, light-proof (to simulate night), and have a white-noise generator. The external silence will press in on you. The white noise is not for comfort; it is to prevent auditory hallucination.
*   **Tools:**
    *   **NO WEAPONS.** They are worse than useless.
    *   A robust, hard-copy journal and wax pencils (pens may fail, electronics may distort). Documenting your thoughts is your primary defense against reality softening.
    *   A manually-wound chronometer. Do not rely on digital timers. You need a tangible, ticking (or sweeping) reminder of time's passage.
    *   A ball of ultra-strong twine. For marking your path physically, in a way the soft geometry cannot distort.
    *   Geiger counter, EMF reader, passive radiation badge. The "material" of The Stilled may emit anomalous signatures.

#### **3. ON-SITE PROTOCOLS**

**A. Arrival & Initial Calibration**
Upon transition, do not move immediately. Sit. Observe for one full cycle of your chronometer (e.g., one hour). Let the shock of the stillness wash over you. Listen to the sound of your own body. Activate your HUD's metric grid. Let your mind accept its first, crucial lesson: *the place does not care that you are here.*

**B. Navigation & Exploration**
*   **The Rule of Threes:** Never lose sight of three distinct, large "Stilled" behind you. Their relationship to each other will distort, but if you can keep them all in view, you maintain a crude triangulation.
*   **Trust the String, Not Your Eyes:** Unspool twine as you go. Anchor it to nothing—just lay it on the ground. Its physical path is more real than the path you perceive. Retracing it is your only guaranteed way back to your entry point/shelter.
*   **HUD is Gospel:** Your suit's sensors measure distance by laser rangefinder and inertial tracking. Believe these numbers over your proprioception. If your HUD says you've walked 1 km but your legs feel like 10, ignore your legs.
*   **Do Not Touch The Stilled.** Not out of fear of retaliation, but because of ontological contamination. Their stillness is absolute. Your touch—warm, moist, alive—is the most violent, chaotic act this plane has witnessed in millennia. The psychological recoil of interacting with such perfect stasis could be severe. Furthermore, their material may have properties that affect your mind or physical form directly.

**C. Psychological Maintenance**
*   **Structured Routine:** Divide your subjective time into strict, short blocks: 8 hours "sleep" in pod (with white noise), 8 hours "active" (exploration, journaling, maintenance), 8 hours "rest" (in suit, practicing anchor meditation, reviewing journal). Adhere to this religiously. Without it, time will dissolve.
*   **Journal Verbosely:** Record everything, especially contradictions. *"Spire A appeared to be 100m left of Spire B. After walking for 10 minutes, Spire A is now directly behind Spire B, though I walked in a straight line."* This externalizes the perceptual warping.
*   **Do Not Investigate the Primitive Structures.** They are tombstones for a previous visitor's sanity. They represent a futile struggle against the environment. To study them is to follow that path.
*   **Auditory Hallucination Protocol:** You will hear things. Whispers, distant chords, your name. This is your brain trying to fill the silence. Note it in your journal, label it as "Auditory Phantasm #X," and return to your anchor.

**D. Interaction with the Environment**
*   **The Brass Lichen:** It is inert. Do not consume it. It may be the closest thing to a "fossil" here.
*   **The Plate-Crust:** It is stable. You cannot dig it. Do not try.
*   **The Air:** Your suit will process it. It is safe, but dead.

#### **4. HAZARDS & WARNINGS (DIRECTIVE PRIORITY)**

*   **The Stillness Sickness:** A progressive apathy, a feeling that motion and intention are pointless. Symptoms include skipping routine entries in your journal, turning off your white noise "to hear the silence better," and eventually, sitting down between two Stilled and deciding not to get up. **Countermeasure:** Strict routine. Anchor meditation. If you feel its pull, return to your pod and engage a pre-recorded, emotionally charged message from your home (a loved one's voice, lively music).
*   **Perceptual Melt:** When the soft geometry causes such disorientation that you can no longer trust your HUD readings or even the feel of the twine in your hand. You may see the Stilled *almost* move. **Countermeasure:** Immediate shutdown. Retreat to pod. Sleep cycle. Recalibrate from your last trusted journal entry.
*   **The Epiphany:** The sudden, crushing understanding that you are standing in the aftermath of everything. That all stories are over, all conflicts resolved, all energy spent. This is the truth of the place. It is not malicious, but it can break a mind that insists on narrative and purpose. **Countermeasure:** Your mantra. *"I am temporary. My story is not over. This is not my page."*

---

#### **5. EXTRACTION & AFTERCARE**

Set a hard deadline for extraction before you depart. Do not extend it. When the time comes, follow your twine back.

Upon return, you will undergo **mandatory** re-integration therapy. The silence will have gotten into your bones. You may find normal sounds painful, normal movement vulgar, and normal change stressful. You have been to a place where the universe has sighed its last sigh and found it peaceful. Remembering how to live in the noise and chaos of becoming will be your final challenge.

**Remember:** You are not an explorer here. You are a visitor to a cosmic graveyard where the graves are too beautiful to mourn. Be quiet. Be still within your motion. And leave no trace.
