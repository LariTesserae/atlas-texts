## Preparation Briefing: The Bathysphere
**Location Classification:** Artificial Planetary Construct (Aquatic); Sterile-Industrial; Curated Void.

**Core Concept:** You are not visiting an ocean. You are entering a manufactured reality—a water-filled precision instrument on a planetary scale. Every sensation, every law, and every life form is the result of deliberate engineering. Your preparation must therefore be twofold: for the undeniable physical realities of deep water, and for the profound psychological uncanniness of a world without accident.

### I. PHYSICAL & ENVIRONMENTAL PROLOGUE

**The Medium:**
You will be suspended in approximately 9,000 cubic kilometers of filtered, potable-grade water. It is cool (a constant 12°C/54°F), utterly clear, and chemically stabilized. There is no current, no tide, no thermocline. This sameness is deceptive; it can lead to severe sensory disorientation. You are not swimming in a sea, you are floating in the world’s largest aquarium tank.

**Pressure:**
The Bathysphere is a sphere of water *12,000 km in diameter*. While you will likely operate in the "shallows" near the basaltic floor, the sheer volume means ambient pressure is significant and uniformly high—equivalent to the deep abyssal plains of a natural ocean (~400-600 atmospheres). **You will be crushed to paste without pressure adaptation.**

**Light & Time:**
The light source is a global grid of luminous panels simulating a perpetual, shadowless noon. There is no day/night cycle, no sunrise, no stars. Your circadian rhythm will be destroyed within hours. The light is uniform, flattening perception of depth and size. A distant gantry looks as crisp as one ten meters away, making distance estimation nearly impossible.

**Sound:**
Forget the quiet of the deep. The Bathysphere *sings* its functionality. The dominant sound is a pervasive, sub-bass **hum** from the conduit towers—a vibration felt in your viscera more than heard. Overlaying this are the distant, travelling *clangs*, *whirs*, and *hisses* of active machinery. Sound carries with terrifying clarity and little attenuation. Your own movements will be acoustically broadcast for kilometers.

**The Emptiness:**
This is the most critical environmental factor. The density of structures is so low that visual reference points are rare. In the open water, you may see nothing but blue-black void in all directions for hours. The "seafloor" is a perfectly flat, dark plane curving up to meet the shell. Vertigo and agoraphobia are common, even in experienced travelers.

### II. ESSENTIAL EQUIPMENT & PHYSIOLOGICAL PREPARATION

**A. Pressure Integration (Non-Negotiable):**
You cannot use a submarine or a rigid suit. The scale demands mobility. You must undergo **Full Cellular Pressure-Equalization Treatment (FCPET)**. This is a weeks-long regimen of nanoprobes and osmotic enhancers that saturate your tissues with stabilizing colloids, allowing your body to equalize internally to the ambient pressure. Post-treatment, you will feel a constant, dense fullness, like being hugged by a giant. Your breathing mechanics change entirely.

**B. Breathing Apparatus:**
Your lungs will be filled with water. You will breathe the Bathysphere's water via an **Atmospheric Fractionator Gill (AFG)**. This is an external unit, usually worn across the back and chest, that extracts dissolved oxygen (maintained at optimal levels by the environmental systems) and vents metabolized CO2. The sensation is like breathing cold, thick air through a regulator. The gill's gentle *whir-click-hiss* becomes your personal rhythm track. **Carry a redundant secondary unit.** Gill failure means you drown in perfectly breathable water—a uniquely Bathyspheric irony.

**C. Thermal Regulation:**
The constant 12°C will leach body heat. Undergarments must be full-body **thermosynthetic weave** that converts ambient mechanical vibration (from the ever-present hum) into warmth. An outer layer is for protection, not insulation.

**D. Propulsion & Navigation:**
*   **Propulsion:** You will need a **compact hydrojet pack**. Swimming unaided across these distances is impossible. The pack should be electric-quiet; the native fauna ignores the low hum but is startled by cavitation bubbles or loud thrust.
*   **Navigation:** The uniform lighting and lack of features make visual navigation useless. You must have a **Triangulation Implant** or handheld unit that locks onto the resonant frequencies of the conduit towers. These towers form the only reliable coordinate grid (a Cartesian matrix inscribed on the basalt floor). Your navigator will display your position as a set of alphanumeric coordinates (e.g., Tower Sector Gamma-7, Vertical Stratum 22). **Mark waypoints obsessively.** Getting lost means drifting in a featureless blue void until your gill's power fails.

**E. Communication:**
Radio is useless. Use **low-frequency acoustic pulsers** tuned to the Bathysphere's own hum spectrum. Communication is slow, text-like (encoded pulses), and public—anything you "say" can be heard by anyone or anything with a receiver within a hundred kilometers. Assume no privacy.

### III. PSYCHOLOGICAL PROTOCOLS

**1. Time Management:**
Bring multiple independent timers set to a 24-hour cycle from your point of origin. Adhere rigidly to a schedule of "wake," "work," and "sleep" periods. Without this, you will slip into a timeless stupor within days.

**2. Sensory Anchoring:**
The uniform sensory field can induce dissociative states. **Create anchors.**
*   **Touch:** Carry a textured object (a stone, a tool) from your home reality.
*   **Taste:** Use flavored nutrient pastes. The water itself is tasteless.
*   **Sound:** Curate your acoustic environment. Play soft, complex music through bone conduction to counter the monolithic industrial hum. White noise is your enemy here.

**3. Confronting the Uncanny:**
Everything you see is *young* and *sharp*. There is no erosion, no moss (except the engineered algal patches), no softening. This produces a subconscious aesthetic dread—a sense of being inside a diagram, not a world. Acknowledge this feeling. Name it: "The Precision Anxiety." It is a normal response to a universe without wear.

**4. The Solitude:**
You may be the only conscious being within ten thousand cubic kilometers. The habitat blocks are dark and empty. The feeling is not of being alone in nature, but of being alone in a *factory* built for gods, currently shut down. Prepare for this with meditation, pre-recorded dialogue, or a companion. Solo travel here is not recommended for first-timers.

### IV. HAZARDS & LOCAL INTERACTIONS

**A. The Structures:**
*   **Sharpness:** Every edge is a 90-degree angle, unbattered by time. A casual brush against a gantry crossbeam can result in a surgical laceration. Move with deliberate care.
*   **Activation:** This is a functional system. While dormant, a pump housing or filtration bank may suddenly initiate a cycle, creating violent local vortices or pressure differentials. Treat all machinery as potentially active. Listen for changes in the ambient hum—a rising pitch often precedes activation.
*   **Conduit Towers:** Do not approach within 50 meters. The vibrational field can disrupt your gill's function and induce nausea and organ resonance. The ceramic sheathing is also extremely hot from energy transfer.

**B. The Engineered Fauna:**
The ecosystem is a closed-loop nutrient recycler. It is simple, efficient, and indifferent to you.
*   **Glassfish & Algal Patches:** Harmless. The algal fuzz is the primary producer. Do not disturb it.
*   **Filter-Wings:** Placid, slow-moving giants. Give them a wide berth. Their wake is substantial.
*   **Copper-Scaled Hunters (C-Scales):** The apex predators. They are biomechanical marvels, optimized for ambushing schools of glassfish. **You are not on their prey spectrum.** However, they are curious and may investigate you. Do not panic, do not make sudden movements with your hydrojet. Hold position. They will inspect you, register you as non-prey (too large, wrong thermal signature, strange sound profile), and depart. **If you flee using high-thrust jetting, you may trigger a chase reflex.** They are faster than your pack.

**C. The Void Itself:**
The greatest hazard is the sheer, curated emptiness. It can engender a paralyzing apathy or a frantic desire to *see something*. Panic in the void leads to disorientation, reckless jetting, and eventual loss of all reference points. If you feel the void panic rising, stop. Focus on your breath (the gill's rhythm). Look at your timer. Touch your anchor object. Re-check your coordinates. Move only with purpose.

### V. SUPPLIES & LOGISTICS

**Pack for total self-sufficiency. There are no resupply points, no friendly outposts.**

*   **Nutrition:** Concentrated nutrient blocks, caloric density high. You will have little appetite; eating must be scheduled.
*   **Power:** Redundant power cells for gill, hydrojet, nav-unit, and thermal weave. Recharge via universal induction pads is theoretically possible from the conduit towers, but this requires dangerous proximity.
*   **Tool Kit:** Ceramic-bladed cutter (metal tools can react with water chemistry), bonding sealant for emergency gill/hull repairs, high-lumen directional lamp (to cut through the uniform gloom for close work), sample containers.
*   **Shelter:** You cannot sleep adrift. Bring a **collapsible anchor-hammock** to tether to a structure. Sleeping on the basaltic floor is safe but psychologically grueling.

### VI. DEPARTURE & MINDSET

Upon transit into the Bathysphere, you will materialize suspended in the medium. The first moments are critical:

1.  **Orientation:** Ignore the breathtaking, terrifying vista. Immediately activate your nav-unit. Get your coordinates.
2.  **Systems Check:** Gill flow. Thermal warmth. Hydrojet response.
3.  **Acclimatization:** Float still for five minutes. Listen. Feel the pressure. Let your mind accept that this water is your new atmosphere. You are not drowning. You are breathing a liquid sky.

You are entering a monument to a will that sought to replace chaos with order, nature with design. It is the ultimate expression of control, and yet, in its vast, empty silence, it whispers of the loneliness inherent in such control. You are a visitor to a finished dream. Respect its precision, fear its emptiness, and never forget that every single element around you, including the water in your lungs, was *put there* by an intelligence that saw a blank slate and decided to fill it with this profound, beautiful, and chilling solitude.

**Final Note:** If you hear a new sound—a melodic tone not part of the standard industrial hymn—do not investigate. Report its coordinates and direction via acoustic pulse. That is not part of the design. Its origin is unknown.
