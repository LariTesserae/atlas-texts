### II. The Gear: Your Second Skin
You are an ordinary human. The Throat will kill an ordinary human in microseconds. Therefore, your gear must become you.

4. **Traction and Buoyancy Control**: You will be weightless in a liquid tornado. The suit needs micro-thrusters (ion-drive or fluid-jet) for stabilization and movement. Magneto-adhesive soles for clinging to the basalt when you find purchase. Neutral buoyancy is a myth here; you will need constant, subtle adjustments to avoid being hurled into a shimmering curtain that can flay your suit.
